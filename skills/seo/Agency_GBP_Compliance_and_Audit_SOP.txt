AGENCY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP): GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
Document Type: Internal Skill-Set & Quality Assurance (QA) Framework
Audience: GMB Executives, Account Managers, Content Copywriters, Graphic Designers
Version: 2.0 (White-Label Agency Edition)

PURPOSE:
This guide serves as an internal agency blueprint to audit, cross-verify, and 
troubleshoot Google Business Profile (GBP) update rejections before publishing. 
Google's automated AI filters deploy strict algorithmic checks (OCR, YMYL, 
anti-spam). This document provides the skills necessary to identify and fix 
triggers across our core niches, with a primary focus on the Healthcare sector.

================================================================================
SECTION 1: THE FOUR PRIMARY REJECTION RADARS (WHY POSTS FAIL)
================================================================================

1. THE TELEMETRY FILTER (Direct Contact Data)
   - Rule: Google strictly forbids phone numbers, email addresses, and raw URLs 
     within the post body copy. 
   - Intent: Google forces agencies to use native Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons 
     so they can control click metrics and prevent direct lead scrapers.

2. THE OCR RADAR (Optical Character Recognition on Graphics)
   - Rule: Google's visual parser scans images for embedded alphabets. Graphics 
     resembling traditional newspaper display ads, high-percentage text overlays, 
     or images with phone numbers/footers embedded are auto-rejected.

3. THE YMYL ALGORITHM (Your Money or Your Life)
   - Rule: Content impacting a user's health, financial stability, or safety 
     undergoes critical semantic scanning. Absolute medical claims, diagnostic 
     jargon, or guarantees trigger hard rejections.

4. SYSTEMIC KEYWORD STUFFING (Hashtag and Meta Manipulation)
   - Rule: Piling repetitive localized keywords (e.g., #BestHospitalInCity 
     #TopClinicCity) into a single block triggers spam filters.

SECTION 2: CORE NICHE CASE STUDIES & COMPLIANCE BLUEPRINTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICHE A: HEALTHCARE & HOSPITALS (CRITICAL FOCUS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Policy Danger Zone: Direct disease management advice, diagnostic claims, 
  and listing provider mobile numbers.

❌ COMPONENT BREAKDOWN - REJECTED PATTERN:
  - Image: Flyer containing cross-sections of a disease, doctor profile pic, 
    with a thick footer band showing Phone, Email, and Website URL.
  - Text: "Control your High Blood Sugar! Our expert Diabetologist cures 
    chronic diabetes issues. Call +91 XXXXX XXXXX for a same-day appointment."
  - Why it fails: OCR flags the footer text; Text body violates contact data 
    policies; Medical algorithm flags the absolute claim ("cures chronic diabetes").

Verified Compliant Strategy:
  - Image: High-resolution, real-world photograph of a well-lit consultation 
    room, or a doctor interacting naturally with a patient (Zero text overlay).
  - Copywriting: Focus strictly on general wellness, everyday lifestyle adjustments, 
    and educational awareness. Use soft verbs ("manage", "support", "explore").

✅ AUDIT-READY TEXT TEMPLATE:
  "Prioritizing daily physical wellness doesn't mean changing your entire 
  routine overnight. Small, mindful adjustments to your everyday nutritional 
  habits can significantly support long-term vitality and steady energy levels. 
  
  If you are looking to build a balanced lifestyle plan tailored to your personal 
  routines, our specialty care team is here to guide you. Schedule an internal 
  evaluation to evaluate your wellness goals."
  [Action Button Configured: "Book Now" or "Call Now"]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICHE B: REAL ESTATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Policy Danger Zone: Explicit pricing, aggressive financing jargon, repetitive 
  links, and using stock renderings that look like computer-generated spam.

❌ COMPONENT BREAKDOWN - REJECTED PATTERN:
  - Image: Digital 3D rendering with a giant bright badge overlay: "STARTING AT 
    ONLY $450k! 85% SOLD OUT!"
  - Text: "Invest in the best 2BHK luxury apartments now. High ROI guaranteed. 
    Click here to book: www.realestatestate-link.com/project."
  - Why it fails: Price metrics mixed with urgency language ("85% Sold Out") 
    triggers promotional spam filters. Raw URLs in text cause instant drops.

Verified Compliant Strategy:
  - Image: Real photographs of completed model apartments, structural progress 
    images, or actual site architecture shots under clear daylight.
  - Copywriting: Frame around architectural features, neighborhood dynamics, 
    and lifestyle amenities.

✅ AUDIT-READY TEXT TEMPLATE:
  "Experience contemporary architectural design paired with seamless functional 
  spaces. Our newest residential development focuses on maximizing natural light, 
  open-concept floor plans, and close proximity to premium neighborhood spaces. 
  
  Explore the site layout and schedule an accompanied walk-through with our 
  property consulting team."
  [Action Button Configured: "Learn More" linking directly to the landing page]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICHE C: EDUCATION & INSTITUTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Policy Danger Zone: Guaranteed job placement claims, specific pricing structures, 
  unverified academic credentials, or using generic stock photos of students.

❌ COMPONENT BREAKDOWN - REJECTED PATTERN:
  - Image: Banner graphic with corporate logos and text: "100% Placement 
    Guaranteed! Batch starts Monday!"
  - Text: "Enroll in our Data Science program for just $999. Admissions closing 
    soon. Contact admissions@edu-domain.com to lock in your seat."
  - Why it fails: "100% Guaranteed" commercial claims trigger commercial fraud 
    algorithms. Raw email address inside the body copy is an instant infraction.

Verified Compliant Strategy:
  - Image: Candid, authentic pictures of actual lecture halls, campus facilities, 
    workshops, or interactive lab equipment.
  - Copywriting: Center the narrative on curriculum modules, skill development, 
    and academic environment.

✅ AUDIT-READY TEXT TEMPLATE:
  "Advancing your professional skill set requires a comprehensive, hands-on 
  curriculum. Our technical educational pathways focus on deep-dive modules, 
  practical project experience, and collaborative learning environments. 
  
  Review our upcoming course structures and curriculum timelines to find the 
  right track for your career objectives."
  [Action Button Configured: "Sign Up" or "Learn More"]

SECTION 3: THE INTERNAL 4-STEP CROSS-CHECK AUDIT PROCESS
================================================================================
Before publishing any client update on GBP, the Account Executive must check off 
every step in this protocol:

STEP 1: CAPTION CLEANSE (TEXT CHECK)
  - Scan for symbols: 📞, ✉️, 🌐, 📍. Ensure no string resembling an email, 
    phone number, or link exists behind these icons.
  - Check adjectives: Remove absolute words like "Cure", "Guaranteed", "100%", 
    "Cheapest", "Best".

STEP 2: GRAPHIC DE-MARKETING (IMAGE CHECK)
  - Run the "Thumb Test": If the image looks like a flyer you would hand out 
    on a street corner, do not post it to GBP.
  - Ensure any phone numbers or website URLs embedded in the image footer 
    are cropped or removed entirely.
  - Prioritize real photos over graphic designs.

STEP 3: ACTION BUTTON INTEGRATION (CTA CHECK)
  - Ensure the destination link attached to "Book", "Buy", or "Learn More" 
    matches the exact domain verified on the core GBP listing profile. 
    Cross-domain redirects can cause flags.

STEP 4: REJECTION REMEDIATION LIFECYCLE
  - If a post gets rejected: Do NOT keep trying to post variations of the same 
    text immediately. This flags the profile for spam behavioral loops.
  - Protocol: Delete the draft entirely, wait 15 minutes, switch the creative 
    to a zero-text real photo, use heavily simplified, non-promotional text, 
    and re-attempt.
