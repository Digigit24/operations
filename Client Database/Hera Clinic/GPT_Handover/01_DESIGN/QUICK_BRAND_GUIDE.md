# Quick Brand Guide

## Current proposed system — needs client confirmation

- Personality: calm, reassuring, educational, sophisticated, compassionate and medically intelligent.
- Core colours: Hera Magenta `#E80088`; Hera Indigo `#283090`.
- Canvas: white, warm ivory and champagne-beige neutrals; avoid dominant pink photo lighting.
- Typography: refined editorial serif for major headlines plus clean sans-serif for body/CTA. Use no more than two families. Exact licensed fonts are not confirmed.
- Logo: use uploaded native artwork unchanged. Preferred on white/warm ivory. Maintain clear space equal to the symbol head diameter. Never stretch, redraw, recolour, box or add effects.
- Layout: generous negative space, structured grid, one clear focal point, short copy and strong hierarchy.
- Photography: realistic Indian women, natural skin texture, calm expressions, warm daylight, modern clinical/editorial setting. Avoid stereotypical stock poses, graphic medical imagery and exaggerated emotion.
- CTA: calm action language—`Book Consultation`, `Call +91 88059 81909`, `Learn More`. Avoid panic or guaranteed outcomes.
- Address: omit until wing conflict is resolved.

## Legacy/unverified system

- Dusty Rose `#C98FA1`, Soft Blush `#E8C7D1` and deep plum/editorial neutrals appeared in older instructions.
- Treat these as supporting/legacy colours only until the client confirms the revised system.

## Hard restrictions

- No medical guarantees, cure claims, fear tactics, before/after treatment claims or unsupported rankings.
- No patient identity/story without documented consent.
- No address, price, success rate, patient count or rating until verified.
- Do not say `best` in paid/promotional creative without compliance review; live website usage is not approval.
