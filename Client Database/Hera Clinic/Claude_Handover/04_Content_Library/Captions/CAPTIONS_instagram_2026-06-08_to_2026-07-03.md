## 3 · Fri 12 Jun · Educational — What PCOS is + common signs · IG square
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal imbalance that affects the ovaries and overall health — and many women live with it without knowing. Common signs: irregular periods, acne or oily skin, weight gain, hair thinning, mood changes and fatigue, and sometimes difficulty conceiving. Early diagnosis and the right care make a real difference.

## 11 · Wed 1 Jul · CAROUSEL (5 slides) — Delivery Myth Buster
C-section or normal delivery — which is "better"?

Neither. And the pressure to pick a "right" answer causes far more worry than it should.

In this Myth Buster series, we clear up three of the most common beliefs about delivery:

❌ "C-section moms didn't really give birth." → Every mother who carries and delivers a baby, by any method, is extraordinary.

Trust your doctor. Trust your body. Both are enough. 💗
