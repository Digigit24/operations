# HERA CLINIC — DESIGN QUICKSTART (read before starting any creative)
Active system: Doc 02 Design Instructions **v2** (in 01_Source_Documents). Notion tracks a v3 — check there before major work. Full rules live in the source doc; this is the 2-minute version.

## Brand personality
Founder-led, hyper-local, editorial-premium women's healthcare. Calm, reassuring, medically intelligent. NOT a Canva fertility template, NOT a metro IVF chain.

## Colors
**Brand DNA (never recolor):** Hera Magenta `#E80088` (accent of last resort, ≤5% of canvas) · Hera Indigo `#283090` (workhorse — headlines, dark backgrounds)
**Editorial neutrals:** Warm Ivory `#FAF7F5` (default bg) · Champagne `#EFE1D6` · Soft Blush `#E8C7D1` · Muted Mauve `#B68FA3` · Deep Plum `#4B2E39` (CTA bg) · Charcoal `#2D2D2D` (body)
**Pink budget:** Blush + Mauve ≤ 25% of canvas. Never magenta-on-indigo or indigo-on-magenta.

## Fonts
Playfair Display (headlines) + Inter (body). One serif + one sans only. Never italicize the doctor's name; credentials line always accompanies it: **MBBS, DNB OBGY, FMAS**.

## Logo (file: hera-clinic-logo_official.png, 504×280px, near-black background — request transparent master from client)
- Native two-color on ivory/champagne/white; white version on plum/indigo; NEVER on magenta.
- Clearspace = height of the head dot. Min 64px digital / 20mm print. No recoloring, stretching, shadows, containers.
- Placement: bottom-left on photo posts, 12% canvas width.

## Layout
Max **3 modules** per canvas (hook / body / trust+CTA). IG square: 64px padding. Story: 220px top + 380px bottom safe. GMB: design 1200×900, content centered in 1080×720.

## CTA style
Rounded rect, 12px radius, Deep Plum bg, white Inter 600 label. Approved labels ONLY: Book Consultation · Schedule a Visit · Talk to Dr. Sairandhri · Call +91 88059 81909 · WhatsApp Us. Banned: Guaranteed Results, 100% Success, Miracle Treatment, Best in India, "Consult Our Surgeon".

## Image style (AI-generated)
90–95% photorealistic, Indian women age-matched to topic, soft daylight 5200–5600K, visible skin texture, editorial asymmetry with 30–40% negative space, NO text inside the image. Hard avoids: cartoon anatomy, hearts/sparkles, stethoscope-around-neck, pregnant-belly-hands stock pose, pink/magenta wash, Western-only models. Master prompt template: 01_Source_Documents/Hera_Clinic_02_AI_Prompting_Guide.md §4.

## Important restrictions
- **No address on any creative** until wing (B/C/D) is resolved. Phone + "Punawale, Pune" only.
- Disclaimer on every clinical claim (Inter 8–10pt, Mauve #B68FA3): *"Individual results vary. Consult Dr. Sairandhri Kate-Shinde for medical advice specific to you."*
- Use **PCOS** — do not use "PMOS" without doctor sign-off (see CONFLICTS_REGISTER #2).
- Trust claims only from the allow-list (see CLIENT_MASTER_FACTS).

## Post dimensions
IG square 1080×1080 · IG carousel 1080×1080 (or 1080×1350) · Story/Reel 1080×1920 · GMB 1200×900 · Website hero 1920×800 desktop / 750×1100 mobile · LinkedIn 1200×627 · WhatsApp 1080×1080 (≥36px type, no thin lines/magenta gradients)

## Common client/QA revision requests observed
Wrong terminology (PMOS→PCOS), unverified address printed, off-list CTAs, missing disclaimers, hours mismatch, pink/hearts/sparkles overload, name-casing errors ("Dr. Sairandhri kate - shinde"), plural "Hera Clinics", unverified statistics.
