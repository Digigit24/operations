# Audience Profiles

## Segment 1 — US CPA Firm Partners / Managing Directors (Namrata's audience)
**Who:** Partners, Managing Partners, MDs, Directors of Operations, COOs at US accounting firms (11-200 headcount), primarily in California, Texas, New York, Illinois, Florida.
**Pain points:** CPA talent shortage crisis, 20-30% staff attrition, seasonal hiring pressure, manual audit processes, delayed client reporting, high cost per audit hour, rising PCAOB scrutiny, partner burnout.
**What moves them:** Data-backed comparisons (e.g., $73K-$95K salary-gap figures), audit-grade governance framing (not "cheap labor"), case studies of Big 4 India operations, myth-busting on quality/security/client disclosure.
**Content territory:** GCC setup process/costs/timelines, India vs. other offshore destinations, talent benchmarks, US-India workflow mechanics, IRC §7216 compliance.

## Segment 2 — Indian Startup Founders / CFOs (Kamal's + Brand Page audience)
**Who:** Founders and CFOs at growth-stage Indian companies considering IPO, business setup, entity restructuring, or strategic collaborations.
**Pain points:** Uncertainty on entity structure choice (WOS/LLP/Branch/Liaison), IPO readiness gaps, JV structuring risk, delays from avoidable process mistakes.
**What moves them:** Step-by-step frameworks, decision trees, readiness checklists with scoring.

## Segment 3 — CFOs / Finance Directors / Compliance Heads (Nidhi's audience)*
**Who:** CFOs managing regulatory complexity, India-US cross-border operations, NRIs with Indian entities, startups pre-institutional-funding.
**Pain points:** FEMA/RBI/MCA filing complexity, transfer pricing documentation gaps, cross-border tax traps, corporate governance readiness before funding rounds.
*See Conflict 1 — this audience description matches the "Compliance, Taxation & DD" framing, not the alternate "Internal Audit" framing used in the Lead Strategy doc.

## Segment 4 — Mid-Market Founders / PE-Backed Companies / Family Businesses (Vikas's audience)
**Who:** Founders considering exits or acquisitions, PE-backed companies, family businesses evaluating succession.
**Pain points:** Valuation multiple confusion, due diligence red flags killing deals late, deal-structure risk allocation, post-merger integration failure (70% of M&A value destruction happens in first 100 days per the content library).

## Segment 5 — Korean Executives (Korea landing page audience)
**Who:** CFOs, Strategy Heads, Group Heads-India, VCs at Korean companies evaluating India entry, M&A, or GCC setup — spans Chaebol-adjacent mid-market manufacturers (EV/battery, auto components, electronics, specialty chemicals) and Korean investment officers (Mirae Asset, KIC, KB, Hana-style profiles).
**Pain points:** No board-ready KRW cost picture for India entry, tax/withholding structure uncertainty, inability to trust an Indian target's financials without audit-grade DD, existing India subsidiaries feeling like an ungoverned "black box."
**What moves them:** Trust signals surfaced early (ICAI registration, ISO cert, peer review — not buried in a footer), numbers-as-design treatment, dense factual content (Korean B2B audiences read landing pages thoroughly, per the doc's own designer note), FAQ sections as the primary objection-clearing layer.
