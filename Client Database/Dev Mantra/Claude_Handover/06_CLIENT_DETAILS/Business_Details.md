## The Core Market Thesis (US CPA Firms)

Dev Mantra's positioning material frames the US CPA profession as under structural pressure: talent shortage crisis, rising audit complexity, increased PCAOB scrutiny, margin compression, partner burnout (with cited pain points of 20-30% staff attrition, seasonal hiring pressure, manual audit processes, delayed client reporting, high cost per audit hour).

Their pitch: move CPA firms from "labor arbitrage" (the failed old outsourcing model — low-cost BPO mindset, weak audit documentation, no regulatory alignment) to "Automation-First + Audit-Grade Governance" — an India-based GCC model with a 3-layer structure:
1. **Automation Engine** (AI audit tools, workflow orchestration, automated sampling, smart document management, exception tracking)
2. **Expert Delivery Team, India** (audit associates, tax compliance specialists, financial reporting analysts, data & analytics, internal QA)
3. **CPA Partner Oversight, USA** (final review, client advisory, regulatory sign-off, PCAOB-aligned documentation review)

Claimed cumulative impact of this model: 25-40% faster audit turnaround, 40-60% cost efficiency, 20-35% reduction in cost per engagement, 24-hour work cycles, 99% documentation accuracy, 50% increase in engagement capacity.

**Engagement models offered:** Model A (Dedicated GCC — full India audit team, long-term, custom automation stack), Model B (Hybrid — core automation engine + seasonal capacity scaling), Model C (Managed Automation Service — process transformation + tech implementation only, no dedicated team).
