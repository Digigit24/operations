# Client Preferences & Common Revision Requests

## Documented Design Pivot (the clearest evidence of an actual client preference in this workspace)
Per `DevMantra_Personal_Brand_Design_System.md` Section 6.4: the client's own feedback rejected the dark-navy/glassmorphic system as looking "too AI-generated," triggering a switch to a "White Editorial Carousel System." The rejected traits, named explicitly in the document itself:
- Dark gradients + glassmorphic cards
- Glowing cyan/blue borders on everything
- Ambient color blobs everywhere
- Dark navy backgrounds as the default
- "Everything floating on blue"

The preferred replacement traits:
- White `#ffffff` or off-white `#f4f6fb` as default background
- Typography as the primary design element
- Thin 1px `#eeeeee` rules as row separators
- 4px left-border callout boxes (no rounded corners, no shadow)
- Flat solid bars in data charts (no gradients, no glow, no 3D)
- Photorealistic images that look like editorial photographs, not stock photos
- Generous whitespace

**This is the single most important documented client preference in the project** — apply it as the default going forward rather than the earlier all-dark system.

## AI Prompting Philosophy (explicitly finalized after a design session)
"Tell the AI: Concept + Feeling + Key Facts + Palette + Name. Do NOT tell it: Layout + Composition + Structure + Where to place elements." Over-directing produces generic AI output; under-directing with strong conceptual framing produces premium editorial results.

## Carousel Rules (non-negotiable per source docs)
- One idea per slide
- One visual element per slide (photorealistic image OR diagram, never both)
- Break content across more slides rather than cramming into fewer

## Content Voice Preferences
- No exclamation marks (or max one, deliberately used)
- No corporate press-release openings ("We are proud/pleased/excited to announce")
- Closings must be a thought or question, never a direct pitch — this is stated as a rule, not a suggestion, in both the brand voice skill and the LinkedIn strategy doc
- Personal profiles write in first person ("I"); company brand page never does

## Common Revision Pattern Identified
The Personal Brand Design System document itself is essentially a record of an iterative design session — for each of the 4 personal profiles, it shows a "Round 1" and sometimes "Round 2" of alternate palette options, suggesting the client/design team iterated multiple times before landing on final approved options. This suggests: expect multiple palette/concept rounds to be normal for this client, budget time for it.
