## 2. WHY THIS STRUCTURE

**Every day of the working week has a Dev Mantra presence on LinkedIn.** A prospective client who follows any combination of these five accounts sees Dev Mantra-associated content Monday through Friday without repetition or overlap.

**Each person builds a distinct audience.** Vikas attracts M&A-minded founders and promoters. Nidhi attracts CFOs and compliance heads. Namrata attracts CPA firm partners and GCC decision-makers. Kamal attracts founders evaluating business setup and capital markets options. These are four different buyer profiles, all relevant to Dev Mantra.

**The brand page collects all of it.** Through collab tagging, every personal post also appears on the brand page. Someone who discovers the brand page sees a full-spectrum advisory firm. Someone who discovers an individual sees a focused specialist.

## 4. CONTENT FORMAT RULES

### Personal Profiles (Monday–Thursday) — Articles & Text

The four personal profiles write, not design. The content reads as genuine professional perspective.

- **Primary:** Text posts (400-700 words), LinkedIn articles (1,000-2,000 words)
- **Occasional:** Single-image post with a data hook, document/PDF post
- **Avoid:** Carousels on personal profiles. A carousel on a personal feed looks like branded marketing, not a professional sharing their thinking.
- **Voice:** First person. "I" is appropriate. Opinions framed as opinions. Experience shared directly.
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 per post. Light. Heavy hashtag use on personal profiles undermines the personal voice.

### Brand Page (Friday) — Carousels & Visual Content

The brand page is the library. Content is designed to be swiped, saved, and referenced later.

- **Primary:** Carousels (8-12 slides) — checklists, comparisons, cost breakdowns, frameworks
- **Secondary:** Infographics, stat cards, compliance calendars
- **Occasional:** Short text for regulatory alerts
- **Voice:** Third-person or "we." Never "I." Never promotional. Useful first.
- **Captions:** 150-300 words. The carousel does the work. The caption frames it.
- **Hashtags:** 20-25 per post in three tiers (broad, niche, brand). Always include #DevMantra.

## 5. AMPLIFICATION — HOW PERSONAL PROFILES BOOST THE BRAND PAGE

The brand page's organic reach is limited by LinkedIn's algorithm, which deprioritises company pages relative to personal profiles. The four personal profiles fix this.

**When a brand page post goes live (Friday):**
1. All four personal profiles engage within 60 minutes — a substantive comment (minimum 2 sentences), a like, and if the content is relevant to their vertical, a reshare with personal commentary.
2. This early engagement from four connected profiles tells the algorithm the content has traction and pushes it to a wider audience.

**When a personal profile post goes live (Monday–Thursday):**
1. The other three profiles engage within 60 minutes — comment and like at minimum.
2. The brand page likes the post.
3. Because the post is collab-tagged, it already appears on the brand page. The cross-engagement ensures it gets distributed through all four personal networks simultaneously.

**Daily engagement (all four profiles, every working day):**
- 3-5 comments on relevant posts from people in their target audience
- Reply to every comment on their own posts within the same day

## 7. PAID BOOST — BRAND PAGE FRIDAY POSTS

The brand page's Friday carousel posts are the primary boost candidates.

**Budget:** ₹500 per day, run for 2-3 days per boosted post.

**Frequency:** 2-3 boosted posts per month. Not every Friday post gets boosted — only the ones that show organic traction in the first 24 hours.

**Selection rule:** If a Friday carousel gets above-average engagement by Saturday morning, boost it Saturday through Monday. Paid spend amplifies what is already working. It does not rescue what is not.

**Targeting:** Match the content topic.
- Virtual CFO content → Target Indian startup founders, CFOs, SME promoters
- GCC/CPA firm data → Target US-based accounting professionals, CPA firm partners
- Compliance/regulatory content → Target CFOs, finance directors, compliance heads in India and US
- Use job title, industry, company size, and geography filters in LinkedIn Campaign Manager

**What to track per boost:**
- Paid impressions vs. organic impressions
- Click-through rate to lead magnet
- Cost per click
- New followers gained during the boost window
- Any profile visits or DMs attributable to the boost

**Monthly paid budget:** ₹3,000-4,500 (2-3 boosts × ₹1,000-1,500 each)
