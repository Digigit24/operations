# Carousel Content — Design Rules & Friday Brand-Page Calendar

## Where Carousels Fit
Carousels are used **only** on the Dev Mantra brand page (Fridays) — never on the four personal profiles, which use text posts and articles. ("A carousel on a personal feed looks like branded marketing, not a professional sharing their thinking." — LinkedIn Strategy v3.)

## Canvas & Structure Rules (from Personal Brand Design System + Infographic KB)
- Canvas: **1080×1440px (3:4 ratio)** locked for all carousels (note: the infographic KB separately lists 1080×1350px as the general "carousel slide" default — confirm which applies before production, see `01_DESIGN/Brand_Quick_Reference.md`)
- One idea per slide, non-negotiable
- One visual element per slide — photorealistic image OR diagram, never both
- Footer: `devmantra.com` (or individual's name + devmantra.com) on every slide

## Slide-by-Slide Formula (from Brand Voice Skill)
| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 (Cover) | Hook only. Large type. Date/source line small underneath. |
| 2 (Context) | The problem or situation being addressed. 3-5 sentences max. |
| 3-4 (Body) | One idea per slide. Clear headline at top. 4-6 sentences of body. |
| 5 (Closing) | The implication or business question — not a services slide. |
| 6+ (optional) | Source/credit attribution and Dev Mantra branding. |

## Carousel Companion Caption Formula
Hook (one strong line) → Bridge (one sentence on what the carousel shows) → Body (3-5 lines max, key insight only) → Close ("Swipe for the full breakdown" — the one acceptable CTA) → Hashtags (full 20-25 tag set). Caption length: 150-300 words.

## Design Concept Types for Carousel Slides (from the Infographic Prompt KB — 8 types)
1. Shadow Concept (hidden value/myths) — e.g. coin → dollar-sign shadow
2. Split Reveal (surface vs. depth) — e.g. rough stone → diamond interior
3. Simple 3D Icon (single clean object)
4. Physical Metaphor (relay baton, alarm clock, delivered package)
5. Path/Journey Illustration (four roads splitting = entity decision)
6. Data Viz as Image (bar chart, iceberg proportion, dashboard dials)
7. Editorial Thumbnail (dark, cinematic, for blog headers — not carousel slides)
8. Typographic Structural (flags, checklists, dial labels)
