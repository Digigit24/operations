## Amplification Protocol (every week, all profiles)
- When a personal-profile post goes live: the other 3 profiles comment + like within 60 minutes; the brand page likes it.
- When the Friday brand-page post goes live: all 4 personal profiles engage within 60 minutes — comment (min. 2 sentences), like, and reshare with commentary if relevant to their vertical.
- Daily (all profiles): 3-5 comments on relevant posts from the target audience; reply to every comment on their own posts same-day.
