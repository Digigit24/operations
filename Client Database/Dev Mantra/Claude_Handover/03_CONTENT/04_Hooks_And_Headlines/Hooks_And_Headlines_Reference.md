# Hooks, Headlines & Closing Lines — Reference Library
*Source: `LinkedIn_writer_devmantra` (brand voice skill). These are the actual approved patterns and worked examples, not generic advice.*

## Hook Rules
- One sentence. Stands alone. Forces a pause.
- Never start with "We are pleased to announce," a question with an obvious answer, or "In today's world."
- Lead with the most interesting fact, tension, or implication.
- Must work within LinkedIn's ~210-character "see more" cutoff.

## Carousel Title Naming Patterns
- **Number-led:** "3 Things the India-Brazil Summit Actually Decided" / "10 Agreements. 91 Days. Here Is What Matters."
- **Data-led:** "$73K. The Salary Gap US CPA Firms Are Sitting On." / "Pay 10% Instead of 100% — CCFS-2026 Explained"
- **Stakes-led:** "MCA Just Opened a Compliance Relief Window. It Closes July 15." / "Your ROC Backlog Has a 91-Day Exit Window."
- **Reframe-led:** "What the India-Brazil Summit Was Actually About" / "The CPA Talent Crisis Has a Different Solution Than You Think"

## Closing Line Patterns (never a pitch)
- "The question for leadership teams is whether your business is positioned to benefit — or still structured around the dependencies they are designed to replace."
- "The firms still waiting are running the same team cost model they had five years ago."
- "The window is 91 days. It does not return."

## Worked Example — Hook, Correct vs. Incorrect

**INCORRECT:** "We at Dev Mantra are excited to share important updates about the newly notified Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme 2026, which presents a valuable opportunity for companies to regularize their compliance status!"
*Why it fails: starts with "we," uses "excited," exclamation mark, buries the news.*

**CORRECT:** "MCA just opened a compliance relief window for defaulting companies. It runs from 15 April 2026 to 15 July 2026. After that, regular penalties and enforcement action resume."
*Why it works: immediate stakes, plain language, specific dates.*

## Worked Example — Closing, Correct vs. Incorrect

**INCORRECT:** "Dev Mantra's expert team is here to help you navigate these complex regulations. Reach out today for a consultation and let us guide your compliance journey!"

**CORRECT:** "This is a finite window for a specific category of liability. Whether your company has one year's pending filing or several, the cost arithmetic under CCFS-2026 will almost always be materially better than what follows after July 15."

## Banned Hook/Body Words (partial list — full list in Content_References)
Transformative, game-changing, pivotal, crucial, vital, leverage (as verb), synergy, exciting, thrilled, deep dive, unpack, empower, innovative, seamless, robust, holistic, landscape (abstract), testament, stakeholders, journey (metaphorical), "the CFO space."

## Five Post-Type Hook Formulas
1. **Regulatory/Policy update:** hook = plain-language headline of what changed
2. **Data/Comparison:** hook = the most surprising or commercially significant number
3. **Geopolitical/Macro:** hook = the single most consequential fact
4. **Thought Leadership:** hook = a counterintuitive or underexplored angle
5. **Carousel Companion Caption:** hook = same rules, one strong line, then "bridge" sentence on what the carousel shows
