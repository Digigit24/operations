# Performance Targets & KPI Guarantees
*Source: `Dev_Mantra_marketing_Agreement_1.pdf`, Section 3. These are the agency's contractual performance guarantees to Dev Mantra — treat as commitments to check against, not aspirational marketing copy.*

## Month 1-3: Foundation Building
- Website traffic: 30% increase (target: 2,000-3,000/month)
- First page rankings: 3-5 keywords
- LinkedIn company followers: 2,500
- YouTube subscribers: 50-100
- Qualified leads: 10-15/month

## Month 4-6: Growth Phase
- Website traffic: 3,000-8,000/month
- First page rankings: 8-12 keywords
- Domain Authority: 35-38
- LinkedIn company followers: 4,000
- YouTube subscribers: 200-400

## Month 7-12: Optimization & Scale
- Website traffic: 12,000-18,000/month
- First page rankings: 15-20 keywords
- Domain Authority: 40-45
- LinkedIn company followers: 7,000+
- YouTube subscribers: 1,000-1,500

## Key Performance Indicators (tracked on the stated cadence)
- Website traffic and conversion rates — monthly
- SEO keyword rankings — monthly
- Social media engagement and follower growth — bi-weekly
- Lead generation and quality — daily
- ROI on paid advertising campaigns

## Cross-Check: Baseline vs. Target (from the competitor analysis, dated Jan 30 2026)
| Metric | Baseline (per competitor analysis) | Month 1-3 Target (per agreement) |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn followers | 1,291 | 2,500 |

| Organic traffic | 2K-5K/month (competitor analysis) | 2,000-3,000/month by Month 3, 12,000-18,000 by Month 12 |

**Note:** The competitor analysis's own baseline (2K-5K/month) is already close to or above the Month 1-3 target range (2,000-3,000/month) in the agreement — worth flagging to the client/agency to confirm which baseline number is current, since the two documents were produced around the same time (Jan-Feb 2026) but don't perfectly align.
