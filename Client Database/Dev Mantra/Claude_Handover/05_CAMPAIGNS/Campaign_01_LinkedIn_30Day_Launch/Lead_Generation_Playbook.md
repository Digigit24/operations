### ⚙️ Connection Request Protocol (All Profiles)

**Rules — non-negotiable:**
- No pitch in the request
- No link
- Under 300 characters
- Reference something specific — their company, industry, or a shared professional context
- After acceptance → no immediate DM. Let content appear in their feed for 1–2 weeks first
- If they engage with a post → warm follow-up DM is appropriate. Reference the specific comment

**Templates:**

> **Template A — Cold, industry match**
> *"Hi [Name], I work in [M&A / internal audit / GCC / IPO advisory] in India — your background in [X] caught my attention. Would value connecting with someone working on similar areas."*

> **Template B — Post engagement (warm)**
> *"Hi [Name], I commented on your recent post about [topic]. Your perspective was useful — would be glad to stay connected."*

> **Template C — Mutual context**
> *"Hi [Name], we're both connected to [Name/Company]. I'm in [vertical] — would value being in each other's networks."*

### 📋 AND/OR Operator Quick Reference

| Operator | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `AND` (between fields) | Narrows results | Combining Job Title + Industry + Geography |
| `OR` (within a field) | Broadens within that dimension | Multiple job titles: Founder OR CEO OR Promoter |
| Quotes `" "` | Exact phrase match | "Managing Partner", "internal audit" |
| `-` (minus) | Excludes term | -recruiter -"talent acquisition" |

> **Saved list naming convention:** Always append the date — e.g., *"US CPA Partners Apr26"* — so you know when to refresh. Recommended refresh cycle: every 60 days.

### 🔗 Method 2 — LinkedIn Free Boolean Search

Type Boolean logic directly into LinkedIn's People search bar. Limited to ~100 results but instant and free.

**Example:**
```
(Founder OR CEO OR Promoter) AND (IPO OR "business setup" OR "joint venture") AND India
```

**What's available on LinkedIn free:**

| Filter | Available? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keywords | ✅ Yes | Use Boolean logic in search bar |
| Location | ✅ Yes | City or country level |
| Current Company | ✅ Yes | Good for targeting specific firms |
| Industry | ✅ Yes | People search filter |
| Connection degree | ✅ Yes | 1st, 2nd, 3rd+ |
| School / University | ✅ Yes | Warm outreach opportunity |
| Job Title | ⚠️ Limited | Results capped ~100 |
| Company headcount | ❌ No | Sales Navigator only |

### 💬 Method 3 — Comment Farming (Free · Highest Quality Leads)

Find posts in your niche with 50+ comments. Every commenter is pre-qualified — they are actively engaged with your exact topic.

**Process:**
1. Search for a relevant hashtag or keyword post
2. Sort by **Latest** — find posts with high comment counts
3. Click every commenter → check profile → if relevant, connect
4. Message: *"I saw your comment on [post topic] — solid point. Would value connecting."*

**Accept rate on this method: 50–70%**

### 📣 Method 7 — Organic Lead Mining (Zero Cost · Compounds Over Time)

#### Event Attendee Mining
LinkedIn shows everyone who has attended or expressed interest in events — these people have self-identified as interested in a specific topic.

**Process:**
1. Search LinkedIn Events → filter by topic
2. Click "Attendees" tab → browse by title
3. Connect with: *"I saw you attended [Event] — I'm in the [M&A / internal audit / GCC / IPO] space and would value connecting."*

**Target events per profile:**
- **Vikas:** India M&A Conclave · India PE/VC Summit · Business Succession Webinars
- **Nidhi:** IIA India Annual Conference · Risk Management India Summits · Internal Audit Workshops
- **Namrata:** CPA Firm Growth Summit · NASSCOM GCC Conclave · Accounting Outsourcing Webinars
- **Kamal:** SME IPO Workshops by BSE/NSE · Startup India events · SEBI investor education programs

---

#### Competitor Follower Mining
Competitors identified in the Dev Mantra analysis: CFO Bridge · CFO Services · Grant Thornton · Resurgent India · CFOSME

Their followers are pre-qualified Dev Mantra prospects.

**Process:**
1. Go to competitor's LinkedIn page
2. Check recent posts → click who liked/commented
3. View their profile → if relevant → connect with context: *"I noticed you engage with content in the [CFO/GCC/internal audit] space — I'm in the same area and would value connecting."*

#### Inbound via Content (Profile Views → Connections)
Every performing post generates profile views. These are warm, inbound leads.

**Process:**
1. Post goes live → profile views spike within 48 hours
2. Check "Who viewed your profile" → filter by relevant titles
3. Connect with: *"I noticed you viewed my profile — happy to connect if the content I share on [M&A / internal audit / GCC / IPO] is relevant to what you're working on."*

**Accept rate: 60–80%** — they already came to you
