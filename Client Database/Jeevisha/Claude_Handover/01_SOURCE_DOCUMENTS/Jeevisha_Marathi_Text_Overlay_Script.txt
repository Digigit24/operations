Jeevisha Marathi Text Overlay Script System
AI Instruction Framework for Creating Viral Marathi Medical Storytelling Reels

Based on the existing Jeevisha scripts and performance patterns.

CORE OBJECTIVE

These scripts are not educational explainers.

They are emotional micro-stories disguised as medical cases.

The goal is:

Make the viewer emotionally stop scrolling
Create curiosity
Build trust in the doctor
Make chronic pain feel understood
Position Jeevisha as the “last hope after failed treatments”
Create urgency without sounding salesy
Make people save/share/send the reel to family

The viewer should feel:

“This sounds exactly like someone at my home.”

NOT:

“This is a medical awareness video.”

SCRIPT FORMAT STRUCTURE

Every script follows this exact emotional storytelling arc:

1. HOOK (First 2–4 lines)

Start with:

A shocking symptom
A strange daily-life limitation
An emotional struggle
A hidden suffering
A specific behavior

The hook must instantly create curiosity.

Examples:
“तो 9 बोटांनी typing करायचा”
“तीने बाहेर जाणं बंद केलं”
“त्याने खाणं बंद केलं होतं”
“4 महिन्यांपासून तो bed वर झोपलाच नव्हता”

The hook should NOT start with disease names.

Never start with:

“आज आपण sciatica बद्दल बोलणार आहोत”
“This patient had cervical radiculopathy”
“Symptoms of CRPS are…”

That kills retention immediately.

2. HUMAN SUFFERING SECTION

After the hook:
show how the condition affected normal life.

Use:

Family impact
Sleep problems
Job stress
Emotional breakdown
Social withdrawal
Fear
Embarrassment
Hopelessness

Examples:

Manager ला कळलं तर job जाईल
नातवंडांनाही ओळखू शकत नव्हता
स्वतःच्या मुलालाही स्पर्श करू देत नव्हती
Surgery चा डर pain पेक्षा मोठा होता

This is the emotional engine of the script.

3. FAILED SYSTEM / FAILED TREATMENTS

Almost every script contains:

Multiple doctors
Wrong diagnosis
Endless tablets
Surgery recommendation
Waiting for months
“Everything is normal”
“Live with it”

This creates:

Viewer frustration
Emotional validation
Positioning for Jeevisha

Important:
Never aggressively insult other doctors.

Instead:
show that the “pain generator” was missed.

Tone:
smart, calm, diagnostic.

4. THE TURNING POINT

This is where the doctor notices:

One ignored symptom
One scan detail
One nerve pattern
One functional clue

Examples:

“Pain movement मध्ये नव्हता — rest मध्ये होता”
“MRI मध्ये exactly कोणती nerve दबली आहे दिसलं”
“हा gynaec problem नव्हताच”

This creates:

Authority
Intelligence
Trust

Doctor should sound observant.
Not dramatic.

5. THE PROCEDURE SECTION

This is extremely important.

The treatment is described:

minimally
confidently
precisely

The procedure should sound:

advanced
targeted
modern
non-surgical
image-guided
scientific

Common wording style:

“One needle. Imaging. Precision.”
“nerve पर्यंत थेट पोहोचलो”
“Ultrasound guidance ने”
“targeted block”
“exact source”

DO NOT over-explain medicine.

No long technical teaching.

Keep it cinematic and minimal.

6. EMOTIONAL RESOLUTION

The ending is never:

“Pain reduced by 80%”
“VAS score improved”

Instead show:

Human moment
Emotional recovery
Return to life

Examples:

“त्याने स्वतः नाश्ता बनवला”
“ती walk ला गेली”
“तो parking मध्ये रडला”
“त्याने मुलाचा हात धरला”

This is the emotional payoff.

7. FINAL CTA STYLE

The CTA is subtle.

Never hard-sell.

Never:

“Book now”
“Limited slots”
“Call today”
“Best pain clinic”

Instead use:

“Follow करा”
“Save करा”
“Share करा”
“Someone at home needs to know this exists”

Tone:
empathetic + hopeful.

LANGUAGE STYLE RULES
PRIMARY LANGUAGE
Marathi
SECONDARY LANGUAGE
English medical terminology

Examples:

nerve
MRI
surgery
ultrasound
PRP
cervical epidural
imaging
disc bulge
migraine
painkillers

This hybrid language is critical.

Pure Marathi sounds unnatural in modern urban medical content.

Pure English loses local emotional connection.

WRITING STYLE RULES
Sentence Style
Very short lines
One thought per line
Designed for text overlay pacing
2–8 words per line ideally
PACING RULES

Every line should feel:

cinematic
pausable
emotionally weighted

Viewer should subconsciously anticipate:

“What happened next?”

TONE PROFILE

The tone is:

emotionally intelligent
observant
calm
clinical
empathetic
understated
non-salesy

NOT:

loud
motivational
preachy
fear-mongering
overdramatic
EMOTIONAL THEMES THAT WORK BEST
Highest-performing emotional triggers:
“Nobody listened”
“Everything looked normal”
“Surgery was recommended”
“Pain affected family life”
“Young age suffering”
“Hidden pain”
“Failed treatments”
“Loss of identity”
“Finally someone understood the problem”
CHARACTER DESIGN RULES

Patients should feel:

real
middle-class
relatable
hardworking
emotionally exhausted

Use:

teacher
office worker
mother
elderly patient
athlete
father
working woman

Avoid:

overly rich characters
exaggerated drama
unrealistic miracles
MEDICAL POSITIONING RULES

The clinic positioning should communicate:

advanced pain management
minimally invasive treatment
precise diagnosis
image-guided procedures
surgery avoidance when possible
second-opinion expertise

But indirectly.

Never sound like an ad.

VISUAL COMPATIBILITY RULES

Scripts must work as:

black-screen text overlays
cinematic B-roll edits
emotional background music reels
slow pacing videos

Therefore:

avoid long paragraphs
avoid complicated medical explanations
avoid statistics
avoid lists
avoid too much education

This is storytelling content.
Not informational content.

IDEAL SCRIPT LENGTH
Reel Duration:

45–60 seconds

Total Lines:

18–30 short overlay lines

STRUCTURE TEMPLATE (REUSABLE)

Use this framework:

Strange symptom / emotional hook
Daily life suffering
Previous failed treatments
Emotional frustration
Doctor notices hidden clue
Real diagnosis revealed
Precise minimally invasive treatment
Emotional recovery moment
Soft CTA
WHAT TO AVOID
NEVER:
Start with disease explanation
Use heavy medical jargon continuously
Sound like a hospital advertisement
Use exaggerated miracles
Promise guaranteed cure
Overuse emojis
Use motivational language
Use generic awareness tone
Use “5 symptoms of…”
Use listicle format
CONTENT DNA SUMMARY

The Jeevisha script style is:

“Emotion-first medical storytelling.”

NOT:

education-first
treatment-first
promotion-first

The pain is the protagonist.

The doctor is the calm problem-solver.

The treatment is the turning point.

The emotional recovery is the reward.

OUTPUT FORMAT RULES FOR AI

When generating future scripts:

Output ONLY the Marathi script
Keep English medical terms intact
Use cinematic line breaks
Each line should feel subtitle-friendly
Avoid paragraphs
Maintain emotional realism
End with subtle CTA
