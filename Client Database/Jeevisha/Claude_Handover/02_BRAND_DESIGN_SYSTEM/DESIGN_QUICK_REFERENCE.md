## Brand personality
Calm. Considered. Evidence-led. Premium editorial healthcare — never a flyer clinic, never a fear-marketing surgical chain. Visual feel: Premium. Editorial. Breathing.

## Fonts
- Headlines: Inter / Onest / Manrope / Satoshi / DM Sans (modern sans). Festival & reflective posts: Playfair Display / Cormorant (editorial serif).
- Body: same sans family, lighter weight, generous line height.
- Max 3 type sizes per creative. Never more than 3 typefaces.

## Logo
- Placement: top-left default; top-center for festival posts; top-right only when hero image dominates left.
- Size: 8–12% of canvas width (square), 10–13% (story). Clear space = height of the logo icon.
- Never: stretch, heavy shadows, busy-photo placement, random container shapes.

## Layout
- One focal point. One headline. 3–6 supporting elements max.
- 60px edge protection on 1080×1350. Footer 10–14% of height, royal blue with green accent, always clean.
- No text over faces, hair, hands, or medical scans.
- Whitespace is a brand asset — if it looks busy, it's wrong.

## Image style
Photorealistic Indian patients/doctors, calm composed expressions, soft natural light, editorial framing. Real clinic equipment (C-arm, USG). No plastic stock faces, no exaggerated smiles or pain grimaces.

## CTA style
Pill button, royal blue or green, white text, soft shadow. One CTA per creative.
Approved: Book a consultation · Get a specialist opinion · Bring your MRI — let's discuss · Explore non-surgical options · Talk to Dr. Sanjog · Send your reports on WhatsApp.
BANNED: Book Now, Hurry, Limited Slots, Don't Wait, Save ₹X, 30% Off, "Free ..." offers.

## Preferred dimensions
- Feed post: 1080×1350 (4:5) default; 1080×1080 square as needed
- Story/Reel cover: 1080×1920
- GMB post image: 1200×900 (4:3) — see GMB Foundation §8

## Hard restrictions (never)
Red pain-glow overlays · DNA helix · ECG waveform backgrounds · stethoscope-on-shoulders pose · floating pills/syringes · handshake/diversity-circle stock · 3D horror anatomy · before/after pain splits · fear copy ("Your spine is in danger") · superlatives without proof · urgency/discount framing.

## Approved design types (from client feedback)
Photorealistic (very strong) · Light Background (DEFAULT look) · Festival/Occasion (very strong) · Promotional (strong) · Text Heavy 75% (strong when done like "5 Signs Your Back Pain Needs Expert Attention") · Infographic (approved but must not be text-dense) · Dark Background (use sparingly).
