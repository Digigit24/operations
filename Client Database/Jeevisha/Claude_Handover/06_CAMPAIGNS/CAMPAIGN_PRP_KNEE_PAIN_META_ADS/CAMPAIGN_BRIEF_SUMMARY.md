# CAMPAIGN — PRP FOR KNEE PAIN (META ADS)
Status: Brief complete + client-revised (all "free" language removed — 16 instances reframed). Launch status UNVERIFIED. Deliverable: PRP_Knee_Pain_Meta_Ads_Campaign_Brief.docx (13 May 2026, delivered in chat — retrieve the file from chat "Top 5 campaign ideas and competitive analysis").

## Goal
Qualified PRP-eligible knee pain leads in Pune/PCMC. Cold CPL target ₹200–300; retargeted ₹50–120.

## Hero offer (REVISED — no "free")
"MRI Review + Knee Assessment with Dr. Sanjog Mekewar" — 15 min, send MRI via WhatsApp, specialist tells you if you're a PRP candidate. Backup for no-MRI users: Knee Pain Assessment via same WhatsApp flow.

## Lead tiering
MQL: phone + name + pain duration · SQL: + MRI + age 45+ + pain ≥3 months + consult within 2 weeks · Treatment lead: SQL who books and shows.

## Metrics (90-day)
MQL→Consult 35–45% · Consult→PRP booked 25–30% · Revenue/patient ₹25K–₹1.5L · Min ROAS to scale 5x.

## Budget (Month 1): ₹75,000–₹1,25,000
ToFu 20% (₹15–25K) · MoFu 45% (₹35–55K) · BoFu 35% (₹25–45K).

## ICP stack
1. Surgery-Anxious Senior (55–70, TKR-recommended) — primary
2. Caregiver decision-maker (35–55)
3. Midlife Mobility Seeker (45–60, early OA) — volume
4. Active Adult / Sports Recovery (25–45) — separate ad set, separate visual language

## Funnel
- ToFu (Video Views): PRP education, knee-pain myths, doctor authority, defensible stats. KPI: cost/ThruPlay <₹1.5, 25% completion, 5,000+ retargeting pool in 30 days. Soft CTA only.
- MoFu (LPV + Lead): comparisons (PRP vs cortisone/HA/replacement), patient stories 60–90s, doctor credibility, process transparency, cost transparency. KPI: LPV <₹15, time-on-page >90s.
- BoFu (Leads + WhatsApp Messages, split test): MRI-review offer ad, second-opinion closer, patient story 60s. Retargeting + 1% lookalike + narrow high-intent cold.

## Campaign structure
3 campaigns by funnel stage. ToFu ₹500–800/day (Advantage+, PCMC+15km, 35–70). MoFu ₹1,200–2,000/day split across 4 ICP ad sets. BoFu ₹800–1,500/day (retargeting / lookalike / high-intent cold).

## Targeting notes
Lock to PCMC + 15km (25km cold max), NOT "Pune". Separate EN/MR/HI creative per ad set. Exclude existing patients. BoFu heavier 6–11 PM; sports creative mornings + weekends.

## Compliance flags
- "20 free MRI reviews this week" urgency ad concept conflicts with the no-free decision AND brand anti-urgency rules — DO NOT RUN without explicit approval.
- "80% of knee replacements recommended too early" stat must be verified before use.
