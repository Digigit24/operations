She opened the same drawer every day.
Painkillers. A pain spray. A neck belt from Amazon.

28 years old.
Neck pain since her first job.
"It's just my laptop posture."

Then her fingers started going numb.
Mid-code. Mid-sentence.

She didn't tell anyone.
What if they thought she couldn't do the work?

Three physios. Two months. Same pain.

At Jeevisha, the doctor didn't guess.
The MRI showed exactly which nerve.
Pinched — in the neck. Not the wrist.

One targeted, image-guided treatment.
No surgery. Home the same day.

Now the drawer just holds pens.

She types all day.
And doesn't think about her hands anymore.
