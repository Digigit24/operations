Everyone told him to fix his sleep.

Less coffee.
No screens after 11.
"It's the night shifts."

But it wasn't the screens.
At 2 AM, when everything went still —
his back would start.

A dull ache no position could fix.
He'd scroll till 4.
Then log in at 9, running on nothing.

For a year, he thought he was just a bad sleeper.

At Jeevisha, one question changed everything:
"Does the pain wake you —
or does the sleeplessness?"

The pain did.
And pain has a source.

They found it. Treated it. No surgery.

The first night he slept 7 hours straight,
he sat on the edge of the bed and just breathed.
