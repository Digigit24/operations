# Script E1 — "Normal" (English shortform, IT audience)
Pain: Chronic back pain · Emotion: 3 years of quiet acceptance → regret + relief · ~24 sec
Status: DRAFT (delivered file: Jeevisha_Pain_IT_Pune_Scripts_EN.md) · Source: chat "Pain clinic scripts for IT professionals in Pune" (8 Jul 2026)

He was 31.
And his back had hurt for 3 years.

"It's normal," he'd say.
"Everyone on my team has it."

Some mornings he couldn't tie his shoes.
He learned to sit perfectly still in meetings.
He stopped playing on Sundays.

Painkillers in the desk drawer.
A ₹40,000 chair that didn't help.
"This is just IT life."

Then one flight to Goa —
he couldn't stay seated the whole way.

At Jeevisha, one scan showed it.
Not "wear and tear."
One nerve. One exact spot.

One image-guided procedure. No surgery.

Two weeks later,
he tied his shoes without thinking about it.

And realised —
it was never normal.
He'd just gotten used to it.

If your back pain keeps coming back, it's not normal. It's a signal.
