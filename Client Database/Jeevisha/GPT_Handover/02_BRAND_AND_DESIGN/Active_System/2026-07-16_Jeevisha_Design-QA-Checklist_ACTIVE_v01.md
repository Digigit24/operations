# Design QA Checklist

1. Does the design use the active name candidate rather than legacy “Hospital” naming?
2. Is the phone number drawn from the confirmed NAP register?
3. Is there one focal point and one CTA?
4. Is 65–75% of the canvas light/white/ivory?
5. Is Healing Green an accent rather than the dominant colour?
6. Are all edges protected and is text clear in two seconds?
7. Is any text covering a face, hair, hand or scan?
8. Is the photo real/credible and appropriate to Pune audiences?
9. Are medical claims case-dependent and doctor-reviewed?
10. Are patient stories consented and verified?
11. Is the footer present but not oversized?
12. Is the file named with date, brand, asset type, topic, status and version?
13. Is approval evidence logged before `APPROVED` or `LIVE` is used?
