# Image and Photography Direction

## Use
- Realistic Indian patients and doctors
- Natural skin tones and composed, credible expressions
- Dr. Sanjog listening, examining or explaining
- Real consultation room, procedure room, C-arm, USG and clinic environment
- Soft natural window light for human scenes; clean clinical light for procedure/equipment
- Editorial 50 mm / 85 mm lens feel; shallow depth of field for human moments
- Function-focused moments: walking, working, sleeping, holding a family member’s hand

## Avoid
- Plastic stock photography
- Exaggerated pain grimaces
- Red pain-glow overlays
- Before/after transformation claims
- Surgical gore, blood or intimidating equipment close-ups
- Generic stethoscope poses, handshakes, floating pills, DNA or ECG decoration
- Identifiable patients without written consent
- Text over faces, hair, hands or scans

## AI negative prompt
No Western-only stock look, no distorted hands, no unreadable text, no medical gore, no dramatic pain face, no red glow body overlay, no miracle transformation, no neon medical-tech aesthetic, no watermark, no giant logo.
