# Active Designer Brief — Read Before Starting

**Brand:** Jeevisha Spine Pain & Regenerative Clinic *(canonical name still awaiting founder/NAP sign-off)*  
**Personality:** Calm, considered, evidence-led, premium specialist care. Hope without promise. Authority without arrogance.  
**Audience:** Desk-job professionals 28–45 and treatment-fatigued chronic pain patients 40–65.  
**Core position:** Regenerative pain medicine + surgery avoidance, without demonising surgery.

## Locked visual direction

- **Primary:** Royal Blue `#073B8E`
- **Secondary:** Deep Navy `#061B3D`
- **Accent:** Healing Green `#45B42A` — accent only
- **Backgrounds:** White `#FFFFFF`, Warm Ivory `#F8F7F2`, Soft Clinical Blue `#EAF3FF`
- **Typical ratio:** 65–75% white/ivory, 15–20% blue/navy, 5–10% green
- **Primary type:** modern sans — Inter / Onest / Manrope / Satoshi / DM Sans
- **Festival/reflective type:** restrained editorial serif — Playfair / Cormorant
- **Logo:** default top-left; never on busy imagery; official master file is missing
- **Layout:** one focal point, one headline, 3–6 supporting elements maximum, 60 px safe margin on 1080×1350
- **Image style:** photorealistic Indian doctor/patient, soft natural or clean clinical light, composed expressions, real clinic/equipment
- **CTA style:** one soft, specific invitation — “Book a consultation”, “Bring your MRI — let’s discuss”, “Send your reports on WhatsApp”
- **Default social size:** 1080×1350
- **GMB size:** 1200×900, 4:3, minimal text and moderate branding

## Never do

- Cure, guarantee, “best”, 100%, instant or permanent-result claims
- Fear, countdown, discount or FOMO marketing
- Red pain-glow bodies, dramatic pain faces, generic Western stock doctors
- Text over faces, hair, hands or medical scans
- Use “Hospital” unless the client confirms the registered category/name
- Publish testimonials/case stories without documented consent and outcome verification
- Treat the HTML visual demo as a final public asset

## Current design feedback found

A recent knee-cartilage creative was described as directionally okay but still required: white/cream colour, removal of top-left “Jeevisha Hospital”, smaller bottom content and a handwriting treatment for “KNOW THIS OPTION”. This is revision evidence, not final approval.
