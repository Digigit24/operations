# Active Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary authority | Royal Blue | #073B8E | Headlines, footer bands, primary brand elements |
| Secondary / serious | Deep Navy | #061B3D | Body copy, premium contrast |
| Accent / recovery | Healing Green | #45B42A | CTA detail, icon accent, one or two keyword highlights |
| Background | Medical White | #FFFFFF | Dominant background |
| Background | Warm Ivory | #F8F7F2 | Premium calm and festival posts |
| Panel | Soft Clinical Blue | #EAF3FF | Soft panels and background fields |
| Surface | Light Grey | #EEF2F5 | Dividers and cards |
| Body text | Charcoal | #252A32 | Long-form body copy |
| Restricted accent | Soft Pain Red | #F05A5A | Tiny clinical marker only; never glow/overlay |
| Festival only | Warm Gold | #D8B76A | Minimal occasion detail |

**Ratio:** 65–75% white/ivory; 15–20% blue/navy; 5–10% green. Research teal/amber is a discarded/legacy direction.
