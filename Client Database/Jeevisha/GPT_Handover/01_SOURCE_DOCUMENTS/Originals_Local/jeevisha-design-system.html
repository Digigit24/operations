AI Prompting Examples

AI Prompting Guide §8

Three copy-ready prompt blocks — pulled directly from the AI Prompting Guide. Use these as templates when generating creatives in ChatGPT, Midjourney, GPT Image, or Flux.

Less-Text / Photorealistic Promotional
Example 03
Create a premium image-led healthcare post for Jeevisha.

Post format:    Instagram portrait
Output size:    1080 × 1350
Design style:   Less text / photorealistic

Text:           Move Freely Again.
Sub:            Advanced spine and regenerative care.
CTA:            Book a Consultation
Footer:         Jeevisha Spine Pain & Regenerative Clinic, Pune
                +91 91191 11837 · jeevishapainclinic.com

Visual:         Photorealistic Indian doctor-patient scene,
                soft natural window light, calm composed expressions,
                clean clinic interior, royal blue + healing green accents

Avoid:          Text over faces/hair/hands. Stock-photo plasticity.
                Pain glow overlays. Cure-claim language.
Text-Heavy 75% — Awareness
Example 02
Create a premium text-heavy 75% medical awareness post for Jeevisha.

Post format:    Instagram portrait
Output size:    1080 × 1350

Headline:       5 Signs Your Back Pain Needs Expert Attention

Points:
  1. Pain lasting longer than 2 weeks
  2. Tingling or numbness in the legs
  3. Pain worsening while sitting or standing
  4. Sleep disturbed by pain
  5. Daily movement becoming difficult

CTA:            Consult Jeevisha for expert spine care

Style notes:    Editorial serif headline. Sans body. Royal blue + healing
                green accents. Generous whitespace. Numbered rows with thin
                dividers. One small supporting visual, max.
Festival / Occasion — Guru Purnima
Example 01
Create a premium Guru Purnima social media post for Jeevisha.

Post format:    Instagram portrait
Output size:    1080 × 1350
Design style:   Festival / occasion
Mood:           Elegant · spiritual · premium · calm · grateful

Main text:      GURU PURNIMA — Honouring the wisdom that heals.
Sub:            With gratitude to every guide, mentor and healer
                who lights the path to strength, recovery and hope.
Footer:         Warm wishes from Jeevisha Spine Pain & Regenerative Clinic

Visual:         Soft ivory background. Royal blue + healing green palette.
                Subtle warm gold halo / moon-disc. Mandala line art.
                Lotus or diya elements used minimally.

Avoid:          Loud clipart. Generic festival templates. Overstated celebration.
