# SECTION 1 — SEO STRATEGY OVERVIEW

## Strategic positioning for SEO

Jeevisha competes in three keyword territories. Ranked by opportunity and effort:

| Territory | Competition | Effort | Strategic priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wakad / PCMC local pain** | Moderate (Painex dominates) | Low–medium | **#1 — Win this first** |
| **Surgery avoidance / second opinion** | Low (largely unclaimed) | Medium | **#2 — Brand moat** |
| **Regenerative medicine (PRP, stem cell, biologics)** | Low locally | Medium–high | **#3 — Category authority** |

The win-strategy is *not* to compete head-on for "pain management Pune" (Painex owns it through patient-volume claims and multi-location density). The win is to dominate Wakad-specific local intent, claim the surgery-avoidance content gap entirely, and build category authority for regenerative pain medicine as the modality.

## 12-month SEO objectives

1. Rank #1–3 in Google Map Pack for "pain clinic Wakad" and 4 sister-area queries
2. Rank top 5 organic for 15 commercial-intent local keywords
3. Build 40+ pages of indexed, SEO-optimized content (service pages, location pages, condition pages, blog)
4. Generate 50+ Google reviews (target by Month 6)
5. Establish content authority for "surgery avoidance" and "regenerative pain medicine" clusters
6. Drive 30+ qualified consultation enquiries per month from organic search by Month 9

## What this SEO foundation will NOT do

- Compete on pure patient-volume keywords ("largest pain clinic Pune")
- Chase national-scale terms ("best pain doctor India")
- Build content that contradicts the brand voice (no urgency, no cure claims, no clickbait)
- Optimize for keywords Jeevisha cannot fulfill on (e.g., spinal cord stimulators we don't offer)

# SECTION 4 — SEARCH INTENT MAPPING

Three-funnel approach. Each funnel stage requires different content, different CTAs, and different conversion architecture.

## TOFU (Awareness)

**Patient state:** Symptoms noticed. No diagnosis yet. Searching for explanations.
**Search behavior:** "Why does X hurt", "What causes Y", "Difference between A and B".
**Content type:** Educational blogs, condition explainers, symptom guides.
**CTA tone:** Soft. "Learn about your condition" / "Take our free pain assessment" / "Download the guide".
**Conversion expectation:** Email capture or content engagement. Not consultation booking yet.

**Priority TOFU pieces:**
- "Why does my knee hurt when climbing stairs?"
- "5 morning habits that worsen back pain"
- "Is your desk job damaging your spine?"
- "Difference between muscle pain and nerve pain"

## MOFU (Consideration)

**Patient state:** Aware home remedies don't work. Comparing treatment options. Researching clinics.
**Search behavior:** "PRP vs cortisone", "Best pain clinic Wakad", "Alternatives to surgery".
**Content type:** Comparison guides, treatment explainers, doctor credential pages, case studies, video testimonials.
**CTA tone:** Medium. "Book a consultation" / "Speak with our specialist" / "Get a second opinion".

**Priority MOFU pieces:**
- "PRP vs cortisone for knee pain"
- "5 non-surgical alternatives to knee replacement"
- "What is interventional pain management?"
- "When to see a pain specialist vs orthopedic surgeon"

## BOFU (Conversion)

**Patient state:** Ready to book. Final clinic comparison. Looking for proof.
**Search behavior:** "Pain clinic Wakad phone number", "Dr Sanjog Mekewar appointment", "Knee pain treatment cost Wakad", "Jeevisha reviews".
**Page needs:** Location pages, doctor profile, pricing transparency, reviews, clear booking flow.
**CTA tone:** Direct. "Book your consultation" / "Call/WhatsApp" / "Bring your MRI — let's discuss".

**Priority BOFU assets:**
- Location landing pages (Wakad, PCMC, Hinjewadi)
- Doctor profile (Dr. Sanjog Mekewar)
- Pricing/cost transparency page
- Reviews page with embedded Google reviews
- Sticky WhatsApp + Call CTAs on mobile

# SECTION 6 — TECHNICAL SEO CHECKLIST

The website team's checklist. Implement before content rollout — fixing technical foundations after content is built creates rework.

## 6.1 Page-level fundamentals

For every indexable page:

- [ ] Unique title tag (50–60 characters)
- [ ] Unique meta description (140–160 characters)
- [ ] One H1 per page, matching primary keyword intent
- [ ] Logical H2/H3 structure (don't skip levels)
- [ ] Image alt text on every meaningful image
- [ ] Internal links to related cluster content
- [ ] Schema markup applied (per Section 5)
- [ ] Canonical URL set
- [ ] Mobile-responsive (Google mobile-first indexing)
- [ ] Page loads in <3 seconds on 4G mobile

## 6.2 Title tag patterns

Use these formulas for consistency:

| Page type | Title formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | `[Brand] — [Positioning] in [Location]` | `Jeevisha — Regenerative Pain Medicine Clinic in Wakad, Pune` |
| Service page | `[Service] in [Location] — [Brand]` | `PRP Therapy for Knee Pain in Pune — Jeevisha Clinic` |
| Condition page | `[Condition] Treatment in [Location] — [Brand]` | `Back Pain Treatment in Wakad — Jeevisha Clinic` |
| Location page | `Pain Clinic in [Location] — [Brand]` | `Pain Clinic in Hinjewadi — Jeevisha Clinic` |
| Blog post | `[Title] \| [Brand]` | `How to Avoid Knee Replacement Surgery \| Jeevisha Clinic` |

## 6.3 Meta description voice

Write in brand voice. Restrained. Educational. Always paired with a soft invitation.

**Template:**
> *Non-surgical [condition] treatment in [location], led by Dr. Sanjog Mekewar (FIPM, FIAPM). [One specific benefit]. Book a consultation at Jeevisha Clinic, Wakad.*

**Example:**
> *Non-surgical knee pain treatment in Wakad, led by Dr. Sanjog Mekewar (FIPM, FIAPM). Regenerative options including PRP therapy for the right candidate. Book a consultation at Jeevisha Clinic.*

## 6.4 URL structure

- All lowercase
- Hyphens, not underscores
- No dates in URLs (allows evergreen content updates without URL change)
- Keep URLs descriptive but under 60 characters
- Use cluster hierarchy where natural: `/services/prp-therapy/` `/conditions/back-pain/`

## 6.6 Performance + Core Web Vitals

Target metrics (per Google Page Speed Insights):
- **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):** < 2.5s
- **FID (First Input Delay):** < 100ms
- **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift):** < 0.1
- **Mobile speed score:** > 80
- **Desktop speed score:** > 90

Common fixes:
- Compress hero images (WebP or AVIF format)
- Lazy-load below-the-fold images
- Defer non-critical JavaScript
- Use a CDN for static assets
- Minimize render-blocking CSS

## 6.7 Crawlability + indexation

- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] robots.txt configured correctly (allow indexing of all public pages; block admin paths)
- [ ] No accidental `noindex` tags on indexable pages
- [ ] Canonical tags set on all duplicate-risk pages
- [ ] 301 redirects implemented for any URL changes
- [ ] No broken internal links
- [ ] No orphan pages (every page reachable from at least one other page)

## 6.8 Mobile UX (Google mobile-first indexing)

- [ ] Tap targets at least 48×48px
- [ ] Font size at least 16px for body text
- [ ] No horizontal scrolling required
- [ ] Click-to-call button always visible

# SECTION 7 — INTERNAL LINKING ARCHITECTURE

Internal linking is the cheapest SEO lever. Done well, it lifts cluster-wide rankings without new content. Done poorly, it leaves authority stranded on orphan pages.

## Linking rules

1. **Every blog post links to its cluster pillar.** No exceptions.
2. **Every pillar links down to its top 3 spokes.** Above-the-fold or in a "Related" module.
3. **Every condition page links to relevant treatment pages.** A back pain page must link to epidural injection, RFA, and PRP-for-back-pain treatment pages.
4. **Every treatment page links back to relevant condition pages.** Bidirectional.
5. **Location pages link to relevant condition pages with local context.** "Knee pain patients in Wakad — view our Knee Pain Treatment page."
6. **The doctor profile is linked from every service page.** Trust signal + internal authority distribution.
7. **The homepage links to all 5 cluster pillars within the first scroll.** Cluster pillars need direct authority transfer.

## Anchor text guidance

Use natural, descriptive anchor text. Avoid:
- "Click here" (zero SEO value)
- Exact-match keyword stuffing ("best pain clinic Wakad best pain clinic Wakad")
- Generic anchors ("learn more")

Use:
- "Read more about non-surgical knee treatment options"
- "Dr. Sanjog Mekewar's full credentials and approach"
- "How PRP therapy works for knee osteoarthritis"

# SECTION 9 — REVIEW GENERATION STRATEGY

Reviews are a top 3 local SEO ranking factor and a top trust signal for new patients. Currently weak (testimonials on website, but limited Google reviews).

## 6-month review targets

- Month 1: 10 Google reviews
- Month 3: 25 Google reviews
- Month 6: 50 Google reviews
- Month 12: 100+ Google reviews

## Review request system

1. **Trigger:** 48 hours after a successful procedure or follow-up.
2. **Channel:** WhatsApp message with direct review link.
3. **Message template (brand voice — restrained, optional):**

   > *Hi [name], thank you for trusting Jeevisha with your care. If you've been comfortable with your experience, a short Google review would help others in Wakad find us. The link below takes you straight there. No pressure — only if you'd like to.
   > [direct Google review link]
   > — Team Jeevisha*

4. **Response policy:** Every review (positive or negative) gets a thoughtful, brand-voice response within 48 hours.
5. **Reviewer recognition:** Patients who leave detailed reviews get a personal thank-you note from Dr. Sanjog.

## Negative review response

# SECTION 13 — COMPLIANCE NOTES (HEALTHCARE SEO)

SEO must respect the healthcare niche overlay. Key constraints:

- **No outcome guarantees in title tags, meta descriptions, or schema descriptions.** "Best results", "guaranteed relief", "permanent cure" — all forbidden.
- **No comparative superiority claims without proof.** "Best pain clinic in Pune" requires verifiable basis or it violates ASCI guidelines.
- **No fear-based meta descriptions.** "Don't suffer one more day" — off-brand and compliance-risky.
- **Doctor credentials must be accurate everywhere.** "FIPM, FIAPM, DNB, DA, MBBS" — exact format, no inflation.
- **Patient testimonials require written consent.** Especially in schema markup (Review schema) and on reviews pages.
- **No specific cure claims in FAQ schema.** Use "may help / can support" framing per Brand Brain §7.
