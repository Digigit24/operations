# Consent and Licence Requirements

No patient/model consent records or stock licences were found.

Before patient-related content is used, record: anonymized person code, asset ID, signed date, channels, duration, permitted edits, withdrawal process, document location and approver. Never store diagnosis or unnecessary health details in the marketing register.

Stock/AI assets require a source and licence record. Final images must not be treated as consented merely because they are public or were uploaded to the project.
