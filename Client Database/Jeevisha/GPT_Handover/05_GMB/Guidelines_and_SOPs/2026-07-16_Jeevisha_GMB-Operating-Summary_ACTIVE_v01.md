# GMB Operating Summary

## Before any edit
1. Obtain Manager access.
2. Confirm owner remains with the client.
3. Lock canonical NAP and business-name approach.
4. Record current categories, services, hours, posts, photos, Q&A and performance.
5. Confirm WhatsApp Business, appointment URL and review link.

## Posting
- Two posts per week after approval.
- Helpful, local, professional, moderately branded.
- 80–250 words; one CTA; 2–5 hashtags if used.
- Images: 1200×900, 4:3, minimal overlay text, authentic clinic/doctor visuals.
- Never reuse aggressive Meta ad copy unchanged.
- No guarantees, fake urgency, unsupported numbers, review screenshots or identifiable patients.

## Reviews
- Optional one-time request after a meaningful care milestone.
- No “5-star” pressure.
- Reply within 48 hours; no diagnosis or patient detail in public replies.
- Escalate negative reviews privately before responding.

## Photos
- Real clinic, team, equipment and access photos.
- Written consent for identifiable patients.
- No stock photos, text-heavy posters, pain-glow anatomy or watermarks.

## Measurement
Track calls, directions, website clicks, bookings, reviews, rating, response time, posts, photos, Q&A and local rankings. Separate actual platform data from targets.
