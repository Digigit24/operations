## Caption

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy uses concentrated platelets from your own blood to support healing in damaged tissues — typically joints, tendons, or spinal discs.

The procedure:
1. A small blood draw (similar to a routine blood test)
2. Centrifugation to concentrate platelets
3. Image-guided injection into the target area

Same day. Usually 60-90 minutes total.

It's not appropriate for every condition or every patient. Evaluation matters.
