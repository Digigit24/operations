## Caption

Most back pain resolves on its own. Some doesn't.

Warning signs that warrant a specialist evaluation:
1. Pain lasting more than 2 weeks
2. Tingling or numbness in legs
3. Pain that worsens at night or during sleep
4. Difficulty with daily movements
5. Pain that worsens despite rest

A specialist evaluation can identify whether your back pain is mechanical, nerve-related, or structural — and what the right approach should be.
