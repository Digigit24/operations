## Caption

At Jeevisha, every patient visit begins with three things:

1. We listen to your full story, not just symptoms.
2. We review your imaging together — explain what it shows, what it doesn't, and what it means.
3. We discuss every reasonable option, ranked by evidence — not by our preferred procedure.

Then we plan. Together.

This is what evidence-led, patient-first care actually looks like in practice.
