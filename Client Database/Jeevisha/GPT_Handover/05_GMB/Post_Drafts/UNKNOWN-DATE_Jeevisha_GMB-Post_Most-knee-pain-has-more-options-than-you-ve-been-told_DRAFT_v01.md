## Caption

Before recommending knee replacement, we ask: have all non-surgical options been explored? For many patients, the answer is no.

At Jeevisha, we evaluate before we treat. We discuss every option — including regenerative therapies, image-guided procedures, and physiotherapy — and recommend surgery only when it's truly the right call.

If you've been told you need knee surgery, consider a second opinion first.
