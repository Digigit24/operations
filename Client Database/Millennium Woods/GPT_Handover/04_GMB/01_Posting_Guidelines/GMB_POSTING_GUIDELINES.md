# Google Business Profile Posting Guidelines

**Source basis:** May 28 Design Instructions, AI Prompting Guide, Strategic Research and Site Extraction.  
**Status:** Internal operating guidance; no formal Prompt 05 GMB Foundation or client approval was found.

## Hard gates before GBP execution

1. Verify GBP ownership/access.
2. Lock the exact real-world business name.
3. Lock canonical NAP and the entrance map pin.
4. Confirm phone, website, hours and program/service list.
5. Remove all “Little Millennium” contamination.
6. Confirm whether the school is independent and whether SSSPM/Sai Sakal must appear.
7. Confirm photo rights and parent/guardian consent.
8. Establish an approval and publication ledger.

## Content character

GMB content must be:

- helpful;
- local;
- professional;
- moderately branded;
- non-spammy;
- free from aggressive urgency;
- free from unsupported education, safety or development claims.

Do not reuse Meta ad copy unchanged.

## Recommended post categories

- Service/program update
- School update
- Parent education
- Event, only when confirmed
- Offer, only with approved price, dates and conditions

## Image rules

- Preferred size: 1200×900 (4:3); 1080×1080 is acceptable for repurposing.
- Keep text minimal and one focal message.
- Use real school images only with rights and consent.
- Do not show child name tags, private information or unsafe situations.
- Avoid stock-stiff/AI-plastic children, clutter and rainbow templates.
- Use the approved logo only after the lockup hierarchy is confirmed.
- Do not bake an address into the image while NAP remains unresolved.

## Copy rules

- Lead with a useful local parent question or verified update.
- Use one CTA.
- Do not use “best,” “No.1,” guaranteed outcomes or unverified ratings.
- Treat CCTV, staff training, hygiene, ratio, schedule and program claims as verification-required.
- Avoid long hashtag blocks. Local relevance should come from accurate profile fields and natural wording.
- Do not name competitors.

## Healthcare rule in the original handover brief

The source handover brief mentioned healthcare compliance generically because it was designed for multiple industries. This client is an education business, so healthcare-specific rules are not applicable. Child safeguarding, advertising truthfulness and privacy remain mandatory.

## Publication QA

- Business name matches approved real-world name.
- NAP and hours match the approved fact sheet.
- Program/age/session facts are current.
- CTA works.
- Image rights and child consent recorded.
- No competitor text or template residue.
- No unsupported rating/superlative.
- Client approval recorded.
- Live URL and date added to `GMB_CONTENT_INDEX.csv`.
