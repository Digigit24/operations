# GMB Review Response Templates — Internal Starter

**Provenance:** Created for this handover as a controlled operating aid. These were not recovered as client-approved templates.

## Positive review

> Thank you for sharing your experience with Millennium Woods Techno Kids Pre-School. We appreciate your kind words about [specific verified aspect]. Our team is glad that your family felt supported. Thank you for being part of our school community.

## Neutral/mixed review

> Thank you for taking the time to share this feedback. We value your comments about [specific point]. We would like to understand the details and follow up appropriately. Please contact the school through the verified phone/email on our profile so the team can assist.

## Negative review

> Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We are sorry to hear that your experience did not meet expectations. To protect family and child privacy, we will not discuss details publicly. Please contact the school through the verified contact on this profile so the appropriate team member can review the matter directly.

## Rules

- Never reveal a child’s name, attendance, behaviour, health, family details or internal incident information.
- Do not argue, blame or disclose private records.
- Do not use a generic address/phone until the profile data is verified.
- Escalate safeguarding, injury, billing, legal or serious service complaints to the responsible client owner before replying.
- Record the review URL, rating, response, approver and response date.
