# Missing Production Assets

The following were not found as governed production files:

- Approved logo pack: SVG/AI/EPS/PDF, transparent PNGs, monochrome/reverse and avatar variants.
- Brand font files or font licences.
- Approved social posts, ad creatives or GMB images.
- Final JPG/PNG/PDF/MP4 exports.
- Editable Canva, Figma, PSD, AI or CDR sources.
- Premiere Pro or After Effects projects.
- Raw school, classroom, exterior, facility, program and event photography.
- Founder, principal, teacher and team portraits.
- Raw video, B-roll, voiceovers, subtitles, thumbnails and reel covers.
- Stock-asset purchase receipts and licences.
- Parent/model consent records for identifiable children.
- Campaign landing-page assets.
- Published-content exports and URLs.
- Analytics screenshots/exports.

Nothing should be moved into an approved or final folder until its source, approval, rights and consent fields are recorded.
