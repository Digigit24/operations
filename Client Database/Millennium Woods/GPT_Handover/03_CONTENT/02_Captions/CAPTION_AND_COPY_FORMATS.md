# Caption and Copy Formats

These are **operational templates assembled from the active voice and compliance rules**. They are not client-approved copy.

## Social caption structure

1. Parent-centred hook: one clear emotional or practical question.
2. Useful explanation: 2–4 short sentences.
3. Verified proof: only approved program, process, facility or team facts.
4. One CTA: `Book a Visit`, `Enquire Now`, `WhatsApp Us`, or `Visit Us`.
5. Contact block: use phone only after verification; do not print the address until NAP is locked.
6. Hashtags: 5–10 relevant tags; avoid spam blocks.

## Short caption template

> [PARENT HOOK]  
> [ONE USEFUL IDEA OR VERIFIED BENEFIT]  
> [ONE VERIFIED PROOF POINT]  
> [CTA]

## Long caption template

> [RELATABLE PARENT SITUATION]  
>  
> [EDUCATIONAL OR REASSURING EXPLANATION]  
>  
> [VERIFIED SCHOOL PROCESS / PROGRAM FACT]  
>  
> [SOFT CTA]  
> [APPROVED CONTACT]

## GMB caption structure

- Local and helpful.
- 80–150 words as a practical operating target, not a platform rule.
- One service/update topic.
- One moderate CTA.
- No aggressive urgency.
- No long hashtag block.
- No unsupported “best,” “No.1,” ratings or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not reuse Meta ad copy unchanged.

## Meta ad fields

**Primary text:** Parent pain point → benefit framing → verified proof → CTA.  
**Headline:** 3–8 words.  
**Description:** One clarifying fact; no repeated urgency.  
**CTA:** Book Now / Learn More / WhatsApp, matching the real landing flow.

## CTA library

### Preferred, subject to workflow confirmation

- Book a Free School Tour
- Book a Visit
- Enquire Now
- WhatsApp Us
- Visit Us
- Explore Programs

### Avoid

- Guaranteed Results
- Make Your Child a Genius
- Best Preschool
- No.1 Preschool
- Hurry—Last Chance
- Seats Are Filling Fast, unless current capacity evidence exists
