# Content Calendar Recovery and Rebuild

## Historical record

A 30-day social-media calendar was reportedly delivered on 2026-05-13. The retrievable project record confirms the strategy, channels, pillars and several concepts, but the exact day-by-day entries are not accessible.

## What was recovered

- Platforms: Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp Status.
- Formats: Reels, carousels, static posts and stories.
- Pillars:
  - child development and parenting;
  - safety and trust;
  - school environment and facilities;
  - learning through play;
  - teacher bonding and care;
  - parent testimonials;
  - admissions and awareness;
  - emotional/relatable parenting;
  - festivals and celebrations;
  - interactive stories and Reels.
- Named Reel directions:
  - “Your Child’s First School Should Feel Like Home”
  - “What Looks Like Play Is Actually Learning”
- Primary objective: school visits/admissions, supported by education and trust.

## Preservation decision

The missing 30 entries have **not** been fabricated. The 75-item idea register is the recoverable source pool for rebuilding a new calendar.

## Gate before scheduling

Do not set publication dates until these are resolved:

1. current session and admissions status;
2. canonical NAP;
3. program/age bands;
4. operating and tour schedule;
5. logo pack and brand-system approval;
6. child-photo consent and available assets;
7. approver and approval SLA;
8. publishing access and KPI baseline.

## Recommended new calendar fields

Content ID · Date · Platform · Format · Campaign · Topic · Copy status · Design status · Fact-check status · Consent status · Client approval · Scheduled URL · Published URL · KPI.
