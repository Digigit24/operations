# Ad Copy and Angle Library

**Status:** Strategic draft directions. No ad account, spend, approval, publication or performance evidence was found.

## Angle 1 — Trust-led preschool search

**Primary hook:** “Searching for a preschool near Wakad you can actually trust?”  
**Audience:** Local parents comparing schools  
**Value proposition:** Warmth, safety and modern learning, supported only by verified facts  
**CTA:** Book a Free Tour  
**Blockers:** Canonical locality, safety evidence, tour workflow, approved logo and current session.

## Angle 2 — Working-parent day care

**Primary hook:** “Day care that feels like home — minutes from Hinjewadi.”  
**Audience:** Dual-income IT-corridor families  
**Value proposition:** Reliable care plus structured early learning  
**CTA:** WhatsApp Us  
**Blockers:** Travel-time wording, active day-care program, age band, hours, pricing and WhatsApp setup.

## Angle 3 — Parent testimonial / social proof

**Primary hook:** Use a real approved parent quote, not a reconstructed review.  
**Audience:** First-time anxious parents  
**Value proposition:** Other families’ documented experience  
**CTA:** Book a Visit  
**Blockers:** Review source, quote permission, child/family consent, rating platform verification.

## Angle 4 — Future-ready enrichment

**Primary hook:** “A future-ready start for curious little minds.”  
**Audience:** Aspirational parents  
**Value proposition:** Approved enrichment programs such as Everyday Math, Sign Language and Art Studio  
**CTA:** Explore Programs  
**Blockers:** Confirm current program availability and avoid guaranteed outcomes.

## Angle 5 — Admission season

**Historical direction:** “Admissions Open — limited seats, low ratio.”  
**Audience:** High-intent parents  
**CTA:** Book a Tour  
**Risk:** The session, availability and ratio are not approved. Do not use this line unchanged.

## Angle 6 — Retargeting after website or video engagement

**Hook:** “Still deciding? See the school before you choose.”  
**Audience:** Warm visitors/viewers  
**CTA:** Book a Visit  
**Blockers:** Tracking/consent setup, landing page, real tour process and current contact details.

## Platform roles

- **Meta:** Emotional awareness, video, testimonial and retargeting.
- **Google Search:** High-intent local queries after a verified landing page and conversion tracking exist.
- **WhatsApp:** Enquiry conversion after the correct number, ownership and response workflow are confirmed.

## Compliance

No fabricated statistics, “best/No.1,” guaranteed development, unsupported scarcity, unverified ratings, competitor naming or unconsented child/family images.
