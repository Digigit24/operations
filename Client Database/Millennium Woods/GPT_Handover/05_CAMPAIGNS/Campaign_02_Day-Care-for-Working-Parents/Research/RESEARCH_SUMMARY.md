# Research Summary — Day Care for Working Parents

**Source:** Strategic Research; Site Extraction.  
**Audience:** Dual-income parents in Wakad, Tathawade, Marunji and Hinjewadi.  
**Pain:** Guilt/anxiety about leaving a child, safety concerns, long workdays, commute and reliability.  
**Value proposition:** Warm, trusted care near home/work with visible routines and learning support.  
**Location constraint:** IT-corridor catchment; travel-time claims pending.
