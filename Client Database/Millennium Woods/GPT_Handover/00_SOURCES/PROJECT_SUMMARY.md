## Business and positioning

The working proposition is a preschool and day-care centre serving young families in the Wakad–Marunji–Tathawade–Hinjewadi corridor. Its strongest strategic position is:

> **The warm, premium, future-ready neighbourhood preschool.**

The emotional product is parental peace of mind: a safe, warm environment where children develop confidence, communication, curiosity and school readiness. The practical offer is preschool admission, day care and a low-friction school tour.
