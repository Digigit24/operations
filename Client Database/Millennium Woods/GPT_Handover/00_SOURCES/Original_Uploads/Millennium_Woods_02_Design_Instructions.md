# BRAND DIRECTION SUMMARY

Millennium Woods Techno Kids operates in preschool + early-learning + day care. Emotionally the brand sells:

- safety and emotional trust
- nurturing growth and confidence for children
- peace of mind for busy (often IT-corridor) parents
- modern, future-ready early education ("Techno Kids" made credible, not gimmicky)

The brand should feel: **warm · premium · emotionally secure · modern · joyful but never noisy · structured yet playful · parent-trust-first.**

**Strategic visual positioning (from Prompt 01):**
> "The warm, premium, future-ready neighbourhood preschool" — editorial and modern, deliberately *above* the clip-art/rainbow norm of the category, while staying joyful and child-friendly.

**Avoid:** cheap kindergarten aesthetics · outdated clipart · uncontrolled rainbow overload · generic AI children · low-quality type · crowded layouts.

# LOGO — ACCURATE EXTRACTION & USAGE

The official mark is a **circular badge on a black ground** with a royal-blue outer ring, a child-with-lightbulb mascot, and a multi-color wordmark.

**Logo elements & colors (visually estimated — verify vs. brand kit):**

**Logo placement rules:**
- Preferred: top-left or centered, with generous clear space (≥ the height of the "M" on all sides).
- The logo is a **dark circular badge.** On light/cream layouts, place it on a subtle white/cream chip or keep enough breathing space so the black ground reads as intentional, never as a box error.
- Never stretch, recolor, add shadows to, or place the logo on a busy/low-contrast background.
- **On uniforms/fabric:** match perspective and fabric folds; blend with realistic lighting; never float. *(Do not specify a uniform color — see open items / AI guide.)*

# CORE COLOR SYSTEM — RECONCILED

The brand has **two color truths** that must be used together, not confused:

### A. Logo-true colors (always correct, identity-anchoring)
Use for: logo lockups, brand moments, anywhere fidelity to the mark matters.
`Brand Orange #EE6A23` · `Brand Red #E23B2C` · `Brand Blue #2F5DC0` · `Brand Pink #D63C8E` · `Brand Maroon #9E2B2B` · `Brand Teal #1AA3B0` · `Brand Yellow #F6C84A` · `Black #0A0A0A`.

### B. Refined editorial palette (the "premium uplift")
A restrained, parent-trust palette derived from the logo, used to lift everyday creatives above the category. **This is a deliberate refinement of the logo, not a replacement** — keep the logo's warm orange/red and royal blue present so creatives still read as *this* brand.

| Purpose | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Warm background | Soft Cream | `#FFF6EA` |
| Premium dark ground (on-brand — matches badge) | Near-Black / Deep Navy | `#0A0A0A` / `#0E2A66` |
| Primary warm accent | Brand Orange | `#EE6A23` |
| Secondary warm | Brand Red / Coral | `#E23B2C` |
| Trust blue | Brand Blue / Deep Navy | `#2F5DC0` / `#0E2A66` |
| Highlight | Warm Yellow | `#F6C84A` |
| Playful accent (sparing) | Brand Pink | `#D63C8E` |
| Support | Calm Teal | `#1AA3B0` |
| Growth accent (sparing) | Soft Green | `#57B947` |

**Balance rule:** ~70% neutral (cream **or** premium black) · ~20% brand color (orange/blue lead) · ~10% strong CTA accent.

**Important correction to the original file:** dark backgrounds are **on-brand**, not "use sparingly" — the logo itself is black-ground. Premium dark layouts (cream type, gold/orange accents) are a legitimate primary look for this brand, especially for announcements and premium/admissions moments.

**Never:** uncontrolled 5+ saturated colors competing at once · neon · muddy low-contrast pastels · any palette that buries the warm orange/blue identity.

# TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

**Headline:** bold rounded sans, highly readable, emotionally warm — matches the logo's rounded playful wordmark.
Recommended: **Baloo 2 · Fredoka · Poppins ExtraBold · Nunito ExtraBold.**

**Body:** clean modern sans, high readability — **Poppins · Nunito · DM Sans.**

**Rules:** strong hierarchy · one dominant headline per creative · generous line height · emphasis via weight/color (use Brand Orange or Blue for highlights), never via rainbow text.

# LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

**Philosophy:** editorial · premium · emotionally warm · Instagram-native · modern.

**Rules:** strong hierarchy · one dominant focal point · clear CTA block · large readable type · soft rounded shapes · comfortable spacing · layered but never cluttered.

**Composition:** left-right splits · large child image + info section · photoreal + infographic hybrid.
**Avoid:** equal-weight layouts · floating elements · sticker overload · over-decoration.

# PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

**Use:** real Indian children · authentic candid smiles · warm natural light · clean classrooms · emotionally safe environments.
**Avoid:** stock-stiff posing · exaggerated/AI smiles · fake classrooms · harsh flash · overexposure.
**Lighting:** golden soft daylight · indoor natural light · gentle cinematic warmth.

> **Child-image safeguarding note:** only use photographs of real enrolled children with documented parental consent. For paid ads or reach campaigns, prefer consented real photos or clearly-AI/illustrated children. Never imply endorsement by a child or family without consent.

# CLAIMS & COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS (education sector) — NEW

These are mandatory for every creative, caption, GMB post, ad, and AI prompt:

1. **No guaranteed outcomes.** Never promise academic results, IQ gains, "guaranteed admission to top schools," etc. Use "supports/nurtures/helps develop."
2. **No unverified superlatives.** Avoid "best," "No.1," "#1 preschool," "top-rated" as factual claims unless backed by a citable, current source the PM has approved.
3. **Ratings are not claims until verified.** The Justdial 4.8★ / 254-rating figure is a **directory signal only** — do **not** use it as a marketing claim ("Rated 4.8★ by parents!") until the listing is verified by client/PM (per approved control #1).
4. **No fabricated stats** — no invented enrollment, success rates, awards, or accreditations.
5. **Safety claims must be true** — only state CCTV/ratio/trained-staff/hygiene facts confirmed by the school.
6. **Child safeguarding** — consented imagery only (see Photography).
7. **No competitor naming** — never reference "Little Millennium" or other competitors.

# SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN SYSTEM

1. **Photorealistic** — large child image + soft editorial type. Best for: admissions, emotional/trust posts.
2. **Light background** — cream + orange/blue accents. Best for: educational/info posts.
3. **Dark background** — near-black + warm gold/orange accents (**on-brand, not rare**). Best for: premium announcements, annual day, celebrations.
4. **Infographic** — icon-led, clean sections, minimal copy. Best for: tips, benefits, education.
5. **Info + photorealistic** — *most recommended.* Real children + infographic blocks + editorial layout.
6. **Text-heavy 50 / 75 / 100** — escalating copy density for awareness, carousels, announcements.
7. **Festival posts** — elegant, premium, restrained festive accents; no loud templates.
8. **Offer / ad creatives** — clear hook · emotional parent benefit · visible CTA · footer (NAP pending).
