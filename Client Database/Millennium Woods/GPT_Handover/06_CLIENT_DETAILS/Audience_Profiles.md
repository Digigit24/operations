# Audience Profiles

**Status:** DRAFT strategic personas based on research, not CRM/analytics segmentation.

## ICP 1 — Working IT-Corridor Parent

- Dual-income family in Wakad, Tathawade, Marunji or Hinjewadi.
- Primary need: reliable day care plus structured early learning near home/work.
- Pains: time pressure, guilt, safety, commute and schedule fit.
- Trust triggers: real facility/team proof, clear hours, easy visit, verified reviews.
- Channels: Google Maps/Search, Instagram, WhatsApp and parent groups.
- Preferred CTA: Book a free tour / WhatsApp, after workflow confirmation.

## ICP 2 — First-Time Anxious Parent

- Enrolling a first child in play group/nursery.
- Primary need: emotional reassurance and a gentle transition.
- Pains: separation anxiety, safety and whether the child will be seen/cared for.
- Trust triggers: teacher faces, parent stories, routines and visit experience.
- Content: FAQs, behind the scenes, readiness education, testimonials.

## ICP 3 — Aspirational “Best-for-my-child” Parent

- Values a modern, premium-feeling, well-rounded learning environment.
- Primary need: credible differentiation and enrichment.
- Trigger: future-ready narrative supported by real programs.
- Risk: Avoid unsupported “best,” “advanced” or guaranteed outcomes.

## ICP 4 — Relocating Family

- New to the Pune/Wakad corridor and searching cold.
- Primary need: accurate local details, reviews, directions and quick contact.
- Channels: Google Search/Maps and directories.
- Critical dependency: correct canonical NAP and GBP.

## Shared buyer psychology

- Emotional-first decision: safety, warmth and belonging.
- Rational justification: curriculum, ratio, hours, facilities and logistics.
- Desired transformation: a confident, school-ready, emotionally secure child.
- Compliance: Present outcomes as support/nurture, not guarantees.
