# Brand Quick Reference

**Read before designing. Status: CONDITIONAL ACTIVE.**

## Brand personality
Warm, premium, emotionally secure, modern, joyful without noise, structured yet playful, parent-trust-first.

## Primary working colours
Use a restrained system led by:
- Warm orange `#EE6A23`
- Royal blue `#2F5DC0`
- Warm yellow `#F6C84A`
- Soft cream `#FFF6EA`
- Deep navy `#0E2A66`

Secondary accents, used sparingly: red/coral `#E23B2C`, pink `#D63C8E`, teal `#1AA3B0`, soft green `#57B947`.

**Colour authority is pending official vector verification.**

## Font system
- Headlines: Baloo 2, Fredoka, Poppins ExtraBold or Nunito ExtraBold.
- Body: Poppins, Nunito or DM Sans.
- Use one dominant headline and strong mobile readability.

## Logo placement
Top-left or centred with generous clear space. Never stretch, recolour, rotate, shadow or place over clutter. The supplied horizontal logo and the documented circular badge conflict; use the available horizontal file provisionally and do not invent a badge crop.

## Design style
Editorial preschool design: one focal child/teacher image, rounded cards, clean hierarchy, comfortable whitespace, subtle learning motifs and a strong CTA area.

## Image style
Real or highly realistic Indian children, natural expressions, warm daylight, authentic classroom context. No plastic AI faces, distorted anatomy, stock-stiff posing or fake classrooms. Use real pupils only with documented parental consent.

## CTA style
Rounded pill/rectangle, high contrast, one primary action:
- Book a Free Tour
- Book a Visit
- Enquire Now
- WhatsApp Us
- Visit Us

## Important restrictions
No “Little Millennium”, “best/No.1”, guaranteed outcomes, unverified ratings, fake scarcity, invented address, assumed uniform colour or unsupported safety/academic claims.

## Preferred dimensions
- Instagram square: 1080×1080
- Instagram portrait/carousel: 1080×1350
- Story: 1080×1920
- Meta ad: 1080×1080 or 1080×1350
- GMB: 1200×900 or 1080×1080
- Website banner: 1920×700 or 1600×600
- WhatsApp: 1080×1080 or 1080×1350
