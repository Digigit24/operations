# Client Design Preferences and Revision Signals

## Confirmed or strongly recovered preferences
- Parent-friendly, cheerful and trust-building rather than academic-pressure messaging.
- Playful warmth with professional polish.
- Big rounded child-friendly headline typography.
- One clear message and one focal subject.
- Consistent spacing, footer style, logo size and headline hierarchy across carousels.
- Avoid text over a child's face; slide 5 of the June carousel was specifically revised for this.
- Admission-focused CTAs should remain warm rather than discount-like.
- Realistic children and classroom scenes are preferred over generic AI or clip-art.
- Keep mobile readability high.

## Legacy visual preferences
Earlier work used yellow, orange, pink, sky blue, mint green, white and dark blue contact strips, plus doodles, stars, balloons, paper planes and classroom images. These are historical tendencies, not current approved rules.

## Common revision risks inferred from the record
- Remove irrelevant or unverified copy.
- Correct stale session dates.
- Simplify crowded layouts.
- Preserve faces and negative space.
- Standardize the footer and logo.
- Replace assumptions such as yellow uniforms or exact address.
- Verify all operational claims before final export.

## Approval status
No documented client design approval or rejection log was found. These preferences are source-derived working guidance, not formal sign-off.
