# AI Image Prompting Guide

## Production rule

Generate the visual first, then add real typography and the approved logo in a design tool. AI-generated text and logos are not final assets.

## Required inputs

Brand, platform and size; topic; parent-facing hook; supporting line; verified key points; CTA; verified contact treatment; audience; visual subject; mood; offer; verified trust proof; consent/rights plan; status.

## Master prompt

```text
Create a premium, warm, modern preschool visual for Millennium Woods Techno Kids Pre-School.

Format: [PLATFORM AND SIZE]
Topic: [TOPIC]
Parent-facing hook: [HOOK]
Supporting message: [SHORT SUPPORTING LINE]
Verified points only: [POINTS]
CTA area: [CTA]
Audience: young parents in the Wakad–Marunji–Hinjewadi corridor.
Visual subject: realistic Indian preschool child/teacher/parent interaction in an authentic, clean early-learning environment.
Mood: emotionally safe, warm, joyful, parent-trusted, premium and natural.
Composition: one dominant focal subject, editorial hierarchy, rounded information areas, 30–40% breathing room, clear safe area for real typography and logo added later.
Lighting: warm natural daylight, soft shadows, realistic skin tones.
Palette direction: soft cream, warm orange, royal blue and yellow accents; controlled secondary pink/teal/green.
Motifs: subtle lightbulb, learning shapes or soft doodles only.
Do not generate final logo or long text inside the image.
Avoid: plastic AI faces, distorted hands, adult-looking children, clutter, rainbow overload, stock-stiff posing, fake classroom, fear-based messaging, competitor names, invented address, unverified claims, assumed uniform colour.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
generic kindergarten flyer, rainbow clutter, cheap clipart, plastic child, distorted hands, extra fingers, asymmetric eyes, adult facial proportions, fake smile, harsh flash, oversaturated skin, Western stock classroom, unsafe child pose, readable fake logo, garbled typography, watermark, competitor branding, medal or award, rating badge, guaranteed result, invented CCTV equipment
```

## Format guidance

- Instagram square 1080×1080: one image, headline area, three proof chips maximum.
- Instagram portrait/carousel 1080×1350: stronger vertical storytelling and negative space.
- Story 1080×1920: keep UI-safe top/bottom margins.
- GMB 1200×900: minimal text; business details usually remain in the listing.
- Website banner 1920×700: subject on one side, headline/CTA space on the other.
- WhatsApp 1080×1080/1350: large text and one action.

## Content-specific visual prompts

### Admissions / tour
Warm child arrival or parent-child school visit; reassuring staff interaction; open CTA area. Do not print session, age bands, address or scarcity until verified.

### Day care
Working-parent emotional reassurance, age-appropriate activity, calm staff care and real routine cues. Do not imply exact hours unless approved.

### Safety
Clean entrance/classroom, caring supervision and orderly pickup context. Do not fabricate CCTV hardware, badges or security systems.

### Activity-based learning
Hands-on blocks, clay, water play, art or role play. Avoid absolute “no worksheets” framing until curriculum confirmation.

### Future-ready enrichment
Age-appropriate sign language, everyday math, art, movement or language activity; no futuristic gadgets or exaggerated tech claims.

### GMB
Original-looking local school photo, minimal overlay, one clear topic. Never reuse aggressive Meta-ad copy.

## Logo handling

The current project contains a horizontal logo file but a source document describes a circular badge. Add only the supplied file provisionally and do not create a circular crop. Replace with the client-approved vector variant when supplied.

## Final QA

Realistic anatomy; age-appropriate child; consent/rights path; no private child information; no generated logo; room for real type; no claims; no address; no competitor; no face overlap; correct export size.
