# Full Design Guidelines

## 2. Current conditional design system

### Brand feel
Warm, premium, emotionally secure, modern, optimistic, joyful but never chaotic. The creative should reassure a parent first and entertain a child second.

### Strategic visual position
Avoid the category's generic rainbow/clip-art look. Present the school as an editorial, modern, future-ready neighbourhood preschool while preserving warmth and accessibility.

### Layout
- One dominant message and one dominant focal subject.
- Large readable headline; supporting copy short.
- Rounded cards, soft shapes and generous spacing.
- Use split layouts or a large child image plus information block.
- Keep 30–40% usable negative space when photography supports overlaid type.
- Never overlay text across a child's face; this was a recovered revision for carousel slide 5.
- Final CTA should be clear but not aggressive.

### Photography and casting
- Indian preschool-age children and local-feeling classrooms.
- Natural, candid expressions and warm daylight.
- Authentic teacher-child interaction.
- Avoid overexposure, harsh flash, fake smiles, Western-stock defaults and impossible classrooms.
- Real enrolled pupils require parental consent and a usage record.

### Icon and motif system
Minimal rounded icons, thin-outline educational symbols, soft doodles and a subtle lightbulb/curiosity motif. Avoid emojis as permanent design assets, low-resolution clip-art and sticker overload.

### Typography
Use rounded sans-serif headlines and clean modern sans-serif body copy. Keep one hierarchy, two type families maximum and no rainbow text.

### CTA and footer
One action per design. Until NAP is locked, use the phone only after client verification and omit the address. Never fill an address placeholder with a guess.

## 3. Current conditional colour system

Use roughly 70% neutral, 20% brand colour and 10% CTA/accent. Lead with orange and blue; use yellow as a highlight. Cream or deep navy can be the main ground. Dark layouts are allowed, but black-badge-specific placement is not yet authorized.

See `Colour_Palette.md` for current, sampled, legacy and unverified palettes.

## 4. Social and campaign formats

### Photoreal trust/admissions
Large child or parent-child moment, emotional hook, limited benefit proof and a visible tour/enquiry CTA.

### Light educational
Cream background, blue/orange hierarchy, simple icons and one idea per panel.

### Dark premium announcement
Deep navy or near-black, warm orange/yellow accents, elegant restrained celebration. Do not assume the logo itself is a black badge.

### Infographic
Three to five concise cards; each card contains one label and one short explanation.

### Carousel
Slide 1: curiosity gap.  
Middle: one idea per slide.  
Final: one verified CTA.  
Keep numbering, logo scale, margins and footer behavior consistent.

### GMB
1200×900 preferred, minimal text, useful local information, one CTA and no spam urgency.

## 5. Copy and claim guardrails

- Use “supports”, “helps develop”, “encourages” and “designed to nurture”.
- Do not promise IQ, fluency, admission outcomes, confidence or academic success.
- Do not use “best”, “No.1”, “top-rated” or rating counts without dated approved evidence.
- Do not manufacture seat scarcity.
- Safety, CCTV, ratio, educator, hygiene and facility statements must be in the approved-claims register.
- Do not reference competitors.

## 6. Production QA

Check:
1. Correct source logo file and clear space.
2. Mobile readability in two seconds.
3. One focal point and no face overlap.
4. Controlled palette and consistent fonts.
5. Realistic, safe imagery.
6. Verified session, age, offer, phone and CTA.
7. Address omitted until canonical NAP approval.
8. Consent/rights record attached.
9. Status remains DRAFT/PENDING_CLIENT until approval evidence is logged.
10. Final export entered into `ASSET_INDEX.csv`.

## 7. Legacy visual system

May 13 sources used a pastel-forward preschool palette and a more colourful, cheerful tone. They remain useful for historical reference but are superseded. Do not silently merge legacy colours, footer structures or stale admissions copy into the current system.
