# Typography Guide

## Conditional active recommendations

### Headlines
Baloo 2, Fredoka, Poppins ExtraBold or Nunito ExtraBold.

### Body
Poppins, Nunito or DM Sans.

## Rules
- Use no more than two type families per creative.
- Use one dominant headline, normally 3–8 words.
- Keep body copy short and mobile-readable.
- Use weight, size and one accent colour for emphasis; never rainbow text.
- Maintain generous line height and avoid condensed body text.
- Use real type in Canva/Figma/Adobe after image generation.
- Confirm commercial/font licensing before print or large-scale paid use.
- Never share or redistribute font files through this handover.

## Carousel hierarchy
- Cover: large emotional hook.
- Middle: slide title under eight words; one idea; body normally under 25–40 words.
- Final: one CTA and verified contact treatment.
