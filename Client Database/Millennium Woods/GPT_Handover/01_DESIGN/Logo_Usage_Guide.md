## Provisional use

- Use the supplied horizontal file only when a white or light background is appropriate.
- Keep clear space approximately equal to the height of the capital “M”.
- Prefer top-left or centred placement.
- Scale proportionally.
- Keep the full mascot, tagline and wordmark visible.
- Do not crop into a circular avatar unless an approved avatar asset is provided.
- Do not recolour, trace, stretch, rotate, add shadows, put inside a coloured pill or redraw text.
- Do not use AI to recreate the logo.
- For small avatars, request a dedicated approved icon/monogram.

## Required logo pack

Collect:
1. Primary horizontal vector: AI/EPS/SVG/PDF.
2. Secondary badge/stacked lockup, if valid.
3. Social-avatar version.
4. Transparent PNG exports.
5. One-colour and reverse versions.
6. RGB, CMYK and spot-colour values.
7. Minimum-size and clear-space specification.
8. Written hierarchy stating where each version is used.
