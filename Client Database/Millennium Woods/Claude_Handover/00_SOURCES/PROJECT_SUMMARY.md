> Last updated: 2026-07-16 · Prepared as a handover from available project history.

## The strategic story (why this client, why now)
Millennium Woods sits in a crowded but high-demand IT-corridor micro-market (Wakad–Tathawade–Marunji–Hinjewadi, Pune) full of dual-income families who need reliable day care and structured early learning. It has two under-used advantages: a **strong-but-invisible reputation** (4.8★ / 254 Justdial ratings vs only ~186 Instagram followers) and a **premium visual identity** that already out-classes the category's clip-art norm. The winning play is *fix what's broken + amplify what's strong* — not spend more.
