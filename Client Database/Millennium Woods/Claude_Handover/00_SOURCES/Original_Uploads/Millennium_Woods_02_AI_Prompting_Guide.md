# UNIVERSAL AI VISUAL RULES

**Realism:** highly photorealistic · editorial-quality · natural expressions · realistic classrooms. Avoid cartoonish/plastic AI children, fake smiles.
**Lighting:** soft daylight · warm indoor light · gentle cinematic warmth. Avoid hard shadows, studio-flash feel, blown highlights.
**Composition:** strong hierarchy · one dominant focal point · layered editorial · left-right layouts · large child image + infographic section.
**Background:** airy · warm · uncluttered · premium — cream textures, soft classroom blur, subtle gradients, **or premium near-black grounds (on-brand)**. Avoid busy/noisy patterns.
**Premium cues:** soft shadows · rounded cards · breathing space · controlled palette · editorial type · clean CTA areas. Avoid rainbow overload and generic kindergarten templates.
**Spatial density:** medium · layered but breathable · organized info blocks.

**Brand palette to reference (sync w/ Design Instructions):** warm Brand Orange `#EE6A23` + Red `#E23B2C`, Brand Blue/Navy `#2F5DC0`/`#0E2A66`, Warm Yellow `#F6C84A`, Soft Cream `#FFF6EA`, with Pink `#D63C8E` / Teal `#1AA3B0` as sparing accents. Keep the logo's warm orange + royal blue identity present.

# PROMPT WRITING RULES

**Always mention:** realistic Indian children · premium editorial composition · modern Instagram aesthetic · clean type hierarchy · warm lighting · soft cream or premium-dark background · warm-orange + royal-blue brand palette.

**Always avoid:** cartoonish AI look · rainbow overload · generic school-template feel · over-saturation · unrealistic anatomy · text crowding · competitor names · unverified claims/stats/ratings · assumed uniform colors or addresses.

---

# TEXT-LENGTH RULES

**Single posts:** headline 3–8 words · supporting line < 18 words.
**Carousel slides:** 25–40 words/slide max.
**Stories / GMB posts:** very minimal text, one CTA.
