## B. Refined editorial palette (the "premium uplift")
| Purpose | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Warm background | Soft Cream | `#FFF6EA` |
| Premium dark ground | Near-Black / Deep Navy | `#0A0A0A` / `#0E2A66` |
| Primary warm accent | Brand Orange | `#EE6A23` |
| Secondary warm | Brand Red / Coral | `#E23B2C` |
| Trust blue | Brand Blue / Deep Navy | `#2F5DC0` / `#0E2A66` |
| Highlight | Warm Yellow | `#F6C84A` |
| Playful (sparing) | Brand Pink | `#D63C8E` |
| Support | Calm Teal | `#1AA3B0` |
| Growth (sparing) | Soft Green | `#57B947` |

**Balance:** ~70% neutral · ~20% brand colour (orange/blue lead) · ~10% CTA accent.
**Dark backgrounds are ON-BRAND** (the logo is black-ground) — a legitimate primary look for premium/admissions/announcements, not "use sparingly."
**Never:** 5+ saturated colours competing · neon · muddy pastels · anything that buries the warm orange/blue identity.

> ⚠️ CONFLICT: the archived "Master Brand Brain" specifies CTA pink `#F93B7B`. That system is UNVERIFIED/superseded. Use `#D63C8E` from this approved palette.
