# BRAND QUICK REFERENCE — Millennium Woods Techno Kids

> Read this before designing anything. Full detail: `Full_Design_Guidelines.md`. AI prompts: `AI_Image_Prompting_Guide.md`.
> **Active system only.** A conflicting older "brand brain" is archived in `99_ARCHIVE` — ignore it unless the PM says otherwise.

## Brand personality
Warm · premium · emotionally secure · modern · joyful but never noisy · structured yet playful · parent-trust-first. Positioning: *"the warm, premium, future-ready neighbourhood preschool."* Deliberately above the clip-art/rainbow norm.

## Primary colours
- Soft Cream `#FFF6EA` (default background)
- Brand Orange `#EE6A23` (primary warm accent)
- Royal Blue `#2F5DC0` / Deep Navy `#0E2A66` (trust)
- Near-Black `#0A0A0A` (premium dark ground — **on-brand**, the logo is black-ground)

## Secondary / accent colours
- Brand Red / Coral `#E23B2C`
- Warm Yellow `#F6C84A` (highlight)
- Brand Pink `#D63C8E` (playful, sparing)
- Calm Teal `#1AA3B0` (support) · Soft Green `#57B947` (growth, sparing)

**Balance rule:** ~70% neutral (cream OR premium black) · ~20% brand colour (orange/blue lead) · ~10% CTA accent.

## Font system
- **Headlines:** bold rounded sans — Baloo 2 / Fredoka / Poppins ExtraBold / Nunito ExtraBold.
- **Body:** clean sans — Poppins / Nunito / DM Sans.
- One dominant headline per creative; emphasis via weight/colour (orange or blue), never rainbow text.

## Logo placement
Top-left or centred, clear space ≥ height of the "M" on all sides. It's a dark circular badge — on cream, sit it on a subtle white/cream chip or give it breathing room. Never stretch, recolour, shadow, or place on busy backgrounds.

## Design style
Editorial · premium · warm · Instagram-native. Strong hierarchy, one focal point, soft rounded shapes, comfortable spacing, photoreal + infographic hybrid (the most-recommended format).

## Image style
Real Indian children · candid warm smiles · natural golden daylight · clean real classrooms. **Consented imagery only.** Avoid stock-stiff posing, fake/AI smiles, plastic children, harsh flash.

## CTA style
Rounded pill · high contrast · bold type. Primary = Brand Orange or Pink fill + white text. Secondary = Deep Navy/Blue fill + white text. Approved copy: "Book a Free Tour" · "Enquire Now" · "WhatsApp Us" · "Admissions Open" · "Visit Us." Never "Click here" or guarantee-style CTAs.

## Important restrictions (compliance)
- ❌ No address in footers/creatives → use `[CANONICAL NAP — PENDING]` + phone +91 9325149578.
- ❌ Never write "Little Millennium" or any competitor name.
- ❌ No "best/No.1/top-rated" claims; no guaranteed outcomes; no fabricated stats.
- ❌ Don't state the 4.8★ rating (or any rating/award) as a claim until verified.
- ❌ Don't assume uniform colour — say "school uniform" generically until confirmed.

## Preferred post dimensions
IG square 1080×1080 · IG portrait 1080×1350 · Story 1080×1920 · FB/IG ad 1080×1080 or 1080×1350 · Website banner 1920×700 · GMB post 1200×900 (4:3) or 1080×1080.
