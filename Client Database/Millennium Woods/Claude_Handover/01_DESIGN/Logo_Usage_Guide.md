## The mark
A **circular badge on a black ground**: royal-blue outer ring, a child-with-lightbulb mascot, a multi-colour "Millennium Woods" wordmark (warm orange→red), blue "Techno Kids / Pre-School", magenta "Fun • Learn • Grow", and a maroon "Marunji – Wakad" pill.

## Placement rules
- Preferred top-left or centred; clear space ≥ height of the "M" on all sides.
- Dark badge on light/cream layouts → sit on a subtle white/cream chip OR keep enough breathing room that the black ground reads as intentional.
- Never stretch, recolour, add shadows, or place on busy/low-contrast backgrounds.
- On uniforms/fabric (AI or real): match perspective + fabric folds; never float.
