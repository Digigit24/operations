# TYPOGRAPHY GUIDE — Millennium Woods Techno Kids

> From audited Design Instructions (SRC-02).

## Headline
Bold rounded sans, highly readable, emotionally warm (matches the logo's rounded wordmark).
**Recommended:** Baloo 2 · Fredoka · Poppins ExtraBold · Nunito ExtraBold.

## Body
Clean modern sans, high readability.
**Recommended:** Poppins · Nunito · DM Sans.

## Rules
- Strong hierarchy; one dominant headline per creative.
- Generous line height.
- Emphasis via weight/colour (Brand Orange or Blue) — **never** rainbow text.
- Max ~2 type families per piece; max ~3 hierarchy levels per piece.
- Must be mobile-readable.
