# AUDIENCE PROFILES (ICPs) — Millennium Woods Techno Kids

## ICP 1 — The Working IT-Corridor Parent (PRIMARY)
Dual-income family in Wakad/Tathawade/Hinjewadi, one/both in IT/services. Wants trustworthy day care + structured early learning near home/work. Pains: guilt/anxiety leaving the child, time-poor, safety fears. Triggers: clean facility, low ratio, CCTV, warm staff, easy tour booking, strong reviews. Channels: Google Maps, Instagram, WhatsApp, parent groups. CTA that works: **"Book a free tour."**

## ICP 2 — The First-Time Anxious Parent
Enrolling first child; highly emotional. Needs reassurance, warmth, proof other parents trust the school. Responds to testimonials, behind-the-scenes warmth, founder/teacher faces.

## ICP 3 — The Aspirational "Best-for-my-child" Parent
Willing to pay for premium; wants modern, future-ready development. Responds to the "techno / future-ready" angle, enrichment programs, premium visual brand.

## ICP 4 — The Relocating Family
New to Pune/Wakad, searching cold on Google/Maps. Wins on strong local SEO + GBP + reviews + easy contact.

## Buyer psychology (short)
- **Fears:** "Will my child be safe?" · "Am I a bad parent for using day care?" · "Is this place actually good or just marketing?"
- **Desires:** a confident, happy, school-ready child · convenience · pride in a good choice · belonging.
- **Trust-fast levers:** authentic photos of *this* school, named teachers, parent testimonials, visible ratio/safety, instant WhatsApp/call, easy tour booking.
