# PROJECT SUMMARY — Neurological Society of Pune (NSP)

## Who the client is

The **Neurological Society of Pune (NSP)** is a registered academic society (Societies Registration Act), founded in **2010**, made up of **70+ neurologists and neurosurgeons** practising in Pune. Its stated purpose is to propagate advances in neurology through continuous academic activity: monthly case discussions, conferences, and education for postgraduate trainees (PGs).

**It is a society, not a clinic or hospital. It has no patients, no treatments, no service address.** This single fact drives every rule in the project.

Source: `Pune_Neuro_Society_01_Strategic_Research.md` (verified from puneneurosociety.org homepage/About).

## Who the audience is

Peers — not patients:
1. Senior consultant neurologists / neurosurgeons in Pune (member core)
2. Mid-career neurologists (engaged members)
3. Neurology PGs / residents (member pipeline)
4. National & international invited faculty
5. Concerned public (small, selective, secondary)

Full profiles: `06_CLIENT_DETAILS/Audience_Profiles.md`.

## What NSP runs (its "products")

- **Monthly Neuromeets** — backbone. Monthly hospital case-discussion meets since 2010.
- **NSP Approach Meet** — signature biennial conference (brand-defining).
- **PG Approach Meeting** — annual PG-focused meeting.
- **Invited Lectures** — national & international speakers.
- **Public Awareness Drives** — small, secondary social-media workstream.
- **National conferences** — hosted IANCON 2011; hosting **ICTRIMS 2026**.

## The core strategic insight

NSP has genuine academic credibility and real international convening power (ICTRIMS 2026 pulls tier-one global faculty), but **none of that is yet compounding into a lasting digital knowledge asset.** The opportunity is to make NSP "a city society that behaves like an institution" online — a structured knowledge archive, distinctive visual identity, year-round PG content, and a clear member-value narrative. Peer societies look generic; NSP can occupy this lane.

## Three positioning options (client must choose — Step 06)

- **A — "The Pune neurology knowledge archive"** (editorial, library-like; best for long-term authority)
- **B — "The PG-friendly society"** (contemporary, accessible; best for member-growth velocity)
- **C — "The international-convening regional society"** (premium, conference-grade; best for prestige)

The base brand system works under all three; only light styling shifts. ⬜ Not yet locked.
