## The Society-Lens filter (apply to every prompt)
Before generating, confirm: audience is a peer (not patient) · framing is academic (not commercial) · no clinical clichés · 90/7/3 colour discipline · one focal element + whitespace · looks like an institution (not a startup/hospital/wellness brand).

## Brand anchor block (paste into every prompt)
```
Ink Black #0A0E1A, Paper Cream #F8F5EE, Stone Grey #2C2F36,
Brain Teal #0E7C7B (primary accent, sparingly),
Heritage Ochre #C68B2A (prestige accent, very sparingly).
Multi-colour brain mark appears only in the NSP logo, never elsewhere.
No pure white. No bright cyan. No clinical blue gradients. No green wellness palette.
Editorial-academic, magazine-grade. Reference: NEJM / Nature Reviews Neurology.
```

## Do / Don't for prompt-writing
- **Do:** declare "academic society, not a clinic" first; specify hex codes; specify serif+sans+mono; pick one of the 13 design types; include the anti-pattern list; state output size last.
- **Don't:** use clinical words ("healthcare", "patient care"); use vague marketing words ("modern", "vibrant", "eye-catching", "professional") — they trigger generic stock-AI output; ask AI to generate real named faces; ask AI to redraw the NSP logo (designer places it).

## Workflow
AI generates the visual base (scenes, editorial illustrations, mood plates) → **designer finalises typography, logo, CTA, and layout grid in Figma.** Typography and logo are never left to the generator.
