# BRAND QUICK REFERENCE — NSP
### Read this before starting any design work. (~2 min)

## The one rule
**NSP is a medical *society*, not a clinic/hospital. Audience is peers (doctors, PGs, faculty), never patients.** Editorial-academic, not clinical-consumer.

## Brand personality
An **institution, not a club.** Scholarly, convening, considered, confident — never loud, never boastful, never "warm & caring" (warmth is a clinic value).

## Primary colours (neutrals — 90% of every layout)
- Ink Black `#0A0E1A` — headlines, primary text, dark backgrounds (never pure `#000`)
- Paper Cream `#F8F5EE` — default background (never pure white `#FFFFFF`)
- Stone Grey `#2C2F36` — body text
- Cream White `#FDFCF8` — card surfaces

## Accent colours (used sparingly)
- **Brain Teal `#0E7C7B`** — primary CTAs, links, key accents (~7%)
- **Heritage Ochre `#C68B2A`** — prestige moments only: orations, conference branding (~3%)
- Ratio rule: **90 neutral / 7 teal / 3 ochre.** The multi-colour logo is the ONLY place all colours appear together.

## Font system
- **Source Serif Pro** — display / headlines (weights 500–600, tight letter-spacing)
- **Inter** — body & UI (400 body, 600 buttons)
- **IBM Plex Mono** — dates, times, metadata
- All open-source. Body line length ≤ 70 characters.

## Logo placement
Multi-colour woven-brain in grey ring. Top-left (web header, most posts) or bottom-right (YouTube thumbnails). Clear space = half the logo's diameter. **Never recolour, stretch, crop, or place on busy photos.**

## Design style
12-col editorial grid, generous whitespace, asymmetric compositions (think *NYT Magazine*, not PowerPoint). Hierarchy: **eyebrow → headline → standfirst → body → metadata → CTA.** One focal element per section.

## Image style
Documentary photography of **real** NSP people/events only. No stock. No over-processing. Illustration = editorial two-tone line art (Ink + Teal), NEJM-grade.

## CTA style
Brain Teal button, Cream White text, **2px corners (not pill-shaped)**, no shadow/glow. Society verbs only: Read / Watch / Register / View / Download / Become a member. **Never** "Book appointment", "Call now", "Limited slots", "Don't miss".

## Hard restrictions (never do)
- ❌ Clinical icons (stethoscope, syringe, pill, heart-line, caduceus)
- ❌ Stock photos of doctors; smiling-doctor-in-white-coat
- ❌ Pure white or pure black; blue-gradient hero blocks; green-on-white wellness look
- ❌ Bright cyan / electric teal (reads tech-startup)
- ❌ Rounded-pill buttons; gradient overlays on photos; confetti/balloons
- ❌ Generic brain icons other than the logo
- ❌ Patient-acquisition framing; commercial superlatives; scarcity/hype

## Preferred post dimensions
- Instagram: **1080 × 1350** (portrait, default)
- LinkedIn: **1200 × 1500** (portrait)
- YouTube thumbnail: **1280 × 720**
