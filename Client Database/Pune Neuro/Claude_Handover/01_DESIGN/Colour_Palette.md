# COLOUR PALETTE — NSP

**Philosophy:** Foundation = neutrals (90%). One teal accent (7%). One ochre prestige accent (3%). The logo brain is the only place all seven+ colours appear together. Source: Design Instructions §3 (🟡 RECOMMENDED — small refinements possible after Step 02 feedback).

## Foundation neutrals (the 90%)
| Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink Black | `#0A0E1A` | 10 / 14 / 26 | Headlines, primary text, dark backgrounds |
| Paper Cream | `#F8F5EE` | 248 / 245 / 238 | Default page background, large surfaces |
| Stone Grey | `#2C2F36` | 44 / 47 / 54 | Body text, secondary headlines |
| Marble Grey | `#6B7280` | 107 / 114 / 128 | Captions, metadata, secondary text |
| Soft Stone | `#D9D6CE` | 217 / 214 / 206 | Hairlines, dividers, surfaces |
| Cream White | `#FDFCF8` | 253 / 252 / 248 | Card surfaces, overlay panels |

## Accents
| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accent 1 | Brain Teal | `#0E7C7B` | 14 / 124 / 123 | Primary CTAs, links, key accents (~7%) |
| Accent 1 dark | Deep Teal | `#075050` | 7 / 80 / 80 | Hover / dark-mode accents |
| Accent 2 | Heritage Ochre | `#C68B2A` | 198 / 139 / 42 | Orations, prestige, conference (~3%) |
| Tertiary (charts only) | Quiet Magenta | `#9C3A6F` | 156 / 58 / 111 | Data-viz only |
| Signal — alert | Signal Red | `#B33A3A` | 179 / 58 / 58 | Errors, deadlines, rare urgency |
| Signal — success | Quiet Green | `#3A7A4E` | 58 / 122 / 78 | Confirmations |

## Ratio rule
- Standard layout: **90% neutral · 7% Brain Teal · 3% Heritage Ochre.**
- Prestige/conference moments: inversion permitted — Ink Black background, Paper Cream text, Heritage Ochre accent; Brain Teal stays ~7%.

## Combinations that work (✅ LOCKED)
- Ink Black bg + Paper Cream text + Brain Teal CTA
- Paper Cream bg + Ink Black text + Brain Teal link
- Cream White card on Paper Cream + Stone Grey body + Brain Teal pull-quote
- Ink Black bg + Heritage Ochre headline (orations only)

## NEVER use (✅ LOCKED)
- ❌ Pure white `#FFFFFF` backgrounds (reads clinical) — use Paper Cream / Cream White
- ❌ Teal + Ochre + Magenta together as primary colours (only the logo is multi-colour)
- ❌ Blue-gradient hero blocks · green-on-white · royal-blue-with-red-CTA
- ❌ Bright cyan / electric teal (tech-startup drift)
- ❌ Pure black `#000` (too harsh) — use Ink Black `#0A0E1A`
