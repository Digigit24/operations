| Programme | Cadence | Audience | Role |

## Positioning of the programme portfolio
- Backbone product: Monthly Neuromeets (low-cost, high-frequency, community-binding)
- Signature product: Approach Meet (biennial, brand-defining)
- Halo product: hosting national meetings (ICTRIMS 2026 = convening power)
- Pipeline product: PG Approach Meeting
- Outreach product: Public Awareness Drives (secondary)
