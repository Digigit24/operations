# Project Summary — Neurological Society of Pune (NSP)

**Last updated:** 2026-07-17

## Client identified
Neurological Society of Pune (NSP), also referred to in project files as Pune Neuro Society. NSP is an academic/professional medical society of neurologists and neurosurgeons in Pune, not a clinic, hospital, or patient-service provider.

## Confirmed core facts from available project files
- Founded / established: 2010.
- Member count: 70+ stated on the public homepage and project research.
- Legal/category framing: registered society under the Societies Act.
- Composition: neurophysicians, neurologists and neurosurgeons practising in Pune.
- Current activity universe: Monthly Neuromeets, NSP Approach Meet, PG Approach Meeting, invited lectures, public awareness drives, and ICTRIMS 2026.
- Website: puneneurosociety.org.
- Social handles recorded: YouTube `@neurologicalsocietyofpune`, Instagram `@neurosociety_pune`, Facebook references vary between `NSI.Pune` in earlier research and `neurologicalsocietypune` on current web/search results.

## Strategic interpretation
NSP is best understood as an academic institution-building brand. Its most important opportunity is to turn live academic programming into a durable digital knowledge asset: lecture archive, case library, PG hub, conference summaries, and society-level public awareness where appropriate.

## Current active brand system
Editorial-academic-institutional. Restrained, scholarly, peer-to-peer. Use cream/ink/stone neutral base with Brain Teal and Heritage Ochre accents. The multi-colour brain appears only in the official logo.

## Current active / mentioned campaigns and workstreams
- ICTRIMS 2026 Pune — event date already passed relative to this handover date; performance and post-event assets not found.
- NSP Approach Meet — biennial signature conference; Approach Meet 2025 appears in public social/search context but needs asset extraction.
- Monthly Neuromeets — recurring backbone programme.
- PG Approach Meeting — annual PG-focused programme.
- Public Awareness Drives — mentioned as ongoing but requires clinical review and positioning limits.
