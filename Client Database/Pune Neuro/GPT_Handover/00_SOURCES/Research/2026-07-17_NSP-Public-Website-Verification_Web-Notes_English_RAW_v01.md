# Public Website Verification Notes — NSP

**Date checked:** 2026-07-17

These notes summarise current public-facing website/search observations used for the handover. They are not a substitute for client confirmation.

## Current website observations
- Homepage presents the brand as “Neurological Society of Pune” and uses the line “Advancing Neurology Education & Practice Since 2010.”
- Homepage states NSP was established to propagate advances in neurology through continuous academic activities and scientific discussions.
- Homepage shows “2010 Established” and “70+ Members”.
- Website navigation includes Home, About, Committee, Faculty, Activities, NeuroMeets, Conferences, Contact, and a Join Now link.
- NeuroMeets page describes monthly interactive clinical case discussions hosted by Pune hospitals.
- Activities page includes orations, 50+ teaching cases, practical neurology sessions, international/national faculty, interactive meeting features, and Puneri hospitality.
- Contact page exposes a phone number, an email to `ajit@ascenthospitality.in`, and a Kothrud address. This conflicts with design guidance that says no public phone, no street address, no map, and no GMB link.
- IAN events listing confirms ICTRIMS 2026 took place June 12–14, 2026 at Hotel Hyatt Regency, Pune, organised by Neurological Society of Pune.
