HANDOVER PROMPT
Act as a senior project manager, brand strategist and digital asset archivist.
You are currently inside a project, workspace or conversation dedicated to one specific client.
Your job is to review the entire available project history and create a complete, simplified client handover
system containing all important information generated, uploaded, discussed, approved or referenced so far.

Do not ask me to provide the client name unless the workspace clearly contains multiple unrelated clients.

Create a clean client folder system that allows a new project manager, designer, content writer, social media
manager, video editor, SEO executive or ads specialist to understand and continue the project without
depending on previous team members.
