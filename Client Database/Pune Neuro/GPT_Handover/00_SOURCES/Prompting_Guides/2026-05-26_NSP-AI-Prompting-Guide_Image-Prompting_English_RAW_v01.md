## 0. The Society-Lens Filter (apply to every prompt)

Before generating any image for NSP, the prompt must pass these checks:

✅ **Is the audience a peer (doctor / PG / faculty), not a patient?**
✅ **Is the framing academic / educational / institutional, not commercial?**
✅ **Does the image avoid clinical clichés (stethoscope, syringe, pill, white coat, smiling doctor)?**
✅ **Does the colour discipline respect 90/7/3 (neutrals / Brain Teal / Heritage Ochre)?**
✅ **Is there one focal element, generous whitespace, editorial hierarchy?**
✅ **Does it look like an institution, not a startup, hospital, or wellness brand?**

If any check fails, revise the prompt before generating.

## 1. Universal AI Image Prompting Framework

The framework below works across Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, Leonardo, GPT Image, Reve, Runway, and Claude Design.

### Core visual DNA (the spine of every NSP prompt)

```
Visual style: editorial-academic, institutional, restrained, premium-modern, magazine-grade
Lighting: cool natural daylight OR warm tungsten interior — never flash-flat, never studio-clinical
Composition: asymmetric editorial layout, generous whitespace, single focal element, magazine-page hierarchy
Realism level: documentary-photographic OR clean two-tone editorial illustration (no 3D render, no AI-painterly, no cartoon)
Camera feel: 35mm or 50mm equivalent, shallow depth of field for portraits, full depth for environment shots
Background style: muted, considered, never cluttered — paper cream, ink black, or environmental context
Human presence: real NSP people only (never generated faces) OR symbolic silhouettes if illustration
Emotional framing: intellectual seriousness, peer-equality, institutional confidence
Colour restraint: 90% neutrals (ink black, paper cream, stone grey), 7% brain teal accent, 3% heritage ochre accent
Texture: subtle paper-grain in print-style assets, clean flat surfaces for digital
Premium cues: deep blacks, warm cream paper, editorial typography, generous margins, considered crops
Infographic style: medical-textbook line art, two-tone (ink + teal), confident strokes, NEJM-grade rigour
AI clichés to avoid: glossy 3D, hyper-saturation, fake-bokeh, painterly mood, magical realism, sci-fi neon, generic stock-photo lighting
```

### Brand colour anchor block (paste into every prompt)

```
Ink Black #0A0E1A, Paper Cream #F8F5EE, Stone Grey #2C2F36,
Brain Teal #0E7C7B (primary accent, used sparingly),
Heritage Ochre #C68B2A (prestige accent, used very sparingly).
Multi-colour brain mark appears only in the NSP logo, never elsewhere in composition.
No pure white. No bright cyan. No clinical blue gradients. No green wellness palette.
```

## 6. Design Type Prompt Add-Ons (13 types)

Append the relevant add-on to the master template based on the design type required.

### Type 1 — Photorealistic

```
This is a documentary-photographic creative.
Real NSP faculty, members, or PGs at NSP events (no AI-generated faces).
Documentary photography style: 35mm or 50mm equivalent, shallow depth of field on portraits, 
full depth on event halls. Cool natural daylight or warm tungsten interior light.
Restrained colour grading — gentle desaturation, no Instagram filters, no vignette.
Tight, considered crops on faculty. Wide shots that convey scale on event-floor images.
Text panel (if present) sits in a Cream White or Ink Black slab beside or beneath the image.
Logo top-left or bottom-right corner.
```

### Type 2 — Light background

```
Paper Cream background #F8F5EE (NEVER pure white).
Ink Black serif headline at large size.
Stone Grey body, generous line-height.
Brain Teal eyebrow at top, all-caps with letter-spacing.
Optional Brain Teal hairline rule under the eyebrow.
Logo placed top-left or bottom-right at standard size.
Editorial whitespace — 80px+ safe zone from all edges.
```

### Type 3 — Dark background

```
Ink Black background #0A0E1A.
Cream White serif headline at large size.
Heritage Ochre #C68B2A used for prestige accents — eyebrow text, hairline rule, 
or orations-specific headline word.
Brain Teal used only for primary CTA buttons.
Logo on dark background — multi-colour brain reads beautifully here. 
Top-left or bottom-right.
Reserved for premium moments: conference announcements, oration cards, milestone posts.
```

### Type 4 — Infographic

```
Editorial line illustration in two-tone palette: Ink Black + Brain Teal (or Ink Black + Heritage Ochre).
Line weight 1.5–2px, confident strokes.
Reference style: NEJM clinical illustrations, Nature Reviews Neurology schematics, 
medical-textbook line art.
Generous whitespace. Labels in IBM Plex Mono at small caption size.
Source citation at bottom in IBM Plex Mono, Marble Grey.
NO cartoon, NO 3D-rendered, NO flat-vector-Dribbble style, NO emoji.
Topic: [academic topic — e.g., "MS treatment escalation pathway"].
```

### Type 5 — Info + photorealistic

```
Split-frame composition: 60–70% real photography (faculty portrait, event scene), 
30–40% Cream White or Ink Black text slab.
Photo and text must relate directly (e.g., faculty portrait + lecture title and credentials).
Eyebrow → headline → supporting line → attribution.
Logo in the text-slab corner.
```

### Type 6 — Text-heavy 50%

```
Pull-quote layout. Source Serif Pro at very large size (typically 60–80px on social formats), 
occupying ~50% of the frame.
Quote in Ink Black on Paper Cream, or Cream White on Ink Black for prestige variant.
Attribution beneath quote in Inter mono caption, Marble Grey: "— Name, Credentials, Institution".
Optional Brain Teal opening quote-mark glyph at large scale (use sparingly).
NSP logo bottom corner.
```

### Type 7 — Text-heavy 75%

```
Article-card layout. Eyebrow → editorial serif headline → 2–3 paragraphs of summary body → CTA.
75% of frame is text. Optional 25% photographic element (faculty portrait, small thumbnail, 
or session imagery).
Strict typographic hierarchy. Body in Inter 400, line-height 1.6, Stone Grey on Paper Cream.
Logo bottom corner.
```

### Type 8 — Text-heavy 100%

```
Pure typographic statement. Display serif at very large size (90–140px on social formats).
Single focal sentence on Paper Cream OR Ink Black background.
Minimal supporting type (small attribution or date in mono).
Reserved for milestone moments — NSP anniversaries, founding-day reflections, 
society-day statements.
Use sparingly — overuse dilutes the impact.
```

### Type 9 — Academic milestone (replaces "festival post")

```
Editorial restraint, not party motifs. 
For NSP anniversaries, World Brain Day, World Stroke Day, founding-day reflections.
Heritage Ochre accent permitted for prestige milestones.
NO confetti, NO balloons, NO fireworks, NO Hindi/Marathi shubh-kamna decorative tropes 
unless reviewed and society-appropriate.
A society wishes peers in a peer-appropriate way — restrained, dignified, considered.
```

### Type 10 — Conference / event creative

```
For ICTRIMS 2026, Approach Meet, PG Meeting, Invited Lectures.
Conference logo lockup with NSP logo (NSP logo top-left, conference logo top-right or below).
Date strip in IBM Plex Mono — e.g., "JUN 12—14 · 2026 · HYATT REGENCY · PUNE".
Headline serif at large size — name of the session, oration, or session theme.
Faculty grid (if multiple speakers) — 4 to 8 faculty thumbnails with name + institution in mono.
CTA in Brain Teal: "Register for ICTRIMS 2026" / "View the programme" / "Download the schedule".
Hard rule: NO "limited seats", NO "don't miss", NO "last chance". Use factual urgency: 
"Registration closes April 30" / "Abstract submission deadline April 30".
```

### Type 11 — Programme announcement

```
Eyebrow tag (e.g., "MONTHLY NEUROMEET" / "PG APPROACH MEETING") at top.
Date and venue strip in mono beneath eyebrow.
Editorial serif headline — the topic or session theme.
Faculty / presenter name and institution in mono caption.
CTA — society-appropriate.
Whitespace generous. No promotional hype.
```

### Type 12 — Carousel cover

```
Opening slide of an educational carousel.
Eyebrow at top (category tag — e.g., "LECTURE SUMMARY", "CASE DISCUSSION", "CONFERENCE RECAP").
Editorial serif headline — promise of the carousel.
Optional 1-line standfirst.
"Swipe →" indicator in Brain Teal at bottom-right.
Slide-counter dots optional at top-right (e.g., "1 / 8").
Logo bottom-left.
```

### Type 13 — Educational carousel slide

```
Slides 2–N of an educational carousel.
One concept per slide.
Consistent grid: slide number in mono top-right (e.g., "02 / 08"), 
continuation indicator bottom-right.
Each slide: small eyebrow → mid-size serif sub-headline → 2–4 lines of body OR diagram + caption.
Maintain typographic discipline across all slides — same scale, same grid, same accents.
Final slide always carries the CTA (society-appropriate).
```

| Conference creative | 20–55 words | Conference name + date + venue + 1 headline + CTA |

| Carousel inner slide | 25–80 words | One concept; depends on slide role |
| YouTube thumbnail | 4–10 words | Headline must be readable at 320×180 preview size |

### What AI generates vs. what gets finalised manually

**AI image generators produce:**
- Photographic-style scenes (when no real NSP photo exists yet)
- Editorial illustrations (infographics, treatment pathways, neuroanatomy line art)
- Background plates, mood images
- Conference faculty grid layouts (placeholder)

**Manually finalised in Figma / Canva / Photoshop:**
- All typography (better control over hierarchy, kerning, weight)
- Logo placement (precise positioning)
- Date strips, mono metadata
- CTAs (consistent button system)
- Final text content (proof-read, fact-checked)

**Recommended workflow:** AI generates the visual base → designer finalises typography, logo, CTA, layout grid in Figma.

## 9. Prompt Writing Rules

### Do ✅

- Always declare the society context first ("Neurological Society of Pune — academic society — not a clinic")
- Always specify the colour palette with hex codes
- Always declare typography direction (editorial serif headline + clean sans body + mono metadata)
- Always specify the design type from the 13 types
- Always include the anti-pattern list (no clinical iconography, no stock, no clichés)
- Always specify output size at the end
- Use "editorial," "institutional," "considered," "restrained" as anchor descriptors

### Don't ❌

- Don't use clinical vocabulary in the prompt ("healthcare," "patient care," "medical treatment")
- Don't use marketing vocabulary ("modern," "engaging," "eye-catching," "vibrant") — these produce generic AI output
- Don't ask for "best-in-class," "world-class," "premium feel" — too vague, produces stock AI
- Don't use "minimalist" alone — too thin; pair with "editorial-academic"
- Don't ask for "professional" — it triggers stock-photo behaviour
- Don't ask AI to generate real human faces of named people (use real photography)
- Don't ask AI to reproduce the NSP logo (logo is placed by designer, not regenerated)

## 12. Society-Specific Compliance Rules for AI-Generated Content

### Hard prohibitions in any AI-generated NSP image

- ❌ No AI-generated photographs of real, named people (use real NSP photography)
- ❌ No AI-generated faces of "doctors" or "patients" — produces stock-AI clichés
- ❌ No clinical iconography (stethoscope, syringe, pill, hospital cross, ECG line)
- ❌ No patient imagery
- ❌ No before/after framing
- ❌ No treatment-result claims
- ❌ No "consult Dr. [member name]" referral functions
- ❌ No commercial superlatives ("best," "world-class," "leading," "premier")
- ❌ No scarcity / urgency tactics in conference creatives ("only 50 seats," "last chance")

### Required disclaimers (when applicable)

| Content type | Required disclaimer |
|---|---|
| Public-awareness post | "This information is for general awareness. Consult your doctor for individualised advice." |
| Treatment-pathway infographic | Source citation (peer-reviewed guideline or original publication) |
| Lecture-summary post | Speaker attribution + date + event |
| Sponsored content | "Supported by [sponsor name]" clearly labelled |
| Statistical claim | Source + year of data |

### Pre-publication review for AI-generated content

All AI-generated images for NSP go through:

1. **PM check** — brand, type, society lens, anti-patterns
2. **Clinical lead check** (for any clinical / public-awareness content) — factual accuracy, attribution, disclaimer
3. **Designer finalisation** — typography, logo, CTA, layout grid in Figma
4. **Final approval** by NSP secretary / president (for high-stakes assets — ICTRIMS, Approach Meet, oration cards)
