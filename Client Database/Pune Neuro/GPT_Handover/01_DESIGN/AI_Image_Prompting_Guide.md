## Universal context
Neurological Society of Pune is an academic society of neurologists and neurosurgeons in Pune. It is not a clinic, hospital, or patient-service provider.

## Prompt spine
Editorial-academic, institutional, restrained, magazine-grade, asymmetric composition, generous whitespace, single focal element. Use Ink Black, Paper Cream, Stone Grey, Brain Teal and Heritage Ochre. Place the official logo manually; do not ask image tools to recreate it.

## Core restrictions
No clinical icons, no stock doctors, no AI-generated named doctors, no patients, no before/after, no treatment-result claims, no “best/world-class/leading” claims, no scarcity tactics.

## Required disclaimers
- Public awareness: “This information is for general awareness. Consult your doctor for individualised advice.”
- Treatment pathway: cite peer-reviewed guideline/source.
- Lecture summary: include speaker attribution, event and date.
- Sponsored content: label sponsor clearly.

## Workflow
AI generates visual base → designer finalises typography/logo/layout in Figma/Canva → PM brand check → clinical review where applicable → final NSP approval.
