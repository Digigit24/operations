| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Ink Black | `#0A0E1A` | Headlines, primary text, dark backgrounds |
| Foundation | Paper Cream | `#F8F5EE` | Default background, large surfaces |
| Foundation | Stone Grey | `#2C2F36` | Body text, secondary headlines |
| Foundation | Marble Grey | `#6B7280` | Captions, metadata |

| Accent 1 dark | Deep Teal | `#075050` | Hover states, dark-mode accents |
| Accent 2 | Heritage Ochre | `#C68B2A` | Orations, conference prestige moments |
| Tertiary | Quiet Magenta | `#9C3A6F` | Data viz only, very sparingly |
| Signal | Signal Red | `#B33A3A` | Errors, factual deadlines only |
| Signal | Quiet Green | `#3A7A4E` | Confirmations/success |

## Usage rules
- Neutrals carry most layouts.
- Brain Teal is functional, not decorative.
- Heritage Ochre is reserved for prestige.
- Logo is the only place all logo colours appear together.
- Do not use pure white, royal-blue/red CTA combinations, green wellness palettes, bright cyan, clinical blue gradients, or black `#000000`.
