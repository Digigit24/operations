# Brand Quick Reference — NSP

## Brand personality
Institutional, scholarly, convening, considered, confident. NSP must feel like an academic society and intellectual community, not a clinic, hospital, club, startup, or consumer wellness brand.

## Primary colours
- Ink Black — `#0A0E1A`
- Paper Cream — `#F8F5EE`
- Stone Grey — `#2C2F36`
- Brain Teal — `#0E7C7B`
- Heritage Ochre — `#C68B2A`

## Colour ratio
90% neutrals, 7% Brain Teal, 3% Heritage Ochre. The multi-colour brain should appear only in the official logo.

## Font system
- Headlines: Source Serif Pro, fallback Lora/Georgia.
- Body/UI: Inter, fallback system sans.
- Dates/metadata: IBM Plex Mono, fallback Menlo/Consolas.

## Logo placement
- Web header: top-left.
- Social: top-left or bottom-right depending on composition.
- YouTube thumbnail: bottom-right, avoiding platform duration overlay.
- Minimum clear space: half logo diameter.

## Design style
Editorial-academic-institutional: generous whitespace, hairline rules, cream panels, mono date strips, strict hierarchy.

## Image style
Use real documentary academic/event photography. Avoid stock doctors, stethoscopes, pills, syringe, hospital icons, generic brain icons, blue gradients, glow effects, and cartoon/3D medical visuals.

## CTA style
Use society verbs: Read, Watch, Register, View, Download, Become a member, Explore past events. Never use clinic verbs like Book appointment, Consult Dr. X, Get treatment, Call now.

## Preferred dimensions
- Instagram portrait/carousel: 1080×1350.
- Instagram square: 1080×1080.
- Story/Reels cover: 1080×1920.
- LinkedIn portrait: 1200×1500.
- YouTube thumbnail: 1280×720.
- Website hero: 1920×800.
- Conference banner: 1920×1080.

## Restrictions
No patient-acquisition framing. No referral to any specific member practice. Public-awareness content needs clinical review and disclaimer. Sponsored content must be labelled.

## Approval status
This is the active draft system from project design files. It still needs client confirmation for typography, accent palette, reference designs, positioning, and public-awareness appetite.
