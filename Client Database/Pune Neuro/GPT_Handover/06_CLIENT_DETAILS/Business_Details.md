# Business / Society Details

NSP is an academic/professional society of neurologists and neurosurgeons in Pune. Its purpose is continuing neurology education, academic discussion, peer convening, PG education and selective public awareness.

## What NSP is not
- Not a clinic.
- Not a hospital.
- Not a patient acquisition platform.
- Not a referral engine for member practices.
- Not a local service business by default.

## Trust signals found
- Established in 2010.
- 70+ members stated publicly.
- Monthly Neuromeets continuing since formation.
- IANCON 2011 hosted by NSP.
- NSP Approach Meet is a national-format biennial conference.
- ICTRIMS 2026 hosted in Pune with IAN Sub-Section on CNS Auto-Immune Disorders.
- Heritage anchors mentioned in research: Dr. RS Wadia and Dr. PG Divate.

## Claims requiring caution
Any “best”, “leading”, outcome, patient story, review count, experience count, patient number or success-rate claim requires primary evidence and client/clinical approval.
