# Brand Positioning

## Current strategic frame
NSP is a city-anchored academic neurology society that can behave like an institution online. It already has academic programming, heritage, members, and convening power. The opportunity is to make this value permanent and discoverable.

## Positioning options to confirm
### A. The Pune neurology knowledge archive
Best for long-term authority. Focus on lecture archive, case library, PG resources and conference takeaways.

### B. The PG-friendly society
Best for future member pipeline. Focus on PG Approach Meeting and year-round exam-oriented learning.

### C. The international-convening regional society
Best for prestige and conference signalling. Focus on ICTRIMS, international faculty, IAN partnerships and Approach Meet.

## Recommendation
Choose one primary positioning, then keep the other two as support pillars. Do not run all three equally.
