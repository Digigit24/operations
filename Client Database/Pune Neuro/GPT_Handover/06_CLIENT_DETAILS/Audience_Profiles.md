# Audience Profiles

## 1. Senior Consultant
Established Pune neurologist/neurosurgeon. Needs peer respect, efficient academic value, institutional pride, named academic roles and serious programming.

## 2. Mid-Career Neurologist
5–15 years post-DM/MCh. Wants case-presentation opportunities, CME/academic depth, reputation building and subspecialty exposure.

## 3. Neurology PG
DM/DNB Neurology or MCh Neurosurgery resident. Needs curated, exam-aligned, case-based learning and mentorship visibility.

## 4. National / International Faculty
Senior invited speaker. Needs credible host, serious audience, good moderation, logistical professionalism and reusable recorded outputs.

## 5. Concerned Public Member
Occasional audience for public-awareness content only. Must receive general awareness, not individualised advice or referral pathways.
