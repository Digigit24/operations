# Carousel Content Reference

## Valid carousel themes
- Lecture summary
- Case discussion
- Conference recap
- PG explainer
- Public awareness, only with disclaimer and clinical review

## Structure
Cover: eyebrow + editorial headline + standfirst + slide counter + logo.  
Inner slides: one concept per slide, slide number top-right, small eyebrow, sub-headline, 2–4 lines body or diagram + caption.  
Final slide: society-appropriate CTA.

## Important
No slide should contain patient identifiers, individualised treatment advice, unsupported claims, or referral copy.
