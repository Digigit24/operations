# Brand Positioning

## Working strategic position

Prime is the locally accessible, doctor-led option for patients in PCMC who need clear surgical guidance and appropriate minimally invasive treatment without the impersonality of a large corporate hospital or the infrastructure uncertainty of a small clinic.

## Transformation

From: “I am scared, unsure what is involved and do not trust the process.”  
To: “I understand the plan, know who is responsible and can return to normal life safely.”

## Differentiation pillars — DRAFT

1. focused procedure expertise;
2. direct named-surgeon access;
3. recovery-aware treatment planning;
4. local accessibility;
5. transparent process and expectations.

Each pillar becomes publishable only after its operational claim is verified.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. patient symptom/question;
2. direct answer;
3. approved evidence and context;
4. local/process relevance;
5. one specific next step.

## Avoid

Generic multi-speciality slogans, luxury cues, startup-aggressive ads, fear/shame, generic “compassionate excellence” language, unsupported superlatives and broad service claims.
