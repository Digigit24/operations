# Audience Profiles

## 1. Working adult with a surgical symptom

- Location: Moshi/PCMC/Bhosari/Chakan/Pune.
- Concerns: downtime, income loss, pain, cost, insurance and whether surgery is necessary.
- Needs: plain diagnosis explanation, realistic timeline, transparent process and a low-friction booking route.
- Content: doctor-led reels, recovery/process guides, cost/insurance information only after verification.

## 2. Family decision-maker / caregiver

- Concerns: safety, doctor credibility, hospital backup, hidden bills and who will operate.
- Needs: approved doctor credentials, real facility proof, written process and clear follow-up.
- Content: doctor profile, hospital tour, consultation process, verified reviews and FAQ.

## 3. Embarrassed or avoidant piles/fissure/fistula patient

- Concerns: shame, pain, examination, graphic content and privacy.
- Needs: discreet language, non-graphic visuals, confidentiality and diagnosis-first education.
- Content: symptom differences, myths, confidential consultation and prevention.

## 4. Acute abdominal-symptom patient/family

- Concerns: whether pain is urgent, where to go and operating availability.
- Needs: clinically approved red flags and exact emergency contact/hours.
- Content: appendix/gallbladder red flags; never use marketing urgency.

## 5. Search/Maps decision-stage patient

- Behaviour: mobile-first, compares reviews, directions, doctor and phone quickly.
- Needs: accurate GBP category, hours, phone, services, photos and responses.
- Content: helpful local updates, real photos and one clear action.

## Shared fears documented in the brand strategy

Procedural pain, work/income loss, unnecessary surgery, surprise billing, junior-surgeon substitution, loss of dignity and distance from help.
