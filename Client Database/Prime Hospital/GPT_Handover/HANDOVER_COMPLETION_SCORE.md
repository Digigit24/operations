## Interpretation

The handover is strong enough for controlled onboarding and remediation. It is not ready for uncontrolled publishing because facts, claims, approvals, rights and live reporting remain incomplete.

The fastest score improvement will come from approvals, canonical client details, asset retrieval/rights and current analytics—not additional ideation.
