# Instagram Weekly Publishing Rhythm

- 2× doctor-led educational reels
- 1× trust post: verified patient review, actual hospital/facility, approved qualification, procedure explanation or team introduction
- 1× local conversion post: consultation availability, verified insurance support, landmark/location guidance or approved booking route

Measure profile visits, contact-button taps, qualified enquiries, booked consultations and consultation-to-procedure conversion. Do not judge the system on follower count alone.
