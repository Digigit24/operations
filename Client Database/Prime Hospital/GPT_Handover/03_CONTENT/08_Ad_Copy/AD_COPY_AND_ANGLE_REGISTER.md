# Ad Copy and Angle Register

No paid-ad creative had clear publication, spend or approval evidence. The following source-derived angles are DRAFT.

## Laser piles consultation

**Primary-text angle:** Fear of surgery should be addressed with diagnosis, treatment options and case-dependent recovery—not a claim that every case is painless.  
**Headline options:** `Piles symptoms affecting daily life?` / `Ask whether laser treatment suits your case`  
**CTA:** `Book Consultation`  
**Risk:** less-pain/minimal-bleeding/faster-recovery claims need medical approval.

## Hernia consultation

**Primary-text angle:** Explain why a lump or pain during lifting/coughing deserves evaluation and compare treatment options.  
**Headline options:** `A hernia needs a clear plan` / `Understand your hernia treatment options`  
**CTA:** `Consult Our Surgeon`  
**Risk:** do not use fixed 5–7-day return-to-work promises.

## Gallbladder

**Primary-text angle:** Recurrent upper-abdominal pain after meals may require assessment; ultrasound/report review can clarify options.  
**Headline:** `Repeated gallbladder pain? Get it assessed.`  
**CTA:** `Book Consultation`  
**Risk:** do not promise one-day stay or fixed cost before approval.

## Doctor continuity

**Primary-text angle:** Patients want to know who will assess and operate. Explain the actual Prime process and exceptions.  
**Headline:** `Know who is responsible for your care`  
**CTA:** `Book with Dr. Bhalerao`  
**Risk:** “same surgeon always operates” is unverified.

## Local access

**Primary-text angle:** Specialist surgical care in Moshi may reduce travel friction for PCMC families.  
**Headline:** `Surgical consultation closer to home`  
**CTA:** `Get Directions` / `Book Consultation`  
**Risk:** confirm exact services and hours.
