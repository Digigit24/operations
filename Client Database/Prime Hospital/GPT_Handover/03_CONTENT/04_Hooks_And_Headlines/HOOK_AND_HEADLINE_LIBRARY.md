- “Compassionate Care. Modern Medicine.” — conflicts with the Master Brand Brain’s vocabulary rules.

## Headline construction rules

- 4–9 words for designed social/GMB image.
- Use the condition/problem first.
- Prefer a question or concrete patient outcome.
- Avoid “best”, “top”, “world-class”, “state-of-the-art”, “cutting-edge”, “guaranteed” and fake urgency.
