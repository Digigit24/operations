## Production rules

- Prefer Marathi or Hindi speech where natural, with accurate English subtitles.
- Show the doctor in the first two seconds where feasible.
- Keep one reel to one question.
- Use calm clinical language; no guarantees, exaggerated outcomes or fear.
- Include Moshi/PCMC naturally.
- Close with one approved action.
