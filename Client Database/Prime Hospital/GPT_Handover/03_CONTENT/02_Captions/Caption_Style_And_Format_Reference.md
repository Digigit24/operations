# Caption Style and Format Reference

## Prime social caption structure

1. Lead with the patient question or fear.
2. Answer it directly in one or two short paragraphs.
3. Add specific, clinically reviewed context.
4. Close with one specific next step.
5. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags at most.

## Tone

Calm, direct, educational and respectful. Use simple patient language. Avoid emotional theatre, forced empathy, sales pressure and generic hospital slogans.

## CTA examples — require canonical channel

- WhatsApp your report for an assessment.
- Book a consultation with Dr. Bhalerao.
- Call the hospital if symptoms are severe.
- Ask whether this treatment is suitable for your case.
- Get the approved cost/insurance information before deciding.

## Hashtag rule

Use 3–5 specific tags, for example a combination of:
`#Moshi` `#PCMC` `#LaparoscopicSurgery` `#PilesAwareness` `#HerniaCare`

Do not stuff location or treatment keywords. Vary tags by topic and channel.

## Medical footer/disclaimer options

- `This content is for awareness and does not replace medical consultation.`
- `Treatment and recovery depend on diagnosis and individual health factors.`
- `Consult a qualified doctor for personalised advice.`

Select the disclaimer based on the claim, not as a ritual footer.
