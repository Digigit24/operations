# Instagram Audit Summary

**Source:** July 2026 competitive and Instagram audit  
**Status:** directional public-surface audit; refresh with Instagram Insights

## Working observations

- The account was described as early-stage with limited visible social proof.
- Treatment-led content and doctor identification were positive foundations.
- The bio did not clearly communicate location, specialist procedures, doctor identity and booking route.
- The feed over-relied on condition-promotion posts and lacked human/doctor/facility proof.
- Highlights and recurring content series were recommended.
- Publicly indexed figures were approximately 16 posts, 7 followers and 0 following at the time of observation, but these are not first-party verified and may be outdated.

## Recommended operating system

- 2 doctor-led reels weekly
- 1 trust/facility/review/doctor post weekly
- 1 local conversion post weekly
- Highlights: Doctor, Piles Care, Hernia, Gallbladder, Reviews, Hospital, Insurance, Location

## Business KPIs

Profile visits, link/contact taps, qualified enquiries, booked consultations and consultation-to-procedure conversion. Do not overvalue followers or video views without enquiries.
