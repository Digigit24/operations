## Important evidence distinction

The document is a strategy, not proof of implementation or current rankings. Search volumes are labelled live estimates rather than verified Ahrefs/Semrush volumes. Prime-specific cost, timeline, insurance, same-surgeon and response-SLA statements are drafts.
