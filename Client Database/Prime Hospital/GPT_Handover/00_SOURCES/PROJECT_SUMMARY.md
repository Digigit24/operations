# Project Summary

## Executive summary

Prime Speciality Hospital has a strong strategic foundation but an incomplete governance and implementation layer. The project produced a doctor-led positioning system, patient-psychology model, visual guidelines, reusable AI prompts, a live-SERP SEO plan, a dated live-data Google Business Profile audit, social-content concepts, bilingual reel scripts, GMB copy, a flyer production chain and at least one publicly matched Instagram reel.

The decisive issue is not a shortage of ideas. It is the absence of a controlled canonical-facts and approval system. Two phone numbers, multiple business-category descriptions, three visual systems, inconsistent hours, unclear doctor-credential wording and numerous unverified medical/commercial claims are present across the materials.

## Project evolution

- **October 2025:** broad reels/YouTube plan and a separate 15-post educational calendar.
- **May 2026:** website/design audit; four English/Marathi doctor-led reel scripts; reusable design and prompting systems; Master Brand Brain; logo-palette correction; live-SERP SEO foundation; live-data GMB foundation.
- **Late May–June 2026:** piles/fissure/fistula/hernia social and GMB concepts; revision cycles; 1080×1920 flyer variants; surgical-awareness captions; public advanced-surgical-care promo reel.
- **July 2026:** comprehensive project audit and competitive/Instagram audit; this consolidated production handover.

## Strongest completed foundations

- patient psychology and local surgical positioning;
- calm, direct, information-first brand voice;
- current working blue/green/white visual tokens;
- detailed prompt templates and image safety rules;
- keyword, page and blog architecture;
- dated GBP performance baseline;
- a sizeable content-idea backlog;
- a practical 30/60/90-day remediation direction.

## Main gaps

- no signed canonical fact sheet;
- no evidence-led medical claims register;
- limited surviving exact script/caption text;
- no reliable approval/publication ledger;
- no editable design masters/vector logo package;
- no documented patient/model consent archive;
- no verified GA4/GSC/Meta/CRM/lead-source reporting;
- implementation of website/GBP recommendations largely unverified.

## Recommended operating sequence

1. Resolve canonical facts and clinical claims.
2. Approve Brand System v2.0.
3. Correct website/GBP/social NAP and trust issues.
4. Instrument call, WhatsApp, form and booking conversions.
5. Publish doctor-led educational content and high-intent treatment pages.
6. Record every approval, publication URL and result in the supplied indexes.
