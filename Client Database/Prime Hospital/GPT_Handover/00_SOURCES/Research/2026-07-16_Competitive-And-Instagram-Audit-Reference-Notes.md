# Competitive and Instagram Audit — Reference Notes

**Referenced File Library item:** `Prime_Speciality_Hospital_Competitive_and_Instagram_Audit.docx`

## Strategic direction preserved

- Position Prime around local advanced surgical/minimally invasive care rather than generic breadth.
- Core strategic message: specialist surgical care in Moshi without central-Pune travel.
- Priority competitive set: ReLive Surgical, KK Care and Dhanashree Hospital.
- Benchmark Accord Hospital for scale, trust and publishing consistency.
- Measure calls, WhatsApp actions, booked consultations and procedure conversion—not followers or reach alone.

## Recommended content architecture

- Two doctor-led reels per week.
- One trust/facility/review/doctor post per week.
- One local conversion post per week.
- Twelve first-month reel topics are registered in `03_CONTENT/03_Content_Ideas/CONTENT_IDEA_REGISTER.csv`.

## Data limitation

Public Instagram counts and competitor observations are directional and may have changed. Instagram Insights, first-party website analytics, CRM/appointment data and live competitor tools are required before budget or performance conclusions.
