## 6. Master Prompt Template

```text
Create a modern, professional healthcare creative for Prime Speciality Hospital.

Client/Brand: Prime Speciality Hospital
Post format: [GMB thumbnail / Instagram post / carousel / ad / website banner]
Design style: [selected style]
Topic: [topic]
Main headline: [headline]
Supporting line: [supporting line]
Key points:
- [point 1]
- [point 2]
- [point 3]
- [point 4]
CTA: [CTA]
Contact details: [phone number / website / location]
Website/URL: [website]
Audience: [target audience]
Visual subject: [visual subject]
Mood: calm, trustworthy, compassionate, modern medical
Logo placement: top-left, clear and visible
Brand colors: Prime Blue #0057D9, Prime Navy #003B8F, Prime Green #0BAA2B, White #FFFFFF, Soft Grey #F5F7FA, Soft Blue Tint #EAF3FF
Output size: [size]

Design direction:
Use a clean white and soft clinical background with strong Prime Blue headline typography and Prime Green accents. The creative should feel trustworthy, compassionate, modern, and patient-friendly. Use rounded cards, clean medical icons, blue-green checkmarks, soft shadows, and a readable footer/contact strip.

Layout:
Keep logo at the top-left. Use one strong headline, one short supporting line, and 3–5 service/facility points in clean icon cards. Use a modern hospital interior or doctor-patient visual, not hospital exterior. Add a deep blue footer strip with green circular icons for call, website, or location.

Typography:
Use bold modern sans-serif typography similar to Poppins, Montserrat, or Inter. Keep all text mobile-readable.

Avoid:
Do not use teal-heavy branding, dark gloomy hospital visuals, hospital exterior, graphic medical images, blood, wounds, scary emergency scenes, clutter, cheap flyer styling, fake awards, or unsupported claims.

Compliance note:
Do not promise guaranteed results, 100% cure, risk-free treatment, or “best hospital” claims without proof.
```

## 13. Text-Length Rules

### GMB Thumbnail

- Headline: 4–7 words
- Supporting line: 6–12 words
- Service points: 4–5 points, 2–5 words each
- CTA: 2–4 words
- Footer: short only

### Instagram Post

- Headline: 5–9 words
- Supporting line: 8–14 words
- Points: 3–5 short points
- CTA: 2–4 words

### Carousel Cover

- Headline: 5–10 words
- Supporting line: 8–14 words
- Swipe cue: 3–6 words

### Website Banner

- Headline: 5–9 words
- Supporting line: 12–22 words
- Key points: 3 short bullets

## 17. Medical Compliance Rules

Never use:

```text
100% cure
Guaranteed recovery
No risk
No complications
Best hospital without proof
Completely painless
Permanent cure guaranteed
```

Use safer alternatives:

```text
Advanced care options
Experienced specialists
Modern facilities
Treatment depends on diagnosis
Consult a qualified doctor
Results may vary
```

Recommended disclaimer:

```text
Treatment results may vary. Please consult a qualified doctor.
```

## 19. Final QA Checklist

Before finalizing a prompt or design, check:

- Does it use the actual Prime logo palette?
- Is blue the trust color and green the healing/accent color?
- Is the logo top-left and readable?
- Is the headline readable in 2 seconds?
- Are service points short?
- Is there enough white space?
- Is the footer readable?
- Does it avoid hospital exterior when not needed?
- Does it avoid graphic visuals?
- Does it look modern, not like a cheap flyer?
- Does it feel compassionate and trustworthy?
