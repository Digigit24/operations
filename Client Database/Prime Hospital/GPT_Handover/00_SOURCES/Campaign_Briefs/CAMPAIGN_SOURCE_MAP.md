# Campaign Source Map

Campaign folders in `05_CAMPAIGNS` are derived from the following evidence:

- Master Brand Brain: strategic audience, fears, positioning and voice.
- SEO Foundation: funnel intent, keywords, service pages, 30 blogs and cost/insurance themes.
- GMB Foundation: 12 post drafts, 15 Q&As, dated profile data and 90-day plan.
- July competitive/Instagram audit: competitor hierarchy, 12 reel topics, publishing rhythm and conversion KPIs.
- Conversation/audit reconstruction: bilingual scripts, static concepts, revisions, flyer chain and published promo reel.

Every campaign folder records what is available, what is absent and which claims require approval.
