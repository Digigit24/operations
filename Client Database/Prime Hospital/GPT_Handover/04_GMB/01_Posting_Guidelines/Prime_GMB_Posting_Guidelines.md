# Prime Google Business Profile Posting Guidelines

## Core standard

Every post should read like a helpful local hospital update—not a performance advertisement.

## Content priorities

1. condition and procedure education;
2. genuine hospital/doctor/facility updates;
3. verified service information;
4. practical patient preparation and local directions;
5. consented and accurately sourced review/recovery proof;
6. current events/camps only when operationally confirmed.

## Caption structure

1. Short question or local service headline.
2. Helpful information in natural language.
3. Prime mention and local relevance.
4. One approved CTA.
5. One canonical phone/booking route if needed.
6. Two to five relevant hashtags at most.

Recommended copy length: roughly 80–250 words.

## Image rules

### Designed post image
- Prefer 1200×900 (4:3) for GBP-safe utility.
- 1080×1080 project templates may be retained only after preview testing.
- 5–20% text coverage; headline ideally 3–8 words.
- Small corner logo; one CTA maximum.
- Moderate branding, natural lighting and realistic local context.

### Real GBP photo library
Use real, unfiltered facility and team photos whenever possible:
- cover: source reference proposes 1332×750;
- logo/profile: source reference proposes 250×250;
- general interior/exterior: at least 720×540 where possible.

A real exterior photo is useful for navigation and verification. The “avoid exterior” rule applies to making it the default hero of designed introduction thumbnails—not to the genuine GBP photo library.

## Do

- keep language helpful, local and professional;
- vary hooks and sentence structure;
- use one phone number only after canonical confirmation;
- use real facility photos and current information;
- respond to genuine user questions and reviews;
- check medical copy with a qualified reviewer;
- state case dependence where required.

## Do not

- use fake urgency, countdowns or “last chance”;
- use keyword stuffing, all caps or emoji spam;
- repeat the same post/template;
- publish guaranteed cure, permanent cure, no-risk, no-complication or “best” claims;
- show blood, wounds, surgical close-ups or misleading before/after results;
- publish fake reviews, certificates, screenshots or patient stories;
- synthetically seed Q&A from personal accounts;
- include more than one phone number;
- reuse a Meta ad unchanged as a GMB post.

## Approval gate

Before scheduling, record:
- content ID and version;
- clinical reviewer;
- facts/claim sources;
- canonical NAP;
- image licence/consent;
- client approval;
- scheduled date;
- final URL after publication.
