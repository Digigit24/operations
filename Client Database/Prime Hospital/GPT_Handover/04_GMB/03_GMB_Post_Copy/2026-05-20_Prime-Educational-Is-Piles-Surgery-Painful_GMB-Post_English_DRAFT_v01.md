Headline: Is Laser Piles Surgery Painful? The Honest Answer

One of the most common questions we get: "Doctor, how painful will it be?"

Honest answer: significantly less painful than you're imagining.

Laser piles surgery uses local anaesthesia. Most patients experience mild discomfort 
for 1–2 days after the procedure — similar to a muscle soreness. Over-the-counter 
pain relief is usually sufficient.

What you won't experience: the severe post-operative pain associated with traditional 
open surgery.

Recovery timeline: 3–5 days for most patients before returning to office work.
