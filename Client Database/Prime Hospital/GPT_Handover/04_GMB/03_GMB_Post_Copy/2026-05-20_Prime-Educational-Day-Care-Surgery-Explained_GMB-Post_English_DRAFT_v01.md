Headline: What Is Day-Care Surgery? Can You Really Go Home the Same Day?

Day-care surgery (also called same-day discharge surgery) means: you arrive at the 
hospital, have your procedure, recover briefly, and go home the same day or the 
next morning.

At Prime Speciality Hospital, most of our laparoscopic and laser procedures are 
day-care eligible:
→ Laser piles surgery: same-day discharge (most cases)
→ Laparoscopic hernia surgery: discharge within 24 hours
→ Laparoscopic gallbladder surgery: discharge within 24 hours

This is possible because modern minimally invasive techniques cause less trauma to 
the body. Less trauma = faster recovery = earlier safe discharge.
