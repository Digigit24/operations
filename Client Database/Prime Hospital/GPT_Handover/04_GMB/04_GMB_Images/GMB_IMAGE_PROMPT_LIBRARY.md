# GMB Image Prompt Library

## Service education

> Create a professional local healthcare image for Prime Speciality Hospital. Show an Indian surgeon explaining a report to an adult patient in a clean, bright consultation room. Natural expressions, calm mood, no eye contact with camera, realistic Indian setting, strong negative space for a 3–6-word headline. Use subtle Prime Blue and Prime Green environmental accents. No graphic medical detail, no stock-photo pose, no text generated inside the image.

## Facility update

> Documentary-style photograph of a clean hospital reception/consultation area in Moshi, India, with staff performing real work. Natural warm side light, accurate equipment, uncluttered composition, no fake luxury, no staged group pose, no text or floating graphics.

## Endoscopy education

> Calm Indian doctor explaining an endoscopy report to an adult patient. Non-scary monitor/equipment in the background, bright hygienic interior, natural skin tones, no procedure in progress, no graphic anatomy, no claim of a result.

## Hernia awareness

> Respectful Indian adult in everyday clothing during a consultation with a surgeon, subtle gesture toward the abdomen, no exaggerated pain, no body-part close-up, clean clinical interior, natural light, space for a short educational headline.

## Negative prompt

No blood, wounds, open surgery, graphic anorectal imagery, dramatic pain face, fake review, fake certificate, large logo, repeated phone number, banner-ad styling, neon colours, holograms, foreign-looking stock setting, distorted hands or text artifacts.

## Design overlay rule

Add logo, headline and CTA in the design application. Use one approved phone number only. Do not make the post look like a sales flyer.
