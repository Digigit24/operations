# Review Request Workflow

## Source context

The May 2026 GMB Foundation proposed asking for reviews 24–48 hours after discharge and a follow-up after five days. Use this only as a fair, privacy-respecting workflow.

## Safe message template

> Hi [First name], we hope your follow-up is going well. If you would like to share feedback about your experience at Prime Speciality Hospital, you can use our official Google review link: [approved link]. Your feedback helps the team improve and helps local patients understand what to expect. Thank you — Team Prime.

## Rules

- Ask all eligible patients consistently; do not review-gate.
- Do not offer discounts, gifts or priority service.
- Do not ask for a five-star rating.
- Do not include diagnosis or procedure in the message unless necessary and privacy-approved.
- Respect opt-out preferences and applicable messaging/privacy law.
- Use the current official review link and canonical business profile.
- Do not ask staff/friends to impersonate patients.
- Record request date and channel without storing unnecessary clinical details.
