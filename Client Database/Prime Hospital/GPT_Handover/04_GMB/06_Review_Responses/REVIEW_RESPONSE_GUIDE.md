# Review Response Guide

## Operating rule

Respond to every genuine review promptly and personally. Mention only what the reviewer already made public, and never reveal diagnoses, procedure details, billing details or private patient information.

## Five-star review with text

> Thank you, [First name], for taking the time to share your experience. We are glad the team could explain the process clearly and make your visit more comfortable. Please contact the hospital through the approved channel if you need follow-up guidance. — Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi

## Five-star rating without text

> Thank you, [First name], for your rating. We appreciate your trust in the team at Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi. — Team Prime

## Three/four-star feedback

> Thank you, [First name], for the honest feedback. We would like to understand what could have been better. Please contact the hospital through the official number shown on our profile so the team can review it privately. — Prime Speciality Hospital

## Negative review

> [First name], thank you for raising this. We are sorry the experience did not meet expectations. To protect privacy, we will not discuss clinical details here. Please contact the hospital through the official profile number so the appropriate team member can review the matter. — Prime Speciality Hospital

## Review with no identifiable patient record

> Thank you for the message. We cannot identify the visit from the information shown here. Please contact the hospital through the official profile number with the visit details so we can check privately. — Prime Speciality Hospital

## Do not

- use identical boilerplate on every review;
- mention diagnosis/procedure unless the reviewer has already made it public and it is safe to acknowledge;
- confirm or deny patient status publicly;
- offer incentives for reviews;
- ask only satisfied patients;
- reuse named patient quotes in marketing without consent;
- argue, threaten or disclose records.
