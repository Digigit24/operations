# Typography Guide

## Current working production system

- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold/SemiBold or Montserrat Bold
- **Subheads/labels:** Poppins SemiBold/Medium or Inter SemiBold
- **Body:** Inter Regular; Open Sans or Lato are acceptable substitutions
- **Data/figures:** Inter tabular figures where available
- Maximum two font families on any surface.

## Approximate social hierarchy

### 1080×1080
- Eyebrow: 24–30 px
- Main headline: 56–78 px
- Supporting line: 28–38 px
- Card/bullet text: 26–34 px
- CTA: 30–38 px
- Footer/contact: 22–28 px

### 1080×1350
- Headline: 64–92 px
- Supporting line: 34–44 px
- Body points: 30–38 px
- CTA: 34–42 px

## Rules

- Keep headlines short, direct and readable in two seconds.
- Prefer left alignment for educational and conversion content.
- Use green on one or two words only.
- Never use script fonts, thin medical headlines, long paragraphs or tiny footer text.
- Ensure English/Marathi/Hindi fonts render all characters correctly; proofread native-language line breaks.

## Unresolved alternative

The Master Brand Brain recommends a modern serif such as Fraunces or Source Serif 4 for headlines and Inter/Geist/Manrope for body. This direction may be used only after a documented channel-level decision. Do not mix serif and Poppins headline systems inconsistently.
