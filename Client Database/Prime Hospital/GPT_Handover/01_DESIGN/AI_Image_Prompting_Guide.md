# AI Image Prompting Guide

## Visual DNA

Create **editorial documentary healthcare photography** with explicit Indian context. The mood is calm, clear, competent and reassuring—not staged corporate promotion.

### Include

- Indian doctor, patient, family or staff;
- real-feeling Prime-style consultation room or hospital interior;
- soft natural side light;
- warm-neutral highlights with blue/green environmental accents;
- subject off-centre with useful negative space;
- realistic skin tones, clothing and expressions;
- doctor looking at/explaining to the patient, not posing at camera;
- quiet, focused staff activity;
- shallow depth of field where appropriate.

### Exclude / negative prompt

Stock-photo grin, arms-crossed doctor, thumbs-up, floating holographic anatomy, DNA overlays, futuristic chrome hospital, theatrical blue surgery lighting, blood, wounds, open surgery, graphic anatomy, exaggerated pain, fake award wall, fake patient testimonial, foreign-looking generic setting, text inside generated image, distorted hands, oversaturated neon green, glassmorphism, gradient mesh, spa imagery.

## Prompt scaffold

> Editorial documentary photograph for Prime Speciality Hospital. [Indian subject and action]. [Specific real-feeling hospital interior]. Soft natural side light from [direction], warm-neutral highlights, subtle Prime Blue and Prime Green environmental accents, calm focused mood, realistic Indian clothing and features, negative space for design copy, shallow depth of field, no eye contact with camera, no stock-photo pose, no holograms, no graphic medical detail, no text in image.

## Subject templates

### Doctor consultation
Indian male surgeon in a fitted white coat explaining a report to a middle-aged Indian patient and family member; hands naturally indicating the report; calm expressions; bright consultation room; no staged smile.

### Staff in action
Indian nurse preparing a clean patient-care area; real task in progress; warm natural light; equipment present but non-scary; no pose.

### Recovery
Indian adult patient walking comfortably with a family member in a bright corridor; realistic, modest expression; no claim of guaranteed outcome.

### Facility
Actual-feeling reception, consultation room, corridor or equipment setup; clean, lived-in, credible; no luxury hotel or sci-fi aesthetic.

## Consistency controls

- Do not generate a likeness of Dr. Bhalerao unless a permitted reference image is supplied and approved.
- Maintain the same wardrobe, environment and colour grading across campaign image sets.
- Add all logos and typography in the design layer, not in the generated image.
- Treat AI images as synthetic illustration unless the client explicitly authorises their use; never imply they depict real patients, staff or facilities.
