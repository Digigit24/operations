# Brand Quick Reference — Read Before Designing

**Status:** Current working reference, pending formal client approval  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

## Brand personality

Trustworthy, specialist-led, calm, clear, modern medical, locally accessible, patient-respectful and recovery-focused. The emotional territory is **quiet relief and restored normalcy**, not fear, luxury, hype or exaggerated transformation.

## Primary colours

- Prime Blue `#0057D9`
- Prime Navy `#003B8F`
- Prime Green `#0BAA2B`
- Prime Dark Green `#067A1B`
- Medical White `#FFFFFF`

## Secondary/support colours

- Soft Clinical Grey `#F5F7FA`
- Soft Blue Tint `#EAF3FF`
- Soft Green Tint `#EAFBEF`
- Charcoal `#1C2430`
- Slate Grey `#5E6B75`
- Controlled Red `#D64545` — emergency/warning only
- Soft Amber `#F5A623` — attention only

## Font system

**Current working social/GMB system:** Poppins or Montserrat for headlines; Inter/Open Sans/Lato for body.  
**Unresolved strategic alternative:** editorial serif headlines + geometric sans body from the Master Brand Brain. Do not mix both systems within one campaign.

## Logo placement

- Default: top-left.
- Top-centre: formal announcement only.
- Top-right: when left-aligned headline needs the left side.
- Footer: only when top area is crowded.
- Never stretch, recolour, rebuild, shadow heavily or place on a busy image.
- Keep the logo as a trust marker, not the headline.

## Design style

White-dominant, blue-led hierarchy, green recovery cues, generous spacing, short mobile-readable copy, rounded utility cards, consistent line icons and restrained shadows. Use editorial restraint: remove anything that does not help comprehension or trust.

## Image style

Realistic Indian doctor/patient/hospital-interior imagery; natural expressions; bright hygienic spaces; calm consultation or staff-at-work moments. Avoid graphic procedures, blood, exaggerated pain, cliché hero poses, fake facilities, floating holograms and foreign-looking stock.

## CTA style

One specific CTA: `Book Consultation`, `WhatsApp Your Report`, `Call for Appointment`, `Consult Dr. Bhalerao` or another approved next step. Rounded blue/green button with white text. No fake scarcity, countdown, “limited slots” or aggressive urgency.

## Important restrictions

- Do not publish “Best”, “#1”, “100% cure”, “guaranteed”, “risk-free”, “completely painless”, “no complications” or “permanent cure”.
- Treat all cost, recovery, same-day, insurance, experience and procedure-time claims as unverified until signed off.
- Do not use identifiable patient stories or photos without documented consent.
- Use only the canonical phone/hours after client confirmation.
- For GMB designed thumbnails, keep text restrained; genuine GBP photo uploads may include the real exterior.
- Avoid old teal-heavy assets unless clearly labelled legacy.

## Preferred dimensions

- Instagram square / carousel / GMB designed thumbnail: `1080×1080`
- Instagram portrait: `1080×1350`
- Instagram story / reel cover: `1080×1920`
- Website banner: responsive source around `1920×800`
- GBP real-photo/post reference: favour `1200×900` (4:3), with minimal text overlay
