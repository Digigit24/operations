# Client Design Preferences and Revision Patterns

## Documented preferences / revision evidence

From the 25 May 2026 piles-vs-fissure revision cycle:

- remove doctor photos when requested;
- reduce logo prominence;
- personalise the final slide;
- correct qualification formatting to `FMAS`;
- remove the `10 years experience` line.

From later design guidance:

- prefer interior care/consultation scenes over a hospital exterior in designed GMB introduction thumbnails;
- keep logo top-left and readable;
- use the actual blue/green logo palette, not teal-heavy branding;
- keep posts clean, mobile-readable and non-graphic;
- avoid cheap flyer styling, fake awards and unsupported claims.

## Common production preferences inferred from repeated instructions

- one strong idea per creative;
- short headline and 3–5 points maximum;
- one CTA;
- readable phone/location footer;
- realistic Indian visual context;
- calm and non-fear-based medical framing.

## Status

These are documented project preferences, not a substitute for a signed creative brief. The only explicit revision evidence recovered relates to the piles-vs-fissure project; do not generalise every revision into an absolute rule without campaign context.
