# BUSINESS DETAILS
## Prime Speciality Hospital

---

## What the business is

A focused, doctor-led, minimally invasive surgical hospital — not a general multi-speciality hospital, not a clinic. Per the Master Brand Brain: *"It is a focused minimally invasive surgical center — narrow in scope, deep in expertise, regional in catchment."*

**Market role:** The bridge between two unsatisfactory extremes — expensive, impersonal corporate hospitals (Apollo, Jupiter, Sahyadri) and undertested, single-doctor neighbourhood clinics. Prime offers modern surgical technique + direct senior-surgeon access + hospital-grade safety + local accessibility + transparent pricing.

## What the business actually sells (emotionally)

"The return to ordinary life." A patient arrives scared with hernia pain; three days later they're home; by day thirty the episode is forgotten. The product being sold is the disappearance of the problem, not the procedure.
