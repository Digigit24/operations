## Offers / positioning around cost

- **Cost transparency is a core differentiator** — no local competitor publishes price ranges; Prime is positioned to own this.
- **Insurance:** Cashless insurance accepted; described as working with "most major insurance providers" and "20+ insurance networks" (the "20+" figure appears in `01_research.md` §Buyer Fear Map and should be verified before being quoted to patients, as it does not reappear with the same specificity elsewhere).
- **No discount/urgency offers** are used or recommended anywhere in the brand system — explicitly banned ("Flat 30% OFF" style language, countdown timers, fake scarcity are all on the Do-Not list).
