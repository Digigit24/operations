# PROJECT SUMMARY
## Prime Speciality Hospital — Full Engagement Narrative
*Compiled from complete project history through June 25, 2026*

---

## The client, in brief

Prime Speciality Hospital is a surgical hospital in Moshi, PCMC (Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation), Pune, led by **Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao** (MBBS, MS General Surgery, FMAS — Fellow of Minimal Access Surgeons of India), a minimally invasive/laparoscopic and GI surgeon with 10+ years of practice. The hospital treats laser piles, hernia, gallbladder stones, appendicitis, fissure/fistula, thyroid, breast conditions, and performs diagnostic endoscopy. The managing digital agency is **Digitech Solutions**.

The hospital's standout asset walking into this engagement was **108 Google reviews at 4.9★** — an exceptional trust signal with no close competitor in its geographic catchment (Moshi–Bhosari–Chakan) — sitting alongside a website that had not been meaningfully updated since January 2024 and a Google Business Profile that was completely inactive (zero posts in 90 days, 10% review reply rate, wrong business category).

## What onboarding found (Steps 01–07)

**Research (01):** Identified Prime's real emotional product as "the return to ordinary life," not surgery itself. Built out three ICPs (Ramesh — hernia, working-age man; Priya — piles, homemaker; Sunil — gallbladder, business owner), a full buyer fear/desire/objection map, 30 prioritised blog topics, and 6 named local/national competitors, with **Dr. Shrikant Kurhade / KK Care Hospital** identified as the single most dangerous direct competitor (same catchment, stronger on-paper credentials, but thin, keyword-stuffed content across four separate websites).

**Design (02) & Prompting (02b):** Locked in the Blue/Green/White palette matching the actual logo, Poppins/Inter typography, a full photography direction (editorial documentary, warm side-light, explicitly anti-stock/anti-cliché), and ready-to-use AI image prompt templates with negative prompts.

**Site Extraction (03):** A full Firecrawl-powered crawl of 13 live pages found the site averaging ~350 words per service page, a broken JavaScript stat counter showing "1+" for every statistic, several abandoned WordPress theme demo pages ("Dr. James B. Smith" etc.) still live, and the homepage title/meta both using the banned word "Best."

**SEO Foundation (04):** A live-SERP-verified keyword map across primary, geo, symptom, cost, and long-tail queries, ready-to-paste JSON-LD schema (LocalBusiness, FAQPage), a 30-post blog content calendar, and a 90-day action plan. Headline insight: "laser piles treatment Moshi" has zero dedicated competition and is expected to rank within 30–45 days of a single well-written page going live.

**GMB Foundation (05):** A live Windsor.ai data pull showing 3,929 impressions / 161 calls / 352 direction requests / only 35 website clicks over 90 days — i.e. the GMB profile is working extremely well as a discovery and navigation tool but the website is failing to convert that interest. Full rewrite of the GBP description, 12 ready-to-use GMB posts, 15 Q&A seed pairs, and review-response templates were produced.

**Brand Brain (06):** The master "operating system" — audience psychology (7 named fears), positioning statement, full voice system with banned-words list and hook structures, and a visual/AI-image-prompting DNA section. (Note: this document's colour/typography section conflicts with every other design document — see `OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_CONFLICTS.md`, Conflict 5.)

**Design System (07):** An interactive HTML reference consolidating type scale, spacing, colour tokens, icon set, and voice examples — confirmed to use the Blue/Green/Poppins/Inter system, not the Brand Brain's teal/serif system.

## What the June 2026 monthly cycle did (Steps M1–M6)

**Audit (M1):** Synthesised the GMB and site-extraction data into one action plan. Top insight: Prime is invisible for its own procedure keywords on GMB (all traffic is branded search) because of the wrong category and zero posting activity.

**Strategy (M2):** Set the month's primary objective — convert existing trust capital into 40–60 new WhatsApp/call inquiries within 60 days — via three levers: GMB category/posting fix, homepage + 2 service page rewrites, and review-reply catch-up. Paid ads and full redesign were explicitly scoped **out** of June.

**Website Rewrite Plan (M3):** Phased a full 16-page rewrite across three months, with complete blueprints (structure, word targets, meta, FAQ sets) written for the homepage, the laser piles page, and the Dr. Bhalerao page (Phase 1 — June).

**Task Creator (M4):** 24 tasks across setup, GMB, SEO, creative, and reporting, with a dependency map and Notion-ready task descriptions.

**Content Calendar (M5):** 22 fully-written content pieces for June — 4 GMB posts, 8 Instagram posts, 6 Facebook posts, 2 blog articles, 2 WhatsApp broadcasts — focused on laser piles as the month's lead procedure.

**Client Report (M6):** Reported three wins (GMB category changed, all 27 unanswered reviews replied to, laser piles page rewritten and live) and one open risk (GA4 install status unconfirmed). The report itself still has several KPI fields waiting on live data — see `OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_CONFLICTS.md`, Gap 2.
