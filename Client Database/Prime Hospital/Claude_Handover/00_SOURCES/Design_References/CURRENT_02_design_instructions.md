# DESIGN INSTRUCTIONS
## Prime Speciality Hospital — Onboarding Step 02 (Output 1 of 2)
*Generated: June 2026 | Based on research.md + live website analysis*

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Core Promise

**Brand:** Prime Speciality Hospital — Led by Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao
**Core promise:** Advanced minimally invasive surgical care. Personal attention. Local access. Transparent process.
**Design mission:** Make a patient who is scared about surgery feel: *"I am safe here. This is the right place."*

---

## 2. Brand Personality Traits

| Trait | Design Expression |
|---|---|
| **Trustworthy** | Clean layouts, real photography, no hype copy |
| **Specialist-led** | Dr. Bhalerao visible and prominent across all surfaces |
| **Calm and reassuring** | Generous whitespace, soft backgrounds, unhurried layouts |
| **Modern medical** | Contemporary typography, precise iconography, clinical precision |
| **Patient-friendly** | Readable type sizes, plain language, approachable visuals |
| **Practical, not overdramatic** | No fear imagery, no graphic medical visuals |
| **Local but premium** | PCMC references + editorial quality photography |
| **Procedure-confident** | Specific numbers, recovery timelines, cost ranges |

---

## 3. Target Audience & Emotional Tone

**Primary audience:** Middle-class and upper-middle-class PCMC families (Moshi, Bhosari, Chakan, Pimpri-Chinchwad) needing laparoscopic/GI surgery.

**The design must make visitors feel:**
- "I am safe here."
- "This doctor knows what he is doing."
- "I can afford this and I understand what I'm paying for."
- "I don't need to travel to central Pune."
- "This is not a scary place."

**Avoid making visitors feel:**
- Panic or fear (no graphic medical imagery)
- Shame (no stigmatising language about piles/fistula)
- Distrust (no countdown timers, no fake scarcity)
- Overwhelmed (no cluttered layouts)

## 5. Typography System

### Primary Fonts
- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold / Poppins SemiBold (Google Fonts)
- **Body:** Inter Regular / Inter Medium (Google Fonts)
- **Alternative if unavailable:** Montserrat Bold (headlines), Open Sans Regular (body)

### Size Scale

| Element | Desktop Size | Mobile Size | Weight | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Hero H1 | 52–64px | 32–40px | Bold 700 | #1C2430 or #0057D9 |
| Section H2 | 36–44px | 26–32px | SemiBold 600 | #1C2430 |
| Sub-section H3 | 24–30px | 20–24px | SemiBold 600 | #1C2430 |
| Card headline H4 | 18–22px | 16–20px | SemiBold 600 | #1C2430 |
| Body text | 16–18px | 15–16px | Regular 400 | #1C2430 |
| Secondary body | 14–16px | 13–15px | Regular 400 | #5E6B75 |
| Caption / label | 12–13px | 12px | Medium 500 | #5E6B75 |
| CTA button | 15–17px | 15px | SemiBold 600 | #FFFFFF |
| Footer text | 13–14px | 12–13px | Regular 400 | #FFFFFF |

### Typography Rules
**DO:**
- Keep headlines short and direct (6–10 words maximum)
- Use Prime Blue for primary headlines on white backgrounds
- Use green to highlight 1–2 key words in a headline
- Maintain 1.5–1.6 line-height for body text
- Use sentence case for most headlines (not ALL CAPS)

**DON'T:**
- Use script or decorative fonts
- Use thin weights (below 400) for medical or headline text
- Center-align body paragraphs
- Place long paragraphs inside social media thumbnails
- Use more than 2 font families on any surface

## 6. Layout Principles

### Social Media (1080×1080 — Square)
- Left-align logo, top area
- Strong blue or white headline, 56–72px
- 3–5 bullet points in rounded cards
- Deep blue footer strip with contact info
- 60–80px margin from all edges
- No more than 40% text coverage on photorealistic posts

### Social Media (1080×1350 — Portrait)
- Same rules as square with 40% more vertical space
- Use for testimonials, doctor profiles, procedure explainers

### Social Media (1080×1920 — Stories)
- Safe zone: Keep all content within 250px from top and bottom
- Large single headline, minimal copy
- WhatsApp CTA prominently placed

### GMB Thumbnails (1080×1080)
- Blue/white dominant; facility or doctor visual
- Max 5 key points as icon cards
- Prime Care Strip at bottom: Blue + Green icons + contact

### Website Sections
- Max content width: 1200px centered
- Section padding: 80–120px vertical
- Card spacing: 24–32px gap
- Hero: Full viewport height on desktop, 70vh mobile

### WhatsApp Creatives (1080×1080)
- Clean, readable at small size (WhatsApp thumbnail is tiny)
- Max 3–4 lines of copy
- Phone number + website in footer

## 7. Shape System

**Cards:** 12–16px border-radius; subtle box-shadow (0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08))
**Badges:** Pill shape (full border-radius); for labels like "Available 24/7"
**Icons:** Line-style, 2px stroke, monochromatic (blue or green, never mixed on same icon)
**Dividers:** 1px solid #E8ECF0 or a thin blue wave separator
**CTA buttons:** 8px border-radius; not fully round, not square — slightly rounded
**Check icons:** Green (#0BAA2B) circular badge with white checkmark

---

## 8. Visual Motifs (Recurring Elements)

1. **Prime Care Strip:** Deep blue bottom strip + green circular icons + white contact text (used in GMB, social, ads)
2. **Green Checkmark Badge:** Used for all benefit lists and feature points
3. **Blue Wave Divider:** Soft wave between sections to add depth without clutter
4. **Pill Labels:** "Available 24/7", "FMAS Certified", "Cashless Insurance" — used as trust badges
5. **Stat Cards:** White card + large blue number + small grey label (e.g., "108 Reviews | 4.9★")
6. **Doctor Credential Card:** Photo circle + name + degree string + specialization

## 9. Photography Direction

**Style:** Editorial documentary. Real hospital, real doctor, real patient interactions (with consent).

**Lighting:** Soft directional side-light from windows. Morning or late afternoon color temperature (3200–4500K). Warm undertones. Never fluorescent overhead.

**Subjects to shoot:**
- Dr. Bhalerao in consultation (explaining to patient — side angle, not frontal)
- Dr. Bhalerao reviewing case notes at desk (natural light, window behind)
- Hospital reception — clean, organized, welcoming
- Consultation room — desk, diplomas on wall, warm lighting
- OT/Operating Theatre — wide shot, no graphic medical content
- Patient recovery — calm, comfortable, not clinical-scary
- Patient with family at discharge — relief expression

**Subjects to AVOID:**
- Surgeon pointing at CT scan
- Smiling doctor holding clipboard
- Stethoscope on keyboard
- Hands cradling pills
- DNA helix over blue gradient
- Glass tower hospital exterior
- Doctors high-fiving in OT
- Stock photo Indian families

---

## 10. Visual Clichés to Avoid

- Any image with the word "BEST" or "#1" visible
- Gradient mesh backgrounds (startup aesthetic)
- Glassmorphism panels
- 3D blob shapes
- Dark mode design
- Bento grid primary layouts
- Auto-rotating carousels
- Cursor-trailing animations
- DNA helix
- "The caring touch" (doctor's hand on patient's hand close-up)

---

## 11. Low-Trust Design Patterns to Avoid

- Countdown timers of any kind
- "X people viewing this now" notifications
- Pop-ups within 5 seconds of page load
- "Flat 30% OFF on piles surgery this month!" language
- Star ratings without review source attribution
- Stock testimonial faces
- "Only 3 slots left this month" fake scarcity
- Auto-playing video with sound
- Fake "As seen on" media logos

---

## 12. Industry-Specific Compliance Notes

**Never claim:**
- "100% success rate"
- "Guaranteed cure"
- "No side effects"
- "Best hospital in India/Pune/PCMC"
- "Risk-free surgery"

**Safe alternatives:**
- "Experienced in 1,200+ laparoscopic procedures" (specific, verifiable)
- "Most patients return home within 24–48 hours" (outcome without guarantee)
- "All procedures follow AIIMS and standard surgical protocols"

**Disclaimer to include on service pages:** "Individual results may vary. Consult Dr. Bhalerao for a personalized treatment assessment."

- [ ] Brand colors used correctly (blue dominant, green accent, white background)
- [ ] Poppins/Inter fonts only
- [ ] No stock photo faces
- [ ] Logo placed correctly (top-left, clear space)
- [ ] Footer strip includes phone + website

**Text and layout:**
- [ ] Headline under 12 words
- [ ] No text overlapping on faces/hands/hair in photos
- [ ] Contact info verified correct
- [ ] CTA present and clear

**Medical/legal safety:**
- [ ] No "best", "guaranteed", "100%" claims
- [ ] Disclaimer present on procedure-specific creatives

**Brand consistency:**
- [ ] Would this look at home alongside the 108-review GMB profile?
- [ ] Would Dr. Bhalerao approve this message?
