- thyroid lump causes
- hernia in women symptoms
- swollen vein near anus

**Content format that wins:** Blog posts in plain language, 700–1,000 words, H2 headings as patient questions, no jargon. Hindi keyword variants capture additional volume.

**CTA approach:** Soft. "If you've had this for more than 2 weeks, a 10-minute consultation can tell you exactly where you stand." Link to WhatsApp.
