**Task: GMB — Change primary category from "Hospital" to "Surgical Centre"**

**Why this task matters:**
GMB primary category determines which keyword searches Prime appears in. "Hospital" makes Prime invisible for "hernia surgeon", "piles treatment", "laparoscopic surgery" — Prime's actual service keywords. Changing to "Surgical Centre" will surface procedure keyword impressions within 30–45 days. This is the highest-impact 30-minute action in the entire June plan.

**What to do:**
1. Log into Google Business Profile (business.google.com)
2. Click "Edit Profile" → Business Category
3. Change primary category from "Hospital" to "Surgical Centre"
4. Add secondary categories: General Surgeon, Proctologist, Gastroenterologist, Medical Clinic
5. Save — Google may prompt for re-verification. Monitor GBP daily for 3 days after.
6. Screenshot the change and save to Notion → Assets

**Definition of Done:**
GBP primary category shows "Surgical Centre". Secondary categories include General Surgeon + Proctologist. Screenshot saved to Notion.

**Dependencies:** None — can start immediately.

**Resources:** GMB Foundation §2 (category recommendations) | Strategy §3.1 (GMB strategy)
