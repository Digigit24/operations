13
Voice & copy examples
Calm. Confident. Educational. Direct. The cadence of a senior surgeon explaining to one patient. Specific over superlative. Numbers over adjectives. Lead with the patient's question, answer it directly, add context, close with WhatsApp.
Headline — procedure
Laser piles treatment. 30 minutes. Same day home. No cuts.
Body copy — procedure page opener
Laser treatment removes the cause. It doesn't manage the symptom — it resolves the problem. The procedure takes 30 minutes. Most patients return home the same afternoon and are back to normal activity within 3–5 days.
Trust statement
108 patients gave us 4.9 stars. We intend to keep that.
CTA copy
WhatsApp Dr. Bhalerao's team — we'll give you a clear picture of your options before you commit to anything.
Differentiator
The doctor you meet in consultation is the doctor who operates. No junior substitution. No bait-and-switch.
Never write like this
Banned — superlatives
"Our world-class, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art compassionate holistic surgical team is committed to transforming healthcare in PCMC."
Banned — fake urgency
"Only 3 consultation slots left this week! Book NOW before they're gone!"
Banned — guarantee
"100% painless. Guaranteed results. Risk-free surgery."
14
Anti-patterns — what to never use
These patterns undermine brand trust and are never permitted in any Prime asset.
✕Countdown timers ("Book in 00:45:12")
✓Persistent WhatsApp CTA — always available
✕Surgeon pointing at CT scan (hero image)
✓Doctor in consultation — side angle, warm light
✕Stethoscope on keyboard (tech-meets-medicine cliché)
✓Real hospital interior, reception, consultation room
✕DNA helix over blue gradient
✓Blue tint + white + green accents — brand palette only
✕Smiling doctor holding clipboard (stock pose)
✓Editorial documentary — candid, genuine interactions
✕Gradient mesh backgrounds (startup aesthetic)
✓Flat white backgrounds — clean, clinical, trustworthy
✕Glassmorphism or dark mode design
✓Light-dominant layouts — white + blue + green
✕"Flat 30% OFF on piles surgery this month!"
✓Transparent cost ranges — shared upfront, no discounts
✕Star ratings without source attribution
✓"108 Google reviews · 4.9★" — attributed, verifiable
✕Pop-ups within 5 seconds of page load
✓Sticky WhatsApp button — always accessible, never intrusive
✕"World-class, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art"
✓"1,200+ laparoscopic procedures. Dr. Bhalerao, FMAS."
The one QA test for every asset
"Would Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao say this out loud to a patient sitting in front of him?"
If not — rewrite it.
