## 1. Strategic Brief

**Client:** Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi, PCMC, Pune
**Period:** June 1 – June 30, 2026
**Prepared:** June 2026 | v1.0 — Month 1

**The Strategic Situation:**
Prime has an extraordinary GMB trust asset (108 reviews, 4.9★) that no PCMC competitor can quickly replicate, but is generating only 35 website clicks per month because the GMB category is wrong, the website is stale, and zero content or posts are reaching new patients. The infrastructure is there — the activation layer is missing.

**The Primary Objective:**
Fix the GMB and website foundation so that Prime's existing trust capital converts into 40–60 new patient WhatsApp/call inquiries from organic channels within 60 days.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. Achieve top 10 Google ranking for "laser piles treatment Moshi" (zero competition — achievable in 30 days with one rewritten page)
2. Grow GMB impressions by 30% through category change + 4 posts published this month
3. Respond to all 27 unanswered reviews and establish 90%+ review reply rate going forward

**What we are NOT doing this period:**
- Paid advertising (no GA4 tracking = can't measure — defer to Month 2)
- Full website redesign (rewrite Phase 1 pages only — 3 pages this month)
- New social media account creation (focus on GMB first — higher ROI)

**Strategic Bet:**
"Laser piles treatment Moshi" has zero dedicated competition in Google Search. One well-written 800-word page with FAQ schema, published this month, will rank in the top 5 within 30–45 days. This single page will generate 15–25 qualified inquiries per month once ranked.

## 3. Channel Strategy

### 3.1 GMB (Google Business Profile)

**Role:** Primary organic discovery channel. The #1 converting surface for Prime right now.

**Goal:** Grow monthly GMB impressions from 1,310 to 1,700+ by end of June through category change + post activation.

**Current state:** 3,929 impressions / 90 days. Wrong category. 0 posts. 10% review reply rate.

**Strategy:**
- Change category → Surgical Centre (30-min task, highest impact action)
- Add secondary categories: General Surgeon, Proctologist, Gastroenterologist
- Post 4 times this month (procedure-specific posts — piles, hernia, gallbladder, patient story)
- Respond to all 27 unanswered reviews this week
- Seed 15 Q&A pairs on profile

**Post plan for June:**
- Week 1: "Laser piles treatment at Prime — 30 minutes, same day, no cuts"
- Week 2: Educational — "Is laser piles treatment painful? What to expect"
- Week 3: Hernia — "Laparoscopic hernia repair — back to work in 5 days"
- Week 4: Trust — "108 patients. 4.9 stars. Here's what they say about Prime"

## 4. Cross-Channel Playbook

**Funnel architecture:**

```
AWARENESS
  ↓ GMB post (procedure-specific) reaches local searching audience
CONSIDERATION
  ↓ User finds Prime on Maps, sees 4.9★ + 108 reviews → clicks website
CONVERSION
  ↓ Homepage hero — WhatsApp CTA → Dr. Bhalerao team responds within hours
RETENTION
  ↓ Post-discharge WhatsApp → request review → grow review count
```

**Content repurposing system:**
1 GMB post → Instagram caption (when social activated) → WhatsApp broadcast → future blog topic

**Channel dependencies:**
- GA4 must be installed before any paid campaigns
- Homepage title/meta must be fixed before Search Console is useful
- Piles page rewrite must be live before it can rank
