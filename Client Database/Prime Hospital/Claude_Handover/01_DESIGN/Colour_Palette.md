## Usage ratio
**White 60% · Blue 25% · Green 10% · Others 5%**

## Rules
- Never combine Emergency Red with Prime Green (clashing)
- No dark-mode backgrounds — off-brand for this healthcare identity
- White is always the dominant background — never let blue or green dominate a full surface
- Green is reserved for positive/healing cues (checkmarks, success, recovery) — never used for warnings
- Red is reserved for genuine emergency messaging only, used sparingly
