## 4. Master Prompt Template

Copy and paste this template for any creative.

```text
Create a modern, premium healthcare social media creative for Prime Speciality Hospital.

Client/Brand: Prime Speciality Hospital
Website: [website]
Post format: [Instagram square post / carousel / story / ad / LinkedIn / website banner / WhatsApp creative]
Design style: [design style]
Topic: [topic]
Main hook: [headline]
Supporting line: [supporting line]
Key points:
- [point 1]
- [point 2]
- [point 3]
CTA: [CTA]
Contact details: [phone number / WhatsApp / address]
Website/URL: [website]
Audience: [target audience]
Visual subject: [visual subject]
Mood: [mood]
Offer/details: [offer or write “none”]
Trust proof: [doctor name / experience / reviews / facilities / location]
Compliance/safety note: [disclaimer or safety note]
Output size: [size]

Brand direction:
Use a clean modern surgical hospital look. Use a white and soft ice-blue background with deep medical blue and teal accents. The design must feel trustworthy, specialist-led, calm, and patient-friendly. Use bold readable typography, clean spacing, rounded medical cards, subtle shadows, and strong CTA placement.

Typography:
Use a modern sans-serif style similar to Poppins, Montserrat, or Inter. Make the headline bold and readable. Keep body text short.

Color palette:
Deep Trust Blue #063B73, Surgical Teal #00A7A5, Appointment Blue #0077C8, Medical White #FFFFFF, Ice Blue #EEF8FB, Charcoal Navy #172A3A, Slate Grey #5E6B75.

Layout:
Use a mobile-first layout with strong hierarchy. Keep logo space at the top. Use a clear headline zone, visual zone, key points zone, and CTA/footer zone. Keep enough white space.

Imagery:
Use realistic Indian medical visuals. Avoid graphic disease imagery, blood, wounds, open surgery visuals, shame-based visuals, or exaggerated pain expressions.

CTA:
Use a rounded blue or teal button with white text. CTA must be clearly visible.

Footer:
Add a neat footer/contact strip with hospital name, location, phone/WhatsApp, and website.

Compliance:
Do not promise guaranteed cure, permanent results, 100% painless treatment, or risk-free surgery. Use safe medical language. Add disclaimer where needed: “Treatment results may vary. Consult a qualified doctor.”

Create the design as a polished, modern, scroll-stopping healthcare creative. It should not look like an old hospital poster template.
```

## 5. Style-Specific Prompt Add-ons

### A. Photorealistic Add-on

```text
Use a high-quality photorealistic Indian healthcare scene. Show a confident Indian doctor or surgeon in a clean consultation room. Keep the patient expression calm and realistic. Use soft hospital lighting, clean background, and modern medical equipment. Add text in a clean overlay card, not directly on a busy photo.
```

### B. Light Background Add-on

```text
Use a white and soft ice-blue background. Keep the layout clean, airy, and premium. Use deep blue headlines, teal accents, rounded content cards, and minimal medical icons. This style should feel educational and easy to read.
```

### C. Dark Background Add-on

```text
Use a deep medical blue background with white typography and teal/amber accents. Keep the design premium and high-contrast. Use this only for strong hooks, warning signs, myth-busting, or carousel covers. Do not make the design look scary or black-heavy.
```

### D. Infographic Add-on

```text
Create a clear medical infographic using numbered cards, icons, arrows, timelines, or checklists. Keep each point short. Use deep blue and teal as the main colors. Make the information understandable at a glance. Avoid clutter and tiny text.
```

### E. Info + Photorealistic Add-on

```text
Split the layout between a realistic doctor/patient photo and a clean information section. The image should support the message, not dominate it. Use 3 key points maximum. Add a clear CTA button and footer strip.
```

### F. Text Heavy 50% Add-on

```text
Use approximately 50% text and 50% visual. Keep the headline large, add a short supporting line, 3 bullet points, and one CTA. Use a doctor or treatment-related visual on the other half.
```

### G. Text Heavy 75% Add-on

```text
Use approximately 75% text with small supporting icons or minimal illustration. Use clear hierarchy and spacing. Break information into cards. Avoid long paragraph blocks.
```

### H. Text Heavy 100% Add-on

```text
Use a typography-led layout with no main photo. Use clean cards, divider lines, icons, and strong headline hierarchy. Make the post look editorial and premium, not plain.
```

### I. Festival / Occasion Post Add-on

```text
Create a healthcare-friendly festival/occasion post. Keep Prime Speciality Hospital’s blue/teal/white identity visible. Add subtle festive elements only. Keep the message respectful, warm, and simple. Do not make the post look like a generic festival template.
```

### J. Offer / Ad Creative Add-on

```text
Create a conversion-focused ad creative. Use a symptom-led hook, short benefit points, doctor/hospital trust proof, and a clear appointment CTA. Keep the offer visible but do not make the design look cheap or discount-heavy. Include contact details clearly.
```

### K. Carousel Cover Add-on

```text
Create a strong carousel cover with one bold headline, one short supporting line, brand logo space, and a swipe cue. Use a clean medical visual or icon system. Make it scroll-stopping and readable in under 2 seconds.
```

### L. Educational Carousel Slide Add-on

```text
Create one educational carousel slide with one idea only. Add slide number, headline, short explanation, and one visual/icon. Keep the design consistent with the Prime Speciality Hospital brand system. No CTA except on the last slide.
```

## 7. Prompt Writing Rules

Follow these rules for every prompt:

1. Mention brand name clearly.
2. Mention exact post size.
3. Mention design style.
4. Keep headline short.
5. Define visual subject clearly.
6. Define color palette.
7. Specify logo placement.
8. Specify CTA placement.
9. Add footer/contact strip.
10. Add safety note for medical claims.
11. Tell the generator what to avoid.
12. Avoid vague words like “nice,” “good,” or “beautiful.”
13. Use layout instructions, not only mood words.
14. Keep one main message per creative.
15. Use real local relevance: Moshi, PCMC, Pune where useful.

---

## 8. Text-Length Rules for Image Generation

AI image tools often fail when given too much text. Use these limits.

### Instagram Square Post

- Headline: 5–9 words
- Supporting line: 8–14 words
- Bullet points: 3 points max, 3–5 words each
- CTA: 2–4 words
- Footer: short only

### Carousel Cover

- Eyebrow: 2–5 words
- Headline: 5–10 words
- Supporting line: 8–14 words
- Swipe cue: 3–6 words

### Educational Carousel Slide

- Headline: 4–8 words
- Body: 15–30 words max
- Points: 2–4 bullets max

### Story

- Headline: 4–8 words
- Body: 8–12 words
- CTA: 2–4 words

### Website Banner

- Headline: 5–9 words
- Supporting line: 12–22 words
- Key points: 3 short bullets

### WhatsApp Creative

- Headline: 4–7 words
- Body: 8–12 words
- CTA + phone must be large

## 9. Medical Compliance and Safety Rules

### Never Use

```text
100% cure
Permanent cure guaranteed
No risk
No complications
Completely painless
Guaranteed recovery
Best doctor in Pune
Best hospital without proof
Instant cure
Avoid surgery forever
```

### Safer Alternatives

```text
Advanced treatment options
Reduced discomfort
Faster recovery in suitable cases
Minimally invasive approach
Specialist consultation
Treatment depends on diagnosis
Consult a qualified doctor
Results may vary
```

### Required Disclaimer When Needed

```text
Treatment results may vary. Consult a qualified doctor.
```

or

```text
This content is for awareness only and does not replace medical consultation.
```

## 11. Final QA Checklist

Before sending a prompt or final creative, check:

- Is the brand name included?
- Is the output size included?
- Is the topic clear?
- Is the headline short enough?
- Is the visual subject clear?
- Is the design style selected?
- Are brand colors mentioned?
- Is the CTA included?
- Are contact details included?
- Is the medical claim safe?
- Is there a disclaimer if needed?
- Is the creative mobile-readable?
- Does it avoid graphic visuals?
- Does it look modern and premium?
- Does it feel like Prime Speciality Hospital, not a generic healthcare template?
