# LOGO USAGE GUIDE
## Prime Speciality Hospital

---

## The logo

Two logo files are on hand:
- `primelogoforwebsite.png` — primary logo lockup: green cross/"P" mark + "Prime Speciality Hospital" in blue wordmark + green tagline ("GI Surgery | Laparoscopy | Endoscopy | Piles | Hernia | Thyroid")
- `Logo_and_Footer.png` — the same logo shown above the "Prime Care Strip" footer treatment (deep blue bar, white contact text: phone numbers, website, address)

Both files live in `01_DESIGN/Approved_Design_References/` and `02_ASSETS/01_Logos/` in this handover.

The logo is the **ground truth** for brand colour: blue wordmark + green mark confirms the Prime Blue `#0057D9` / Prime Green `#0BAA2B` palette used throughout the current design system.

## Placement

| Context | Placement |
|---|---|
| Educational posts, carousels | Top-left |
| Ads, offer creatives | Top-right |
| Formal announcement posts | Centre-top |
| Text-heavy creatives | Footer strip |
| GMB thumbnails | Top-left |

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "P" mark on all sides. Never crowd the logo with other elements.

## Sizing

| Format | Logo width |
|---|---|
| 1080×1080 | 130–190px |
| 1080×1350 | 150–220px |
| Website/GMB thumbnails | Visible but not oversized — a trust marker, not the headline |

## Backgrounds

**Use the logo on:** White · soft grey · soft blue tint · soft green tint
**Avoid:** Placing the logo over busy patient photos · adding a drop shadow unless needed for contrast · stretching, recolouring, or otherwise altering the logo · sizing it up like a watermark

## The "Prime Care Strip" footer (signature motif)

A deep blue bar beneath the logo, with green circular icons and white contact text — phone number(s), website, and/or address. This is the standard footer treatment across GMB thumbnails, ads, and social posts. See `Logo_and_Footer.png` for the exact reference execution.
