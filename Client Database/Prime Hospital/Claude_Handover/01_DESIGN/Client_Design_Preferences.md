# CLIENT DESIGN PREFERENCES
## Prime Speciality Hospital

*No formal client feedback log, revision request tracker, or approval correspondence was found anywhere in the project. Everything below is inferred from what the agency has produced and repeatedly reinforced across documents (an implicit preference, not a confirmed client instruction), plus the explicit rules the brand documents state. Treat inferred items as a starting point, not a substitute for asking the client directly.*

---

## Preferences confirmed by repeated documentation (high confidence — these are stated directly in the brand docs, not just inferred)

- **Doctor-forward:** Dr. Bhalerao's name, face, and credentials should appear prominently and consistently — this is treated as a non-negotiable trust anchor across every document (research, brand brain, website plan, GMB posts).
- **WhatsApp-first:** Every document, without exception, treats WhatsApp as the primary conversion channel, ahead of phone calls and far ahead of web forms. 90.3% of GMB impressions are mobile — this reinforces a mobile/WhatsApp-first design requirement.
- **Cost transparency:** Every service-page blueprint and several GMB posts explicitly include a cost range. This is treated as a differentiator against competitors who hide pricing, not an optional nice-to-have.
- **No superlatives, ever:** The banned-words list appears verbatim, or near-verbatim, in every single content and design document in the project (research, design instructions, prompting guide, brand brain, website plan, content calendar). This is the most consistently enforced rule in the entire account.
- **Restraint over density:** Multiple documents explicitly call for showing "roughly 30% of what most hospital websites currently display" (Brand Brain) and generous whitespace throughout.

## Preferences inferred from what was actually approved into production (medium confidence)

- Editorial/documentary photography over stock imagery was chosen and reinforced across three separate document generations (design instructions, prompting guide, brand brain) — this looks like a firm creative direction rather than a one-off suggestion.
- The "Prime Care Strip" footer motif (deep blue bar, green icons, white contact text) was carried from the design instructions all the way through to the actual `Logo_and_Footer.png` reference file — this appears to be a settled, approved element.
- Blue/Green over teal — see `OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_CONFLICTS.md` Conflict 5. The fact that every actual deliverable (logo, design system HTML, published GMB posts) uses Blue/Green suggests this was the client's implicit preference once the real logo was seen, even though it isn't stated as an explicit written decision anywhere.
