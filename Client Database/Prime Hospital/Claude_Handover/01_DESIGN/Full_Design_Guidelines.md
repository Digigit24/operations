## Prime Speciality Hospital
*Consolidated from `02_design_instructions.md` (current) + `07_design_system.html` (current, interactive) + cross-checked against the actual logo. Where the Master Brand Brain's visual section conflicts, this document follows the system actually in use — see the note at the end.*

## 1. Brand Overview & Core Promise

**Brand:** Prime Speciality Hospital — led by Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao
**Core promise:** Advanced minimally invasive surgical care. Personal attention. Local access. Transparent process.
**Design mission:** Make a patient who is scared about surgery feel: *"I am safe here. This is the right place."*

## 2. Brand Personality → Design Expression

| Trait | Design Expression |
|---|---|
| Trustworthy | Clean layouts, real photography, no hype copy |
| Specialist-led | Dr. Bhalerao visible and prominent across all surfaces |
| Calm and reassuring | Generous whitespace, soft backgrounds, unhurried layouts |
| Modern medical | Contemporary typography, precise iconography, clinical precision |
| Patient-friendly | Readable type sizes, plain language, approachable visuals |
| Practical, not overdramatic | No fear imagery, no graphic medical visuals |
| Local but premium | PCMC references + editorial quality photography |
| Procedure-confident | Specific numbers, recovery timelines, cost ranges |

## 3. Target Audience & Emotional Tone

**Primary audience:** Middle-class and upper-middle-class PCMC families (Moshi, Bhosari, Chakan, Pimpri-Chinchwad) needing laparoscopic/GI surgery.

**Design must make visitors feel:** "I am safe here." / "This doctor knows what he's doing." / "I can afford this and understand what I'm paying for." / "I don't need to travel to central Pune." / "This is not a scary place."

**Design must avoid:** Panic or fear (no graphic medical imagery), shame (no stigmatising language about piles/fistula), distrust (no countdown timers, no fake scarcity), overwhelm (no cluttered layouts).

## 5. Typography System

- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold / Poppins SemiBold (fallback: Montserrat Bold)
- **Body:** Inter Regular / Inter Medium (fallback: Open Sans Regular)

| Element | Desktop | Mobile | Weight | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Hero H1 | 52–64px | 32–40px | Bold 700 | `#1C2430` or `#0057D9` |
| Section H2 | 36–44px | 26–32px | SemiBold 600 | `#1C2430` |
| Sub-section H3 | 24–30px | 20–24px | SemiBold 600 | `#1C2430` |
| Card headline H4 | 18–22px | 16–20px | SemiBold 600 | `#1C2430` |
| Body text | 16–18px | 15–16px | Regular 400 | `#1C2430` |
| Secondary body | 14–16px | 13–15px | Regular 400 | `#5E6B75` |
| Caption / label | 12–13px | 12px | Medium 500 | `#5E6B75` |
| CTA button | 15–17px | 15px | SemiBold 600 | `#FFFFFF` |

**Do:** Keep headlines to 6–10 words max. Use blue for primary headlines on white. Use green to highlight 1–2 key words. Line-height 1.5–1.6 for body. Sentence case, not ALL CAPS.
**Don't:** Script/decorative fonts. Weights below 400 for medical/headline text. Centre-aligned body paragraphs. Long paragraphs inside social thumbnails. More than 2 font families on any surface.

## 6. Layout Principles by Format

- **Social square (1080×1080):** Logo top-left, blue/white headline 56–72px, 3–5 bullet cards, deep-blue footer strip, 60–80px margins, ≤40% text coverage on photo posts.
- **Social portrait (1080×1350):** Same as square, +40% vertical space — testimonials, doctor profiles, procedure explainers.
- **Stories (1080×1920):** Keep all content within 250px of top/bottom. Single large headline, minimal copy, prominent WhatsApp CTA.
- **GMB thumbnails (1080×1080):** Blue/white dominant, facility or doctor visual, max 5 points as icon cards, Prime Care Strip footer.
- **Website sections:** Max content width 1200px centred, 80–120px vertical section padding, 24–32px card gaps, hero full-viewport desktop / 70vh mobile.
- **WhatsApp creatives (1080×1080):** Max 3–4 lines of copy, phone + website in footer, must read clearly at thumbnail size.

## 7. Shape System

- **Cards:** 12–16px radius, subtle shadow `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`
- **Badges:** Pill shape (full radius) — e.g. "Available 24/7," "FMAS Certified"
- **Icons:** Line-style, 2px stroke, monochromatic (blue or green — never mixed on one icon)
- **Dividers:** 1px solid `#E8ECF0` or a thin blue wave separator
- **CTA buttons:** 8px radius — slightly rounded, not fully pill, not square
- **Check icons:** Green circular badge with white checkmark

## 8. Recurring Visual Motifs

1. **Prime Care Strip** — deep blue bottom bar + green circular icons + white contact text (used across GMB, social, ads)
2. **Green Checkmark Badge** — all benefit/feature lists
3. **Blue Wave Divider** — soft wave between sections
4. **Pill Labels** — "Available 24/7," "FMAS Certified," "Cashless Insurance"
5. **Stat Cards** — white card + large blue number + small grey label (e.g. "108 Reviews | 4.9★")
6. **Doctor Credential Card** — photo circle + name + degree string + specialisation

## 9. Photography Direction

**Style:** Editorial documentary. Real hospital, real doctor, real (consenting) patient interactions.
**Lighting:** Soft directional side-light from windows. Morning/late-afternoon colour temperature (3200–4500K). Warm undertones. Never fluorescent overhead.

**Shoot list:** Dr. Bhalerao in consultation (side angle, not frontal) · reviewing notes at his desk (window light) · hospital reception (clean, welcoming) · consultation room (desk, diplomas, warm light) · OT wide shot (non-graphic) · patient recovery (calm, not clinical-scary) · patient + family at discharge (relief expression).

**Avoid:** Surgeon pointing at a CT scan · smiling-doctor-with-clipboard · stethoscope on keyboard · hands cradling pills · DNA helix over blue gradient · glass-tower hospital exterior · doctors high-fiving in OT · generic stock-photo Indian families.

## 10. Visual Clichés to Avoid

Any image with "BEST"/"#1" visible · gradient mesh backgrounds · glassmorphism panels · 3D blob shapes · dark mode · bento-grid primary layouts · auto-rotating carousels · cursor-trailing animation · DNA helix · the "caring touch" hand-on-hand close-up.

## 11. Low-Trust Patterns to Avoid

Countdown timers · "X people viewing this now" · pop-ups within 5 seconds of load · "Flat 30% OFF!" language · star ratings without source attribution · stock testimonial faces · fake scarcity ("only 3 slots left") · auto-playing video with sound · fake "as seen on" media logos.

## 12. Compliance Notes

**Never claim:** "100% success rate" · "guaranteed cure" · "no side effects" · "best hospital in India/Pune/PCMC" · "risk-free surgery"
**Safe alternatives:** "Experienced in 1,200+ laparoscopic procedures" · "Most patients return home within 24–48 hours" · "All procedures follow standard surgical protocols"
**Disclaimer for service pages/procedure creatives:** *"Individual results may vary. Consult Dr. Bhalerao for a personalised treatment assessment."*

## 13. Design Quality Checklist

**Visual:** Brand colours correct (blue dominant, green accent, white background) · Poppins/Inter only · no stock-photo faces · logo correctly placed with clear space · footer includes phone + website
**Text/layout:** Headline under 12 words · no text over faces/hands/hair · contact info verified · clear CTA present
**Medical/legal:** No "best"/"guaranteed"/"100%" claims · disclaimer present on procedure-specific creatives
**Brand consistency:** Would this look at home next to the 108-review GMB profile? Would Dr. Bhalerao approve this message?
