## Rules

**Do:**
- Keep headlines to 6–10 words maximum
- Use Prime Blue for primary headlines on white backgrounds
- Use Prime Green to highlight 1–2 key words within a headline
- Maintain 1.5–1.6 line-height for body text
- Use sentence case for most headlines — not ALL CAPS

**Don't:**
- Use script or decorative fonts
- Use weights below 400 for medical or headline text
- Centre-align body paragraphs
- Place long paragraphs inside social media thumbnails
- Use more than 2 font families on any single surface

---

## ⚠️ Conflicting typography direction on file

`06_brand_brain.md` recommends a **serif** headline font (Fraunces, Source Serif 4, or PP Editorial New) paired with a geometric sans body (Inter, Söhne, Geist, Manrope) — a materially different system from Poppins/Inter above. This has not been implemented anywhere. Default to Poppins/Inter unless the client/PM confirms a rebrand toward the serif direction. See `OPEN_QUESTIONS_AND_CONFLICTS.md` Conflict 5.
