## 1. Universal negative prompt — append to every generation

```
no stock photos, no smiling doctor with clipboard, no surgeon pointing at CT scan, no
stethoscope on keyboard, no DNA helix, no blue gradient background, no glassmorphism, no
dark mode, no hospital exterior, no hands cradling pills, no graphic medical imagery, no
blood, no wounds, no frightening clinical scenes, no fluorescent overhead lighting, no
countdown timer graphics, no generic corporate look, no Indian stock photo poses, no white
coat stethoscope portrait
```

## 2. Universal style suffix — append to every generation

```
editorial documentary photography style, warm soft directional window light, mid-format
camera feel, slight grain, natural warm color temperature 3800K, generous whitespace, clean
composition, Prime Blue #0057D9 and Prime Green #0BAA2B color accent, medical trust
aesthetic, Indian healthcare setting, modern and approachable, not corporate, not luxury,
not alarming
```

## 3. Brand colour language (use descriptions, not just hex codes)

- **Prime Blue** → "deep trustworthy medical blue, saturated, not navy, not sky blue — like a premium hospital brand"
- **Prime Green** → "fresh healing green, mid-saturation, not lime, not dark forest — like a positive medical indicator"
- **Medical White** → "clean clinical white background, slightly warm, not stark pure white"
- **Charcoal text** → "very dark charcoal near-black for typography elements"

## 5. Visual subject options

1. Indian male doctor (50s, professional, calm expression) consulting patient in clean modern hospital room, side angle — NOT frontal
2. Indian female doctor or nurse with patient in warm consultation setting
3. Clean modern hospital reception area, warm lighting, organised, welcoming
4. Hospital corridor, empty, clean, soft depth of field editorial

## 12. Pre-publish QA checklist

**Brand:** Prime Blue + Green used correctly · logo top-left with clear space · footer includes phone + website · no banned words (best, guaranteed, #1, world-class)
**Design quality:** Text readable at thumbnail size · no text over faces/hair/hands · premium not flyer-like · contact info correct
**Medical/legal:** No outcome guarantees · no "best hospital"/"100%" claims · disclaimer present if needed
**Brand voice:** Would Dr. Bhalerao say this to a patient? Specific over superlative? Numbers rather than adjectives?
