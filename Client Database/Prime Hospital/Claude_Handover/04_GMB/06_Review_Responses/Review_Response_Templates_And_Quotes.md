## Response templates by review type

**Positive Review — 5 Stars, Detailed (personalised):**
```
Thank you [Name] for taking the time to share your experience. We're genuinely
glad your [procedure/condition] went smoothly and that the recovery has been
comfortable. This is exactly what we aim for — clarity before the procedure and
a smooth return to normal life after.

If you ever have post-operative questions, feel free to WhatsApp us anytime.
And if friends or family in PCMC need surgical care, we'd be happy to help them too.

— Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao & Team, Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi
```

**Positive Review — 5 Stars, No Text:**
```
Thank you [Name] — we appreciate you taking a moment to share your rating.
We hope your recovery has been smooth. If you have any follow-up questions,
we're always reachable at +91 94226 68921.

— Team Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi
```

**Neutral Review — 3–4 Stars with Feedback:**
```
Thank you [Name] for the honest feedback. We take this seriously and would
genuinely like to understand what we could have done better.

Please WhatsApp us directly at +91 94226 68921 — Dr. Bhalerao would personally
like to address your concern.

— Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao, Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi
```

**Negative Review — Genuine Complaint:**
```
[Name], thank you for taking the time to share this. We're sorry your experience
didn't meet expectations, and we'd like to understand and resolve this properly.

Please contact us directly at +91 94226 68921 — we would appreciate the chance
to speak with you.

— Dr. Pankaj Bhalerao, Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi
```
*Medical disclaimer: never discuss treatment details, diagnoses, or clinical specifics in public review responses.*

**Fake / Competitor Review:**
```
Thank you for your message. We have no record of a visit under this name in
our patient records. We'd warmly invite you to contact us directly at
+91 94226 68921 so we can understand your concern.

— Prime Speciality Hospital, Moshi
```
*(Flag for Google removal via GBP → Reviews → Flag as inappropriate)*

**Rule:** Respond to every review within 24 hours. Always mention the procedure/experience. Never reuse an identical template twice — the previous automated boilerplate ("Thank you so much for sharing your experience with us. We're always here to give you special care and smooth recovery...") was flagged as a problem precisely because it read as robotic and impersonal.
