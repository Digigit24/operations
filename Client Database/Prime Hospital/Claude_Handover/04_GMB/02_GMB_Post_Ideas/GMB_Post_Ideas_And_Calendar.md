## Post types
1. **Service Post** — spotlight a specific procedure (1–2/month)
2. **Educational / FAQ Post** — answer a common patient question (1–2/month, no CTA needed)
3. **Update / News Post** — new equipment, facility update, staff addition (as needed)
4. **Event Post** — health camp, awareness day, free consultation day (per calendar — note these expire)
