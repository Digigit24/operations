### Post 6 — Educational: Hernia FAQ
```
Headline: Can a Hernia Heal on Its Own? The Truth

Short answer: No. A hernia does not heal without surgery.

The longer answer: Some hernias are small and grow slowly. Some patients manage
symptoms for years. But the hernia does not close or reduce without surgical repair.

The risk of waiting: a hernia can become "strangulated" — where the trapped tissue
loses blood supply. This is a surgical emergency.

The good news: laparoscopic hernia repair is a day-care procedure. Most patients
go home the next day and are back to desk work in a week.

If you've been told you have a hernia and are wondering whether to treat it now or
```
