## Baseline profile audit (90 days: Feb 19 – May 18, 2026)

**Overall profile strength score: 48/100**

| Metric | 90-Day Total | Monthly Average |
|---|---|---|
| Total impressions | 3,929 | ~1,310 |
| Call clicks | 161 | ~54 (4.1% of impressions) |
| Direction requests | 352 | ~117 (8.96% of impressions — excellent) |
| Website clicks | 35 | ~12 (0.89% of impressions — very low) |
| GMB posts published | 0 | 0 |
| Review reply rate | ~10% (3 of 31 recent reviews) | — |

**Mobile share:** 90.3% of impressions — confirms the WhatsApp-first, mobile-first strategy is the correct one.

**Biggest finding:** GMB is functioning excellently as a discovery and navigation tool (very high direction-request rate) but is failing almost completely to convert that interest into website visits. The gap between direction requests (8.96%) and website clicks (0.89%) is described as "the biggest funnel leak" in the account.

**Top search keywords (90 days):** All branded ("bhalerao hospital" 52/month, "prime speciality hospital moshi" 26/month) — **zero** appearances for "hernia surgeon," "piles treatment," or "laparoscopic surgery," caused by the wrong GMB category and zero posting activity.
