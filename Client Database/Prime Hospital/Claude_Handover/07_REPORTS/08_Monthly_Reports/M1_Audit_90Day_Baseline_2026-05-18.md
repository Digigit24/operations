## §6 CROSS-CHANNEL INSIGHTS

**1. The GMB-to-Website gap is the biggest conversion leak.**
GMB generates 352 direction requests and 161 calls/month from 3,929 impressions. Website gets only 35 clicks. The GMB profile is doing its job — sending people to Prime's door or phone. The website is not converting the small number of people who do click. The homepage headline uses "Best" (low trust) and has no immediate procedure-specific content. Fix: homepage rewrite + WhatsApp sticky CTA.

**2. Prime is invisible for its own procedure keywords on GMB.**
Top GMB keywords are all branded ("bhalerao hospital", "prime speciality hospital"). Zero procedure queries (piles, hernia, gallbladder) are appearing. This is caused by the wrong GMB category ("Hospital" instead of "Surgical Centre") and zero GMB posts. Changing category + posting 4 times/month will surface procedure keyword impressions within 30–45 days.

**3. Tracking is completely blind.**
Without GA4, there is no visibility into which pages convert, where visitors drop off, or whether WhatsApp clicks are happening at all. Every channel decision is currently made without data. GA4 installation is the prerequisite to all paid campaign work.

**4. 27 unanswered reviews are suppressing trust and GMB ranking.**
Google uses review reply engagement as a local ranking signal. 10% reply rate vs 90%+ industry expectation is actively hurting map pack position. 2–3 hours of work to respond to all 27 unanswered reviews.

**5. The 4.9★ / 108 review asset is massively underutilised.**
No competitor in the PCMC zone is close to 108 reviews at 4.9★. This asset isn't mentioned in GMB posts (none exist), isn't featured prominently in the website hero, and isn't used in any known social content. Surface this asset everywhere.
