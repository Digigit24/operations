## Recommended fix (implied by the source material, not yet actioned)

Every planning document treats WhatsApp as the primary conversion channel, yet no document proposes a system for actually counting WhatsApp inquiries beyond asking the doctor's team to report a number at month-end. A basic tracking method (e.g. a shared log, a WhatsApp Business API integration, or a simple daily count from reception) would close one of the most consistently repeated data gaps in the entire account.
