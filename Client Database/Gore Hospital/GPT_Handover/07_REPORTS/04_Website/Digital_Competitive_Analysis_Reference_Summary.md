# Digital Competitive Analysis — Reference Summary

**Original reference:** Gore_Hospital_Digital_Competitive_Analysis.docx, prepared 16 July 2026 in File Library; exact bytes were not mounted.

## Scope

Primary catchment: Ravet, Mamurdi, Kiwale, Punawale, Dehu Road and adjoining PCMC. Closest monitoring set identified Global Hospital, Flora Hospital, Lifeline Multispeciality and Varad Hospital, with Lifepoint as a larger regional benchmark.

## Strategic conclusions

- Public monthly traffic estimates for small local hospitals were not dependable; use first-party conversions.
- Gore’s local relevance is strong but service/locality content depth trails leading peers.
- Website priorities: emergency/ICU, orthopaedics, women’s health, paediatrics, surgery/insurance and locality pages.
- Instagram should shift from volume to doctor-led local video, clear identity and measurable appointment actions.
- Four content territories: emergency & trauma, orthopaedics, women’s health, family & critical care.
- Primary success measure: attributable appointment calls, WhatsApp conversations, directions and completed bookings — not follower count alone.
