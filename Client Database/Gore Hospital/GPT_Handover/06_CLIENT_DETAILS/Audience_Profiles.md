# Audience Profiles

These are strategic personas, not patient records.

## 1. Anita — young Ravet mother

- **Label:** INFERRED strategic ICP.
- 28–38, family healthcare decision-maker, time-poor and digitally active.
- Needs calm paediatric guidance, nearby evening access, hygiene proof and predictable billing.
- Best channels: Google Maps, WhatsApp groups, Instagram and parent recommendations.
- Best content: child-fever guidance, vaccination, paediatrician introduction, maternity/women’s health.

## 2. Suresh — middle-class family decision-maker

- **Label:** INFERRED strategic ICP.
- 38–55, approves major family healthcare spending and cares for parents/children.
- Core fear: unnecessary tests/admission and an expanding bill.
- Needs clear explanation, transparent estimates, Marathi/Hindi comfort and named senior doctors.

## 3. Marathi-speaking elder/caretaker

- **Label:** INFERRED strategic ICP.
- 55–75, values dignity, familiar language and unhurried explanation.
- Best content: Marathi doctor explainers, senior care, directions, hospital process and real staff.
- Do not claim Marathi availability until verified by department.

## 4. Rohit — IT professional with insurance

- **Label:** INFERRED strategic ICP.
- 28–42, compares hospitals digitally and values online booking, modern presentation, cashless clarity and efficiency.
- Needs verified TPA list, digital reports, named specialist pages and reliable conversion paths.

## 5. Kavita and Ramesh — senior couple

- **Label:** INFERRED strategic ICP.
- 60–80, recurring chronic-care and preventive needs.
- Values continuity, a familiar doctor, one local hospital and simple monthly/quarterly routines.

## Shared decision drivers

Distance, recent reviews, named doctors, clear communication, fair billing, insurance clarity, real facility proof and 24x7 confidence. Research indicates patients want to feel heard and not exploited more than they want generic “world-class” language.
