# Brand Positioning

## Current strategic direction

**Primary position — DRAFT:** “Your family’s hospital in Mamurdi — honest care, full-spectrum specialists, founded by a doctor who lives where you live.”

**Core promise — DRAFT:** “Your family’s hospital. Honest care. Close to home.”

**Essence — DRAFT:** Trusted · Warm · Clear.

## What the brand should become known for

A local, founder-visible, multi-generational family hospital where doctors explain clearly, refer appropriately, treat patients with dignity and make costs easier to understand.

## Competitive lane

- Compete on local trust, clear explanation, founder/team visibility, fair process, family continuity and hyperlocal access.
- Do not compete on luxury, lowest price, robotic-surgery theatre, transplant-level tertiary breadth or unsupported “best” claims.
- Larger corporate hospitals may be appropriate escalation destinations; honest referral can strengthen trust.

## Messaging rules

Use: family, honest, listen, explain, close to home, named doctor, clear next step, Mamurdi, Ravet, PCMC.  
Avoid: miracle, cure, guaranteed, world-class, revolutionary, painless, permanent, risk-free, 100%, best in Pune/India, instant results and transformation language in clinical contexts.

## Positioning risk

“Honest billing”, “no padded admissions”, “no surprise bills” and similar statements are strong but operationally binding. Treat them as proposed policy language until management defines and signs the actual process and exceptions.
