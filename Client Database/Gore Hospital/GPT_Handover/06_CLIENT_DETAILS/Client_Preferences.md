# Client and Project Preferences

## Current design preferences inferred from approved working documents

- Build from the colourful official GMH logo rather than generic medical-blue templates.
- Use warm maroon, orange, green, sky blue and restrained gold on cream.
- Editorial, asymmetric, layered and spacious rather than crowded/centred corporate layouts.
- Photorealistic Indian healthcare context; real doctors/families/facility preferred.
- Founder visibility is strategically important.
- Marathi alongside English where audience-appropriate.
- Logo should not sit over busy photography; peacock-feather motif must remain subtle.
- One clear H1 and one CTA; mobile readability and medical compliance are mandatory.

## Historical revision preferences observed

- Simplify crowded layouts and address boxes.
- Make key headline hierarchy stronger.
- Remove irrelevant festive/cultural elements from non-festival health posts.
- Some requests removed doctor photos or changed reference likeness; these are historical decisions, not a universal rule.

## Current restrictions

- Do not use circular gold legacy logos unless the client formally reinstates one.
- Do not use old phone numbers or gorehospital.in.
- Do not use Western-default stock faces, cartoon mascots, 3D medical characters, fear imagery, gore, neon/SaaS effects or generic stock icon packs.
- Do not publish AI output without designer and clinical/compliance QA.
- Do not infer approval from “final”, “latest” or file existence.
