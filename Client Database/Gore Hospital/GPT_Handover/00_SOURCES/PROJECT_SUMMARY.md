# Project Summary

Gore Multispeciality Hospital is a community multispeciality hospital serving Mamurdi–Ravet and the western PCMC corridor. The project progressed from ad-hoc creative production into a structured strategy, design system, prompt system and SEO/GBP foundation.

## Strongest completed foundations
- Comprehensive strategy, audience and competitor research.
- Logo-derived warm visual system and AI creative governance.
- Data-backed SEO baseline for the 90 days ending 24 May 2026.
- GBP-safe content rules and a detailed GBP operating draft.
- Large historical creative inventory and reusable content ideas.

## Main operational gaps
- No signed client fact sheet or approval ledger.
- Conflicting logo, phone, domain, review-count and palette versions.
- No reliable approved/live asset register, source files or consent files.
- No complete video/reel scripts found.
- GBP performance access blocked; analytics conversion tracking absent.
- Medical statistics and several service/facility claims need doctor/client verification.
