## Draft copy

Monsoon months often bring more fever, stomach infections, mosquito-borne illness and breathing complaints. Reduce standing water, use mosquito protection, drink safe water, wash hands regularly and avoid self-medicating persistent fever.

Seek medical advice for warning signs such as breathing difficulty, repeated vomiting, dehydration, bleeding, severe weakness, confusion, persistent high fever or worsening symptoms. This information is general and should be clinically reviewed before posting.

Consult a qualified doctor for advice based on your condition.
