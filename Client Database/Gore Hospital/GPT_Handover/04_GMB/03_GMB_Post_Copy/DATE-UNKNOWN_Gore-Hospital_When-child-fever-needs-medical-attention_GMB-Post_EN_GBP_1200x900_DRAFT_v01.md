## Draft copy

A child’s fever can be worrying, especially at night. Parents should seek prompt medical advice when a baby is very young, the child is unusually drowsy, has trouble breathing, cannot keep fluids down, shows signs of dehydration, has a seizure, or simply seems much more unwell than expected.

This is general information, not a diagnosis. Gore Multispeciality Hospital serves families in Mamurdi–Ravet; confirm the current paediatric and emergency availability before publishing this update.

Contact the hospital team for guidance based on your child’s condition.
