## Draft copy

Knee pain deserves medical review when it follows an injury, causes swelling or locking, makes weight-bearing difficult, keeps returning, or interferes with sleep and daily movement. Not every case needs the same treatment; an examination helps identify the cause and appropriate options.

Gore Multispeciality Hospital’s current roster lists an orthopaedic and trauma consultant, but the doctor’s current schedule and services must be confirmed before publication.

Schedule an orthopaedic consultation through the hospital team.
