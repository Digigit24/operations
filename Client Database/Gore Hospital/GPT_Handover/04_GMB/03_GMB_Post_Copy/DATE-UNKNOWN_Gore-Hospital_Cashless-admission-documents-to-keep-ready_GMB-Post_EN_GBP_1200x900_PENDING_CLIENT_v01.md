## Draft copy

Planning a cashless hospital admission? Families are commonly asked for a valid photo ID, insurance card or e-card, policy details, doctor’s advice and any previous reports. The exact pre-authorisation process and documents vary by insurer and treatment.

Before Gore Hospital publishes this guide, the current TPA partners, insurance-desk contact, exclusions and process must be confirmed in writing. Do not use insurer logos or say “all insurance accepted” without proof.

Contact the confirmed insurance desk for case-specific guidance.
