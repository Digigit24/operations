# Review Generation Workflow

**Status:** DRAFT / privacy and operations approval required.

1. Ask for a review only after a genuine patient interaction and never in exchange for a discount, gift or preferential treatment.
2. Use a neutral QR card at reception and an opt-in WhatsApp follow-up approximately 24 hours after the visit, subject to consent and messaging rules.
3. Ask for an honest review, not a five-star review.
4. Do not ask patients to disclose diagnosis, procedure or personal health information.
5. Route complaints to a private service-recovery process rather than suppressing them.
6. Record review date, link, response date and responsible staff member.
7. Use the correct GBP review link after re-authorisation.

**Working growth target from strategy:** five new reviews per week was suggested, but management must confirm a realistic, ethical target. The SEO baseline showed **73 reviews and 4.9/5** as of May 2026; this is a historical baseline, not a current count.
