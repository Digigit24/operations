# GBP Review Response Templates

**Status:** DRAFT. Personalise every response. Never reveal diagnosis, treatment, admission details, billing data or any other patient information.

## Positive review — general

Thank you, [First name], for taking the time to share your experience. We are glad you felt supported by the team at Gore Multispeciality Hospital. Your feedback will be shared with the staff. We wish you and your family good health.

## Positive review — named doctor

Thank you, [First name], for your kind words about Dr. [Verified name] and the team. We are pleased that the consultation felt clear and supportive. We appreciate your trust in Gore Multispeciality Hospital.

## Positive review — emergency response

Thank you for sharing your experience. We are glad the team could respond promptly and guide you during a stressful time. We appreciate your trust. [Do not confirm clinical details.]

## Neutral or mixed review

Thank you for your feedback. We are pleased that [brief positive point], and we also note your concern about [service point, without repeating health details]. Please contact the hospital administration through the verified number so the team can review the experience privately.

## Negative review — service or waiting time

We are sorry that your experience did not meet expectations. We take concerns about waiting time and communication seriously. To protect your privacy, please avoid sharing medical details here and contact the hospital administration through the verified number with the date/time of your visit so the team can review what happened.

## Negative review — billing concern

We are sorry to hear about your concern. Billing questions deserve a clear, case-specific review. Please contact the hospital administration privately with the visit date and bill reference; do not post personal or medical information publicly. The team will review the matter and respond through the appropriate channel.

## Suspected fake / no-match review

We cannot identify this visit from the information shown, and we do not want to discuss personal information publicly. Please contact the hospital administration through the verified number with the date of visit and relevant reference so the team can investigate.

## Response rules

- Aim to respond within 48 hours, subject to staff capacity.
- Use the reviewer’s first name where appropriate.
- Do not copy-paste an identical response repeatedly.
- Do not argue, diagnose, blame, threaten or offer incentives to remove a review.
- Escalate allegations involving safety, discrimination, fraud, death, legal action or serious billing disputes to management before responding.
