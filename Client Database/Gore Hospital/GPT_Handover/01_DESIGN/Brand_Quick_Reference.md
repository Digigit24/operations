# Brand Quick Reference

## Current working system — not yet backed by a signed client approval sheet
- **Personality:** Trusted · Warm · Clear.
- **Positioning:** “Your family’s hospital. Honest care. Close to home.”
- **Primary visual anchor:** colorful GMH logo uploaded in the project.
- **Palette:** Trust Maroon `#7A3438`, Vitality Orange `#F47B2B`, Wellness Green `#7AB840`, Care Sky `#5BB7DA`, Heritage Gold `#C9A35E`, Auspicious Red `#DB2F25`; Soft Cream `#FBF7EE`; Charcoal `#1E1B1A`.
- **Fonts:** Fraunces for editorial/founder headlines; Onest for modern headlines/CTAs; Inter for body; Mukta for Marathi.
- **Logo:** top-left editorial, top-right ads, carousel footer, bottom-right watermark for stories; minimum clear space 12% of logo height; never over busy photography.
- **Design:** editorial, asymmetric, layered, calm, ample whitespace; use one of five modes: Editorial Trust, Family Warmth, Clinical Clarity, Vital Action, Heritage Wellness.
- **Images:** photorealistic Indian doctors/families and real hospital context; natural skin tones; no Western stock default, gore, panic or fake anatomy.
- **CTA:** one clear action; Trust Maroon default, Auspicious Red only for emergency, green for wellness, gold for premium/senior.
- **Restrictions:** no cure/guarantee/100%/instant/painless/best claims without proof; no patient face/name without consent; no unsourced medical statistics.
- **Preferred dimensions:** Instagram portrait `1080×1350`; square/carousel `1080×1080`; story/reel cover `1080×1920`; GMB `1200×900`.

## Legacy system
Generic medical blue/navy and circular gold-emblem variants are historical. Do not mix them into the current system.

## Approval state
No design rule, logo version, palette or asset in this package is labelled client-approved unless separate approval evidence is added.
