# Typography Guide

| Role | Preferred | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial/founder headline | Fraunces SemiBold/Bold | Cormorant Garamond, Lora, Playfair Display |
| Modern headline | Onest Bold | Montserrat, Poppins |
| Body/caption | Inter Regular/Medium | DM Sans, Source Sans, Manrope |
| CTA | Onest SemiBold | Montserrat SemiBold |
| Marathi headline/body | Mukta Bold/Regular | Hind Vadodara, Yantramanav |

Rules: one H1; serif headline always paired with sans body; no shadows/glows/strokes; Marathi needs larger optical size and about 1.5 line height; finalise typography in Canva/Figma rather than relying on AI image text.

Font files and licences are not included. Confirm commercial availability and workspace access before production.
