# Client Design Preferences

## Evidenced working preferences
- Warm, culturally rooted identity derived from the colorful logo.
- Editorial restraint, human Indian healthcare imagery and founder-led trust.
- Marathi/English bilingual treatment where audience-appropriate.
- Clear hierarchy, moderate branding and strong mobile legibility.
- No generic corporate-blue hospital look; no crowded flyer layout.

## Historical revision patterns
- Requests to simplify address boxes and remove irrelevant decorative elements.
- Some iterations removed doctor photos or changed visual subjects.
- Multiple older outputs used gold/blue treatments, cartoons/3D and varying contact details.

## Unverified preferences
No consolidated, client-signed preference sheet or approval SLA was found. Treat this document as an internal synthesis, not proof of client approval.
