# Logo Usage Guide

## Current working mark
Use `02_ASSETS/01_Logos/DATE-UNKNOWN_Gore-Hospital_Colour-Logo_EN-NA-RAW_v01.jpeg` as the working logo only after checking that the client has selected it. Request SVG/AI/CDR/PDF masters and monochrome/white variants.

## Placement
- Educational/editorial/founder: top-left.
- Paid/offer creative: top-right.
- Carousel internal slide: centred footer strip.
- Story/reel watermark: bottom-right at 70–80% opacity.
- Clear space: at least 12% of logo height on every side.
- Minimum digital width: 60 px.

## Backgrounds
Best on Soft Cream, white or light grey. On maroon/dark green, use an approved white-background or white-outline variant. Never place over busy photography, peacock patterns, faces or dense infographics.

## Prohibited
No stretching, skewing, recolouring, glow, drop shadow, bevel, 3D treatment, internal-element edits or use as a large watermark over people.

## Unresolved
Three circular gold logos exist in the File Library. They are classified legacy/unverified and must not be substituted without signed client direction.
