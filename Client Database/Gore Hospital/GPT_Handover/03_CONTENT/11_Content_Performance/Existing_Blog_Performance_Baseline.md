# Existing Blog Performance Baseline

**Data window:** 90 days ending 24 May 2026.  
**Source:** Gore_Hospital_02_SEO_Foundation.md.

- 12 of 15 measured blog landing pages had 80%+ bounce; several were at 100%.
- `common-lung-diseases-2026-symptoms.html` was the only healthy outlier in the cited set, at 40% bounce.
- `heart-healthy-foods-cardiologists-recommend.html` had about 650 impressions at average position 1.5, zero GSC clicks and 10 GA4 sessions with zero engagement — an anomaly requiring title/meta/intro/redirect investigation.
- The 30-topic new-blog backlog should remain paused until the existing inventory is classified keep/rewrite/301/remove.
- No social, reel, GMB-post, creative or campaign performance dataset was available.
