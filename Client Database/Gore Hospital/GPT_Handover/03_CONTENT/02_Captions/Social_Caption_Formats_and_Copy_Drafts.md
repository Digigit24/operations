# Social Caption Formats and Copy Drafts

**Status:** Draft references. No caption below has publication or approval evidence.

## Recommended social caption pattern
1. Human/scenario hook.
2. Helpful explanation in plain Indian English or Marathi.
3. Named service/doctor only when verified.
4. One action: book, call, WhatsApp, save or get directions.
5. Medical disclaimer where needed.
6. Focused local hashtags; avoid stuffing.

## Draft social primary text examples
### Child fever / paediatrics
“It’s 9 PM and your child has a fever. Knowing which signs need urgent care can reduce panic and delay. Gore Multispeciality Hospital provides local paediatric and emergency support in Mamurdi, near Ravet. Call the hospital for guidance on where to seek care.”

### Honest-care positioning
“Families deserve clear explanations before tests, admission or procedures. Our working brand promise is simple: honest care, close to home. Any billing or admission statement must be supported by a documented hospital policy before publication.”

### Knee pain
“Not every episode of knee pain leads to surgery. A clinical examination can help identify the cause and the right next step. Book an orthopaedic consultation in Mamurdi, near Ravet.”

### Founder-led trust
“Meet Dr. Dattesh Gore, the physician at the centre of Gore Hospital’s family-care story. A founder interview should explain why the hospital was built, how decisions are discussed with families, and when the team refers to higher-level care.”

## Hashtag format
Use 3–8 focused, relevant tags for Instagram and 2–5 for GBP. Example working set: `#Ravet #Mamurdi #PCMC #RavetHospital #PuneHealthcare`. Do not add unrelated trending tags.

## Disclaimer options
- “This information is for general awareness and does not replace a medical consultation.”
- “Symptoms and treatment needs vary. Please consult a qualified doctor.”
- “In an emergency, call the hospital or local emergency services immediately.”
