## Slide 2 of 7 — Habit 01 of 05
### 01 — Walk 30 Minutes Daily
Just 30 minutes of brisk walking lowers blood pressure, improves circulation, and cuts heart disease risk by up to 35%.

**Quick start:** Park farther away. Take the stairs. A 10-min walk 3× a day counts just as much.

- 35% — Lower heart risk
- 30 min — All you need daily
- 0 cost — No equipment needed

## Slide 3 of 7 — Habit 02 of 05
### 02 — Cut Salt & Sugar
Excess salt raises blood pressure. Excess sugar inflames your arteries. Both silently damage your heart — over years, without warning.

**Easy swap:** Replace packaged snacks with fruits and nuts. Limit salt to less than 5g per day (about 1 teaspoon).

**Avoid:** Processed foods · Packaged snacks · Sugary drinks  
**Choose:** Fresh fruits & nuts · Whole grains · Plain water

**Review flags:** All causal phrasing and the 5g limit need clinical review and appropriate source/date.

## Slide 4 of 7 — Habit 03 of 05
### 03 — Sleep 7–8 Hours
Poor sleep spikes cortisol and blood pressure. Deep rest is when your heart actually recovers. Less than 6 hours triples your heart attack risk.

**Tonight:** No screens 45 minutes before bed. Keep the same sleep time every day — even on weekends.

- Under 6 hrs — 3× higher heart attack risk
- 7–8 hrs — Optimal heart recovery

**Review flags:** “triples” and “optimal” are high-risk unsourced claims in the accessible record.

## Slide 5 of 7 — Habit 04 of 05
### 04 — Manage Your Stress
Chronic stress is a direct risk factor for heart attacks. It inflames arteries, raises blood pressure, and disrupts sleep — all at once.

**Try this now:** Box breathing: inhale 4s → hold 4s → exhale 4s → hold 4s. Just 5 minutes resets your nervous system.

Other proven stress busters: 10 min walk · Journaling · Deep breathing

**Review flags:** “resets your nervous system” and “proven” require correction/source review.

## Slide 6 of 7 — Habit 05 of 05
### 05 — Annual Heart Check-Up
80% of heart attacks are preventable. A simple annual check-up catches warning signs years before they become emergencies.

**Get checked for:** Blood pressure · Cholesterol · Blood sugar · ECG · BMI — all covered in one visit at Gore Hospital.

**Who should get checked:**
- Age 30+ with no symptoms
- Family history of heart disease
- Diabetic or hypertensive
- High-stress lifestyle
