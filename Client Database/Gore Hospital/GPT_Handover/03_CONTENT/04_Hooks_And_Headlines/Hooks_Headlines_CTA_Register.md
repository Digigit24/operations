# Hooks, Headlines and CTA Register

## Working brand headlines
- Your family’s hospital. Honest care. Close to home.
- One trusted team for every generation of your family.
- We listen first, then we treat.
- Honest answers. Clear next steps.
- Modern medicine. Marathi warmth. Mamurdi address.
- When something happens at 2 AM, know where to go.

## Scenario-led hooks
- It’s 9 PM. Your child has a fever. What should you do next?
- Knee pain making the stairs harder?
- When does chest pain need emergency care?
- What should you do first after a road accident?
- Cashless admission: what documents do you actually need?
- How do you reach Gore Hospital from Ravet, Kiwale or Mamurdi?

## Doctor/service hooks
- Meet Dr. Dattesh Gore — your family’s first call.
- Looking for a paediatrician in Ravet?
- Need a cardiologist closer to home?
- A family dentist in Mamurdi.
- Dialysis without the long commute — only after service scope confirmation.

## Avoid
“Buy now”, fake scarcity, “limited slots” without proof, cure/guarantee language, “best in Pune/PCMC”, “painless”, “100%”, “instant relief” and fear-based emergency copy.
