# PROJECT_SUMMARY.md — Gore Multispeciality Hospital

## What this project is

A full-service digital marketing engagement for **Gore Multispeciality Hospital** (Mamurdi–Ravet, PCMC Pune), covering brand strategy, visual design system, AI-assisted creative production, website, SEO, Google Business Profile (GMB), and social media content/reporting. Run through a chained "prompt" system (Prompt 00 → 01 → 02 → 04 etc.) documented in a set of markdown strategy files, backed by a client Notion workspace and live analytics connectors (Windsor.ai → GSC + GA4 + GBP).

## The core strategic position

**"Your family's hospital. Honest care. Close to home."** — anchored on:
1. **Founder-led trust** — Dr. Dattesh Gore as the brand's emotional spine
2. **Honest billing** — directly counter-positioned against a named competitor's (Lifepoint's) public reputation weakness around over-admission/over-billing
3. **Multi-generational family care** — child + mother + father + grandparent under one trust anchor
4. **Marathi-comfortable warmth** — even English content should read as culturally rooted, never corporate

## The single most urgent open issue

The website states **"2k+ reviews"**; the real, verified Google Business Profile count is **73**. This has been flagged repeatedly since 24 May 2026 (SEO Foundation §10/§11, progress report P0, GBP audit) and, as of the last available status check, does not appear to have been confirmed fixed. This is a live ASCI misleading-advertising compliance risk in addition to a credibility issue.

## What kind of "content" actually exists today

Important for anyone expecting a large content library: **this is a strategy-and-systems-mature, content-execution-early project.** There is:
- No large bank of finished scripts, reels, or captions — one AI-generated static creative (joint-pain awareness) is the only confirmed finished social asset referenced in the sources reviewed.
- A rich set of **content ideas, angles, and briefs** (30 blog topics, 20 sample post/reel ideas, a full June 2026 15-post calendar with hooks and caption directions, and structured ad-angle research) — these are drafts/briefs, not finished copy.
- A real, if partial, publishing history: 10 Instagram posts logged for May 2026, and a full June 2026 delivery (10 static + 3 reels + 2 carousels, 100% on-schedule) reported in the SMO report — but the actual post copy/creative files were not retrievable into this session.
