## Production note (established 10 July 2026)
AI image models are unreliable at reproducing an exact logo, hex colours, or footer layout.
The working house process is: **generate the background/illustration with an AI image tool
(Higgsfield: Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image 2 / Seedream), then composite the real logo, brand
colours, and footer/contact strip on top via an HTML/CSS + Playwright rendering pipeline** —
do not ask the AI model to draw the logo itself.
