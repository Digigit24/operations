## Elements and what they signal
| Element | Signals | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Maroon "GMH" hexagon | Heritage, gravitas, trusted authority | Primary anchor, founder content |
| Orange human figure | Vitality, family life, motion | Family/paediatric energy |
| Green leaf | Growth, healing, natural | Preventive care, success states |
| Peacock feather | Indian cultural anchor, auspiciousness | Subtle accent only — festival/founder/premium, 8–15% opacity |
| Red dot | Urgency, auspicious mark | Emergency CTAs only, rare |
| Sky-blue arc | Calm, openness | Paediatrics, general health |
| Gold arc | Premium, achievement | Wellness packages, senior care, festival |

## Placement rules
- Top-left: educational/founder/editorial · Top-right: ad creatives/offers · Centred footer: carousel slides · Bottom-right translucent (70–80%): story/reel watermark
- Minimum clear space: 12% of logo height on all sides
- Sizing by format: IG square 14–18% of canvas width · IG portrait 12–16% · IG story 10–14% · LinkedIn 12–15% · Website banner 8–12% · Carousel cover 14% · Carousel internal 10%

## Background pairing
- Best: cream, soft off-white, light grey
- Acceptable: solid maroon or solid dark green (needs a white/outline logo variant not yet produced — see gap above)
- Never: over photography, over busy infographics, over neon/saturated backgrounds, over a peacock-feather pattern (visual conflict)

## Never do
Stretch/skew/distort · add glow/drop-shadow/bevel/3D · use as a watermark over patient/doctor faces · recolour the GMH monogram or any internal element · place on a peacock-feather pattern · use below 60px wide on any digital canvas
