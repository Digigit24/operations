## Recommended cadence
Weekly GBP posts on a Monday/Thursday cadence, alternating English and Marathi, mixing service
highlights, doctor introductions, and festival creatives. (Source: SEO Foundation Days 61–90
task #22.)

## Content-type separation rule (important — from the master extraction instructions and confirmed by the Design Instructions)
GMB content must remain **separate** from Instagram/Meta content, and should be:
- Helpful, local, professional, moderately branded, non-spammy
- Free from aggressive urgency and unsupported medical claims
- Do NOT automatically reuse Meta ad copy as GMB copy

## GMB image specifications
1200×900 minimum, avoid text-heavy images — prefer a clean image with a light overlay (per the
AI Prompting Guide's output-size reference table).

## Required elements per post
Named doctor or specialty where relevant, brief proof point (credentials/experience), clear
CTA, contact details, 24x7 emergency number.
