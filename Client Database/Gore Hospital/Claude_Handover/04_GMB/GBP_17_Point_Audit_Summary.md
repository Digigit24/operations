### Business Name — Compliant
No keyword stuffing; name is clean but lacks locality context in the name itself.
**Recommendation:** Keep "Gore Multispeciality Hospital" exactly as-is. Adding locality to the
GBP *name* field violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension — compensate via description
and services fields instead. Priority: Medium.

### Primary Category — ⚠ Needs verification
Primary category unverified as of the audit; likely "Hospital" but could be miscategorised.
**Recommendation:** Confirm primary = "Hospital." Primary category is the #1 GBP ranking
signal. Priority: High.

### Photos
**Recommendation:** (1) Reach 30+ photos — exterior, reception, OPD rooms, OT (compliance-
permitting), each named doctor portrait, ambulance, pharmacy, diagnostics, dental setup. Listings
with 30+ photos get 42% more direction requests (Google data). (2) Name every photo file with
keywords before uploading (e.g. `gore-hospital-ravet-emergency-room.jpg`). (3) Upload 1–2 short
GBP videos (60–90 sec): hospital tour + Dr. Gore introduction. (4) Add a 360° photo via Google
Street View Trusted Photographer. Priority: High. Ranking impact: High. Conversion impact: High.

### Reviews: Count, Rating & Response Strategy
**Current status:** 4.9/5 across 73 reviews (May 2026). Strong recurring themes: founder
personal involvement, speed, cleanliness, homely ambience, polite staff. GBP Posts table
returns zero rows — no active posting cadence. Dr. Subhangi named in one review but not on the
website's doctor roster. No confirmed review-response strategy.
**Recommendation:** (1) Target 200+ reviews within 2 quarters, 5 new/week minimum. (2) QR code
at billing desk linking to the GBP review URL. (3) WhatsApp post-discharge thank-you message 24
hours after visit, with review link. (4) Train reception: when a patient says "thank you
doctor," hand them the review card. (5) Reply to every review within 48 hours — positive:
thank by name and reinforce the specific positive; negative: acknowledge + invite to call the
founder directly. (6) Verify Dr. Subhangi's current status; add to team page and GBP if
confirmed. Priority: High. Ranking impact: Very High (review count/velocity are top-3 local
ranking signals). Conversion impact: Very High (4.9★ in SERP drives 20–35% above-average CTR).

### FAQs & Q&A Section
**Current status:** GBP Q&A section unverified/likely empty; no confirmed FAQPage schema.
Patients searching "cashless?", "24x7?", "parking?", "Marathi doctors?" get no GBP-level
answer.
**Recommendation:** Seed 15 Q&As on GBP immediately, e.g.: "Is Gore Hospital open 24x7 for
emergency?", "Is cashless insurance accepted?", "Do you have a paediatrician available at

you have dental services?" — plus FAQPage schema on the homepage and key service pages, and
weekly monitoring for user-submitted questions (answer within 24 hours). Priority: High.
Ranking impact: Medium (feeds Google's knowledge panel).
