# Audience_Profiles.md — 5 Ideal Client Profiles (ICPs)

Source: `Gore_Hospital_00_Strategic_Research.md` Section 8. Listed in strategic priority order.

## ICP 1 — "Anita," the young Ravet mother (Volume Driver)
28–38, married, 1–2 children, 2BHK in Ravet/Mamurdi/Punawale. Household ₹12–25L/yr (IT/service
dual income). Primary healthcare decision-maker for the family. Solution-aware — has mom
WhatsApp groups, follows 2–3 Pune mom Instagram pages. **Pain:** time-poor, anxious about
every fever/cough, wants reassurance not over-investigation. **Trust needs:** named
paediatrician with credentials, OPD photos, recent reviews from other moms. **Channels:**
Instagram Reels, WhatsApp groups, Google Maps, local mom blogs. **Emotional driver:** being a
good mother. **Best CTA:** WhatsApp → "Quick question for our paediatrician."

## ICP 2 — "Suresh," the middle-class father (Decision Anchor)
38–55, may have ageing parents in-household. Household ₹10–20L/yr, often single-earner.
Financial signoff for major medical decisions; pulls the trigger on parents' hospitalisation.
**Pain:** fear of being overbilled, distrust of corporate hospitals, language barrier
(prefers Marathi/Hindi). **Trust needs:** Marathi-speaking staff, predictable pricing, founder
visibility, named senior doctors. **Channels:** WhatsApp forwards, Facebook, local Marathi
news, word of mouth. **Emotional driver:** protecting his family + not being taken advantage
of. **Best CTA:** "Speak to Dr. Gore directly."

## ICP 3 — Marathi-speaking parent/in-law (Caretaker & Influencer)
55–75, retired/homemaker, lives with adult children or visits from native village.
Family-supported income. Strong influence on family decisions; often the one who actually
accompanies kids/grandkids to the hospital. **Pain:** language comfort (English-only staff is
intimidating), feeling rushed, complex billing. **Trust needs:** familiar cultural cues
(Diwali puja, Marathi posters), senior-doctor demeanour, named staff. **Channels:** community
word of mouth, Marathi local newspapers, family WhatsApp. **Emotional driver:** dignity and
being respected. **Best CTA (Marathi):** "मराठीतून बोलणारे डॉक्टर येथे उपलब्ध" (Marathi-speaking
doctors available here).

## ICP 4 — "Rohit," the IT professional (High-Value Insurance Patient)
28–42, works Hinjawadi/Wakad, family in Ravet/Punawale. Household ₹15–40L/yr, corporate health
insurance (Star, ICICI Lombard, Cigna TTK, etc.). Highly digital-literate, compares 3+
hospitals, reads Google reviews thoroughly. **Pain:** distance/traffic to Aditya Birla, wants
cashless without paperwork hassle, distrustful of unnecessary admissions. **Trust needs:**
clean modern website, online booking, digital reports, named specialists, cashless TPA panel.
**Channels:** Google search, Instagram, Practo, LinkedIn. **Emotional driver:** efficiency,
not wasting time. **Best CTA:** "Book online in 30 seconds."

## ICP 5 — "Kavita & Ramesh," the senior couple (Chronic & Recurring Revenue)
60–80, retired, living with or near adult children. Pension + family support. Chronic
condition management (diabetes, hypertension, ortho, cardiology, nephrology). Problem-aware
about their conditions, solution-loyal once they find a trusted doctor. **Pain:** regular
consultations, transport, multiple specialists, medicine refill, billing confusion. **Trust
needs:** continuity of care (same nurse/receptionist), founder visibility, slow-paced
compassionate communication. **Channels:** family WhatsApp, word of mouth, walk-in.
**Emotional driver:** being known, being safe. **Best CTA:** "Senior wellness package."

## Buyer psychology (applies across all 5 ICPs)

**Top fear (dominant, category-wide):** "They'll over-investigate to inflate the bill" —
publicly visible in regional Lifepoint reviews. **Counter-message:** honest-billing
positioning, "what you actually need" tone, transparent estimates.

**What buyers say vs. what they actually want:**
| They say | They actually want |
|---|---|
| "Best multispeciality hospital" | A hospital that doesn't make them feel rushed or fleeced |
| "Modern facilities" | Clean, calm, organised — not necessarily high-tech |
| "Experienced doctors" | A doctor who explains in their language |
| "24x7 emergency" | "If something happens at 2 AM, I won't be lost" |
| "Compassionate care" | "I won't have to fight for basic dignity" |

**What makes them delay:** asking the WhatsApp group first, checking Google reviews,
distance/parking, cost worry, anxiety about being upsold.

**What makes them trust quickly:** a relatable recent Google review, a neighbour's
recommendation, seeing the founder's face/credentials clearly, a clean warm reception on the
first visit, predictable post-visit communication.
