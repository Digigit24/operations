## Quick summary (for someone who has 60 seconds)

Gore is a founder-led, Tier-3 community multispeciality hospital in the fast-growing western
PCMC corridor (Ravet/Mamurdi), built around **Dr. Dattesh Gore**. It competes on convenience,
cost, and founder trust — not clinical prestige. The single biggest open strategic
opportunity is **"honest billing"** positioning, directly counter-positioned against a
competitor's (Lifepoint's) publicly documented weakness around over-admission. Real Google
reviews (4.9★/73) already validate this positioning organically. The single biggest **risk**
