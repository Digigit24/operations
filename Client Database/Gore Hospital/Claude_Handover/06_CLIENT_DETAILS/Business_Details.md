# Business_Details.md

## What the business is
Gore Multispeciality Hospital (referred to as "GMH" in the logo monogram) is a
community-based multispeciality hospital in Mamurdi, Ravet, in the rapidly growing western
corridor of PCMC Pune. It positions itself as the "best multispeciality hospital in Ravet,
Pimpri Chinchwad" (current, on-site headline — flagged as generic, see
`03_CONTENT/04_Hooks_And_Headlines/`) and serves families across Mamurdi, Ravet, Akurdi,
Punawale, Hinjawadi, Tathawade, Wakad, and adjacent neighbourhoods. — LIVE_ON_WEBSITE

Built around a small core team led by Dr. Dattesh Gore, who appears to be both founder and
family-physician face of the hospital — multiple patient testimonials name him directly,
suggesting a founder-led trust model rather than an institutional/corporate one. — INFERRED

## Market category and tier
Mid-scale community multispeciality hospital, sitting between standalone nursing homes/clinics
and tertiary super-specialty corporate hospitals. Roughly comparable to Varad (Punawale),
Pulse (Tathawade), Matoshree, City Care — neighbourhood multi-specialty hospitals serving a
5–7 km radius. — INFERRED (competitive analysis)

**Tier structure of the local category:**
- **Tier 1 (₹₹₹):** Aditya Birla Memorial Hospital, Sahyadri Hospitals, Manipal, DPU Super
  Speciality Hospital — 200–500+ beds, NABH-accredited, full transplant/oncology/cardiac
  surgery
- **Tier 2 (₹₹):** Lifepoint (Wakad), Sterling (Wakad), Lokmanya, Niramaya (Chinchwad) —
  80–150 beds, broad specialties
- **Tier 3 (₹–₹₹):** **Gore (Mamurdi/Ravet)**, Varad (Punawale), Pulse (Tathawade), City Care
  (Ravet), Matoshree — 20–80 beds (⚠️ Gore's exact bed count not published — UNVERIFIED),
  walk-in OPD focus, lower-cost cash + basic insurance

## Market maturity
The PCMC western belt (Ravet → Punawale → Tathawade) is in an active growth phase — new
residential towers, young IT-employed families, underbuilt local healthcare infrastructure.
Crowded but not saturated: at least 6–8 community multispeciality hospitals within a 5 km
radius, but the category is full of indistinguishable "best/compassionate/modern/24x7"
messaging — differentiation through messaging is wide open. — INFERRED

## Primary revenue drivers (inferred, not client-confirmed)
1. Walk-in OPD volume — highest frequency, lowest ticket
2. Maternity & paediatrics — high local demand, recurring family relationship
3. Emergency/trauma/dengue/fever-class care — heavily promoted, walk-in conversions
4. Diagnostics & radiology — bundled with consultations
5. Surgeries — orthopaedics, general surgery, dental
6. Insurance + cash mix — ⚠️ exact mix UNVERIFIED

## What the client appears to sell vs. what it's actually selling
- **Surface:** a 24x7 multispeciality hospital with experienced doctors and modern diagnostics
- **Actual (strategic framing):** "Peace of mind for the local PCMC family... You don't need to
  travel to central Pune or pay corporate-hospital prices to get safe, qualified, family-aware
  care. We are your hospital — close to home, affordable, and you'll see the same doctors who
  know your family." — this is agency-authored strategic framing, not a client quote
