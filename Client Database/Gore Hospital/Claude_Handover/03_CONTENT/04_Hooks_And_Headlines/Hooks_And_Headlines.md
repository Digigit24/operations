### Competitor-aware (compliance-safe — no direct naming)
"Why drive 1 hour to Chinchwad for a fever consult?" — positions Gore as the everyday
alternative, not an attack ad.

### Trust-based
- "2000+ families across PCMC trust Gore Hospital" — ⚠️ **DO NOT USE as written.** This mirrors
  the exact "2k+" claim already confirmed false against the real GBP count (73). Any trust
  hook using a family/patient count must use a verified figure.
- "Meet the doctors behind Gore Hospital" — founder-led, real photos, named credentials

### Founder-led
- "Dr. Dattesh Gore: I built this hospital because…" (60–90s reel) — blocked pending founder
  origin-story interview (open item)
- "A day in Dr. Gore's OPD" (vlog-style reel)

### Urgency-based (use sparingly, compliance-safe only)
- "Monsoon dengue cases rising — protect your family" (awareness, not fear-mongering)
- "Free women's health screening — limited slots this month" (only if genuinely time-limited)
