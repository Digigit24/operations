# 08_Ad_Copy

Full ad-angle research (Meta, Google Search, YouTube, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) is reproduced in
../04_Hooks_And_Headlines/Hooks_And_Headlines.md — that file is the primary reference for this
folder to avoid duplicate maintenance. Key points:

- No ad spend has run yet — the June 2026 SMO report explicitly notes "No ads to show during
  this time" on both Facebook and Instagram dashboards; every reach figure reported to date is
  100% unpaid organic.
- The SMO report recommends introducing a modest paid-boost budget (₹3,000-5,000/month) behind
  top-performing organic posts starting July 2026 — not yet confirmed executed.
- All ad copy must comply with Indian healthcare advertising law: Drugs and Magic Remedies
  (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954, MCI/NMC ethical guidelines, ASCI healthcare ad code,
  Consumer Protection Act 2019, MTP Act (maternity content), Drug and Cosmetic Act (pharmacy
  content). See 01_DESIGN/Full_Design_Guidelines.md Section 13 for the full compliance list.
