## From the AI Prompting Guide's ready-to-use examples (00_SOURCES/Original_Uploads/Gore_Hospital_01_AI_Prompting_Guide.md, Example 6)

1. Cover: "Cashless. No paperwork hassle." + "Here's how insurance works at Gore Hospital -> swipe"
2. "Which insurance partners we work with" — ⚠️ BLOCKED: requires the real, confirmed TPA list (open item)
3. "What to bring on day of admission"
4. "How pre-authorisation works"
5. "When you don't need cashless (and why)"
6. CTA: "Questions? WhatsApp our insurance desk"

This is a design-style example in the prompting guide, not a confirmed production brief — treat
as a strong candidate for the next carousel once the TPA-list open item is resolved.

## Carousel design rules (from 01_DESIGN/)
Cover slide must stop the scroll in <1 second, 4-10 word headline, one dominant visual.
Internal slides: consistent footer/numbering ("1 of 7"), 35-45 words max per slide, one
concept per slide. Final slide always includes CTA + WhatsApp + emergency line + brand footer.
