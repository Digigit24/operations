## Analysis: why did reach decline (98–99% drop in views on both platforms vs. prior window)?
Assessed as **platform-side, not content-side**:
- Meta significantly throttled organic distribution industry-wide through Q2 2026, especially
  for non-Reel static/carousel healthcare content
- No paid boost ran behind organic posts this period — a much harder ceiling than in prior
  months
- Instagram still meaningfully outperformed Facebook, suggesting Instagram deserves a larger
  share of monthly output going forward
- Static infographic carousels are increasingly deprioritised vs. Reels/short-form video — a
  format shift, not a quality issue

**Conclusion stated in the report:** "Publishing discipline and content quality were not the
problem this month — the distribution environment was."
