# KUMSS GMB Quick Guide

- Preferred image: `1200×900`, 4:3, JPG/PNG, realistic local-business style.
- Keep text overlay near 5–20%; 3–8 words; one CTA.
- Use real KUMSS activity/facility photography whenever possible.
- Tone: helpful, local, professional, human and moderately branded.
- Caption flow: hook → useful information → natural KUMSS mention → CTA → optional one phone → 2–5 hashtags.
- Avoid guarantees, urgency, fake certificates/reviews, keyword stuffing, all-caps, repeated phones, excess emojis, poster clutter and unverified affiliation/placement claims.
- Do not copy Meta-ad copy directly into GMB.
- Personalise review responses; never argue; move sensitive resolution offline.
