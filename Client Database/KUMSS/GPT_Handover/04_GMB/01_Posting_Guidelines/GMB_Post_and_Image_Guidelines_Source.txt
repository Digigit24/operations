# GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE (GMB) AI CONTENT GENERATION GUIDELINES

## Master Guidelines to Prevent Post Rejections

Version: AI Training Reference
Purpose: Train AI systems to generate Google Business Profile-safe images, captions, and promotional content with minimal rejection risk.

---

# SECTION 1 — CORE PRINCIPLES

Google Business Profile (GBP/GMB) prioritizes:

* Authenticity
* Local relevance
* User safety
* Non-spam behavior
* Trustworthy business communication

Your AI should generate content that looks:

* Helpful
* Informative
* Professional
* Genuine
* Locally relevant
* Human-created

NOT:

* Aggressive advertising
* Clickbait
* Spam
* Fake testimonials
* Misleading claims
* Overdesigned sales creatives

---

# SECTION 2 — GLOBAL AI RULES (APPLY TO ALL POSTS)

## 2.1 Mandatory AI Safety Rules

ALWAYS:

* Use natural language
* Keep content readable
* Use proper grammar
* Maintain professional tone
* Mention business naturally
* Focus on value first, promotion second
* Use realistic claims only
* Use moderate branding

NEVER:

* Generate fake urgency
* Generate exaggerated promises
* Use misleading statistics
* Use fear-based marketing
* Use spam-style formatting
* Use keyword stuffing
* Use excessive emojis
* Use repetitive CTAs
* Use misleading before/after claims
* Generate content resembling scam ads

---

# SECTION 3 — IMAGE GENERATION GUIDELINES

# 3.1 IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS

## Recommended Technical Specs

* Resolution: 1200×900 px preferred
* Minimum: 400×300 px
* Aspect Ratio: 4:3
* Format: JPG or PNG
* File Size: 10 KB–5 MB

---

# 3.2 SAFE IMAGE DESIGN RULES

## AI MUST Generate Images That Are:

* Clean
* Professional
* High resolution
* Realistic
* Easy to read
* Minimalistic
* Brand-consistent

## Preferred Visual Style

* Real photography look
* Soft gradients
* Moderate typography
* Balanced whitespace
* Natural lighting
* Authentic human expressions

---

# 3.3 TEXT OVERLAY RULES

## Safe Text Usage

Recommended:

* 5–20% text coverage
* Short headlines only
* 3–8 words ideal
* One CTA maximum

Example:

* “Summer Dental Checkup”
* “New Batch Admissions Open”
* “Skin Care Consultation”

---

## Avoid Excessive Text

DO NOT:

* Cover full image with text
* Use poster-like layouts
* Use newspaper-style formatting
* Add paragraphs on image
* Add too many offers
* Add multiple CTAs
* Add repeated phone numbers

---

# 3.4 SAFE TYPOGRAPHY RULES

## Recommended

* Sans-serif fonts
* Clean readability
* 1–2 font families maximum
* Moderate font weights

## Avoid

* Flashy fonts
* Over-stylized fonts
* Ultra-bold spam fonts
* Neon effects
* Excessive shadows
* Glow effects
* Distorted text

---

# 3.5 LOGO RULES

## Allowed

* Small business logo
* Corner placement preferred
* Subtle branding

## Avoid

* Giant logos
* Repeated logos
* Watermarks everywhere
* Obstructive branding

---

# 3.6 IMAGE CONTENT RULES

## SAFE CONTENT TYPES

AI SHOULD prioritize:

* Business interiors
* Team photos
* Service process visuals
* Product photos
* Educational graphics
* Event images
* Real customer interactions
* Local business imagery

---

# 3.7 HIGH-RISK IMAGE TYPES

AI SHOULD AVOID:

* Fake celebrity endorsements
* Unrealistic transformations
* Medical gore
* Graphic surgery visuals
* Shocking imagery
* Misleading “before-after” edits
* Extreme beauty edits
* Fake screenshots
* WhatsApp chat screenshots
* Fake reviews
* Fake certificates

---

# 3.8 MEDICAL IMAGE RULES

For healthcare businesses:

## Allowed

* Clean clinic photos
* Doctor portraits
* Consultation visuals
* Educational diagrams
* Symptom awareness graphics

## Forbidden

* Blood-heavy visuals
* Surgical closeups
* Graphic wounds
* Explicit skin disease visuals
* Misleading treatment outcomes
* “Guaranteed cure” imagery

---

# 3.9 EDUCATION BUSINESS IMAGE RULES

Allowed:

* Students learning
* Campus photos
* Classroom visuals
* Event photos
* Career guidance graphics

Avoid:

* Fake placement statistics
* Unrealistic salary claims
* “100% job guarantee”
* Fake rank claims

---

# 3.10 REAL ESTATE IMAGE RULES

Allowed:

* Property photos
* Construction updates
* Interior shots
* Site visits

Avoid:

* Fake luxury exaggeration
* Misleading pricing
* Unrealistic renders without disclaimer

---

# 3.11 IMAGE AI DETECTION RED FLAGS

AI-generated images often get flagged if they:

* Look overpolished
* Have unnatural faces
* Have distorted hands
* Use hyper-saturated colors
* Have unrealistic lighting
* Include unreadable text
* Look like banner ads

Your AI should aim for:
“Professional local business social media post”
NOT:
“Performance marketing ad creative”

---

# SECTION 4 — CAPTION / CONTENT GENERATION GUIDELINES

# 4.1 IDEAL CONTENT STRUCTURE

Recommended structure:

1. Hook
2. Helpful information
3. Business mention
4. CTA
5. Optional contact details
6. 2–5 hashtags

---

# 4.2 SAFE CONTENT STYLE

AI should generate:

* Conversational tone
* Helpful tone
* Informative tone
* Community-oriented tone

NOT:

* Pushy sales language
* Aggressive advertising
* Spam promotions

---

# 4.3 SAFE HOOK FORMULAS

GOOD:

* “Did you know?”
* “Here are some signs of…”
* “Looking for…?”
* “Planning your child’s future?”
* “Simple tips to improve…”

BAD:

* “STOP SCROLLING”
* “LIMITED TIME ONLY”
* “DON’T MISS THIS”
* “BUY NOW FAST”
* “LAST CHANCE”

---

# 4.4 CLAIMS POLICY

## STRICTLY FORBIDDEN CLAIMS

AI MUST NEVER generate:

* “100% guaranteed”
* “Permanent cure”
* “Best in India”
* “No side effects”
* “Instant results”
* “Guaranteed admission”
* “Guaranteed placement”
* “Guaranteed pregnancy”
* “Guaranteed recovery”

---

# 4.5 SAFE CLAIM ALTERNATIVES

INSTEAD OF:
“Permanent hair fall cure”

USE:
“Hair fall treatment options available”

INSTEAD OF:
“Guaranteed weight loss”

USE:
“Personalized weight management support”

INSTEAD OF:
“100% job placement”

USE:
“Career assistance available”

---

# 4.6 CTA GUIDELINES

## SAFE CTAs

* Learn more
* Call us today
* Book your consultation
* Visit our center
* Contact our team
* Schedule an appointment
* Enquire now

---

## AVOID THESE CTAs

* Buy now!!!
* Hurry fast
* Limited stock hurry
* DM immediately
* Only today
* Last few seats hurry

---

# 4.7 PHONE NUMBER RULES

Safe:

* One phone number maximum
* Footer placement preferred

Avoid:

* Repeating number multiple times
* ALL CAPS around number
* Spam-style formatting

BAD:
CALL NOW 9876543210
9876543210
WHATSAPP NOW 9876543210

---

# 4.8 HASHTAG RULES

Recommended:

* 2–5 hashtags only
* Relevant hashtags only

Avoid:

* 20+ hashtags
* Repetitive hashtags
* Trending irrelevant hashtags

---

# 4.9 EMOJI RULES

Safe:

* 0–5 emojis maximum
* Professional usage

Avoid:

* Emoji spam
* Multiple repeated emojis

BAD:
🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 OFFER 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥

# SECTION 6 — SPAM DETECTION AVOIDANCE

# 6.1 MAJOR SPAM SIGNALS

Google may reject posts if AI generates:

* Repetitive templates
* Duplicate captions
* Keyword stuffing
* Excessive promotions
* Too many offers
* All-caps writing
* Too many links
* Too many phone numbers
* Banner-ad style images

---

# 6.2 DUPLICATE CONTENT RULES

AI SHOULD:

* Rewrite every caption uniquely
* Change sentence structure
* Rotate hooks
* Rotate CTA styles
* Vary hashtags

Avoid posting:

* Same image repeatedly
* Same caption repeatedly
* Same design with minor edits

---

# 6.3 KEYWORD STUFFING RULES

BAD:
“Best dentist in Pune best dental clinic Pune affordable dentist Pune”

GOOD:
“Our dental team in Pune offers routine checkups and smile care consultations.”

---

# SECTION 7 — IDEAL GBP POST LENGTH

Recommended:

* 80–250 words

Avoid:

* Extremely short spam posts
* Huge long-form essays

---

# SECTION 8 — GOOGLE TRUST SIGNALS

AI SHOULD GENERATE CONTENT THAT:

* Feels locally relevant
* Reflects real business activity
* Educates users
* Builds credibility
* Encourages genuine engagement
