# Full Design Guidelines

Use the full source files in `00_SOURCES/Original_Uploads` for detail.

1. **Direction:** institutional, agrarian-industrial, Marathi-rooted, quietly modern.
2. **Logo:** preserve full official lockup on institutional surfaces; do not distort, recolor, crop, add effects or recreate with AI.
3. **Color:** warm cream/white surfaces, navy text, saffron action, green trust/skill. Avoid cool-corporate, pastel, neon and coaching saturation.
4. **Typography:** Marathi first on parent surfaces; English supportive; no more than two families per creative; generous Devanagari line height.
5. **Layout:** 8-point spacing, near-symmetry, vertical hierarchy, substantial photography, one main CTA; no hero carousel/parallax.
6. **Photography:** real workshops, faculty, students, facilities, events and industry visits with consent. Avoid CGI, staged handshakes and fake certificate imagery.
7. **GMB:** 1200×900, moderate branding, 5–20% text, short headline, one CTA.
8. **Admission:** show only confirmed year, trades, eligibility, duration, certification and NAP. No placement guarantee or unverified badges.
9. **Approval gate:** verify facts, spelling, consent, rights, dimensions and written approval before export.
