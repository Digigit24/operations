# Business Details

The accessible sources describe KUMSS as a multi-institute Maharashtrian educational trust. The working target is its private ITI in Karhati/Baramati, established in 2020.

## Seven institutes listed in the audit
1. Vasatigruh Vidyalay, Karhati — 1951
2. Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidyalaya — 1993
3. Primary School, Karhati — 1999
4. New English School, Dhakale — 2000
5. Vasatigruh Junior College — 2013
6. KUMSS Private I.T.I Karhati — 2020
7. Gurukul English Medium School — 2021

This public-source list is not a substitute for current legal/academic records.
