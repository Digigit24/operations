# KUMSS — Google Business Profile (GMB) Guidelines & Templates

Google Business Profile content is kept **separate** from Instagram/Meta on purpose. GMB tone is **helpful, local, professional, moderately branded, non-spammy, free of aggressive urgency, and free of unsupported medical/placement claims**. Do **not** reuse Meta ad copy as GMB copy. Sources: `KUMSS_02_Master_Brand_Brain.md` §16 & §23, `KUMSS_01_Design_Instructions.md` §10.

## Image spec
- **1200 × 900 (4:3 minimum).** Real photo of the subject — never stock. Marathi headline above English (or Marathi-only for local audience). Lockup small in a top corner. CTA aligned to post type.

## Post copy length
- Headline 5–10 words (bilingual headline counts as one) · Body 30–80 words English / 25–60 words Marathi · CTA 3–5 words.

## GMB post types
| Type | Structure | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Achievement | headline (bilingual) + 1–2 sentence context + photo + lockup + CTA | as they occur |
| Event / Industry visit | event name + date + brief context + photo + CTA | when it occurs |
| Facility | subject + 1 sentence context + real photo + lockup | monthly |
| Faculty highlight | name + role + brief credential + portrait | 1–2/month |
| Admission update | bilingual update + contact CTA | during cycle |

## GMB don'ts
No "offer"/discount posts · no urgency banners · no external links beyond own website/WhatsApp · no quoted placement % or guaranteed-job claims · no political content · no motivational quotes.

### Achievement (post India Book of Records verification)
**Headline:** "India Book of Records — KUMSS ITI achievement"
```
On [date], over [X] of our students completed a 30-hour continuous skill
demonstration at KUMSS Private I.T.I Karhati. This achievement was
recognized by the India Book of Records.

To us, this is not about the record — it is about what these students
proved they can do.

Visit our institute to learn more about our vocational training programs.
```
**CTA:** Visit us / Call now

### Event — Industry visit
**Headline:** "उद्योग भेट — Industry Visit at [company]"
```
This week, our [trade] students visited [company / industrial setting]
in [location]. They observed real machinery, production workflows, and
work environments — extending their classroom learning into industrial
reality.

Industry-connected training is part of how we prepare KUMSS students.
```
**CTA:** Visit us / Learn more
