# KUMSS — Conflicts & Compliance Risks

Every important contradiction and compliance risk found across sources. **When two sources disagree, both are preserved here — nothing is silently "corrected."** Read before publishing anything.

## 🔴 COMPLIANCE RISKS (highest priority)

### R1. "100% Placement Assistance Guaranteed" live on the website
- **Risk:** Potentially misleading education-marketing claim (ASCI Education Advertising Code / CCPA 2019 exposure). Elevated because the institute is politically prominent.
- **Why it matters:** It is the loudest message on the ITI page while the genuinely strong signals (4.9★, Records, affiliations) are buried. Every day it stays live it can be indexed/archived.
- **Resolution:** **Never reproduce or amplify it in any deliverable.** Get it reworded to "Active Placement Cell" / "Placement Support Program" — a priority client conversation. This is the #1 account risk.

### R2. Affiliations claimed but unverified (DGT / NCVT / DVET / ISO 9001:2015)
- **Conflict:** Claimed on Facebook bio; absent from main website; no certificate displayed anywhere.
- **Resolution:** Do not surface any affiliation badge until portal-verified (NCVT MIS, DGT, DVET) and certificate obtained. Flag as "verification pending" or omit.

### R3. Stock photos presented as institute infrastructure
- **Conflict:** kumss.org ITI trade cards use Unsplash stock (URLs verified) — presented as the institute's own facilities.
- **Resolution:** Replace with real workshop photos (client's FB archive already has usable ones).

### R4. Political-family prominence
- **Risk:** President is a sitting Deputy CM; patron recently deceased. Marketing that leans on this, or misleads, carries multiplied optics/compliance risk.
- **Resolution:** Political context is **background only, never a marketing claim.** Never name family members in marketing copy. Respectful, measured tone in heritage content.

## ⚠️ DATA CONFLICTS

### C1. Trade count — 4 vs 7 vs 14
- **Versions:** kumss.org lists **4** (Electrician, Fitter, Electronics Mechanic, Interior Design) · iti.kumss.org lists **7** (adds Draughtsman Mechanical, Surveyor, Welder) · client stated **"एकूण १४ ट्रेड्स" (14 trades)** in a reel-script conversation (2026-07-14).
- **Why it matters:** Every trade page, admission creative, and SEO plan depends on this. Downstream docs assume 7; the client's "14" is new and unreconciled.
- **Resolution:** Confirm the real, currently-running trade list with the client **and** cross-check the NCVT MIS portal. Do not publish a trade count until reconciled. (Working scripts currently name Fitter/Electrician/Welder/Electronics.)

### C2. Trust name spelling
- **Versions:** "Krishi Udyog Mool Shikshan Sanstha" (kumss.org) vs "Krushi Udyog Mul Shikshan Sanstha" (iti.kumss.org contact) vs lowercase "krishi..." (GMB).
- **Resolution:** Lock one canonical English transliteration + one Devanagari rendering (कृषि उद्योग मूल शिक्षण संस्था); deploy across all surfaces; add to a do-not list.

### C3. Physical address descriptor
- **Versions:** "A/p Karhati, Tal-Baramati, Dist-Pune, 412204" (homepage) vs "6CXH+VQ5, Malwadi, Maharashtra 412204" (ITI footer) vs "...Karhati, Malwadi..." (GMB) vs Plus code "6CX7+2W" (GMB) / "6CXH+VQ5" (footer).
- **Resolution:** One physical campus, multiple descriptors. Standardise to one India-Post-format NAP + confirm the correct Plus code.

### C4. Logo lockup — two versions in use
- **Versions:** current "KUMSS" green-leaves lockup (website/Instagram/Maps — this is the canonical PNG in this handover) vs older "KUMSS — Bright future begins here" lockup (Facebook ITI page).
- **Resolution:** Confirm the current lockup as canonical; update the Facebook page. Keep the older one labelled SUPERSEDED (do not delete).

### C5. Sanskrit shloka — inconsistent rendering ⚠️ (subtle but important)
- **Versions:** The **logo image itself** renders "उध्दरेदात्मनात्मानम्"; the brand documents write "उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम्"; the Day-0 research §1.3 once transcribes it as "उद्भवदात्मनात्मानम्". The correct Bhagavad Gita 6.5 phrase is *uddhared ātmanā ātmānam* = **उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम्**.
- **Why it matters:** This is a scholarly credential on parent-facing surfaces; a literate viewer will notice an error. It also means the logo art may carry a typo.
- **Resolution:** Confirm the intended/correct Devanagari with the client, standardise one rendering across all materials, and check whether the logo art needs correction. Never AI-generate the shloka — typeset it (Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit) or pull from a corrected logo asset.

### C6. Instagram handle for the ITI
- **Versions:** Day-0 research found the ITI content under **@kumss_baramati** (197 followers); the SMO reports list a distinct **@kumss_iti** account (55 posts/13 reels).
- **Resolution:** Clarify which is the ITI's primary admissions account vs the trust's main account; align GMB/website/footer links accordingly.

### C7. Two live ITI websites
- **Versions:** kumss.org/institutes/... (current) vs iti.kumss.org (legacy, stale 19+ months, typos, repeated paragraphs).
- **Why it matters:** Splits SEO authority; confuses searching parents; the legacy site cites the older logo and different trades.
- **Resolution:** Consolidate — 301-redirect iti.kumss.org → the kumss.org ITI section (recommended), or pick one canonical and reduce the other.

### C8. Multiple GMB pins & Facebook pages
- **Versions:** 3 GMB entities (ITI 4.9★/104 · trust 0-reviews · likely Workshop pin) and 3 Facebook surfaces (KUMSS Private I.T.I / krishiudyogmulshikshansanstha / KUMSS.ITI).
- **Resolution:** Route the 4.9★ ITI GMB as primary admissions surface; consolidate/delist the duplicate Workshop pin; clarify the Facebook page hierarchy and the "Pritimohol" external-manager ownership.
