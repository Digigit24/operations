# KUMSS — Questions to Ask the Client

The exact questions to put to the client, in priority order. Grouped by conversation. Phrase sensitively — the trust is in a leadership-transition period, so open heritage/leadership topics with respect.

---

## Discovery Call 1 (highest priority — unblocks the most work)
1. **Scope:** Is this engagement for the ITI only, or the whole 7-institute trust?
2. **Trades:** Exactly which trades are running right now, and how many? (We have three different counts on record — 4 on the main site, 7 on the legacy site, and "14" mentioned recently.) Which ones actually admit students this cycle?
3. **Placement claim:** The website currently says "100% Placement Assistance Guaranteed." We can't use or amplify that — it's a compliance risk. Can we reword it to "Active Placement Cell" / "Placement Support Program"? (Founder-led decision.)
4. **NAP:** What is the single correct institute name (English + Marathi), address, and phone we should standardise everywhere?
5. **Logo:** Which logo is official — the current "KUMSS" lockup or the older "Bright future begins here" one on Facebook? Can you share vector files (SVG/AI/EPS)?
6. **Shloka:** Can you confirm the exact intended Sanskrit line? The logo, the brand docs, and one research note each show it slightly differently — we want to lock the correct rendering.
7. **Websites:** Can we consolidate the two ITI sites (redirect the old iti.kumss.org into the main site)?
8. **Vidya Pratishthan:** What's the relationship (if any), and how do you want KUMSS distinguished from it?

## Documents to request (email after Call 1)
9. DGT / NCVT / DVET registration certificates + ITI Code.
10. ISO 9001:2015 certificate (issuing body + validity).
11. India Book of Records documentation (certificate / official entry).
12. Complete faculty roster with qualifications and photos.
13. Existing photo/video library (workshops, faculty, students, certificates, events).
14. Fee structure per trade — and: are you comfortable publishing fees openly?

## Discovery Call 2 (placement officer + admissions coordinator)
15. Year-wise and trade-wise placement/apprenticeship data; recruiter names / MoUs / sample offer letters.
16. Alumni contacts for 2022/2023/2024 batches — and consent to tell their stories.
17. NAPS apprenticeship pipeline — how does it work here?

## Facilities & consent
18. Hostel: does it exist, what's the capacity, meal/safety/supervision setup? Can we shoot it? (It's referenced in scripts but wasn't confirmed in our audit.)
19. Do you have written consent on file for using students'/alumni names, photos, and stories?

## Access requests (🔐)
20. Owner access to both Google Business profiles (and confirm who manages them).
21. Google Search Console + Google Analytics access.
22. Facebook Business Manager / Page admin access — and who is "Pritimohol" (the external manager on the ITI Facebook page)?
23. Instagram Insights access for all 7 accounts + YouTube channel access.
24. Any Meta Ads / Google Ads accounts already running?

## Strategic / preferences
25. What's the primary business goal for the next 6 months (more inquiries? more admissions? specific trades? counsellor network? recruiter pipeline?)
26. Any design or content preferences, and any recurring revision requests we should know about up front?
27. Preferred approvals workflow — who signs off on content before it publishes, and how fast?
