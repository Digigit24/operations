- **Guardrails:** Calm, home-like reassurance; no urgency.

## Angle
Parent reassurance via facilities — hostel, safety, supervision, "all under one roof."

## Hook (0–3 sec)
Facilities-under-one-roof open — *"Workshops, classrooms, and hostel — all under one roof."*

## Voiceover script
1. (0–6s) [Under-one-roof open — workshops, classrooms, hostel together.]
2. (6–14s) "स्वच्छ, सुरक्षित वसतिगृह. नियमित देखरेख. जेवणाची आणि राहण्याची व्यवस्था." *(Clean, safe hostel. Regular supervision. Meals and stay arranged.)*
3. (14–22s) "शिक्षक फक्त वर्गात नाही — कॅम्पसवर सुद्धा मुलांसोबत असतात." *(Faculty aren't just present in class — they're there with students on campus too.)*
4. (22–30s) "अभ्यास, सराव, आणि विश्रांती — सगळ्यासाठी वेळ आणि जागा." *(Study, practice, and rest — time and space for all of it.)*
5. (30–38s) "घरापासून दूर, पण घरासारखं वातावरण." *(Away from home, but a home-like environment.)*

## Suggested B-roll
- Wide campus exterior, gate/signage, students walking in (morning)
- Hostel room (clean, simple, real — not staged luxury), dining hall, students eating together
- Faculty walking through campus talking with students informally; warden/staff presence
- Library or study room; students relaxing in common area/courtyard; evening campus shot
- Sunset/dusk wide of campus, lights on in hostel windows

## On-screen text
"एकाच कॅम्पसवर — शिक्षण, प्रशिक्षण, आणि निवास" / "सुरक्षित वसतिगृह" / "देखरेखीखाली विद्यार्थी" / "प्रवेश सुरू — २०२६-२७"

## CTA
"कॅम्पस प्रत्यक्ष बघा — भेट द्या." *(See the campus for yourself — visit us.)*

## Music mood
Soft, warm, homely acoustic; unhurried, reassuring.
