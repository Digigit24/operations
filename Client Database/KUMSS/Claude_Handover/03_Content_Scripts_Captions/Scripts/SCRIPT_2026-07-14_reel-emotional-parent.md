## Angle
Speaks to a parent's worry about their child's future — reassurance, not sales.

## Hook (0–3 sec)
- मराठी: **"तुमच्या मुलाला भविष्यात नोकरी हवी आहे, की खरं कौशल्य?"**
- English: *"Do you want a job for your child — or a skill that gets them one?"*
- *(Visual: mother/father looking at son doing homework, worried expression, evening light)*

## Voiceover script
1. (0–5s) "पालक म्हणून सगळ्यात मोठी काळजी असते — मुलाचं पुढे काय होणार?" *(As a parent, the biggest worry is always — what happens next for my child?)*
2. (5–14s) "KUMSS Private ITI, कारहाटी, बारामती — इथे मुलं फक्त पुस्तक वाचत नाहीत, प्रत्यक्ष कार्यशाळेत काम शिकतात." *(At KUMSS Private ITI, Karhati, Baramati — students don't just read books, they learn by working in real workshops.)*
3. (14–22s) "अनुभवी शिक्षक, सुरक्षित वसतिगृह, आणि रोजचं मार्गदर्शन — तुमचं मूल इथे एकटं नसतं." *(Experienced teachers, a safe hostel, and daily guidance — your child is never alone here.)*
4. (22–30s) "सात संस्थांच्या पिढ्यानपिढ्याच्या परंपरेतून उभी राहिलेली संस्था." *(An institution built on a trust's generations-long educational legacy — seven institutes, one mission.)*
5. (30–38s) "मुलाला कौशल्य द्या — कारण कौशल्य असेल, तर भविष्य स्वतःच्या हातात असतं." *(Give your child a skill — because with skill, the future is in their own hands.)*

## CTA
"अधिक माहितीसाठी संपर्क करा — KUMSS Private ITI, कारहाटी, बारामती." *(Contact us for details.)*

## Music mood
Warm, reassuring, unhurried acoustic. Not an ad beat.
