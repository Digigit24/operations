## 1. Who is the client (30-second version)

KUMSS is a Maharashtrian educational **trust (sanstha)** operating **7 institutes** in Karhati / Malwadi, Tal-Baramati, Dist-Pune. The trust's oldest institute dates to **1951**. The engagement focus is the trust's **youngest** institute — **KUMSS Private I.T.I Karhati** (an Industrial Training Institute, established **2020**).

- **CEO of trust + Principal of ITI:** Dr. Vikas S. Nirmal (one person, both roles).
- **Tagline (in logo):** "Bright future begins here."
- **Philosophical anchor (Sanskrit shloka in logo):** उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम् — *"Lift yourself by yourself"* (Bhagavad Gita 6.5).
- **Positioning in one line:** *An established Maharashtrian educational trust offering vocational training built on multi-generational educational service — where students learn real skills to stand on their own.*

## 2. What KUMSS offers

Vocational (ITI) trade training. **Trade count is an open conflict** (4 vs 7 vs a client-stated "14") — see `07_Conflicts_Pending/`. The four trades confirmed on both websites are **Electrician, Fitter, Electronics Mechanic, Interior Design**. Claimed affiliations (all **pending portal verification**): DGT, NCVT, DVET, ISO 9001:2015.

## 3. What brand system is active

**Heritage-led modernism.** Marathi-first (Devanagari treated as first-class, not an afterthought), real institute photography over stock/illustration, institutional palette of saffron + green + navy + cream. Voice is grounded, institutional, specific, calm, honest about limits. Full detail: `02_Brand_and_Design/`.

## 4. The single most important thing to know (the load-bearing risk)

⚠️ **"100% Placement Assistance Guaranteed"** is live on the ITI website. It is a potentially misleading education-marketing claim and the **#1 risk item in the account**. **Never reproduce or amplify it in any deliverable.** Getting it reworded (to "Active Placement Cell" / "Placement Support Program") is a priority client conversation. This rule already governs every content and design document in this handover.
