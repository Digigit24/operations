## Brand personality
Institutional · agrarian-industrial · Marathi-rooted · quietly modern. A 75-year educational *sanstha* upgrading its surfaces — **not** reinventing its identity. Should feel like a well-produced Maharashtra cooperative-bank brochure or a state agriculture-university annual report. Should **not** feel like a coaching-class banner, a SaaS landing page, an international school, or a luxury brand.

## Font system
- **Recommended (Pairing A — Heritage-led):** English headlines Source Serif Pro / EB Garamond; English body Inter / Source Sans; **Devanagari Mukta Vaani**; Sanskrit shloka **Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit**.
- Alternatives: Pairing B (Inter + Hind) modern-institutional; Pairing C (Source Sans + Yantramanav) sober-traditional/government.
- Devanagari rendered ~10% smaller than its English counterpart; line-height 1.6–1.8×; never all-caps Devanagari; max 2 type families per design.
- **Never** use Poppins/Montserrat/Comfortaa as English primary (too consumer), and never system-default Devanagari.

## Logo placement
- Three variants: **Full lockup** (all 5 elements — symbol + wordmark + tagline + shloka + TM) for official surfaces; **Compact** (symbol + wordmark) for social/signature; **Icon-only** (symbol mark) for favicon/profile pics.
- Clear space = height of the "K" in the wordmark. Min sizes: full 200px / 40mm, compact 120px, icon 48px.
- Default placement top-left (documents/web); centre for posters/certificates.
- Never recolour outside the palette, never replace the Devanagari shloka with English, never strip the shloka from official lockups, never add 3D/glow/heavy shadow, never rotate/skew/crop.

## Design style
Symmetric / near-symmetric compositions (asymmetry reads as marketing). Sober information density — text-rich is fine; airy minimalism is **not** the goal. Vertical hierarchy, generous breathing room around logo/headlines. 8pt grid (spacing in multiples of 8). Photography sits with weight — no floating ghost imagery.

## Image style
Documentary, not staged. Warm natural light, golden-hour bias. Real Marathi-medium rural/semi-urban students, faculty, and workshops — **not** coached urban/Western models. Real machinery, real tools, real environments. No cool studio lighting, no CGI/3D renders, no drone real-estate shots, no "study aesthetic" macros. **Stock trade photos are currently a live problem on the website — replace them with real workshop photos.**

## CTA style
Calm, direct, bilingual. Primary CTA = solid Kesari Saffron button, white text. Secondary = outlined navy on cream.
- Marathi: "अधिक माहितीसाठी संपर्क करा" / "प्रवेश घ्या" / "भेट द्या"
- English: "Contact for Details" / "Apply Now" / "Visit Campus"
- Never: "Hurry!", "Don't miss!", "Limited time!", "Last seats!", countdown timers.

## Preferred dimensions
IG square 1080×1080 · IG portrait 1080×1350 · IG story/reel cover 1080×1920 · FB cover 1640×859 · **GMB post 1200×900 (4:3)** · YouTube thumbnail 1280×720 · WhatsApp status 1080×1920.

## Important restrictions (the load-bearing "don'ts")
1. **Never** put "100% Placement" / "Guaranteed Placement" / any quantified placement claim in a design.
2. **Never** show DGT/NCVT/DVET/ISO badges until portal-verified.
3. **Never** lead with political-family visuals.
4. No motivational-quote posts, no urgency banners, no coaching-brand hype, no SaaS/luxury/international-school register, no cool corporate blues, no pastels/neons, no 3D-isometric or "futuristic skill-tech" imagery, no emoji/sparkle clutter.

## The 10-question check before shipping any AI image
Fits KUMSS DNA? · authentically Indian/Marathi subjects? · palette anchored in saffron+green+navy+cream? · warm natural light? · symmetric/formal? · Devanagari readable & correct (if any)? · clean space for text overlay (if needed)? · free of avoided registers? · free of false/misleading content? · would a Baramati parent feel *"this is a real institute for students like mine"*? — All 10 must be **yes**.
