## AI generation rules (from brand brain §7.5)

2. AI-generated images must be disclosed.
3. Never use AI for direct patient-instruction medical advice.
4. AI must follow the voice + visual systems strictly; default AI aesthetics forbidden.
5. AI cannot invent citations — every citation human-verified.
6. Never feed identifiable patient information to AI tools.
