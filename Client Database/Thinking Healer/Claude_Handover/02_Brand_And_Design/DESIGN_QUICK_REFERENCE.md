# DESIGN QUICK REFERENCE — THINKING HEALER
Read this before starting any design. Last updated: 2026-07-16

## Brand personality
Intelligent · warm · reflective · modern · editorial · medically credible. Final feeling: **"Intellectual medicine with human warmth."** Test for every image: *Could this appear in The Atlantic / Aeon?*

## Current active design system (CLIENT_APPROVED via design instructions doc)

**Primary colors:** Primary Blue #2E73B8 · Deep Medical Navy #1D2B4F
**Accents:** Healing Yellow #F2C230 · Warm Orange #F39B1D (selective use only)
**Background:** Soft Ivory #F7F5F0 · **Text:** Charcoal #2B2B2B

**Fonts — headlines:** Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Libre Baskerville
**Fonts — body:** Inter, DM Sans, Source Sans Pro

**CTA style (invitations, never commands):** Read More · Explore the Essay · Continue Reading · Reflect Further. No exclamation marks. No popups/exit-intent.

**Post dimensions supported:** IG Post 1080×1080 · IG Carousel · IG Story · LinkedIn Post · Website Banner · WhatsApp Creative (carousel covers often 1080×1350 per example prompt).

## Hard restrictions (Do-NOT rules)
- No loud gradients, drop shadows, glow
- No Canva healthcare templates, no generic hospital posters
- No fake/smiling stock doctors, no overused stethoscopes
- No spiritual-cliché visuals
- No stock photography; AI-generated imagery must be disclosed
- Max ~3 colors per composition + 1 accent (per brand-brain rules)
- Serif headlines, sans body; no emoji in editorial contexts
- **Banned language in any creative:** healing platform, heals by thinking, heal the body, human spirit, soul of medicine, mindful healing, wellness-style community, cure claims, fear marketing, "Buy Now / Limited Offer / Hurry Up"
