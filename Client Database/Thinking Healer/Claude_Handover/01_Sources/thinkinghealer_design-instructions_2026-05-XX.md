# Thinking Healer — Updated Design Instructions

## Brand Direction
Thinking Healer combines medicine, philosophy, neuroscience, and human-centered reflection.

The brand should feel:
- intelligent
- warm
- reflective
- modern
- editorial
- medically credible

Avoid generic hospital branding.

### Body

---

## Visual Language

Use:
- clean editorial layouts
- neuroscience-inspired diagrams
- subtle AI/medical systems
- modern medical publishing style
- calm structured compositions
- intelligent whitespace

Avoid:
- loud gradients
- Canva healthcare templates
- fake smiling doctors
- overused stethoscopes
- spiritual cliché visuals

---

## Layout Principles

- asymmetric editorial grids
- layered typography hierarchy
- clean spacing
- premium white backgrounds
- selective use of yellow/orange accents

---

## CTA Style

Preferred:
- Read More
- Explore the Essay
- Continue Reading
- Reflect Further

Avoid:
- Buy Now
- Limited Offer
- Hurry Up

---

## Social Media Style Types

### Photorealistic
Cinematic thoughtful medical imagery.

### Infographic
Clean editorial diagrams with structured hierarchy.

### Text Heavy
Essay-style premium educational slides.

### Carousel Covers
Strong philosophical headline + one dominant visual metaphor.

---

## Compliance Rules

- No cure claims
- No fear marketing
- No exaggerated medical promises
- Educational tone only

---

## Final Brand Feeling

“Intellectual medicine with human warmth.”
