## Why Thinking Healer was created

> Every physician thinks. Few write about it. Fewer still examine the thinking itself.

The project describes Thinking Healer as a space for examining philosophical questions generated by clinical practice from inside the consultation room—from the perspective of a physician who has experienced uncertainty, explained diagnoses, and observed the limits of protocol.

It states that Thinking Healer is not a clinic and not a health-tips platform. It is positioned as a knowledge platform and the canonical home of “Medisophy,” a term described as coined by Dr. Shinde for the philosophy of medicine as practised at the bedside.
