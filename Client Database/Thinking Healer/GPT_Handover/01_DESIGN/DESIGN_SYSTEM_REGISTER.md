# Design System Register

## Client-approved rules

None found.

## Unverified current direction

- Warm cream/parchment background.
- Dark teal/navy typography and footer.
- Editorial serif title.
- Medical-philosophy symbolism: anatomical heart, brain, book, stethoscope, clinical diagrams.
- Minimal copy and no doctor photograph in later drafts.
- Small circular `TH` monogram at top.

## Older / legacy direction

- Doctor-led portrait poster.
- Dense professional background and interest sections.
- Multiple icon-led information blocks.

## Common revision requests found

- Remove the doctor photo and replace it with a more conceptual image.
- Reduce text as much as possible.
- Add an extraordinary quote.

## Do not rules

- Do not treat generated monograms as the official logo.
- Do not fabricate or retouch a real doctor identity using an AI likeness.
- Do not use bright, arrogant, or sales-heavy healthcare styling.
- Do not overload the poster with biography copy.
- Do not make treatment, cure, outcome, or superiority claims.
