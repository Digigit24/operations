# AI Image Guidelines

## Positive direction

Create a premium editorial Instagram portrait for a physician-led philosophy-of-medicine knowledge platform. Use a warm cream parchment canvas, deep teal/navy typography, subtle anatomical line drawings, an open book, a realistic anatomical heart or brain model, and a stethoscope. Keep the layout quiet, scholarly, balanced, and text-light. Leave clean space for a verified logo and final copy.

## Negative prompt

No hospital advertisement, no clinic reception, no treatment CTA, no smiling stock doctor, no fake patient, no surgery scene, no bright neon colours, no glossy pharmaceutical style, no clutter, no long paragraphs, no illegible text, no invented logo, no unsupported credentials, no cure claims, no before-and-after imagery.

## Production warning

AI-generated text and identities must be replaced with verified production assets. Generated creatives remain drafts until client approval is documented.
