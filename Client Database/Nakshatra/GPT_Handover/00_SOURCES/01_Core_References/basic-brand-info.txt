NAKSHATRA CLINIC — BRAND DESIGN KNOWLEDGEBASE

Brand:
Nakshatra Clinic is a fertility, IVF, ICSI, IUI, PCOD/PCOS, women’s health, and reproductive medicine clinic. The design language should feel caring, modern, trustworthy, medical, premium, and emotionally reassuring.

Primary Visual Goal:
Every creative should be a scroll-stopper. It should immediately communicate hope, clarity, treatment support, and medical trust. Avoid boring hospital-style posters. Designs must feel premium, photorealistic, clean, and social-media ready.

Core Brand Colours:
Use Nakshatra’s soft fertility-care palette.

Primary colours:
- Deep navy blue: for strong headings, trust, medical credibility
- Magenta / hot pink: for highlights, offers, CTA buttons, important words
- Soft pink: for backgrounds and feminine warmth
- Blush pink: for soft gradients and emotional tone
- Lavender / light purple: for fertility, calmness, and premium feel
- White / off-white: for clean medical spacing and readability

Accent colours:
- Purple-magenta gradient
- Baby pink glow
- Soft cream or pearl white
- Very light grey for clean sections

Avoid:
- Harsh red unless medically necessary
- Too many random colours
- Neon green, orange, or cheap-looking gradients
- Dark, depressing medical visuals
- Overcrowded layouts

Design Style:
The creative should look like it was designed by a senior healthcare brand designer.

Preferred style:
- Photorealistic healthcare visuals
- Premium fertility clinic aesthetic
- Modern Instagram medical campaign style
- Clean infographic sections
- Soft glowing backgrounds
- Smooth pink/lavender gradients
- Realistic human emotion
- Modern typography
- Strong headline hierarchy
- Balanced spacing
- Clear CTA
- Professional doctor/clinic credibility

Typography Direction:
Use modern, bold, clean fonts.

Font feel:
- Bold sans-serif for headlines
- Clean rounded sans-serif for body text
- Elegant script font only for emotional/event creatives, not medical information posts
- Use large, readable headlines
- Keep body text short and structured

Headline style:
- Big, bold, high contrast
- Navy or magenta
- Clear hook in 3–7 words
- Use curiosity where possible

Examples:
- “Could It Be PCOD?”
- “Planning for IVF?”
- “Why Pregnancy Isn’t Happening?”
- “Your Fertility Journey Starts Here”
- “Limited Time IUI Package”

Layout Rules:
Create strong visual hierarchy.

Recommended structure:
1. Logo at top, clean and visible
2. Big hook headline
3. Main photorealistic visual or medical illustration
4. Supporting information in clean cards or sections
5. Offer / package / consultation details if needed
6. CTA strip at bottom with phone, website, or visit message

Spacing:
- Keep enough white space
- Do not fill every corner
- Use clean margins
- Avoid tiny unreadable text
- Important text should be visible even on mobile

Graphic Elements:
Use elegant healthcare graphics.

Good elements:
- Soft rounded cards
- Thin medical line icons
- Pink highlight pills
- Clean bullet boxes
- Subtle glow effects
- Abstract fertility shapes
- Soft bokeh or light particles
- Medical diagrams where needed
- Realistic 3D uterus/ovary illustrations for PCOD/PCOS topics
- Realistic pregnancy/family/doctor visuals for emotional or package posts

Avoid:
- Cheap stock photo collages
- Random icons everywhere
- Overly dramatic sad faces
- Too many boxes
- Low-quality medical diagrams
- Heavy shadows
- Cluttered Hindi poster style unless specifically requested

Image Direction:
Prefer photorealistic, emotionally warm visuals.

Use:
- Happy couple expecting baby
- Caring doctor consultation
- Confident woman in healthcare setting
- Fertility lab / IVF lab inspired visuals
- Soft baby imagery only when tasteful
- Clean medical product/dashboard visuals
- Realistic uterus/ovary medical renders
- Warm clinic environment
- Hopeful expressions

Avoid:
- Overly artificial AI faces
- Distorted hands
- Unrealistic baby visuals
- Too much sadness or fear
- Graphic or uncomfortable medical imagery

Content Tone:
The tone should be supportive, clear, and reassuring.

Use simple language.
Make people feel they can take the next step without fear.

Good tone:
- Hopeful
- Educational
- Sensitive
- Trust-building
- Action-oriented
- Non-judgmental

Avoid:
- Fear-mongering
- Shame-based messaging
- Overpromising results
- Too much medical jargon
- Claims like “guaranteed pregnancy”

Creative Categories:

1. Offer / Package Creatives:
Should feel premium, urgent, and conversion-focused.

Must include:
- Large offer name
- Package price
- What is included
- Validity date
- CTA
- Phone number
- Website
- Clinic logo

Design direction:
White/pink dominant, bold magenta offer text, navy trust text, rounded offer cards, clear bottom CTA bar.

2. Awareness / Education Creatives:
Should feel clean, medical, and easy to understand.

Must include:
- Strong question-based headline
- Medical visual or diagram
- 3–5 simple symptoms or facts
- CTA to consult
- Clinic branding

Design direction:
Use infographic style with soft pink/lavender background, navy heading, magenta highlights.

3. Women’s Day / Occasion Creatives:
Should feel elegant, soft, and emotional.

Design direction:
Use white/pink background, elegant typography, feminine silhouettes, minimal text, premium spacing.

4. Hindi / Hinglish Fertility Creatives:
Should be bold and relatable.

Design direction:
Use stronger magenta backgrounds, big Hindi hook, emotional human visual, clean CTA. Keep it dramatic but not cheap.

5. Doctor / Consultation Creatives:
Should build authority.

Must include:
- Doctor name
- Qualification
- Consultation timing
- Offer/free consultation if applicable
- Phone number and location

Design direction:
Professional, clean, trust-heavy. Use doctor photo if available. Use navy for credibility and magenta for CTA.

Brand Composition Formula:
Use this formula for most Nakshatra creatives:

Soft pink/lavender/white background
+ bold navy headline
+ magenta highlight word or CTA
+ photorealistic healthcare/fertility visual
+ clean information cards
+ bottom CTA strip
+ Nakshatra logo placed cleanly

Creative Quality Standards:
Every design must feel:
- Premium
- Modern
- Medical
- Emotional but not melodramatic
- Clean
- Readable
- Social-media optimized
- Brand-consistent
- Conversion-focused

Do not create basic Canva-style posters.
Do not overcrowd with text.
Do not use random colour palettes.
Do not make the design look like a generic hospital ad.
Do not use low-quality clipart.

Preferred Aspect Ratios:
- Instagram post: 1080x1080
- Instagram portrait post: 1080x1350
- Instagram story/reel cover: 1080x1920
- Clinic campaign poster: 4:5 or 9:16 depending on use

Default Prompt Style for New Creatives:
Whenever creating a Nakshatra Clinic design, use this style:

“Create a premium photorealistic healthcare social media creative for Nakshatra Clinic. Use a soft fertility-clinic colour palette with blush pink, magenta, lavender, white, and deep navy. The design should feel modern, clean, emotionally warm, and medically trustworthy. Use bold modern typography, strong spacing, clean CTA sections, and scroll-stopping composition. Avoid clutter and cheap poster style. Make it look like a premium fertility clinic campaign designed by a senior creative director.”
