# Nakshatra Brand Quick Reference

## Working visual direction

Use the newer editorial system as the default working direction:

- Deep navy: `#0D1F3C`
- Gold: `#C9A84C`
- Gold light: `#E8C97A`
- Teal CTA: `#1B8F7A`
- Warm cream: `#FAF7F2`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted grey: `#8A9BB0`

## Typography

- Headlines and emotional lines: Cormorant Garamond
- Body, buttons and labels: DM Sans

## Logo rule

- Keep the original gradient symbol and navy wordmark unchanged.
- Do not stretch, recolour, redraw or add shadows.
- Use small, controlled placement with clear space.

## Design rule

One strong visual idea per creative. Navy should dominate. Gold highlights the key insight or number. Teal is for action only.

## Conflict warning

Older source material uses pink, lavender and magenta-heavy layouts. Keep that material as historical reference unless the client explicitly requests that style. Do not combine the old and new palettes in one design.
