## D. The eight brand hook patterns · Source: Master Brand Brain §4.9 · Status: reference
1. The Number — "AMH: 0.3. Here's what your previous clinic didn't tell you."
2. The Reframe — "Your previous IVF didn't fail. Your previous protocol did."
3. The Question — "क्या आपको पता है thin lining क्या है?"
4. The Contradiction — "Sperm count was normal. Pregnancy still wasn't happening."
5. The Skipped Step — "Your last clinic checked the embryo. Did they check the lining?"
6. The Diagnosis — "80% of recurrent miscarriages have a cause that one common test misses."
7. The Honest Price — "IVF at Nakshatra: ₹1.20L. Stated openly. No hidden costs."
8. The Permission — "It's okay to ask for a second opinion. That's why our first consults are free."

## E. Headline structures · Source: Master Brand Brain §4.12 · Status: reference
Rules: 8–9 words max, work standalone, no exclamation marks, no stacked questions, no "Discover/Unlock/Transform".
- Specific Reveal: "AMH 0.3. Not the end of natural conception."
- Two-Line: "The embryo was healthy. / The lining wasn't ready."
- Skipped-Step: "Your last clinic checked the embryo. Did they check the lining?"
- Permission: "A second opinion is okay. That's why our first consult is free."
- Number Stack: "15+ years. 2,000+ pregnancies. 70% IVF success."
