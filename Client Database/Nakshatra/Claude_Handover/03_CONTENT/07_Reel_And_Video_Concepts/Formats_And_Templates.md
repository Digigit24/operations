# REEL & VIDEO CONCEPTS — Formats & Templates
*Source: Content Performance Analysis. Status: DRAFT / recommended (not yet produced).*

## Five rotating formats (keep the storytelling DNA, vary the visuals)
1. **Case Study Classic** — text overlays + clinic shots (current format; for complex conditions).
2. **Direct Address** — doctor speaks to camera, minimal text (trust, myth-busting).
3. **Quick Tip Series** — 15-sec rapid-fire tips with on-screen text (saves & shares).
4. **Q&A Reaction** — doctor reacts to DM questions/comments (comments & conversation).
5. **Behind-the-Scenes** — day-in-the-life, lab tour, embryology footage (trust, profile visits).

## Three new templates to introduce immediately (visual reset)
- **Template A — Doctor-to-camera direct address** (no overlay boxes; eye contact builds trust).
- **Template B — Split-screen before/after** (reports/symptoms | healthy outcome; highly shareable).
- **Template C — Ultrasound/lab footage + voiceover-style text** (shifts attention to procedures).

## Also test
- Long-form 60–90 sec reels (1–2/week) for deeper watch time.
- YouTube Shorts + long-form; Facebook Reels; WhatsApp Channel; Marathi/Pune-local; ~30% English for NRI reach.

## The 7 winning patterns to preserve (content moats)
1. Misdiagnosis-revelation story arc · 2. Naming specific lesser-known tests (APS, NK cells, DFI, Asherman's, thrombophilia) · 3. Numbers + age in first 2 seconds · 4. Hinglish code-mixing · 5. Share-driving CTA ("अपने partner के साथ share करो") · 6. Consistent visual signature (now a liability — vary it) · 7. "Hope after failure" emotional promise.
