# 🎯 CREATIVE GENERATION BRIEF
**Campaign: Low AMH — Natural Conception Focus**
**Clinic: Nakshatra IVF & Fertility Clinic, Baner, Pune**

---

## 🧠 OBJECTIVE

Generate high-converting Meta Ad creatives (Facebook & Instagram) focused on:
- Dismantling the fear response triggered by a low AMH number
- Positioning natural conception as a legitimate, evidence-backed first conversation — not a consolation option
- Driving free online consultation bookings (pan-India reach)
- Establishing Dr. Ramit and Nakshatra as the honest specialists who look beyond a single blood test

---

## 🎯 TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary — Women who've received a low AMH result**
Age: 28–40
Location: Pan-India (online consultation) / Primary — Pune West (Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Aundh)
Life Stage: Just received a low AMH report (AMH ≤ 1.0); was immediately told to consider IVF; hasn't committed to a clinic yet
Mindset: Scared. Googling at midnight. Feeling like time is running out. Not sure if the IVF recommendation was premature.

**Secondary — Women actively researching low AMH / natural conception**
Age: 28–38
Life Stage: In research mode; comparing natural approaches vs. IVF; wants a complete picture before making any decision
Mindset: Analytical. Wants evidence, not platitudes.

**Tertiary — Women who've been told "there's nothing to do" about low AMH**
Age: 30–42
Life Stage: Resigned or frustrated; received a low AMH diagnosis + IVF recommendation and nothing else
Mindset: "Is IVF really my only option?" — a question they haven't found a real answer to yet.

---

## 💡 CORE HOOK (IMPORTANT)

This is NOT an IVF ad.
This is NOT "don't worry, it'll be fine."
This is:
👉 *"One number on a blood test is not your complete fertility picture — and someone finally has the full conversation with you."*

The reframe: AMH measures how many eggs remain. It says nothing about egg quality, hormone balance, thyroid function, nutritional status, or your actual chances of natural conception. You were given a number. You were never given the context.

---

## 🔥 KEY VALUE PROPOSITION

Women get:
- A clinically honest reframing of what AMH actually measures — and what it doesn't
- Evidence-based grounding: 2019 JAMA study (no significant difference in natural pregnancy rates, low vs normal AMH, under 40)
- A full hormone panel review before any treatment recommendation
- Specific, actionable variables they *can* improve: Vitamin D, thyroid, egg quality, nutritional deficiencies, ovulation timing
- Online consultation — nationwide, accessible, ₹100 booking (low-barrier entry)
- A doctor who looks at the complete picture: Dr. Ramit, 12+ years reproductive medicine, trained in UK, Dubai, USA

---

## ⚠️ PAIN POINTS TO DRIVE EACH ANGLE

**"The Number Lie" Angle pain point:**
"I saw 0.3 on my AMH report and my doctor said IVF. That was the whole conversation."

**Empathy / Midnight Searcher Angle pain point:**
"I found out about my AMH result at 6pm and by midnight I'd convinced myself I'd never have a baby."

**Control Angle pain point:**
"Every article I read says 'low AMH can't be fixed.' But nobody is telling me what I *can* control."

**Trust / Social Proof Angle pain point:**
"How do I know any clinic is telling me the truth about my real options? They all want me to start treatment."

## 🎨 DESIGN STYLE

Clinically credible but never cold. Emotionally attuned without being sentimental.
Think: the design of a really good specialist's website — not a hospital, not a fertility brand selling hope with pastel colours.
Data-forward where the angle demands it (Concepts 1, 3, 4, 5). Intimately human where the angle demands it (Concept 2).
The AMH number "0.3" or "0.1" should appear large and legible in the concepts that use it — it's the scroll-stopping element. The number they recognise. The number that scared them. We take it back.

---

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy Deep | #0D1F3C | All primary backgrounds |
| Gold | #C9A84C | AMH numbers, key reframe lines, stat callouts |
| Gold Light | #E8C97A | Secondary text on dark backgrounds |
| Teal | #1B8F7A | CTA button / checklist ticks / "solution" column |
| Warm Cream | #FAF7F2 | Concept 2 (empathy) — light mode treatment |
| White | #FFFFFF | Body copy on dark bg |
| Muted Navy-Grey | #8A9BB0 | "What most clinics checked" column — never alarming |

**Hard rules:**
- Gold owns the AMH number — it transforms the number from alarm to subject of honest inquiry
- No red anywhere — even in comparison tables. Muted grey for the "incomplete" column.
- Teal = forward direction only. Checklist ticks, CTAs, solution-side of comparisons.
- Concept 2 is the only concept permitted to use a light (cream) background.

## 📝 TEXT RULES

**Headline:** Max 9 words. Must work without the body copy. It's the only thing people in a scroll read.
**The AMH number** must appear in a prominent position in Concepts 1, 2, and 5 — it's the recognition trigger.
**JAMA study reference:** "2019 JAMA study — no significant difference in natural pregnancy rates, under 40." Keep the attribution clean and brief. Don't overstate the scope.
**CTA options by concept:**
- Concepts 1, 3, 4: "Free Online Consult" (rational, low-friction)
- Concept 2: "Book A Real Conversation" (warmer, matches tone)
- Concept 5: "See Your Full Picture" (curiosity-driven)
**Never use:** "Book Now" (transactional), "Act Fast" (urgency), "Limited Slots" on this campaign — wrong emotional register.

## 🚫 DO NOT INCLUDE

- "Don't worry" or "everything will be fine" — dismissive of real clinical concern
- Baby photos in cold-audience or freshly-diagnosed targeting — wrong moment
- Guaranteed natural conception language — medically inaccurate
- "Your biological clock" — specifically forbidden per brand guidelines
- Fear-based urgency: "if you wait too long" — the opposite of this campaign's entire message
- Over-citing the JAMA study — one clear reference is powerful; repeating it reads as defensive
- Aggressive competitor comparisons — the comparison table (Concept 4) contrasts *protocols*, not clinics
- Long paragraphs in visuals — max 3 lines of body text on any single graphic
- Pastel / floral / soft fertility aesthetics — fundamentally misreads this audience's emotional state
