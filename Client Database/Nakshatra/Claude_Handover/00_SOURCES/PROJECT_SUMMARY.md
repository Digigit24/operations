# PROJECT SUMMARY — Nakshatra IVF & Fertility Clinic

*A complete summary of the account as represented in the project knowledge base, compiled 16 July 2026.*

---

## 1. What Nakshatra is

A boutique, doctor-led IVF and women's-health clinic in Baner, Pune, founded 2010, led by Dr. Ramit Kamate. Positioned deliberately as the **specialist alternative to the IVF chain** — quieter, more specific, more honest, more present than Indira / Nova / Iswarya / Progenesis / Femcare. The strategic wedge is: the same accountable doctor every visit, real numbers instead of superlatives, both partners treated equally, failed cycles investigated rather than absorbed, and boutique access with chain-adjacent quality.

## 2. The state of the project

The project is a **strategy-and-foundations stage account.** What exists is a very well-developed *thinking layer* — brand strategy, voice, visual system, image-prompting system, website audit, competitor/audience research, a social-performance analysis, and three campaign briefs. What does **not** exist yet is the *production and performance layer* — finished creatives, published assets, approvals, patient-consent records, and any live analytics (SEO, GMB, Meta/Google Ads, GA4, GSC).

Two SEO/GMB "foundations" (referred to in the docs as Prompt 04 and Prompt 05) are explicitly **gated and undelivered** — blocked pending verification of Windsor, GA4, GSC and Google Business Profile access.

## 4. The proof numbers (all client-stated, unverified)

70% IVF success · ₹1.20L IVF starting price · 2,000+ pregnancies since 2010 · 4.9★ Google (150+ reviews) · 12+ **or** 15+ years experience (conflict) · Free/₹100 first consultation · Mon–Sun 10–7, 24/7 emergencies. Age-banded success: <35 → 70%; 35–40 → 55–60%; >40 → 40–50%.

## 5. Audience

Research-aware but emotionally vulnerable couples, 28–42, in Pune West (Baner / Aundh / Wakad / Hinjewadi / Pashan / Ravet), largely IT-professional, often after a failed cycle elsewhere. Three ICPs: focused first-time parents; experienced mother-to-be (36–40, low AMH); and the concerned husband (male-factor, badly under-served across the category — the biggest content opportunity). Native register is **Hinglish** (Hindi/Marathi emotional scaffolding + English medical terms).

## 6. What the audience data actually showed (from the performance analysis)

Six real published reels were analysed. Best performers: **APS / blood-clotting reel (682K views, 0.48% share rate)** and **DNA-fragmentation / male-factor reel (270K, 0.52% — the highest share rate)**. The winning formula: a misdiagnosis-revelation story arc, naming specific lesser-known tests (APS, NK cells, DFI, Asherman's, thrombophilia), age + numbers in the first two seconds, Hinglish, a share-driving CTA, and "hope after failure." The account then **plateaued** (down to 10–17K/reel) from format saturation. Biggest untapped opportunity: **male fertility.** The report supplies 40+ fresh content ideas and a 30-day recovery plan.
