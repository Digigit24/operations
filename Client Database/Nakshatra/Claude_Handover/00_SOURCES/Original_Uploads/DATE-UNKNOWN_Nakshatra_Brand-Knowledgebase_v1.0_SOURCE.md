## 4. Brand Voice & Tone

### The Big Idea

Nakshatra speaks like a trusted expert who also happens to be a warm, empathetic friend. Think of the tone as sitting between a knowledgeable doctor and a supportive elder sibling. Never cold. Never preachy. Never fear-mongering.

The brand is dealing with one of the most emotionally vulnerable spaces a couple can be in. Every word carries weight. Copy must be written with that in mind.

### Tone Pillars

**Warm & Hopeful**
The brand leans into hope, not fear. Even when talking about difficult topics (low AMH, failed cycles, male infertility), the framing is always "there is a way forward." Use language that opens doors, not closes them.

**Expert & Credible**
Data-backed. Dr. Ramit's credentials, the clinic's track record, and clinical specifics give the brand authority. The tone should feel informed and confident — but never snobbish. Expertise is a tool for reassurance, not for intimidation.

**Compassionate & Human**
Use "we" and "together" naturally. Acknowledge the emotional journey. Celebrate outcomes loudly (with permission). Speak to both partners in a couple — fertility is not just a woman's concern.

**Transparent & Direct**
State prices. State risks. State success rates by age. This radical honesty is actually a massive differentiator in a sector full of vague promises. Lean into it.

**Locally Rooted**
Pune West identity is strong — Baner, Hinjewadi IT corridor, Wakad. Content should feel like it's from someone who understands life in this part of the city. Occasional Marathi or Hindi resonates well on local platforms.

### Platform Tone Calibration

| Platform | Tone Setting |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Warm, celebratory, visual-first. Short captions. Stories and Reels for education. |
| Facebook | Slightly longer, community-feel. Good for awareness posts, Marathi content, shares. |
| Google Ads | Direct, intent-led. Lead with stats and CTA. Short and punchy. |
| YouTube | Educational, calm, authoritative. Dr. Ramit's voice. Long-form works here. |
| WhatsApp | Conversational, personal. This is a trust-builder channel, not a broadcast. |
| Website Copy | Balanced — professional but warm. SEO-conscious. Informative. |

### Voice: Do's

- "Your journey to parenthood starts here"
- "2,000+ families have found hope with us"
- "We walk with you every step of the way"
- "Transparent pricing — IVF from ₹1,20,000"
- "Free first consultation, no obligations"
- "70% IVF success rate — above national average"
- "Real couples, real results" (for testimonial content)
- "It's okay to ask for help" (destigmatising)

### Voice: Don'ts

- Never use "case," "procedure subject," or other dehumanizing clinical language
- Never use fear-based urgency: "If you wait, it'll be too late" — not acceptable
- Never promise 100% or guaranteed results
- Never ignore male infertility in copy — 40%+ of infertility has a male factor
- Never use stigmatizing language around sexuality, infertility, or MTP services
- Never be vague about pricing when data is available
- Never write only in English — the audience skews Marathi/Hindi bilingual

## 5. Brand Colours

> **Important for PM:** The palette below has been inferred from the clinic's website visual identity. Confirm exact hex codes with the client or request official brand assets before locking production materials.

### Primary Palette

**Navy Deep — #0D1F3C**
The brand's foundation. Used for backgrounds, headers, and anywhere deep trust and authority need to be communicated. The visual anchor of the brand.

**Gold — #C9A84C**
The emotional accent. Signals warmth, hope, and premium quality. Used for highlights, key numbers, pull-quotes, and accent elements. Never overuse — it works because it's selective.

**Gold Light — #E8C97A**
A softer version of gold for text on dark backgrounds, gradient softening, and secondary accents.

### Secondary Palette

**Teal — #1B8F7A**
The action colour. Used for CTAs, links, buttons, and interactive elements. Signals health, growth, and forward momentum. Never use for passive/decorative elements — reserve it for things that need to be clicked or noticed.

**Warm Cream — #FAF7F2**
The content canvas. Background for light-mode layouts. Warmer than pure white — feels welcoming rather than clinical.

**Navy Mid — #1E3D6E**
Mid-weight navy for card backgrounds, secondary sections, and layering.

### Colour Usage Rules

- Navy + Gold = premium trust. Use for hero sections, key CTAs, and brand headers.
- Teal = action only. Don't dilute it with decorative use.
- Never use saturated reds or oranges as brand colours — they trigger urgency and anxiety in this sensitive category, which works against the brand's emotional positioning.
- For social media graphics: dark backgrounds (navy) with gold accents perform better for this category than light / pastel palettes.
- White space and cream backgrounds keep things from feeling overcrowded on educational content.

## 6. Typography

> **Note:** These are recommended typefaces aligned with the brand's premium-but-warm positioning. Confirm if the client has specific font preferences or licences in use.

### Display Font — Cormorant Garamond (Serif)

Used for: Hero headings, section titles, testimonial pull-quotes, doctor's name, emotional statements.

Cormorant Garamond is an elegant, slightly literary serif that communicates warmth and aspiration — the right emotional register for a fertility clinic. The italic variant is particularly powerful for heartfelt phrases and taglines.

- Weights in use: Regular (400), Medium (500), Italic (400i)
- Fallback: Georgia, serif

### Body Font — DM Sans

Used for: Body text, captions, labels, buttons, data, UI elements, social copy.

DM Sans is clean and modern without being cold. It reads beautifully at small sizes and is accessible on both mobile and desktop. A strong workhorse font for a clinic dealing with detailed medical information.

- Weights in use: Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500)
- Fallback: -apple-system, sans-serif

### Type Scale Reference

| Element | Size | Weight | Font |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero Headline | 48–60px | 500 | Cormorant Garamond |
| Section Title | 32–40px | 500 | Cormorant Garamond |
| Sub-heading | 20–24px | 500 | DM Sans |
| Body Copy | 15–16px | 400 | DM Sans |
| Caption / Meta | 12–13px | 400 | DM Sans |
| Button Text | 13–14px | 500 | DM Sans |

### Typography Tone Notes

Serif italic = emotional, aspirational headlines. "Your dream. Our life's work."
Sans medium = action and clarity. "Book your free consultation today."
Never mix fonts outside this system. Consistency is credibility.

## 7. Services Portfolio

### Flagship Service

**IVF — In Vitro Fertilization**
The core offering. Full IVF cycle: ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, lab fertilization, embryo culture, and transfer. Starting from ₹1,20,000. Success rate: 70% (above national average). All variants available — Standard IVF, Natural Cycle IVF, Donor Egg IVF.

---

### Service Categories

#### A. Fertility Treatments

**IUI — Intrauterine Insemination**
Simpler, more affordable first-line fertility treatment. Washed sperm placed directly into the uterus at the time of ovulation. Good for mild male factor, unexplained infertility, and as a stepping stone before IVF.

**ICSI — Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection**
Single sperm injected directly into a mature egg. Recommended for moderate-to-severe male infertility. Often used alongside IVF when standard fertilization may fail.

**Ovulation Induction**
Hormonal stimulation to trigger egg release in women with irregular ovulation (commonly PCOS patients). Can be done standalone or as part of IUI/IVF.

**IVF Specialties — Advanced Options**
- Blastocyst Formation (Day 5 embryo culture)
- Laser-Assisted Hatching
- Micro TESE (for non-obstructive azoospermia)
- TESA & Sperm Retrieval
- Ovum / Egg Donation
- Embryo Freezing & Vitrification (Cryopreservation)

#### B. Female Infertility

**Conditions treated:**
- Low AMH (Anti-Müllerian Hormone)
- PCOS / PCOD (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome/Disease)
- Repeated Implantation Failure
- Blocked Fallopian Tubes

#### C. Advanced IVF Diagnostics & Testing

#### D. Male Infertility

#### E. Obstetrics & Gynecology

## 9. Target Audience

### Primary Audience — Couples Struggling with Infertility

**Profile:** Married or partnered couples, aged 25–42, based in Baner / Hinjewadi / Wakad / Aundh / Pashan / Ravet. Often dual-income (IT professionals are a significant segment). Have been trying to conceive for 6+ months to several years. May have had failed treatment elsewhere. Emotionally cautious and research-heavy before choosing a clinic.

**Pain Points:**
- Fear of judgment or being dismissed
- Confusing medical jargon and unclear costs
- Failed treatment history — trust is broken and fragile
- Anxiety about whether IVF will work "at their age"
- Stigma around male infertility (partners reluctant to seek help)

**What they're searching for:**
"Best IVF clinic in Baner," "IVF cost in Pune," "IVF success rate by age," "ICSI treatment Pune," "low AMH treatment Pune," "male infertility doctor Pune"

**How to reach them:** Google Search Ads, Instagram, Facebook Groups (fertility communities), YouTube education content, local SEO.

### Secondary Audience — Women Seeking Gynecology & Obstetric Care

**Profile:** Women 20–45, Pune West area. May not be struggling with infertility — seeking routine care, pregnancy management, PCOS treatment, or OB/GYN services. Often referred by word of mouth or found via Google.

**What they're searching for:**
"Best gynecologist in Baner," "PCOS treatment Pune," "pregnancy care Baner," "Hysteroscopy Pune"

**How to reach them:** Local SEO, Instagram health content, Facebook, Google My Business.

---

### Tertiary Audience — Professionals Seeking Fertility Preservation

**Profile:** Working professionals, 28–38 years. IT/corporate workforce (Hinjewadi corridor). Not currently trying to conceive but thinking ahead. Egg freezing, sperm freezing enquiries.

**How to reach them:** LinkedIn (awareness), Instagram, targeted Meta ads. Content angle: "Preserve your options. Plan on your terms."

---

### Audience Sensitivities to Keep in Mind

The infertility audience is emotionally vulnerable. Marketing that feels exploitative, rushed, or transactional will backfire badly. The community is vocal on online reviews and health forums. Every interaction — ad, post, WhatsApp reply — shapes trust.

Male infertility content must be handled with extra care. Indian men rarely self-identify as having a fertility problem. Normalizing male fertility testing without shame is both a social responsibility and a business opportunity for Nakshatra.

MTP and sexual health services audiences require absolute discretion. No identifiable testimonials. No exploitative framing. Confidentiality messaging must be upfront.

Open Monday through Sunday, 10 AM to 7 PM. Many smaller clinics are closed weekends. This is a practical differentiator for working IT professionals in the Hinjewadi-Baner belt.

## 14. Things to Never Do

This section is mandatory reading for everyone creating content for this account.

**Never make absolute guarantees.** Do not write "guaranteed pregnancy" or "100% success." It is medically inaccurate, legally problematic, and destroys trust when reality differs.

**Never use shame or urgency as a lever.** "Your biological clock is running out" or "If you don't act now, it'll be too late" — never. This category needs hope, not panic.

**Never treat male infertility as invisible.** If every ad and post only shows women or speaks only to women, the brand is leaving a significant patient segment out and reinforcing unhelpful stigma.

**Never publish identifiable patient content without explicit written consent.** Medical privacy is paramount. Patient images, names, stories, and outcomes require documented consent every single time.

**Never run MTP or sexual health services as promotional content.** These are available services but require careful, discrete handling. If mentioned, the emphasis is always on confidentiality, safety, and non-judgment — not conversion.

**Never ignore negative Google reviews.** Respond promptly, professionally, and empathetically to every negative review. How a clinic responds to criticism is itself a trust signal to prospective patients reading reviews.

**Never create content that looks like it was made for a completely different category** — bright orange sales graphics, flash-sale countdown timers, "limited time offer" copy. Fertility care is not retail. Urgency tactics clash badly with this audience's emotional state.

**Never go silent.** In the fertility category, silence is anxiety. Patients who message on Instagram, WhatsApp, or the website need fast responses. If the client's team cannot respond within a few hours, this needs to be escalated as a business ops issue, not just a marketing one.
