# 🎯 CREATIVE GENERATION BRIEF
**Campaign: 2-Cycle IVF Package**
**Clinic: Nakshatra IVF & Fertility Clinic, Baner, Pune**

---

## 🧠 OBJECTIVE

Generate high-converting Meta Ad creatives (Facebook & Instagram) focused on:
- Making the 2-Cycle IVF package feel like a strategic, caring decision — not a discount
- Removing cost as the reason a couple gives up after one failed cycle
- Driving free consultation bookings and direct package inquiries
- Positioning Nakshatra as the clinic that *planned for your second attempt before you even started*

---

## 🎯 TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary — Couples who've had one failed IVF cycle**
Age: 28–42 (both partners)
Location: Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Aundh, Pashan — Pune West IT belt
Life Stage: Post-failed IVF; emotionally drained; weighing whether to try again
Mindset: They want to try again but the cost conversation is terrifying. One more ₹1.2L+ feels impossible.

**Secondary — Couples researching IVF for the first time**
Age: 28–38
Life Stage: Starting fertility workup; haven't begun treatment yet
Mindset: Research-heavy, cost-conscious, reading everything before committing

**Retargeting Layer — Website visitors**
Visited IVF cost page, services page, or spent 30+ seconds on site
Mindset: High intent. Comparing clinics. Price transparency will convert them.

---

## 💡 CORE HOOK (IMPORTANT)

This is NOT a discount ad.
This is NOT "cheap IVF."
This is:
👉 *"We designed a plan for couples who know one cycle is rarely enough — and we priced it so cost never becomes the reason you stop."*

The reframe: Most clinics charge per cycle. Nakshatra charges for your **outcome plan**.

---

## 🔥 KEY VALUE PROPOSITION

Couples get:
- 2 complete IVF cycles — not one attempt and a bill
- Option A: ₹2.5L (both cycles, freezing billed separately)
- Option B: ₹3L (both cycles + embryo freezing + IV Nutrition Therapy — fully loaded)
- 70% IVF success rate — above national average
- Free first consultation, no obligation
- One doctor (Dr. Ramit) across both cycles — continuity of care
- Open 7 days — works for IT-sector couples with rigid office schedules

---

## ⚠️ PAIN POINTS TO DRIVE EACH ANGLE

**Price Angle pain point:**
"I want to try again but I can't keep spending ₹1.2L+ every cycle. What if the second one fails too?"

**Emotional Angle pain point:**
"The hardest thing about the failure wasn't the procedure. It was having to look at each other and ask — do we do this again?"

**Logic/Data Angle pain point:**
"I've read that IVF success improves over multiple cycles but nobody offers a sensible multi-cycle price. It's like they want you to fail so you come back and pay again."

**Retargeting Angle pain point:**
"I've been on 5 clinic websites comparing costs. Everyone quotes ₹1.2L but nobody tells you what happens if it doesn't work."

**Social Proof Angle pain point:**
"How do I know this clinic is worth trusting with a ₹2.5L decision?"

## 🎨 DESIGN STYLE

Premium. Warm. Clinical-adjacent without being sterile.
Think: the aesthetic of a premium private hospital brochure crossed with a thoughtful editorial piece.
Data-forward where the angle demands it. Emotion-forward where the angle demands it.
Never looks like a real-estate or EdTech ad. Never looks like a discount flyer.

---

## 🎨 COLOUR SCHEME (STRICT)

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy Deep | #0D1F3C | Primary backgrounds, hero sections |
| Gold | #C9A84C | Price callouts (₹2.5L, ₹3L), key stats |
| Gold Light | #E8C97A | Secondary text on dark bg, subheadings |
| Teal | #1B8F7A | CTA button ONLY — never decorative |
| Warm Cream | #FAF7F2 | Emotional angle backgrounds (Concept 2) |
| White | #FFFFFF | Body copy on dark bg |

**Hard rules:**
- No red. No orange. No countdown-timer energy.
- Gold is for numbers and hope — don't use it for warnings
- Cream backgrounds only for Concept 2 (emotional). All other concepts: navy dominant.

---

## 🧩 DESIGN ELEMENTS TO INCLUDE

- Price callout block: "₹2.5L" or "₹3L" large in gold — this is the visual anchor for price concepts
- Two-line offer summary beneath the price
- Checklist items (✅) for the Logic concept — clean, left-aligned
- 4.9★ Google rating badge for Social Proof concept
- "2,000+" stat block in gold for Social Proof concept
- Nakshatra wordmark — bottom right on all creatives
- nakshatraclinic.com — below or beside wordmark
- CTA button in teal: "Book Free Consultation" (preferred) or "Enquire Now"
- Horizontal divider lines — keep them thin and minimal, like a clinical report

---

## 📝 TEXT RULES

**Headline:** Max 8 words. Must work as a standalone line — no context needed.
**Subtext / Supporting line:** Max 15 words. One complete thought. No half-sentences.
**Price callout:** Must always include both options (₹2.5L and ₹3L) — never imply there's only one.
**CTA:** "Book Free Consultation" — never "Buy Now" or "Get Offer" — this is a medical decision, not a purchase.
**Avoid:** The word "package" without qualification — pair it with "IVF plan" or "2-Cycle plan" to keep it clinical-feeling.

## 🚫 DO NOT INCLUDE

- "Guaranteed pregnancy" or "100% success" — ever
- Flash-sale energy: countdown timers, "Only 5 slots left!", sticker-price strikethroughs
- Baby photos in cold-audience ads — wrong funnel stage
- Vague language: "affordable IVF" without the actual number
- Fear triggers: "Your biological clock," "Don't wait too long"
- Cluttered layouts — max 3 text elements visible at a glance
- Testimonials without documented consent
- One-size-fits-all copy — each concept must feel like it was made only for that audience segment
