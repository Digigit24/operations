Words to Avoid: Jargon (“embryo vitrification” without context), fear-mongering (“only X% success”),
hyperbolic claims (“100% guarantee”), overly clinical tone.
Recommended Visual Direction
Visual Mood: Warm, hopeful, confident. A balance between clinical professionalism (clean layout)
and homely warmth (soft lighting, natural smiles). 
Color Territory: Prefer teal or emerald green (health, calm) paired with a warm accent (coral or
peach) and neutrals. This contrasts with the sea of pinks and suggests growth. 
Typography Feel: Clean sans-serif (e.g. Lato, Poppins) for body; maybe a friendly serif or semi-serif
(e.g. Playfair, Merriweather) for headings to convey trust. Clear readability. 
Composition Style: Uncluttered, with strategic white space. Balanced mix of full-width images and
text blocks. Use circular or organic shapes behind text to soften edges. 
Photography: Real, candid-style photos of doctors with patients, joyful couples, healthy babies.
Light, natural illumination. Include some “day in life” visuals (doctor at work, lab equipment). 
Premium Cues: Subtle gold or copper accent in small details (like line separators or icons) to imply
premium care. High-quality imagery (no cheap stock filters). 
Design Restraint: Avoid overly busy graphics; use minimalistic icons. Balanced layout with
consistent margins. Not too many competing colors – stick to 2–3 main colors. 
Anti-Patterns: No cartoony pink cartoon clipart. Avoid grayscale or dreary colors (makes clinic seem
cold). Avoid cutesy fonts or hearts. No gratuitous “baby-bunny” motifs. 
Recommended AI Image Direction
(For generating brand visuals/images via AI, e.g. for social or promo)
Realism Level: Mix of realistic and soft illustration. Photo-realistic images of doctor/patient, but
avoid uncanny. 
Lighting: Bright, daylight or soft indoor light (warm tone). Should feel welcoming. 
Camera Feel: For photos, use mid-closeup or portrait style (doctor with patient, or couple holding
baby), shallow depth-of-field to focus on faces. 
Background Style: Clean, uncluttered (clinic environment or neutral wall); hint of warm tones (offwhite, pastels). Possibly slight bokeh. 
Human Presence: Yes – images should feature Indian-looking couples or families, as clients. Doctors
in smart but not harsh uniforms. Smiles or caring touch. 
Infographic/Illustration: Use flat modern illustrations sparingly for concept visuals (e.g. a stylized
embryo icon), but not too cartoonish. More medical infographics style (clean lines, consistent palette).
Layout Density: Mostly open composition; not text-heavy. If text overlays, keep it minimal (“Your
baby is possible” tagline). 
Color Restraint: Use brand palette only. If generating, ensure images align with teal/coral scheme
(skin tones natural, backgrounds tinted softly). 
Texture: Smooth surfaces, no heavy textures. Possibly subtle gradients or light lens flares to add
warmth. 
Avoid: Overly digital/AI ‘perfect’ people (the uncanny valley). Animal themes or silly memes. Overly
dark or cold medical-looking labs.
