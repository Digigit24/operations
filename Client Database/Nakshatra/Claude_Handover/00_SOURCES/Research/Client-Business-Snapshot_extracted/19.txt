SECTION 10 — OFFER & SERVICE POSITIONING
In Vitro Fertilization (IVF):
What it is: The flagship treatment for most infertility causes (eggs fertilized in lab, embryo transfer). 
Who: Couples/cases where natural conception failed (fallopian blockage, unexplained infertility, male
factor). 
Problem solved: Overcomes issues like blocked tubes or low sperm by doing fertilization outside
body. 
Emotional benefit: Restores hope of pregnancy (“take control of your fertility”). 
Logical benefit: High success rate in one go, personalized embryo selection, ability to use ICSI, PGT
etc. 
Proof required: Success rate data (e.g. “70% success for under-35” ), case studies, before/after
ultrasound photos. Doctor’s IVF accreditations (e.g. FOGSI membership). 
CTA: “Book your IVF Consultation” / “Check your eligibility now.” 
Page Format: Dedicated IVF service page with steps, FAQ sidebar, success statistics. 
Ad Angle: Emphasize the dream (“start your journey to baby”) with numbers (“70% success rate”). 
SEO angle: Target “Best IVF treatment Pune/Baner,” “IVF success Baner,” “Advanced IVF Pune.” 
Cross-sell: Suggest adding “ICSI or PGT” for worried couples, or “Egg freezing” for future planning. 
Intrauterine Insemination (IUI):
What: A simpler fertility procedure (placing sperm in uterus around ovulation). 
Who: Couples with mild male-factor or unexplained infertility, first-line after timed intercourse fails. 
Problem: Addresses lower sperm count or motility with less cost/time than IVF. 
Emotional: “An affordable first step – no need to jump to IVF.” Offers hope if IVF seems too extreme. 
Logical: Less invasive, quicker, much cheaper, can be done multiple times. 
Proof: Chart showing IUI success % for certain cases; maybe mention it’s a standard first step. 
CTA: “Learn about IUI options” or “Start your IUI cycle.” 
Page: IUI service page explaining process, ideal for younger couples. 
Ad Angle: “Simple IUI treatment at Nakshatra – your first step to pregnancy.” 
SEO: “IUI treatment Pune,” “Cost of IUI Pune.” 
Cross-sell: Pair with Ovulation Induction or IVF if needed. 
ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection):
What: IVF variant where a single sperm is injected into egg, for severe male infertility. 
Who: Men with very low sperm count or quality. 
Problem: Solves male-factor cases directly by “bypassing” sperm hurdles. 
Emotional: “Even extreme male infertility can succeed.” Gives hope to men. 
Logical: Higher fertilization rate than IVF in male-factor cases. 
Proof: Show success stories of azoospermia/IUI failures turning to ICSI babies. 
CTA: “ICSI treatment details.” 
Page: Advanced IVF techniques page. 
SEO: “ICSI treatment Pune,” “Best ICSI clinic Pune.” 
Cross-sell: Combine with IVF/egg retrieval, plus genetic testing.
