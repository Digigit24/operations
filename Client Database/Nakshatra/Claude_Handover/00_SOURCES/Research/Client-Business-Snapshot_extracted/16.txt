What frustrates them: Confusing medical jargon, long waiting times, “one-size-fits-all” treatment
approaches. They also dislike pushy sales. 
Trust Factors: Doctor’s expertise and empathy (credentials important), success stories of people like
them. Transparency in process and costs. Testimonials from couples in similar age. Friendly, hopeful
messaging. 
Buying Triggers: Free initial consultation, seeing clinic success stats, positive Google reviews,
approachable staff. Also, seeing technology or finance options (EMI) can tip them. 
Messaging that Works: “We understand your dreams – personalized IVF care with proven success” –
addressing hopes and individual approach. Soft, encouraging tone (“you’re not alone”). 
Messaging to Avoid: Technical heavy jargon, bleak success probabilities (“only X% success”), hard
sells. Overly “medical-speak” without empathy. 
Best Channels: Google search, Facebook/Instagram (for community/posts), fertility blogs, doctor
referrals. Family/friends networks. 
Best CTA: “Book Your Free IVF Consultation” or “Talk to Our IVF Specialist Now.” (simple, inviting, noobligation).
ICP 2 — “Experienced Mother-to-be” (e.g. Sunita, 36–40)
Who: Professional woman (36–40) possibly married, childless or has one but wants another. Urban,
middle-income, possibly career pause for family. 
What they want: Expert care that addresses age-related fertility decline (e.g. low AMH). A clinic that
understands older mothers’ concerns. 
What they fear: Declining fertility due to age, genetic issues, IVF complications (multiple pregnancy,
OHSS). Possibly judgements. 
What frustrates them: Feeling “too old” or “risk” from society/doctors; delays. Misinformation (“over
40 always low success”). 
Trust Factors: Advanced technology & specialized protocols (PGT, donor egg options), high success
stats for over-35s. Clear data showing they still have a good chance here. Compassion,
confidentiality. 
Buying Triggers: Personal reassurance from female staff/doctors, seeing other older-mom success
stories. Free fertility assessment (like AMH test) offer. Testimonials from late-30s patients. 
Messaging: “Even at 40+, your dream of baby is possible” (hopeful). Emphasize personalized plan
and experience with older patients. Positive, strength-based tone. 
To Avoid: Shaming language (“age=problem”), any hint of discouragement. Hard science talk without
empathy. 
Channels: Facebook communities for older moms, Instagram health influencers, Google searches
on “IVF success over 35”. WhatsApp groups. 
CTA: “Schedule your fertility assessment today” (CTA for initial check).
ICP 3 — “Concerned Husband” (e.g. Amit, 35)
Who: Husband who suspects male-factor infertility (e.g. low sperm count). Average income (maybe
blue-collar job). Possibly shy about discussing it. 
What they want: Confidential and effective treatment for male infertility. Clarity on the issue (“Is it
me?”) and affordable solutions (ICSI). 
What they fear: Feeling inadequate, stigma (“masculinity” issues), expensive treatments that may
not work.
