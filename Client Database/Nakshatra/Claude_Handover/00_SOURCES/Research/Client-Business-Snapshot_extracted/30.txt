Icons/Illustrations: If using icons, keep them line-style and consistent (our [37] example cartoon
style is modern, but limited use). Illustrations should be inclusive (not just caucasian). 
Social Media/Ads: Use real photos rather than heavily templated graphics. Stories and reels can be
vibrant; posts can maintain brand palette for recognition. 
Premium Signals: A subtle hint (gold accent, or “NABH Certified” badge) to convey certification.
Avoid everything being pastel-cute – instead, mix professional (doctor in a white coat) and warm
(holding baby). 
Anti-Patterns to Avoid: Ditch overly busy banners or animation. No rotating stock-heavy carousels
with stock baby images – they’re common and not memorable. Avoid “cutie pie” baby graphics or
animals (stork, bunny) which can undermine seriousness. 
SECTION 15 — POSITIONING GAP ANALYSIS
Biggest Market Gap: Clinics over-focus on tech and stats but under-communicate empathy and
partnership. They lack human stories and emotional reassurance. Also, male fertility and psychological
aspects are often neglected. A positioning that authentically combines compassion with cutting-edge
science is underrepresented. 
Biggest SEO Gap: Many competitors target general keywords but neglect long-tail “symptom”
searches (“failed IVF help,” “low AMH solutions”). There’s also opportunity in local content (Punespecific guides, Baner neighborhood content). 
Biggest Content Gap: Apart from technical info, there’s scant content on the patient experience
journey (what to expect at each step, emotional coping). Also little on male infertility awareness or
fertility supplements/diet (readers want practical tips). 
Biggest Visual Gap: An accessible, relatable Indian aesthetic is missing. Visuals are blandly Western
or generic. Very few use warm, authentic Indian imagery or distinctive colors. 
Biggest Trust Gap: Even though statistics and reviews are shown, social proof of real couples (video
testimonials, personal quotes) is underused. Potential trust signals like hospital accreditations or
doctor awards are not front-and-center. 
Biggest Offer Gap: No clinic emphasizes free or low-cost entry (like free consult, affordable
screening) in a strong way; everyone just “says affordable.” Also, bundled programs (like guaranty
programs) are rare. 
Biggest Conversion Gap: Many sites lack a clear funnel incentive (like lead magnet or quiz) – just
“Book Now.” Engaging TOFU content (quizzes, calculators) is a gap for conversion. Also, mobile CTAs
and chat support are inconsistently used. 
Strategic Opportunity Summary
Win Fastest: Differentiate through empathy and partnership. Nakshatra should become known as the
most caring, patient-focused clinic in Pune, not just the most technical. Focus on what patients feel:
understanding, assurance, community. 
Don’t Compete on: Generic “biggest success numbers” or cheapest price. That’s the battleground of
big chains. Instead, compete on quality of experience. 
Ignoring by Competitors: Messaging around male fertility and mental health support (counseling).
Couples care deeply about these but rarely see it in ad copy. Also, post-IVF care (pregnancy follow-up)
is rarely mentioned – an angle for retention. 
What Customers Care About: They want clear guarantees (“we’ll do everything possible”), clarity on
cost, and personal attention. Competitors rarely state guarantees or satisfaction support clearly.
