SECTION 14 — VISUAL CATEGORY RESEARCH
Common Color Schemes: Many fertility/IVF clinics use soft, calming palettes. Common colors include pastel
pinks, purples, blues, and greens. For example, Femcare uses bright pink/white; Progenesis uses pastel
peach/mint; Orion uses white/red; Iswarya uses white/burgundy. Lots of white space plus one accent color.
Overall trends: light neutrals (white, beige) combined with cool greens/blues or warm pastels. (Colorlib notes
“warm tones” and “nature-inspired” layouts for fertility sites ). 
Typography: Websites favor clean, modern sans-serif fonts (Open Sans, Lato, etc.) for readability. Titles
often bold and uppercase. Body text is straightforward. Some premium clinics might use a serif for headers
(to imply trust), but most use sans fonts for a fresh feel.
