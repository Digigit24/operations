Egg & Sperm Freezing (Cryopreservation):
What: Preserving gametes for future use (oncofertility, delaying childbearing). 
Who: Women/men not ready for kids (career focus, upcoming chemo, older single women). 
Problem: Biological clock or health issues may delay pregnancy. 
Emotional: “Securing future parenthood – peace of mind.” 
Logical: Preserve fertility while healthy; use later when ready. 
Proof: Mention technology success; maybe patient testimonials (“I froze eggs at 30, got pregnant at
35”). 
CTA: “Learn about egg freezing” / “Schedule a fertility preservation consult.” 
Page: Fertility preservation services page. 
SEO: “Egg freezing Pune,” “Fertility preservation India.” 
Cross-sell: Package with annual check-ups, “IVF when ready.”
Male Fertility / Sperm Analysis:
What: Diagnostics and treatments for male issues (sperm count, DNA fragmentation). 
Who: Men with fertility concerns or azoospermia. 
Problem: Identify underlying cause; offer treatments like hormone therapy or surgery. 
Emotional: “We address men’s concerns too.” Helps break taboo that fertility is “only woman’s issue.” 
Logical: Comprehensive semen analysis; advanced tests if needed (DNA fragmentation). 
Proof: Show lab accreditation; mention specialized male fertility experts (e.g. sexologist Dr. Kamate). 
CTA: “Book a sperm test.” 
SEO: “Low sperm count treatment Pune,” “Azoospermia surgery Pune.” 
Cross-sell: Leads to ICSI, donor sperm if needed. 
Fertility Surgeries (Laparoscopy/Hysteroscopy):
What: Minimally invasive surgery (laparoscopy for endometriosis/fibroids; hysteroscopy for polyps/
adhesions). 
Who: Women with anatomical issues (blocked tubes, fibroids, PCOD cysts). 
Problem: Fixes physical barriers to pregnancy without full IVF. 
Emotional: “Restore natural fertility by correcting the root issue.” 
Logical: One-time fix can improve chances, often done in synergy with IVF. 
Proof: Before/after imaging; share data on improved fertility post-surgery. 
CTA: “Schedule a consultation for fertility surgery.” 
SEO: “Laparoscopy Pune infertility,” “Fibroid removal Pune.” 
Cross-sell: Pre- and post-surgery IVF/IUI packages. 
Hero Offer: “Comprehensive Fertility Package” – includes full workup + first IVF attempt + one IUI free, at a
bundled price.
Trust-Building Offer: “Free Initial Fertility Assessment” (blood tests + ultrasound consult).
Entry-Level Offer: Discounted “Fertility Checkup” (AMH test, semen analysis).
Premium Offer: VIP IVF Package (luxury room, extended counseling, highest monitoring).
Retention/Upsell: Pregnancy care / OB care for after successful IVF (since they offer OB/GYN too).
Lead Magnet Idea: “Ultimate IVF Success Guide” e-book or webinar. Or “5 Ways to Boost Natural Fertility”
checklist.
