SECTION 6 — SEARCH INTENT MAPPING
TOFU — Awareness Stage
Behavior: Users are learning about infertility and treatments for the first time. They search symptoms
(“can’t conceive,” “repeated miscarriage,” “PCOS and pregnancy”), or broad education (“what is IVF?”). They
may watch YouTube “explainer” videos or read blogs.
Keyword Examples: “why can’t I get pregnant,” “PCOS fertility tips,” “what is IUI,” “fertility diet tips.”
Content Topics: Intro guides (IVF 101, IUI process, male fertility factors), myth-busting (“IVF age facts”),
basic FAQs (“What causes infertility?”).
Best Formats: Blog articles, infographics (“IVF step by step”), short explainer videos, Q&A pages.
Emotional State: Curious but anxious; seeking hope and clarity.
CTA Style: Soft CTAs like “Learn More,” “Download Fertility Guide,” or newsletter sign-up. No hard sell;
invitation to understand options.
MOFU — Consideration Stage
Behavior: Users compare clinics or treatments. They might search “best IVF clinic Pune,” “IVF vs IUI cost,” or
“IVF success rate at Nakshatra.” They consult reviews and testimonials, and perhaps contact clinics for info.
Keyword Examples: “Best IVF clinic Pune,” “IVF vs IUI success,” “top IVF doctor in Pune,” “Nakshatra IVF
reviews.”
Content Topics: Clinic comparisons, doctor profiles, patient stories, service details (e.g. “What happens
during IUI?”). Emphasis on proof (“Our 72% success”), differentiators (AI lab), and trust (accreditation).
Best Formats: Comparison charts, case study videos (“Our patient story”), testimonial pages, detailed
service pages, downloadable checklists.
Emotional State: Hopeful but cautious; looking for reassurance. Concerned about safety and legitimacy.
CTA Style: Medium CTAs like “Book a Free Consultation,” “Chat with Specialist,” or “Check Our Success
Rates.” Start form fills or hotline calls.
BOFU — Conversion Stage
Behavior: Ready-to-act users. They search specific clinics by name or phrases like “Nakshatra IVF contact,”
“Book IVF consultation Baner,” or “IVF cost at Nakshatra.” They might also use “near me” and review queries.
Keyword Examples: “Book IVF consultation Pune,” “Nakshatra IVF clinic phone,” “IVF near me Baner.”
Landing Needs: Trust signals (reviews, certifications), clear service summary, pricing hints, and a simple
contact form.
Trust Signals Needed: Google reviews, patient testimonials, staff credentials, NABH/NABL logos, success
statistics.
CTA Style: Direct, urgent CTAs: “Book Now,” “Call Us Today,” “Get Quote.” Offer hooks like “Free First Visit” or
“Limited Discount.” Possibly WhatsApp chat or call buttons prominent.
