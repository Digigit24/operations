SECTION 12 — AD ANGLE RESEARCH
Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads
Problem-Aware:
Hook: “Can’t conceive after trying hard?” (empathetic question) 
Pain: Anxiety, sadness, confusion. 
Promise: “Nakshatra can help you become parents.” (hope) 
Proof: Include star rating (4.9★) or “15+ yrs experience”. 
CTA: “Learn More” leading to lead gen form. 
Stage: TOFU. 
Format: Carousel of compassionate images (couple, doctor, baby).
Solution-Aware:
Hook: “70% of our IVF patients conceive!”
Pain: Doubt (“Will IVF work for me?”) 
Promise: “High success IVF in Pune – we help couple after couple.” 
Proof: Number stat (2000+ babies) . 
CTA: “Book a Free Consultation”. 
Stage: MOFU. 
Format: Image of happy family + text overlay.
Competitor-Aware:
Hook: “Other clinics not giving results?” 
Pain: Frustration after failed attempts. 
Promise: “Switch to experts – we succeed where others haven’t.” 
Proof: Testimonial snippet (“After countless failures, Dr. Kamate’s team delivered our baby!”) 
CTA: “Talk to our specialist.” 
Stage: BOFU. 
Format: Quote-style image.
Offer-Aware (Promotion):
Hook: “Plan for a baby? Get 20% off your first consultation!” 
Pain: Hesitation on costs. 
Promise: “Start your IVF journey affordably at Nakshatra.” 
Proof: Maybe highlight number of families (social proof). 
CTA: “Book Appointment Today.” 
Stage: BOFU/Decision. 
Format: Banner with discount or gift icon.
