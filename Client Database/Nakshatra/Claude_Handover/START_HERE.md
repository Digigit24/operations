## The client in one paragraph

Nakshatra is a **boutique specialist IVF & fertility clinic in Baner, Pune**, led by **Dr. Ramit Kamate**. Its entire strategy is to be the *opposite of the IVF chain*: the same doctor every visit, real numbers instead of vague claims (70% IVF success, ₹1.20L starting price, 2,000+ pregnancies since 2010, 4.9★ from 150+ reviews), both partners treated equally, and a calm, editorial brand voice in a category full of pink, urgent, baby-stock-photo marketing. The name means "star/constellation" in Sanskrit — a fixed point in an anxious journey.
