## Three things to know before you touch anything
1. **Nothing here has documented client approval.** Treat every asset as draft until the account lead confirms.
2. **There are real fact conflicts** (years in business, which doctor appears on camera, pricing). See `06_CLIENT_DETAILS/FACT_CONFLICT_REGISTER.csv` — do not publish a conflicted fact until resolved.
3. **Medical and price claims are flagged, not verified.** They must be checked with the clinic before use.
