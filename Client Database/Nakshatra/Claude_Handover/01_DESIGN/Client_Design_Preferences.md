## 3. Legacy / older design system (do not use for new work; do not merge)
The **published Instagram reels** used a different visual language: the doctor in-clinic with **bright yellow / orange / green / purple highlight boxes**. The Content Performance Analysis (April 2026) identifies this as a **saturated format** that must be varied, and prescribes new templates (doctor-to-camera direct address; split-screen before/after; lab/ultrasound footage). This legacy look is **inconsistent with the navy/gold system** and is retained only for reference in `99_ARCHIVE/01_Old_Guidelines`.

## 5. Known "common revision" risks to design for
Derived from the account's documented sensitivities (not from real revision history, which is absent):
- Anything reading as generic "pink IVF clinic" will be rejected on strategy grounds.
- Urgency/discount energy (countdowns, price-bursts, "limited slots") is banned.
- Baby/stork/embryo imagery in cold-audience or failed-cycle creatives is off-limits.
- Magenta creeping out of the logo into layout fields.
- More than one CTA, or three+ colours/fonts.
