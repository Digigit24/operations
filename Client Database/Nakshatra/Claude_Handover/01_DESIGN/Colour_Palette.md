# COLOUR PALETTE — Nakshatra

| Token | Hex | Role | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navy Deep | `#0D1F3C` | Authority, safety, depth | Primary backgrounds, hero sections, doctor content |
| Navy Mid | `#1E3D6E` | Layering | Cards, secondary sections |
| Gold | `#C9A84C` | Hope, revelation, premium | Stat callouts, key reveal lines, ONE emphasis point |
| Gold Light | `#E8C97A` | Soft emphasis | Secondary text on dark |
| Teal | `#1B8F7A` | Action, forward motion | CTA buttons, "solution" side, tick marks — **action only** |
| Warm Cream | `#FAF7F2` | Welcome, vulnerability | Light-mode / empathy concepts only |
| Muted Grey | `#8A9BB0` | Problem-side neutrality | "Before"/"what most clinics miss" — never alarming |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Body on dark | Body copy on navy |
| **Identity Magenta** | `#BE3485 → #7A2E8A` (gradient) | Brand identity | **Logo only** + optionally one restrained accent per campaign |

## Hard rules
1. Navy is the dominant background; cream is the exception for vulnerable concepts.
2. Gold is for revelation, not decoration — it owns numbers and one or two reveal points per piece.
3. Teal is for action only — if it's not clickable or the "solution" side, it's wrong.
4. **No red, no orange, no warning colours, ever.** Not a sale, not an emergency.
5. Muted grey replaces red for problem/before states (grey vs teal, never red vs green).
6. No floral/pastel pink fields. Identity Magenta is the only sanctioned magenta, and only per §2.2 of the Design Instructions.
7. **Max two colours + white/cream per creative.** Three is the ceiling and almost always wrong.
