# BRAND QUICK REFERENCE — Nakshatra
*Read this before you start any creative. Full detail: `Full_Design_Guidelines.md` + `AI_Image_Prompting_Guide.md`.*

---

**Brand personality:** A senior specialist who is not in a hurry. Calm, specific, quietly authoritative. The antithesis of the loud, pink, urgent IVF chain. Editorial, adult, considered — think *The New Yorker* / *Cereal* / a private specialist's letterhead.

**Primary colours**
- Navy Deep `#0D1F3C` — dominant background
- Gold `#C9A84C` — revelation, numbers, one key line (never decoration)

**Secondary colours**
- Teal `#1B8F7A` — **CTA / "solution" side only**
- Cream `#FAF7F2` — empathy / vulnerable concepts only
- Gold Light `#E8C97A` — soft emphasis on dark
- Muted Grey `#8A9BB0` — "problem/before" side (never red)
- White `#FFFFFF` — body copy on navy
- **Identity Magenta `#BE3485 → #7A2E8A` — logo only** (optionally one restrained accent per campaign; never a field)

**Font system:** Cormorant Garamond (display/emotional, italic for heart lines, max weight Medium 500) + DM Sans (body/UI/data). **No third font, ever.**

**Logo placement:** small, **bottom-right**, paired with URL. Never large, centred, or animated. On navy use the reversed lockup (white wordmark, gradient symbol kept). Clear space = symbol height on all sides.

**Design style:** high restraint. Max 2 colours + white/cream per creative. One hero element. One CTA. Max 3 lines of body copy. One visual idea. If it feels "too simple," it's right.

**Image style:** documentary, medium-format (Hasselblad look), soft natural window light, real Indian faces, natural skin texture, ≥30% negative space, low-medium contrast, calm mood. No stock smiles, no baby/stork/embryo imagery, no ring light, no 3D/Pixar.

**CTA style:** invitations, not commands. Teal pill, white DM Sans medium. Use "Book a free online consult", "Talk to us", "See your full picture", "Bring your reports". **Never** "Book Now", "Click Here", "Learn More", "Get Started", "Hurry", "Limited Slots".

**Important restrictions (the "never" list):** never pink/pastel fields · never red/orange · never urgency/countdowns/price-bursts · never exclamation marks · never "best in Pune"/"100%"/"guaranteed"/"miracle" · never baby/stork imagery · never generic stock smiles · never name a competitor · never two CTAs · never three colours or three fonts · never reduce padding to fit copy.

**Preferred post dimensions**
- Square 1:1 → 1080×1080, 80px padding all sides
- Story/Reel 9:16 → 1080×1920, 120px top/bottom safe zone (250px top / 300px bottom on Meta placements)
- Banner 16:9 → 100px horizontal / 80px vertical padding
- Web hero → 120px top/bottom desktop, 80px mobile

**The three numbers to place (in gold, never rounded up):** 70% · ₹1.20L · 2,000+ · 4.9★ · 15+ years ⚠️(or 12+ — confirm; see conflicts).
