1. Executive Summary
Nakshatra Clinic's Instagram Reels strategy has produced exceptional results over the past 60 days, with 
multiple posts crossing 100K views and one reel reaching 682K views. However, in the past two days, 
performance has plateaued sharply — videos using the same proven format are now stuck at 10K-17K 
views, a 70-85% drop from peak performance.
This report analyzes six top-performing reels in detail, identifies the exact patterns driving virality, 
diagnoses why the recent drop is happening, and provides a comprehensive 90-day strategic roadmap 
with 40+ creative content ideas to break the plateau and rebuild momentum.
Key Findings at a Glance
• Format saturation is the primary cause — the algorithm has stopped pushing the repeated 
visual template to new audiences.
• Comments are the weakest signal — averaging less than 0.05% of views, far below what the 
2026 algorithm rewards.
• Sub-niche exhaustion — recurrent miscarriage and IVF failure topics have been heavily covered;
fresh sub-niches are needed.
• Strong fundamentals remain — the storytelling structure, Hinglish format, and CTA are working;
only the visual delivery needs variation.
• Untapped opportunity in male fertility — the DNA Fragmentation reel hit 270K with the highest
share rate, signaling huge demand.
