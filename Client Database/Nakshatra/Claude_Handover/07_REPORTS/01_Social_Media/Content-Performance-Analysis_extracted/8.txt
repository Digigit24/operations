5. Strategic Recommendations
These recommendations are organized by priority. Implement Section 5.1 (Immediate Actions) within 7 
days to break the plateau. Section 5.2 (Format Evolution) over the next 30 days. Section 5.3 (Strategic 
Shifts) over 60-90 days.
5.1 Immediate Actions (Next 7 Days)
Action 1: Pause the current format for 3 days
Stop posting the yellow/orange/green highlight-box doctor-in-clinic format for 72 hours. This algorithmic
'reset' allows the system to re-evaluate your content rather than continuing to suppress the saturated 
format. When you return, post a visually different reel.
Action 2: Introduce 3 new visual templates immediately
• Template A — Doctor-to-camera direct address: Dr. Priyank speaking directly to camera with 
subtle captions, no overlay boxes. Builds trust through eye contact.
• Template B — Split-screen before/after: Left side shows reports/symptoms, right side shows 
healthy outcome. Highly shareable.
• Template C — Ultrasound/Lab footage with voiceover-style text: Shifts visual attention to 
medical equipment and procedures, away from doctor portraits.
Action 3: Add a comment-bait line to every reel
Replace or supplement the share CTA with engagement triggers:
• "क्या आपको ये test के बारे में पता था? Comment में बताओ।"
• "अगर आप भी ये face कर रहे हैं तो 'YES' लिखो।"
• "आपकी doctor ने ये test suggest किया था? हाँ या ना?"
• "कौनसी age में आपको ये problem face हुई? Comment करो।"
Action 4: Reduce posting frequency to 4-5 per week
If you are currently posting daily, drop to 4-5 posts per week temporarily. Quality and variation matter 
more than frequency right now. Use the saved time to produce 2 fresh visual templates per week.
Action 5: Repost your top 2 winners as 'reposts' with new hooks
The APS reel and DNA fragmentation reel can be reposted with completely new opening 3 seconds, new 
captions, and a different sub-niche framing. The algorithm treats them as new content if the metadata 
changes substantially.
