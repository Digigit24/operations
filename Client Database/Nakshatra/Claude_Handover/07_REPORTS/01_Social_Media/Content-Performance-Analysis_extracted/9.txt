Recommendation 7: Develop 6 alternative hook formulas
Stop opening every reel with age. Build a rotation of hook types:
• Question hook: "क्या आपको पता है thin lining क्या है?"
• Contradiction hook: "Sperm normal था। फिर भी pregnancy नहीं हो रही थी।"
• Statistic hook: "80% recurrent miscarriages का असली कारण ये test miss करता है।"
• Direct callout hook: "अगर 2 miscarriages हो चुके हैं, ये reel देखो।"
• Myth-bust hook: "डॉक्टर बोलेंगे 'normal है' — पर ये tests miss हो रहे हैं।"
• Story hook: "एक patient ने मुझे 5 साल बाद थैंक्यू बोला। क्यों?"
Recommendation 8: Build series-based content
Series make people follow for the next episode and increase profile visits dramatically. Suggested series:
• "Misdiagnosed Mondays" — A weekly case where a patient was wrongly diagnosed elsewhere.
• "Test Tuesday" — Each week, deep-dive into one fertility test most people don't know about.
• "Myth-Buster Wednesday" — Demolish one common fertility myth per week.
• "Real Story Friday" — Patient testimonial format (with permission).
• "Sunday Support" — Mental health and emotional content for the fertility journey.
Recommendation 9: Drive comments deliberately with engagement loops
• End every reel with a specific yes/no or A/B question.
• Reply to every comment within 1 hour for first 24 hours after posting.
• Pin the most thoughtful comment to encourage similar replies.
• Ask viewers to tag a friend who needs the information.
• Run weekly polls in Stories that drive Reel comments.
