4. Why The Plateau Is Happening
The drop from 50K-100K+ views to 10K-17K is not random. Five specific, identifiable forces are causing 
it. Understanding each is critical to fixing it.
Cause 1: Format Saturation (Primary Driver)
Instagram's 2026 algorithm penalizes repetitive visual signatures over time. When the algorithm sees 
the same template, framing, color scheme, and structure repeatedly, it stops pushing the content to 
new audiences. Distribution gets capped to existing followers and a narrow lookalike pool.
This is the textbook 'creator plateau' — every successful creator hits it. The signal: same format 
performing 70%+ worse than two weeks earlier, even with strong content.
Evidence in your account:
• Yellow highlight box format → orange → green → purple → all visually similar
• Same doctor, same clinic angles, same highlight box style for 30+ posts
• Algorithm has effectively 'matured' your distribution to a fixed audience
Cause 2: Sub-Niche Exhaustion
You've covered the major sub-niches in fertility extensively:
• Recurrent miscarriage (multiple posts)
• IVF failures and complications
• Hormonal disorders (PCOS)
• Immune-related issues (NK cells)
• Clotting disorders (APS)
The audience deeply interested in these topics has now seen multiple posts from you. Each new post 
within the same sub-niche reaches a smaller fresh audience because the saturated audience has already 
been served.
Cause 3: Hook Fatigue
"X साल की। Y बार pregnant हुई।" worked beautifully — but it's now predictable. When viewers can 
anticipate the structure within 1 second, they swipe away faster. Even a 0.5-second drop in average 
watch time can cut algorithmic distribution by 40-50%. The algorithm interprets faster swipes as 'this 
content isn't engaging' and reduces reach.
