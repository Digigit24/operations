The Most Important Metric You're Probably Not Tracking
Views are vanity. The real question: of the 682,000 people who saw the APS reel, how many called your 
number? How many became patients? Build a tracking system at the front desk: every new patient 
should be asked "How did you hear about us?" If you find that 5%+ of new patients mention Instagram, 
you have product-market fit on social. If less than 1% do, the views aren't translating to business and the
strategy needs deeper rethinking.
