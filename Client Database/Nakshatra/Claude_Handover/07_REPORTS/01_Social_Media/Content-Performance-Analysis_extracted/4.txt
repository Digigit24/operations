3. What's Working — The 7 Winning Patterns
After analyzing all six top performers, seven specific patterns emerge consistently. These are your 
content moats and must be preserved as the foundation of any new strategy.
Pattern 1: The Misdiagnosis-Revelation Story Arc
Every top reel follows the same emotional structure:
1. Specific patient introduction (age + situation)
2. Previous attempts and failures
3. Standard medical explanation that fell short
4. The 'aha moment' — a specific test or insight that revealed the real cause
5. Treatment and successful outcome
6. Lesson that empowers the viewer
This isn't generic education — it's a story with a clear villain (the missed diagnosis) and hero (the right 
test). Viewers share it because it confirms their fear that doctors miss things AND offers hope. This 
pattern is the single most powerful element of your content.
Pattern 2: Naming Specific, Lesser-Known Tests
Your highest-performing reels all center on tests and conditions most patients have never heard of:
• APS (Antiphospholipid Syndrome)
• NK Cells (Natural Killer Cell testing)
• DFI (Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index)
• Asherman's Syndrome
• Thrombophilia panel
Viewers feel like they're discovering insider medical knowledge their own doctor never mentioned. This 
'forbidden knowledge' positioning is psychologically irresistible. The 682K APS reel and 270K DFI reel 
prove this — both center on conditions that sound technical and rare but explain failures the audience 
has personally experienced.
Pattern 3: Numbers and Age in the First 2 Seconds
Concrete specificity stops the scroll. Your hooks consistently lead with:
• "34 साल की। 3 बार pregnant हुई।"
• "29 साल की patient आयी।"
• "31 साल का couple, 3 साल से try कर रहे थे।"
• "32 साल का couple। 4 IUIs fail। 1 IVF fail।"
Generic hooks like 'Are you struggling with...' don't perform. Specific ages and numbers create an instant
mental image and signal that a real story is coming.
