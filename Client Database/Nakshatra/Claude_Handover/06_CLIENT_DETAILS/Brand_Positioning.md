# BRAND POSITIONING — Nakshatra

## Authoritative positioning statement
*For research-aware couples in Pune navigating infertility, Nakshatra is the boutique fertility specialist where Dr. Ramit Kamate personally guides every patient — combining 15+ years of international training, radically transparent pricing, and a 70% IVF success rate with a degree of human attention that hospital chains cannot operate at scale.*

## Market role
The **specialist alternative to the IVF chain.** Not louder, bigger, or cheaper — quieter, more specific, more honest, more present. Also owns the local Baner/Aundh/Wakad/Hinjewadi doctor-led corner.

## The five hammers (differentiators — activate 1–3 per campaign)
1. The doctor you actually meet (most defensible).
2. Specifics in a sea of vagueness (70%, ₹1.2L, 2,000+, 4.9★).
3. Both partners treated equally (male fertility = largest untapped demand).
4. Failed-cycle investigation, not absorption.
5. Boutique access, chain-adjacent quality.

## Emotional territory
Owns **"considered hope"** — hope earned through honesty. The feeling to create: *"finally, someone is being honest with me."* The payoff is **relief**, not manufactured joy.

## Market contrast (implicit, never name competitors)
Chains: scale, superlatives, pink/pastel, stock babies, rotating doctors, "Book Now", EMI theatre. Nakshatra: restraint, real numbers, navy/gold, real faces, one doctor, permission CTAs, honest price.

## Competitor set (context, not for public comparison)
Indira · Iswarya · Nova · Progenesis · Femcare (+ Nucleus, Orion, Oasis, Morpheus). Pattern: scale + superlatives, impersonal, pink/blue. Gap Nakshatra owns: personal storytelling, doctor humanity, honest acknowledgement of fear, male fertility.
