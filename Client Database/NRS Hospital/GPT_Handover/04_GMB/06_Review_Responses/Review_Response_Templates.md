# Review Response Templates — Draft

## Positive review
Thank you for sharing your experience with NRS Hospital. We appreciate your feedback and will pass it to the team. We wish you and your family good health.

## Positive review naming a doctor/staff member
Thank you for your kind words. We are glad that [verified staff/doctor name] and the team could support you. We appreciate you taking the time to share your experience.

## Neutral/mixed review
Thank you for your feedback. We value both the positive points and the areas you highlighted. Please contact [verified grievance channel] so the administration can understand the details and follow up appropriately.

## Negative review — service/billing
We are sorry to hear that your experience did not meet expectations. Patient privacy prevents us from discussing individual details publicly. Please contact [verified grievance channel] with the visit date and contact information so the administration can review the matter directly.

## Safety rules
Do not confirm treatment details, diagnoses, admission status or patient identity in public. Do not argue, offer incentives for edits, make outcome claims or copy-paste identical replies. Escalate clinical, billing, abuse and privacy complaints internally.
