# NRS Hospital GMB/GBP Quick Guide

**Status:** Working guidance; NRS-specific GBP foundation and access are not recovered.

## Image
- Preferred: 1200×900 px, 4:3, JPG/PNG, 10 KB–5 MB.
- Use real facility/team/activity images whenever rights are confirmed.
- Keep text to 5–20% of the image; 3–8 word headline; one CTA.
- Small logo in a corner; no giant watermark, repeated logo or ad-poster clutter.
- Avoid graphic medical imagery, fake reviews/certificates, before–after, distorted AI faces/hands and performance-ad styling.

## Copy
- 80–250 words.
- Helpful, local, professional and moderately branded.
- Hook → useful information → natural NRS mention → one CTA → one verified contact → 2–5 hashtags.
- Rotate hooks/CTAs and write each post uniquely.

## NRS-specific restrictions
- Do not use “best/top/No.1/leading/world-class,” cure/reversal, guaranteed recovery or fake urgency.
- Do not publish any current address, floor, phone, insurer, accreditation, doctor, facility, 24×7 scope or price until the fact register is approved.
- Do not cross-post dense Instagram creative unchanged to GBP.
- Patient stories/reviews require source, consent and disclaimer.
