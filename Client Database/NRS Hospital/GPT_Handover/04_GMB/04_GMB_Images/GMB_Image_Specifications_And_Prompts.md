# GMB Image Specifications and Prompt Notes

## Technical specification
1200×900 px (4:3), JPG/PNG, realistic local-business image, minimal overlay, one CTA maximum, small corner logo.

## Recommended image subjects
- Real hospital exterior/entrance and wayfinding
- Reception or consultation environment without patient data
- Verified doctor/staff portrait with consent
- Real community event
- Service process or educational diagram
- Local seasonal awareness image

## Prompt starter
“Create a 1200×900 Google Business Profile image for NRS Hospital, Wakad. It should look like a helpful local hospital update, not an advertisement. Use a realistic Indian hospital/family scene, natural daylight, restrained blue-green branding, generous whitespace, a 3–8 word headline area, small logo space in a corner and no medical gore, fake reviews, before–after, urgency badges or unsupported claims.”

Final logo, text, NAP, credentials, disclaimers and Marathi/Hindi copy must be added manually after verification.
