# Audience Profiles

1. **Wakad family decision-maker (35–50):** manages children/parents; values proximity, doctor relationship, cost/insurance clarity and Marathi/Hindi/English guidance.
2. **Hinjewadi IT professional/couple (26–40):** Google-first, efficiency/insurance driven; values digital reports, English communication, clean facility and quick OPD.
3. **Senior citizen + caregiver (58–80):** prioritises continuity, dignity, Marathi explanation, mobility and predictable cost.
4. **Emergency walk-in:** proximity/action first; captured through Maps/GBP, signage, wayfinding and verified one-tap call.
