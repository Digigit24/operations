## Slide 02 — Definition
“What is Obesity?”  
“Obesity is a medical condition where excess body fat accumulates and affects your health.”  
“It’s not just about looks, it’s about your life.”

## Slide 03 — Why dangerous
- Increases risk of heart disease & stroke
- Leads to type 2 diabetes
- Causes breathing problems & sleep apnea
- Affects joints & mobility
- Raises risk of certain cancers

## Slide 04 — Causes
- Unhealthy diet
- Sedentary lifestyle
- Stress & poor sleep
- Genetics
- Certain medications

## Slide 05 — Warning signs
- Unexplained weight gain
- Fatigue & low energy
- Shortness of breath
- Joint pain
- Snoring & poor sleep

## Slide 06 — Health risks
- Heart disease
- Type 2 diabetes
- High blood pressure
- Fatty liver
- Sleep apnea
- Depression & anxiety

## Slide 07 — “How to Fight Obesity?”
- Eat a balanced, nutritious diet
- Stay physically active
- Sleep well & manage stress
- Regular health check-ups
- Seek expert medical guidance
