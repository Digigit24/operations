## Patient story
Use only with written consent. Keep the patient anonymous unless the release permits identification. Describe the care journey, not a guaranteed outcome. Add: “Individual experience. Results vary. Shared with consent.”

## Banned caption patterns
No “best/top/No.1/world-class,” cure/reversal/permanent result, guaranteed recovery, fake urgency, 20+ hashtags, keyword stuffing or repeated phone numbers.
