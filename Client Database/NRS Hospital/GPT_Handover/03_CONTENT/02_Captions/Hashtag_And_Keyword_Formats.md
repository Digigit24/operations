# Hashtag and Keyword Formats

## GMB
Use 2–5 hashtags only, for example: `#Wakad #PuneHealthcare #HealthAwareness` plus a verified service tag. Do not keyword-stuff captions.

## Social
Use a small, relevant block: local + topic + brand. Example: `#NRSWakad #WakadFamilies #StrokeAwareness #HealthEducation`.
