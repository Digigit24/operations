# Draft 90-Day Content Framework

**Status:** PLANNED / NOT CLIENT-APPROVED

## Month 1 — Trust and fact correction
- Confirm NAP, doctors, services and approvals.
- Doctor-led internal medicine education.
- Emergency access/directions utility.
- Facility/team photography after consent.
- One local GMB update each week.

## Month 2 — Local authority
- Monsoon/seasonal education.
- Senior/caregiver checklists.
- Hospital process: admission, billing, cashless.
- One behind-the-scenes video and one doctor Q&A.

## Month 3 — Conversion and reputation
- Verified doctor/service pages and related content.
- Cashless campaign after panel confirmation.
- Review request/response system.
- Test approved creative and record enquiries/appointments.

Cadence is provisional. Do not schedule medical copy until clinical approval and canonical fact checks are complete.
