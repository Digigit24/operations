# NRS Hospital Project Summary

**Client:** NRS Hospital, Wakad, Pune  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16  
**State:** Strategically useful but operationally incomplete.

## Working direction
NRS should be the **doctor-led neighbourhood multispeciality hospital for the whole family**, differentiated through physician continuity, proximity, calm guidance, local-language trust and operational transparency—not scale, luxury or “world-class” claims.

## Recoverable work
First-pass strategic research; Stage 1 visual and AI-prompt systems; provisional site extraction; general GBP safety guide; NRS Wellness, obesity, nine-tile awareness and consultation draft creatives; prior project audit.

## Not proven
Website fixes, SEO deployment, GBP optimisation, review growth, publication, video production, ads, analytics, approvals, licences and consents.

## Operating rule
Do not publish/reuse legacy creative until canonical facts, clinical claims, NAP, rights/consent and approval evidence are locked.
