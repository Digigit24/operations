## HEALTHCARE COMPLIANCE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Every design decision must respect:

- **NMC Code of Ethics:** No superlative claims about doctors or the hospital ("best", "top", "leading", "no.1", "world-class"). Use *experienced, qualified, trusted, doctor-led* instead.
- **MMC (Maharashtra Medical Council):** State-level adherence.
- **ASCI Code:** No misleading claims, testimonials must be verifiable and consented, before/after imagery restricted.
- **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954:** No cure claims for: diabetes, hypertension, paralysis, stroke, kidney failure, cancer, fits, leucoderma, sexual disorders. Educational angles only.
- **DPDP Act 2023:** Patient stories require explicit consent.

⚠️ Whenever you suggest a headline, CTA, ad copy line, or design element that risks non-compliance, flag it inline with ⚠️ and propose a compliant alternative.
