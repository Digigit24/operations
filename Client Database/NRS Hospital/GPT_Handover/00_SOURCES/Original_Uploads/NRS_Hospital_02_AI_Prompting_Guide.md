## Stage 1 Prompting Direction Summary

NRS Hospital creatives should be generated with an editorial-medical, warm-human, real-photography-led direction. The AI should produce calm, breathable layouts with real Indian families, real hospital spaces, and restrained blue-green branding.

Do not prompt for generic medical clichés: stethoscope-on-keyboard, hands holding hearts, blue glowing doctors, sterile empty corridors, or fake AI doctor portraits. Do not use superlative or cure-claim language in prompts. All outputs should be treated as base imagery or design direction; final logo, typography, contact details, compliance disclaimers, and Marathi/Hindi text should be finished manually in Canva, Figma, or Photoshop.

## Universal AI Image Prompting System

Use this framework across ChatGPT image generation, Midjourney, Claude, Flux, Ideogram, Reve, Leonardo, Canva AI, Runway, NanoBanana, and future image tools.

### Lighting behavior

Prompt for:

- Warm natural daylight
- Soft diffused indoor light
- Real hospital ambience
- Gentle shadows
- Medium contrast

Avoid:

- Harsh fluorescent clinical lighting
- Neon blue medical glow
- Overly cinematic dramatic light
- Blown highlights
- Crushed black shadows

### Composition style

Prompt for:

- Editorial healthcare composition
- Generous whitespace
- Clear focal point
- Calm hierarchy
- Realistic spacing for headline and footer
- One main message per design

Avoid:

- Overcrowded poster design
- Too many badges or labels
- Text over faces
- Random icons everywhere

### Emotional framing

Use:

- Calm
- Dignified
- Human
- Family-warm
- Clear
- Trust-building

Avoid:

- Panic
- Fear-bait
- Luxury distance
- Corporate coldness
- Miracle-treatment energy

### Realism level

Use:

- Photorealistic with documentary feel
- Real Indian faces
- Natural skin texture
- Local family and hospital realism

Avoid:

- Hyper-glossy pharma commercial
- AI-smooth plastic faces
- Fantasy medical visuals
- Unrealistic hospital interiors

### Texture behavior

Use subtle texture only:

- Paper-like off-white background
- Soft gradient wash
- Minimal clinical card textures

Avoid:

- Heavy grain
- Marble
- Glassmorphism
- Metallic gold
- Overdesigned patterns

### Background philosophy

Real spaces are better than blank backdrops.

Preferred backgrounds:

- Real OPD consultation room
- Real waiting area
- Reception
- Doctor cabin
- Clean ward without patients
- Exterior/signage view
- Family home context for awareness posts

Avoid:

- Empty sterile hallways
- Generic white studio
- Unbranded hospital corridor
- Futuristic medical lab

### Camera feel

Prompt for:

- 35mm, 50mm, or 85mm equivalent lens
- Eye-level or slightly warm documentary perspective
- Mild depth of field
- Natural portrait framing

Avoid:

- Extreme wide-angle distortion
- Heroic low-angle doctor shots
- Overdramatic closeups

### Editorial direction

Use this phrase often:

> “serious medical journal restraint, humanised for Indian families”

The design should look thoughtful, not loud.

### Contrast style

- Medium contrast
- Soft but readable
- No pure black unless unavoidable
- Use Deep Clinical Navy instead of black

### Spatial density

- Open and breathable
- 1 hero visual + 1 headline + 1 support line + CTA/footer
- Infographics: maximum 6 callouts

### Color restraint

Prompt for:

- refined blue and green hospital palette
- warm off-white background
- deep clinical navy text
- red only for emergency CTA

Avoid:

- bright stock medical blue
- neon green
- gold luxury
- random rainbow gradients

### Premium cues

Use:

- whitespace
- typography hierarchy
- real photography
- soft shadows
- restrained color
- clean footer

Avoid:

- luxury gold
- marble
- glass
- glossy corporate hospital effect

### Infographic behavior

- Use clean cards.
- Use one icon style.
- Keep callouts short.
- Prioritize hierarchy over decoration.
- Avoid scary anatomy.

### UI styling behavior

- Humanist sans typography
- Rounded cards
- Soft borders
- Generous margins
- Clear CTA system

## 11. Healthcare Compliance Safety Rules

### Diabetes content

Avoid:

- Cure diabetes
- Reverse diabetes permanently
- Guaranteed sugar control

Use:

- Diabetes management
- Regular monitoring
- Physician-guided care
- Lifestyle and medication support

Disclaimer:

`Educational content only. Diabetes care must be personalized by a qualified physician.`

### Hypertension content

Avoid:

- Permanent BP cure
- Stop BP tablets naturally
- Guaranteed control

Use:

- Hypertension management
- BP monitoring guidance
- Doctor-supervised treatment plan

### Cardiac content

Avoid:

- Guaranteed heart attack prevention
- Best cardiac emergency care

Use:

- Chest pain warning signs
- Golden hour awareness
- Seek urgent care if symptoms appear

### Stroke / paralysis content

Avoid:

- Paralysis cure
- Guaranteed recovery after stroke

Use:

- FAST warning signs
- Early recognition
- Emergency evaluation
- Rehabilitation guidance where appropriate

### Maternity / pregnancy content

Avoid:

- Guaranteed normal delivery
- 100% safe pregnancy
- Best maternity hospital

Use:

- Pregnancy monitoring
- High-risk pregnancy consultation
- Doctor-guided antenatal care

### Paediatric content

Avoid:

- Cure child fever instantly
- Guaranteed relief

Use:

- When to consult a doctor
- Fever warning signs
- Child health guidance

### Cosmetic / dental content

Avoid:

- Perfect smile guaranteed
- Before-after promises

Use:

- Consultation-led dental care
- Treatment options explained by doctor

### Testimonial content

Rules:

- Use only with consent.
- Avoid exaggerated outcomes.
- Add disclaimer.

Disclaimer:

`Individual experience. Results vary. Shared with consent.`

### Before/after content

Recommendation: Avoid where possible.

If unavoidable:

- Get written consent.
- Add disclaimer.
- Do not imply guaranteed results.
- Avoid sensitive medical before/after imagery.

### Doctor superlative content

Avoid:

- Best doctor
- Top physician
- Leading specialist
- No.1 surgeon

Use:

- Experienced physician
- Qualified consultant
- Doctor-led care
- Consultant physician and intensivist
