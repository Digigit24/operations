# Content Format Reference

- Social default: 1080×1350; carousel can be 1080×1080 or 1080×1350.
- GBP: 1200×900, 4:3, 5–20% text, 3–8 word headline, one CTA.
- Educational slide: one idea, 30–60 words maximum.
- Carousel cover: 8–18 words.
- Doctor-led post: 15–40 words on creative.
- AI creates base image/layout direction; final logo, type, NAP, credentials, Marathi/Hindi and disclaimers are applied manually.
- Separate Instagram/Meta creative from GBP copy and imagery.
