# Typography Guide — NRS Hospital

**Status:** Provisional; five-reference calibration/client approval pending.

Headlines/display: Plus Jakarta Sans; alternatives Manrope/Inter.  
Body/UI: Inter.  
Marathi/Devanagari: Mukta; alternatives Hind/Tiro Devanagari Marathi.

Use no more than two families, sentence case, short situational headlines, clear hierarchy and generous line height. Avoid all-caps paragraphs, ultra-light weights, justified copy, decorative medical fonts and machine-translated Marathi.

For 1080×1350: headline 72–104 px, subhead 38–52 px, body 30–40 px, CTA 34–44 px, footer 24–30 px. Keep English and Marathi in separate blocks when useful; manually verify matras, punctuation, wrapping and translation.
