# Logo Usage Guide — NRS Hospital

## Source
`02_ASSETS/01_Logos/nrs-logo.png`: horizontal 875×332 transparent PNG; blue/green mark, wordmark and tagline “Nurture, Recover & Secure Life.” No vector, reversed, monochrome or icon-only variant is available.

## Placement and size
- Social square: 120–160 px wide.
- Social portrait: 130–180 px.
- Story/Reel: 150–210 px inside safe zones.
- Blog/banner: 130–180 px.
- Website header: 150–190 px desktop; 120–150 px mobile.
- Top-left default; footer left/centre-left.

## Rules
Keep clear space equal to the NRS letter-group height. Use warm white/light tints or quiet photo space. Do not place over busy images, red blocks or dark fields until a reversed variant is formally approved. Do not recolour, stretch, skew, crop, add glow/shadow/outline, recreate in type or alter the tagline. The legacy “Nurture Wellness & Secure Life” wording is not canonical.
