# NRS Hospital Brand Quick Reference

**Status:** PROVISIONAL working system — not client-approved  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

## Personality
Doctor-led, calm, competent, human, neighbourhood-rooted, transparent, family-trusted and accessible-premium. Never luxury, corporate-cold, aggressive or miracle-led.

## Colours
Primary: Refined NRS Blue `#1F8FC4`, Deep Clinical Navy `#12384F`, Warm Hospital White `#FAFBF8`.  
Secondary: Refined NRS Green `#86A933`, Care Blue Mist `#EAF6FB`, Recovery Green Mist `#F0F7E6`.  
Emergency Red `#D63A31` only for verified urgent action.

## Fonts
Plus Jakarta Sans headlines; Inter body/UI; Mukta Marathi/Devanagari. Alternatives: Manrope, Hind, Tiro Devanagari Marathi.

## Logo
Top-left default. Clear space equal to the “NRS” letter-group height. Do not recolour, stretch, shadow/glow, crop or use symbol-only without approval.

## Design style
Editorial-medical, warm-human, calm hierarchy, generous whitespace, rounded clinical cards, restrained two-tone icons and real-photo-first execution.

## Image style
Real NRS doctors/staff/facility with rights; real Indian families/models; natural daylight; documentary feel; local Wakad context. Avoid stock-blue medicine, AI doctor portraits, stethoscope-on-keyboard, hands holding hearts, dramatic hero poses and sterile empty corridors.

## CTAs
Call Now · WhatsApp Our Team · Book an Appointment · Get Directions · View Cashless Network (after verification) · Read More · Save This Guide.

## Restrictions
No best/top/No.1/leading/world-class, cure or guaranteed outcome, unsupported experience/patient-count/accreditation/insurer claims, unconsented patient images, or dense ad-style GBP images.

## Preferred dimensions
Social 1080×1350; square/carousel 1080×1080; Story/Reel cover 1080×1920; blog 1200×675; GBP 1200×900 (4:3).

## System separation
- Current working system: editorial-medical/warm-human.
- Legacy system: teal/cream/gold, dense poster layouts, mixed serif/sans, wave motifs and repeated footers; reference only.
- Unverified preferences: no direct client preference log recovered.
- Client-approved rules: none explicitly recovered.
