"A thousand years ago, Chinese doctors called it *water poison*. A disease this old should be gone by now — yet 2024 was the worst dengue year ever recorded."

**BRIDGE:** And every monsoon, it shows up right here in Wakad, Pune.

**WHY & HOW:** Dengue spreads through the Aedes mosquito — which breeds not in dirty drains but in *clean* standing water around your home. And it bites during the day.

**PREVENTION:** Once a week, empty and scrub everything that holds water — coolers, pots, buckets, old tyres, even the fridge tray. Screen your windows or use a net, and wear repellent at dawn and dusk.

**WHAT TO DO:** High fever, body ache, headache, or pain behind the eyes? Don't wait. See a doctor early, get tested, rest, drink plenty of fluids — and take only paracetamol for fever.

**WHAT NOT TO DO:** Never take aspirin or ibuprofen — they raise the risk of bleeding. Don't ignore warning signs like bleeding gums, severe stomach pain, or repeated vomiting. And skip the WhatsApp "miracle" cures — get tested instead.

**CTA:** Save this and send it to your family. Worried about a fever this monsoon? Talk to our team at NRS Hospital, Wakad.
