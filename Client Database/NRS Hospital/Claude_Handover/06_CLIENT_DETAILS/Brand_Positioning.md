# BRAND POSITIONING — NRS Hospital (Brand Brain extract, Step 06)
Source: Step 06 Brand Brain (chat d0ed9dbf, 2026-06-25). Built on confirmed public data + 12 labelled assumptions. Retrieve the full ~500-line doc for complete copy bank + ICP messaging maps.

## Positioning statement (one line)
NRS Hospital is Wakad's doctor-led neighbourhood multispeciality hospital — the first call for the families of Kalewadi Phata, whether it's midnight cardiac, Monday diabetes review, or a child's fever at 3 AM.

## Brand marks
- **Tagline (from logo):** "Nurture, Recover & Secure Life" — supporting mark; never a headline.
- **Brand promise (internal north star, never verbatim in ads):** "The doctor who knows your family — ready when you need us."
- **Public-facing compliant claim:** "Your trusted neighbourhood hospital. 24×7. Doctor-led. Close to home."

## The 5 positioning pillars (every piece of comms reinforces ≥1)
1. **Doctor-led care** — anchored by Dr. Survase, by name/credential/voice/philosophy (lifestyle-first, "central doctor" model). NOT "our team of experts".
2. **24×7 neighbourhood proximity** — Kalewadi Phata junction; 5 minutes beats 20 on the highway; the primary trigger for emergency walk-ins.
3. **Family continuity (family-hospital model)** — whole family across generations, same doctor. The open positioning gap no competitor owns.
4. **Transparency** — billing/admission/cashless/referral clarity; "no surprise bills".
5. **Honest, conservative care** — "we treat conservatively first"; smooth referral to Aditya Birla/Sahyadri for cases beyond NRS.

## Voice (5 attributes)
Calm · competent · human · neighbourhood-rooted · plainspoken. Never breathless. Concrete situations over abstractions. Direct, low-pressure CTAs. English primary; Marathi/Hindi for local + social.

## Banned words (compliance)
best · top · No.1 · leading · world-class · guaranteed · permanent cure · cure diabetes/BP/paralysis · miracle · assured results · 100% safe · fastest recovery.

## Market contrast
Not super-speciality scale (Aditya Birla) · not infrastructure-led (Lifepoint) · not niche mother-child (Surya) — but family-relationship-led. Closest peer: Phoenix (also doctor-led, NABH). Aditya Birla = referral partner, not rival.
