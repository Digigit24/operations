## The strategic thesis (one paragraph)
NRS should own *"the Wakad family's first call — a doctor-led, transparent, 24×7 neighbourhood hospital where the same physician knows you, your parents, and your kids."* It should NOT chase super-speciality scale (Aditya Birla's lane) or infrastructure storytelling (Lifepoint's lane). Its structural advantage is a **named, accessible chief physician** (Dr. Survase) plus proximity to the dense Kalewadi Phata junction. The fastest 90-day win is Google Business Profile optimisation + review-volume growth + Kalewadi Phata Map Pack dominance.
