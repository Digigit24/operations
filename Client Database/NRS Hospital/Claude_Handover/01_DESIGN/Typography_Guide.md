# TYPOGRAPHY GUIDE — NRS Hospital (Stage 1)
Source: Step 02 §5. Final font personality pending 5 references.

- **Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans (first choice). Alternatives: Manrope, Inter.
- **Body:** Inter (or Manrope). Regular for body, Medium for emphasis. No ultra-light.
- **Marathi/Devanagari:** Mukta (default). Hind (bilingual neutral). Tiro Devanagari Marathi (festival/editorial emphasis).
- Max 2 families per design. Avoid all-caps on long medical lines. Generous line height for education content. No justified paragraphs.

## Bilingual rules
