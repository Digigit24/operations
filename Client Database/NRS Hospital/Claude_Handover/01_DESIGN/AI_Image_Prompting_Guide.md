# AI IMAGE PROMPTING GUIDE — NRS Hospital
The complete prompting system is the Step 02 source document:
`../00_SOURCES/Prompting_Guides/NRS_Hospital_02_AI_Prompting_Guide.md` (universal image prompt system, required/optional input fields, output-size table, 13 design-type add-ons, master template, 8 worked example prompts, text-length rules, healthcare compliance safety rules, QA checklist).

## Negative prompt (always include)
no "best/top/leading/No.1/world-class"; no cure claims; no stethoscope-on-keyboard; no hands-holding-heart; no AI doctor portraits; no identifiable patient faces; no scary anatomy; no luxury gold/marble/glass; no stock-blue medical look; no clutter.

## What AI generates vs what is finished manually
AI = base imagery, layout concept, mood, background. Manual (Canva/Figma/PS) = logo, exact fonts, phone number, URL, footer, doctor credentials, disclaimers, Marathi/Hindi text, final compliance review.
