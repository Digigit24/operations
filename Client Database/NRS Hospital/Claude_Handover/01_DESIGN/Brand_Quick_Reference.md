# BRAND QUICK REFERENCE — NRS Hospital
**Read this before you design anything.** Full detail: `Full_Design_Guidelines.md` (Step 02) + Brand Brain extract in `../06_CLIENT_DETAILS/Brand_Positioning.md`.
Status: **Stage 1** — Stage 2 refinement pending 5 client visual references.

## Brand personality
Doctor-led · neighbourhood-rooted · calm under pressure · transparent · family-trusted · accessible-premium (NOT luxury) · human-first.

## Positioning (one line)
The doctor-led neighbourhood multispeciality hospital that treats your whole family.

## Primary colours
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Refined NRS Blue | `#1F8FC4` |
| Secondary | Refined NRS Green | `#86A933` |
| Deep trust | Deep Clinical Navy | `#12384F` |
| Base bg | Warm Hospital White | `#FAFBF8` |
| Emergency | Clear Emergency Red | `#D63A31` (emergency CTA only) |

Ratio: ~70% neutrals · 20% blue/navy · 7% green · 3% emergency/caution.

## Font system
- Headlines: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (700/650)
- Body: **Inter** (400/500)
- Marathi/Devanagari: **Mukta** (Tiro Devanagari Marathi for festival emphasis)
- Max 2 font families per design. No ultra-light weights in medical copy.

## Logo placement
Top-left in headers/most posts. Clear space = height of the "NRS" letter group. Never recolour, stretch, add shadow/glow, or place on busy photo / red / navy (unless a white version is made). Show tagline only where there's room; omit on small/emergency posts.

## Design style
Editorial-medical, warm-human, real-photo-led, breathable. Generous whitespace = trust. One message per creative. Rounded cards (14–18px). Two-tone semi-filled line icons (2–2.5px stroke).

## Image style
Real Indian doctors/families/facility, warm natural daylight, documentary feel, mild depth of field, real skin texture. **Never:** stock-blue medical, stethoscope-on-keyboard, hands-holding-heart, AI doctor portraits, empty sterile hallways, scary anatomy, gold/marble/glass.

## CTA style
Sentence case, weight 700, under 4 words. Approved: Call Now · WhatsApp Our Team · Book an Appointment · Get Directions · Talk to Dr. Survase's Team · View Cashless Network. Emergency red only for "Call Now".

## Preferred dimensions
Social default **1080×1350** · Carousel **1080×1350** · Story/Reel **1080×1920** · Blog/Featured **1200×675** · Website hero **1920×800**.

## Hard restrictions (compliance — never break)
⛔ No "best / top / No.1 / leading / world-class". ⛔ No cure / guaranteed / permanent-cure claims (Drugs & Magic Remedies Act). ⛔ No identifiable patient without written consent. ⛔ Testimonials need "Individual experience. Results vary." + consent. ⛔ Don't imply NABH/NABL until confirmed. ⛔ Human-proof all Marathi before publishing.
