## 2. Market and Category Analysis

The global financial advisory services market is large and growing: ~USD 117B in 2025, projected to ~USD 226B by 2035 (CAGR ~6.8%). India-specific management consulting (broader) is growing faster at ~12.7% CAGR.

**Key Drivers:**

- Rising startups/SMEs needing affordable strategic finance (Virtual CFO boom).
- M&A/IPO activity, cross-border expansion (GCCs), regulatory complexity (tax, compliance, FEMA).
- Digital transformation & AI in finance.
- Demand for outsourced expertise without full-time hires.

**India Opportunity:** High growth in MSMEs, foreign investment (GCCs), digital economy. Bangalore as a hub advantages DevMantra. Competition mixes Big 4, boutique CA firms, and specialized virtual CFO/M&A players. Positioning sweet spot: Mid-market, tech-enabled, cross-border focus for SMEs/startups scaling globally.
