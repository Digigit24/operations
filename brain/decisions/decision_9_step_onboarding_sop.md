# Decision: 9-Step Onboarding SOP (Intentionally Minimal)

**Date:** 2026-06-05
**Status:** Confirmed — v1.1 in effect

## Decision
The client onboarding SOP has exactly 9 steps and is intentionally minimal. No steps will be added until at least 3 clients have completed the full onboarding and we have learned what's missing from real friction.

## Rationale
- Over-engineered onboarding is worse than under-engineered. Missing a step you need is fixable. Executing 15 steps you don't need wastes PM time on every single new client.
- The 9 steps cover the full arc: intake → discovery → access → audit → brand brain → skills → Notion setup → 30-day plan → kickoff. This is the logical minimum for a client to be "live and running."
- Steps added speculatively (before friction is observed) become cargo cult process. Real-world friction from 3 clients tells you what to add.
- The SOP file itself says "Do not add further steps until 3 clients have completed onboarding."

## Outcome
- SOP stays at 9 steps until we have 3 full onboarding completions
- After 3 completions: PM retrospective → identify friction → add maximum 2 steps
- Client-specific variations go into client skill files, not the core SOP
