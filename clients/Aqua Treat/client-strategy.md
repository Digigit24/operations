## ⚠ Three Critical Findings (Shape All Downstream Work)

**1. The category is scientifically contested.** Mainstream water-treatment science finds magnetic treatment does not reliably prevent scale and does not remove hardness minerals the way ion-exchange softeners do. A minority of peer-reviewed studies report partial scale reduction under specific conditions. This is not a reason to drop the client — it is the primary constraint on legal and credible positioning. All claims must be framed as *conditioning / scale management*, never as softening or mineral removal.

**2. The live website carries serious health claims.** The Healthcare Applications page claims the product helps clear arteries, regulates heart function, reduces blood sugar and blood pressure, helps kidney ailments/gout, heals wounds, etc. These likely violate India's **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954** and **ASCI rules**. This is a live compliance exposure. None of these claims may appear in any new creative, caption, or asset.

**3. The website contradicts itself on mechanism.** Some copy says the device "removes/eliminates minerals" and "transforms hard water into soft water" (an ion-exchange softener claim); other copy says "minerals remain suspended and water retains its natural composition" (the actual magnetic-conditioning claim). These cannot both be true. The honest, defensible position is: *the device conditions water — it does not soften water by removing minerals.*

**Color ratio:** ~60% white/surface · ~25% indigo · ~10% neutral/ink · ~5% aqua accent

**Rules:** Never combine aqua text on indigo. Never more than one accent hue per creative. No neon. No purple-pink gradients.

### Typography

| Element | Recommended | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Space Grotesk / Sora | Archivo, Arial Bold |
| Body | Inter | Roboto, system-ui |
| Spec tables / numerals | Inter Tabular / IBM Plex Mono | Roboto Mono |

**Rules:** Strong heading hierarchy (H1 ≫ H2 ≫ body). Tight headline letter-spacing (-1% to -2%). Body line-height 1.5–1.65. Emphasis via weight (600/700) and indigo colour — not italics. All-caps only for short labels/eyebrows (+4–6% tracking). No serifs, no rounded "friendly" fonts (undercuts engineering credibility).

### Logo

Single-colour indigo wordmark "Aquatreat" + halftone dot-grid / four-point sparkle motif. A reversed white version is required on indigo/dark backgrounds. Top-left placement (landscape), top-centre (square/portrait). Minimum clear space = height of the "A" on all sides. Never recolour outside the indigo/white pair, stretch, add shadows/glows, or rotate the motif.

### Visual Motifs

**Primary:** the halftone dot-grid + four-point sparkle from the logo — reuse as a subtle brand pattern (corner accents, section dividers, dotted flow lines suggesting water movement). Keep subtle and monochrome (indigo or aqua at low opacity).
**Secondary:** clean inline-pipe / flow diagrams (honest mechanism visuals).
**Avoid:** literal cartoon water droplets, splashes, "aqua" clip-art, glossy 3D bevels, spa/wellness imagery.

### Voice

Confident · Technical-but-clear · Proof-led · Calm · Honest

**Approved language:** "Chemical-free scale management" · "Salt-free water conditioning" · "Helps reduce scale-related problems" · "Low-maintenance, inline solution" · "Protects pipes, geysers and equipment from scale build-up" · "Conditions water by altering how scale-forming minerals behave" · "Engineered in Pune. **Banned (always):** Any health/disease claim · "Softens/removes minerals" unqualified · "NASA technology" (without proof) · "Guaranteed/100% scale removal" · "Transforms hard water into soft water" · Exaggerated savings without data · "World's #1" · "Miracle"

## Target Audience (4 ICPs)

**ICP 1 — Industrial Plant / Maintenance Engineer (Primary B2B):** Plant heads, maintenance/utilities engineers at manufacturing, cement, steel, pharma, food, hospitality plants. Solution-aware, skeptical, ROI-driven. Pains: scale in boilers/cooling towers, downtime, chemical handling cost, energy loss. Needs: named client case studies, spec sheets, site survey, written warranty. **CTA:** "Request a site assessment / Talk to our engineer."

**ICP 2 — Homeowner / Housing Society (Primary B2C):** 30–55, urban Pune homeowners, flat owners, society managing committees. Problem-aware, solution-confused. Pains: scale on taps/tiles/geysers, dull hair/skin, appliance damage, dislike of salt-softener upkeep. Needs: reviews, WhatsApp access, honest before/after, warranty. **CTA:** "WhatsApp for a free hard-water check / get a quote."

**ICP 3 — Commercial Facility Manager:** Facility/ops managers of hotels, hospitals, schools, malls, IT parks. Pains: scale in plumbing/HVAC/laundry, maintenance budgets. Needs: commercial references, warranty, AMC clarity. **CTA:** "Book a commercial water assessment."

**ICP 4 — Agricultural / Farm Buyer:** Progressive farmers, dairy, horticulture, irrigation-system owners. Pains: scale in drip/sprinkler lines, borewell hardness, pump efficiency. Needs: local farmer references, simple proof, vernacular content. **CTA:** "Talk to us about farm/irrigation water."

**ICP 5 (Healthcare) — NOT an acquisition ICP.** The Healthcare vertical depends on the unsupported health claims that must be removed. Do not build any marketing targeting this ICP.

## Compliance Rules (Non-Negotiable)

All rules apply to every creative, caption, headline, social post, GMB post, and website page.

**Absolutely banned:**
- Any disease-treatment or therapeutic claim (heart, artery, kidney, diabetes, blood pressure, wound-healing, gout, arteries, "toxins," "free radicals," "magnetized water improves health")
- "NASA technology" — unless verifiable documented proof supplied by client
- "Removes hardness minerals" / "turns hard water into soft water" / "softens water" (unqualified)
- Guaranteed scale removal ("removes 100% of scale," "eliminates scale completely")
- Exaggerated savings/ROI without client-supplied data
- Fake client logos, certifications, awards, ratings, reviews, testimonials
- Named clients (Tata Steel, UltraTech, BPCL, NBC Bearings, L.G. Balkrishnan, Jyothy Labs, Vanaz) without confirmed permission + substantiation

**Safe, approved language:**
- "Chemical-free scale management"
- "Salt-free water conditioning"
- "Helps reduce scale-related problems"
- "Low-maintenance, inline solution"
- "Protects pipes, geysers and equipment from scale build-up"
- "Conditions water by altering how scale-forming minerals behave, helping reduce scale build-up"
