## ⛔ DO NOT CLAIM / COMPLIANCE SAFETY RULES (applies to every generated creative)

No prompt may instruct the AI to render — and no output may contain — any of the following:

- ❌ **Disease-treatment / therapeutic claims:** heart, artery, kidney, kidney-stone, diabetes, blood sugar, blood pressure, wound-healing, arthritis, gout, pain relief, "toxins," "free radicals," "regulates body pH."
- ❌ **"Magnetized water improves health"** / any drinking-water wellness benefit.
- ❌ **"NASA technology"** unless verifiable proof supplied.
- ❌ **"Removes hardness minerals"** unless technically proven for this device.
- ❌ **"Turns hard water into soft water" / "softens"** — unless correctly reframed as conditioning.
- ❌ **Guaranteed/100% scale removal.**
- ❌ **Exaggerated savings/ROI without data.**
- ❌ **Fake client logos, certifications, awards, reviews, testimonials.**
- ❌ **Medical/wellness/spa imagery**, organs, pulse lines, lab coats implying medical authority.

**Always prefer this safe language in prompts:** chemical-free scale management · salt-free water conditioning · helps reduce scale-related problems · low-maintenance inline solution · industrial/commercial/residential/agricultural applications · proof-led, engineering-grade, honest.

> **Every Master Prompt below ends with a hard compliance line. Never delete it.**

## Universal AI Image Prompting System

A reusable framework across all image AIs. Defaults reflect AquaTreat's indigo+white+aqua, engineered-clean identity.

- **Lighting:** clean, even, slightly cool daylight; soft studio light on product; no dramatic moody glows.
- **Composition:** generous negative space, single focal point, left-aligned text blocks, modular.
- **Emotional framing:** trust, competence, reliability. Calm, not loud.
- **Realism level:** photoreal for product/installations; clean flat-vector for diagrams/infographics.
- **Texture:** matte, crisp; subtle halftone dot-grid motif; brushed-metal only for product housing.
- **Background philosophy:** white/surface or indigo solids; real plant-room/home contexts; never busy.
- **Camera feel:** 35–50mm, straight-on or slight 3/4 for product; honest, documentary for installs.
- **Editorial direction:** premium-industrial, restrained, spec-confident.
- **Contrast:** strong type contrast (indigo on white / white on indigo); one aqua highlight.
- **Spatial density:** airy for B2C; controlled-dense for B2B specs.
- **Colour restraint:** indigo `#38348B`, white, ink `#1B1B2F`; aqua `#13B6C9` ≤5%. No neon, no purple-pink gradients.
- **Premium cues:** white space, alignment, real proof, clean type — not gloss or effects.
- **Infographic behaviour:** accurate, simple, honest mechanism (conditioning, NOT mineral removal).
- **UI styling:** flat, small-radius cards, 2px line icons.

## 6. Style-Specific Prompt Add-Ons (most-used)

## 7. Text-Length Rules for Image Generation

| Creative Type | Text Limit |
|---|---|
| Less-text post | 5–12 words |
| Promotional ad | 15–35 words total |
| Carousel cover | 8–18 words |
| Educational slide | 30–60 words |
| Text-heavy 50% | 60–100 words |
| Text-heavy 75% | 100–160 words |
| Text-heavy 100% | 80–180 words |
| Infographic | 6 callouts max, 4–8 words each |
| Festival post | 20–50 words |

**Generate via AI:** backgrounds, layouts, product/scene imagery, icon-style diagrams.
**Finalize manually (Canva/Figma):** exact logo placement, precise NAP/contact text, legal disclaimers, spec tables — AI mangles small text and numbers.

## 8. Prompt Writing Rules
**Do:** specify exact hexes + sizes; name the design type; keep one CTA; state the compliance line every time; request honest/clean tone; ask for editable text zones.
**Don't:** let the AI free-style claims; allow health/wellness imagery; request "soft water" wording; accept AI-rendered fine print as final; allow more than one accent colour.

## 11. Medical / Legal / Compliance Safety Rules (India)
- **Acts/codes:** Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954; ASCI Code; Consumer Protection Act (misleading ads).
- **Banned phrases:** see top box. **Safe phrases:** chemical-free scale management; salt-free conditioning; helps reduce scale-related problems.
- **Disclaimer:** "Results vary with water chemistry, flow rate and installation conditions."
- **Proof rule:** named clients/awards/certifications only with confirmed permission + evidence.

## 12. Final QA Checklist (before accepting any creative)
- **Brand:** ▢ indigo/white + single aqua ▢ logo + clear space ▢ correct NAP, Pune.
- **Design:** ▢ one focal point ▢ ≤3 type sizes ▢ clean, no clutter ▢ no neon/gradient-purple.
- **Text:** ▢ readable ▢ no overlap on product/faces ▢ correct contact info ▢ within text-length limit.
- **Imagery:** ▢ real/clean ▢ no spa/wellness/medical.
- **Compliance (gate):** ▢ no health/therapeutic claim ▢ no unqualified softening/removal ▢ no NASA-without-proof ▢ no guarantee/100% ▢ no exaggerated savings ▢ no fake proof ▢ disclaimer present if results referenced.
- **Fit:** ▢ matches design type ▢ matches research positioning (honest, engineering-grade).
