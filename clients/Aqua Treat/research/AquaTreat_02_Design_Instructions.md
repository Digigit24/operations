## ⛔ DO NOT CLAIM / COMPLIANCE SAFETY RULES (read before designing anything)

These rules override all creative and aesthetic decisions. If a design needs a banned claim to work, the design is wrong — change the design, not the rule.

### Absolutely banned in any creative, caption, headline, or graphic
- ❌ **Any disease-treatment or therapeutic claim.** No heart/artery, kidney/kidney-stone, diabetes/blood-sugar, blood-pressure, wound-healing, arthritis, gout, acidity/pH-of-the-body, "toxins," "free radicals," or any "magnetized water improves health / cures / treats" claim. *(These are the exact claims currently on the website's Healthcare page — they must never appear in our work and likely breach India's Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 and ASCI rules.)*
- ❌ **"Magnetized water improves health"** or any wellness/medical benefit of drinking the water.
- ❌ **"NASA technology"** — banned unless documented, verifiable proof is supplied by the client.
- ❌ **"Removes hardness minerals"** — banned unless technically proven for this specific device.
- ❌ **"Turns hard water into soft water" / "softens water"** — banned as an unqualified claim. May only be used if correctly reframed (see safe language).
- ❌ **Guaranteed scale removal** ("removes 100% scale," "eliminates scale completely").
- ❌ **Exaggerated savings / ROI without data** ("save 50% on bills," "₹X saved") unless backed by client-supplied evidence.
- ❌ **Fake or unverified proof** — no invented client logos, certifications, awards, ratings, reviews, or testimonials. Named clients (Tata Steel, UltraTech, BPCL, etc.) may only be used with confirmed permission + substantiation.

### Safe, approved positioning language (use these instead)
- ✅ "Chemical-free scale management"
- ✅ "Salt-free water conditioning"
- ✅ "Helps reduce scale-related problems"
- ✅ "Low-maintenance, inline solution"
- ✅ "Protects pipes, geysers and equipment from scale build-up"
- ✅ "For industrial, commercial, residential and agricultural applications"
- ✅ "Engineering-grade, proof-led, honest" category positioning
- ✅ Qualified mechanism: "conditions water by altering how scale-forming minerals behave, helping reduce scale build-up" (NOT "removes minerals")

> **Mechanism honesty rule:** the device *conditions*; it does not *soften by removing minerals*. Every visual that explains "how it works" must reflect this. Never depict mineral removal.

## 1. Brand Personality

AquaTreat is the **engineer in the room** — calm, competent, and honest in a category full of hype. Where competitors shout "miracle / world's #1 / salt-free magic," AquaTreat speaks like a reliable industrial supplier who has fitted units in real plants and knows exactly what the product does and doesn't do.

**Core traits:** Credible · Engineered · Honest · Clean · Dependable · Understated-premium.
**Design must communicate:** *"This is a serious, proven, chemical-free engineering solution — not a gadget."*

---

## 2. Audience and Emotional Tone

**Audiences (from research, in priority order):** (1) Industrial/maintenance engineers, (2) Homeowners & housing societies, (3) Commercial facility managers, (4) Agricultural buyers.

**Emotional state the design must create:** *reassurance and trust.* The dominant buyer emotion in this category is skepticism ("is this a gimmick?"). Design's job is to lower that skepticism through clarity, proof, and restraint — never through louder claims.

- B2B tone: technical, spec-forward, proof-led.
- B2C tone: clean, friendly, reassuring, value-clear.
- Shared tone: honest, uncluttered, confident.

## 4. Logo Usage Guidance

- **Asset:** single-colour indigo wordmark "Aquatreat" + dot-grid/sparkle motif. A reversed (white) version on indigo is required — generate if not supplied.
- **Placement:** top-left (web/landscape), top-centre (square/portrait social), or footer strip.
- **Size rules:** square 1080 → logo height ~7–9% of canvas; portrait 1080×1350 → ~6–8%; story 1080×1920 → ~5–7%; web banner → height ≤ 64px.
- **Clear space:** minimum = height of the "A" on all sides. Never crowd.
- **Do NOT:** stretch, recolour outside the indigo/white pair, add drop-shadows/glows, place on busy photo areas without a solid/scrim panel, or rotate the motif.
- ⬜ Pending Stage 2: confirm whether the dot-grid/sparkle motif is used as a standalone brand pattern (recommended — see Section 7).

## 5. Typography Style

⬜ Final lock Pending Stage 2 — but the direction is clear: **a clean, engineered grotesque/sans for headlines + a highly legible humanist sans for body.** No serifs (wrong era/feel), no rounded "friendly" fonts (undercuts engineering credibility), no condensed display faces.

| Element | Recommended | Alternatives (free/system) |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Space Grotesk / Sora | Archivo, system "Segoe UI"/"Arial Bold" |
| Body | Inter | Roboto, system-ui |
| Numerals/specs | Inter Tabular / IBM Plex Mono (spec tables) | Roboto Mono |

**Rules:** strong heading hierarchy (H1 ≫ H2 ≫ body); tight-but-not-cramped headline letter-spacing (-1% to -2%); body line-height 1.5–1.65; emphasis via weight (600/700) and indigo colour, **not** italics or all-caps paragraphs; all-caps allowed only for short labels/eyebrows with +4–6% tracking.

## 6. Layout Principles

- **System:** clean grid, generous white space, left-aligned text, modular cards. Industrial-clean, not decorative.
- **Composition:** one clear focal point per creative; clear eyebrow → headline → support → CTA flow.
- **Safe zones:** keep text within 8–10% margins; for stories keep critical content out of the top 14% / bottom 18% (UI overlap).
- **Hierarchy:** size + weight + colour do the work; avoid more than 3 type sizes per creative.
- **Density:** B2B can be slightly denser (specs); B2C should be airy.

---

## 7. Visual Motifs and Recurring Elements

- **Primary motif:** the logo's **halftone dot-grid + four-point sparkle** — reuse as a subtle brand pattern (corner accents, section dividers, dotted "flow" lines suggesting water movement through a pipe). Keep it subtle and monochrome (indigo or aqua at low opacity).
- **Secondary motif:** clean inline-pipe / flow diagrams (honest mechanism visuals).
- **Avoid:** literal cartoon water droplets, splashes, swirly "aqua" clip-art, glossy 3D bevels.
- ⬜ Pending Stage 2: festival/occasion motif treatment (only if client wants festival posts).

---

## 8. Photography / Illustration Direction

- **Preferred subjects:** real product unit on a pipe/main line; clean installations (industrial plant rooms, society pump rooms, kitchens); before/after scale on fittings (honest, not exaggerated); engineers/technicians at work.
- **Quality rules:** real, well-lit, in-context photography over stock; if stock is unavoidable, choose neutral industrial/clean-home scenes, never "spa wellness water" imagery.
- **Industrial/professional direction:** crisp, slightly cool tone to match indigo; show scale of equipment for B2B credibility.
- **Avoid:** wellness/spa imagery, people drinking water for "health," glowing "energized water," lab coats implying medical authority, anything implying disease treatment.
- ⬜ Pending Stage 2: lock the exact photo grade/LUT once references exist.

---

## 9. Icon Style Guidance

- **Style:** line icons, 2px stroke, rounded joints, single-colour (indigo default, aqua for active/highlight).
- **Themes:** pipe/flow, shield/protection, leaf (eco/chemical-free), tools/maintenance, factory/home/farm/building (application verticals), warranty seal.
- **Avoid:** filled glossy icons, multicolour gradient icons, medical/health icons (heart, pulse, organs — banned by compliance), emoji as icons.

---

## 10. CTA Button Style

- **Shape:** rectangle with small radius (6–8px). Confident, not pill-soft.
- **Colours:** primary CTA = Aqua `#13B6C9` bg + ink/white text, OR Indigo bg + white text; hover = Accent Dark `#0E9AAB`. One primary CTA per view.
- **Text style:** sentence case, action-led, 2–4 words.
- **Icon:** optional arrow or WhatsApp glyph; no glow.
- **Approved CTA copy:** "Get a quote" · "Free water check" · "Talk to an engineer" · "Request a site assessment" · "WhatsApp us" · "Book installation" · "See applications."
- **Banned CTA copy:** anything implying cure/health/guarantee ("Heal your water," "Guaranteed soft water").
- **Placement:** hero, after proof, sticky mobile bar (call + WhatsApp).

## 12. Copy Tone

Honest, technical-but-clear, confident, calm. Short sentences. Lead with the operational outcome.

**Good examples:**
- "Chemical-free scale management for your pipes and equipment."
- "Salt-free water conditioning. Fitted inline. Low maintenance."
- "Helps reduce scale build-up across industrial, commercial, home and farm water lines."
- "Engineered in Pune. Built for hard water."
- "Talk to an engineer about your water."

**Avoid (with examples):**
- ❌ "Miracle water that cures your body" (health claim)
- ❌ "Turns hard water into soft water instantly" (unqualified softening claim)
- ❌ "World's #1 / NASA-powered" (unverified)
- ❌ "Removes 100% of minerals" (false + banned)

## 13. Social Media Design Style Types

For each type: use case + rules + watch-outs. ⬜ Reference-tuned specifics pending Stage 2.

1. **Photorealistic** — real product/installation hero shots. Rule: keep text minimal, let proof speak. Watch-out: no wellness imagery.
2. **Light background** — white/surface, indigo text, aqua accent. Default for clean educational posts.
3. **Dark background** — indigo/primary-dark, white text. For bold statements/proof.
4. **Infographic** — honest mechanism/comparison diagrams. Rule: accurate, no mineral-removal depiction.
5. **Info + photorealistic** — product photo + 3–5 callouts.
6. **Text-heavy 50 / 75 / 100%** — educational/comparison/FAQ carousels; maintain hierarchy and white space.
7. **Festival / occasion** — restrained, brand-coloured, no claims. ⬜ Pending Stage 2.
8. **Offer / ad creative** — clear single offer ("Free water check"), one CTA; no exaggerated savings.
9. **Promotional** — application-specific (industrial/home/farm).
10. **Carousel cover** — bold indigo, one hook line, motif accent.
11. **Educational carousel slide** — one idea per slide, consistent footer/logo.

## 14. Strong Do and Don't Rules (AquaTreat-specific)

**Do:** lead with honest scale-control benefit · use indigo+white+one aqua accent · show real product/installations · cite proof only when substantiated · keep one CTA per creative · maintain NAP consistency · keep it clean and engineered.

**Don't:** make any health/therapeutic claim · say "softens/removes minerals" unqualified · use "NASA" without proof · invent proof/reviews/awards · use spa/wellness or medical imagery · overcrowd · use neon/gradient-purple · show "Mumbai" · promise guaranteed/100% results.

## 15. Industry-Specific Safety and Compliance Notes

- **Jurisdiction:** India — Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954; ASCI Code; Consumer Protection Act (misleading ads).
- **Highest-risk content:** anything echoing the current Healthcare page. Treat all health/therapeutic angles as banned.
- **Efficacy claims:** the category is scientifically contested (see research §2). Keep all claims to *conditioning / scale-management* scope, qualified, and never absolute.
- **Safer alternative language:** see the compliance box at the top + Section 3 of the AI Prompting Guide.
- **Disclaimer suggestion (where results are referenced):** "Results vary with water chemistry, flow rate and installation conditions."
