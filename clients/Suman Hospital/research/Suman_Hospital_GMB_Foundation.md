### Finding 3 — No GBP secondary categories are set.

The current configuration has Primary = `Hospital` and **zero secondaries**. v1 recommended 9 secondaries. This is the single biggest unclaimed Map Pack relevance lever in the entire profile.

### Finding 4 — Every post uses LEARN_MORE CTA. Zero use Call Now.

Of the **34 posts published in the 90-day window**, 100% use the `LEARN_MORE` CTA — which links through to the broken legacy website. For a 24/7 emergency-positioned hospital, this is operationally backward. Posts about emergency, ICU, or urgent symptoms should use `CALL_NOW`. Posts about consultations should use `BOOK`. The current cadence (~11 posts/month) is also above the v1 sustainable target — and three of those 34 posts are duplicates (the same Dental Treatments post published 3 times within 1 minute on March 24).

### Finding 5 — The GBP business name is keyword-stuffed and at policy risk.

Current GBP name:
> `Suman Multispecialty Hospital & ICU–Best Physician,Diabetologist,Cardiologist | Best Hospital in Moshi Chikhali PCMC Bhosari`

This violates Google's Business Profile naming guidelines (no descriptors, no superlatives, no keyword stuffing). It is currently helping rankings for terms like "best hospital in Moshi" — but it exposes the listing to suspension at any moment. Name remediation is needed but must be **timed and sequenced** to avoid triggering re-verification while the website indexing crisis (per SEO Foundation v3, Section 2) is being resolved.

These five findings reset the priority order. Sections 1–13 below operationalize each.
