**What NSP is:** A 15-year-old academic society of neurologists and neurosurgeons in Pune. Its product is continuing neurology education — delivered through monthly clinical meets, a biennial national conference (Approach Meet), an annual PG-focused meeting, invited international and national lectures, and selective public-awareness work.

## BRAND POSITIONING

### Core positioning
NSP's strongest differentiated positioning is as a regional society that behaves like an institution — with international convening power (ICTRIMS 2026: 8 international + 42 national faculty), a 15-year unbroken academic track record, and the capability to build a structured digital knowledge archive.

### Three positioning options (⬜ client to confirm in Step 06)

**A. "The Pune neurology knowledge archive"** — a society whose product is structured, permanent academic content. Lecture videos, case discussions, PG curriculum. Editorial, restrained, library-like aesthetic. Best for long-term authority.

**B. "The PG-friendly society"** — distinctive value is support of postgraduate education. PG Approach Meeting as the signature programme. Year-round PG content stream. Contemporary but not informal.

**C. "The international-convening regional society"** — defined by ability to bring tier-one international faculty to Pune. Premium, global, conference-grade aesthetic. Heritage Ochre more prominent.

### What NSP must NOT market as
- ❌ Patient acquisition vehicle for member clinics
- ❌ Pharma or device commercial promotion
- ❌ Generic consumer health information portal

### Logo
Multi-coloured woven-brain mark in a grey circular badge with "NEUROLOGICAL SOCIETY OF PUNE" in white around the upper arc. The multi-colour brain is the logo's identity — never recolour it, never crop it, never use the brain mark alone on external assets. Logo is the brand's one permitted burst of colour; rest of the system stays disciplined.

### Brand Voice ✅ LOCKED
Confident, never boastful · Specific, never generic · Peer-to-peer, never patient-friendly · Brief, never breathless · Active, never passive.

**Tone words:** authoritative · scholarly · collegial · considered.

**Tone words to avoid:** friendly · welcoming · caring · compassionate (these belong to clinics, not societies).

**Good copy examples:**
- "Established in 2010 to propagate advances in neurology."
- "Monthly clinical meets across Pune hospitals — case-based, interactive, peer-led."
- "Eight international faculty. Forty-two national experts. Three days."

**Copy to avoid:**
- "Welcome to NSP — your friendly neurology family!"
- "World-class neurological excellence"
- "Trusted by patients across India" (NSP doesn't serve patients)
- "Limited seats — register now!"

## AUDIENCE PROFILES (PEER-FOCUSED — NOT PATIENTS)

1. **Senior Consultant** — Mid-to-late career, 15+ years, established practice. Engages for peer presence, chairing, mentoring. Needs: high-signal programmes, named oration invitations, leadership roles.
2. **Mid-Career Neurologist** — 5–15 years post-DM/MCh, building reputation. Needs: case-presentation opportunities, CME credit, sub-specialty programming.
3. **Neurology PG** — DM or MCh resident at Pune institutions (BJ, AFMC, Sancheti, Ruby Hall, etc.). High learning hunger, exam-driven, future member. Needs: PG-aimed explainers, recorded lectures, "approach to X" content.
4. **National / International Faculty** — Senior neurologist invited as speaker. Needs: well-run programmes, strong moderation, recorded outputs, no disorganised logistics.
5. **Concerned Public Member** — Occasional audience via public awareness content. **Intentionally limited — do not over-index here; it risks diluting NSP's academic identity.**

1. **Neurological Society of India (NSI)** — national apex body

3. **Bombay Neuro Society / Mumbai Neurosciences Society** — peer regional society

**Positioning gap:** Most Indian regional neurology societies look and feel identical — generic medical-association websites, no digital knowledge layer. The gap is a society that behaves like an institution. NSP can occupy this lane.

## COMPLIANCE RULES (NSP-SPECIFIC)

### What applies
- **NMC Code of Medical Ethics** — applies to all member doctors and to society communications involving member doctors. No soliciting patients for member practices.
- **Societies Registration Act** — governance, election, and reporting norms.
- **Pharma / industry sponsorship transparency** — when sponsors appear at NSP events, disclosure is required in academic content.

### Hard guardrails for all NSP content
1. No content shall function as a referral to any specific member doctor's clinic or practice.
2. No content shall claim specific treatment outcomes for individual patients.
3. No content shall make commercial promises (cure, guarantee, "best in Pune," etc.).
4. Public-awareness content shall always include "consult your doctor" framing.
5. Sponsored content must be transparently labelled.
6. All clinical / public-health content must be reviewed by qualified society members before publication.

### CTA verbs — allowed vs forbidden

✅ Allowed: Read / Watch / Register / View / Download / Become a member / Explore / See the schedule

❌ Forbidden: Book appointment · Consult Dr. X · Get treatment · Find a doctor · Free consultation · Call now · Limited slots · Don't miss

### Design compliance
- ❌ No clinical iconography (stethoscope, syringe, pill, heart-line, caduceus, hospital cross)
- ❌ No stock photography of doctors
- ❌ No generic brain icons — the official logo is the only brain mark
- ❌ No gradient overlays on photos
- ❌ No auto-popup CTAs — inappropriate for a society
