## ⛔ READ FIRST: CLAIMS WE NEVER PUT ON A CREATIVE

These rules override every creative and aesthetic decision. If a design only works with a banned claim, change the design and keep the rule.

### Banned in any headline, graphic, caption or ad
- ❌ **"100% placement"**, "guaranteed job", "job guarantee" or "assured placement". The live homepage currently says *"100% placement support"*. Our creatives must not repeat it.
- ❌ **"#1", "Pune's Best Institute", "No.1", "Top-ranked"** or any superlative ranking without a named, dated, third-party source.
- ❌ **Salary figures** ("earn ₹50,000/month", "packages up to ₹X LPA") unless the client supplies verified, dated, consented alumni data.
- ❌ **Recruiter/studio logos used as a promise** (Amazon, Prime Focus, MPC, Tata Elxsi, Yash Raj etc.). Use a logo only with a named, dated, consented alumni placement behind it, and only in a "where our alumni work" context.
- ❌ **Degree / UGC / university language** ("B.Voc", "UGC-recognised degree", "Datta Meghe affiliation") until the client confirms it in writing. This is still **[UNVERIFIED]** in research.
- ❌ **"Only institute teaching AI"** or "AI-proof careers". MAAC and ZICA already claim AI, and nobody can promise a job is AI-proof.
- ❌ **AI-generated artwork presented as student work.** If an image is AI-made, it may never be captioned, tagged or implied as "student work", "made by our student", "portfolio" or "showreel".
- ❌ **AI-generated people presented as real students, alumni or faculty**, or in testimonial or review layouts.
- ❌ **Invented reviews, ratings or counts.** Quote the Google rating or review count only from a live GBP check on the day of design. The site widget showed "212 Google reviews" on 28 Sep 2026; re-check before use.
- ❌ **Fake urgency**, such as "Only 2 seats left!" or "Last day!", unless the intake deadline is real and client-confirmed.
- ❌ **"Arena 25+ years legacy"** or other franchise-level claims until the wording is confirmed against Aptech/Arena franchise guidelines.

### Safe, approved language
- ✅ "Placement assistance": always paired with "Ask how our placement support works"
- ✅ "Practical, portfolio-first training"
- ✅ "Build work you can show"
- ✅ "Mentor-reviewed projects"
- ✅ "Labs with a pen tablet at every workstation" (a verified site claim)
- ✅ "80% practical, 20% theory" (a website claim; keep it as the institute's stated approach, not as a measured outcome)
- ✅ "Central Pune campus on JM Road, Shivajinagar"
- ✅ "AI-assisted workflows, taught alongside strong fundamentals"
- ✅ "Rated by our students on Google" (with the live number only when it has been checked)

## PART A: VISUAL CATEGORY RESEARCH (strategic territories, not yet final identity)

### A1. What the category looks like (Pune animation / VFX / gaming / design institutes)
| Dimension | Category norm | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Colours | Black cinematic backgrounds, neon blue/purple/cyan gradients, glowing edges, lens flares, brand red or orange | Signals "entertainment tech" but is now a cliché, and every competitor post blurs into the next |
| Typography | Heavy geometric or condensed display sans in ALL CAPS, often with 3D bevels, chrome or glow | Energetic but noisy; reads like a 2012 gaming poster |
| Photography | Stock students pointing at laptops, VR-headset "wow" faces, floating CGI heads, astronauts, robots, superhero composites | Low trust: prospects know these aren't the institute's students |
| Layout | Hero headline + form + software-logo wall + recruiter-logo wall + "100% placement" badge | Conversion-familiar but interchangeable |
| Proof | Recruiter logos, "#1 / best / premier", student counts | Unauditable; parents are sceptical |
| Clichés | "Turn your passion into profession", "Unleash your creativity", "Hollywood-level", sparkles, glitch text | Everyone says them |

**Premium signals in this category:** real student renders and showreel frames used at full bleed, process breakdowns (sketch → wireframe → final), calm dark neutral space, restrained type, one confident accent, real labs and faculty, dated and specific proof.

**Low-trust signals:** stock imagery, neon-on-neon, spelling errors (the site has "Get Stared"), generic superlatives, crowded logo walls, obvious template layouts, fake-looking 3D text.

**What competitors overuse:** neon glow (MAAC, ZICA), numeric claim badges (Frameboxx: "95% placements", "45,000+ trained"), urgency framing (ZICA), software-logo walls (almost everyone).

**What Arena JM Road should avoid:** all of the above, plus using Arena's red and yellow as big flat fills that make posts look like a local shop banner.

**Visual gap:** nobody in Pune owns an **editorial, process-led "studio" look** where student work is the hero and the design itself behaves like a production pipeline (frames, layers, keyframes, critique notes). That is exactly Arena's positioning, "portfolio before promises", made visible.

**What would stand out:** a charcoal "studio monitor" base; real work in a clean frame; a single yellow highlight that echoes the logo's yellow band; small monospace production labels (timecode, frame numbers, software tags); and generous negative space. It should feel like a studio's internal review deck, not an institute flyer.

### A2. Recommended strategic territories
| Territory | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Colour | **"Studio Dark + Arena Signal"**: charcoal and graphite base, warm off-white paper for light posts, logo yellow as the one vivid highlight, logo red reserved for CTA and brand moments, steel grey for structure |
| Photography | Real campus, real students at pen tablets and dual monitors, faculty critique over the shoulder, screens showing work-in-progress, JM Road wayfinding |
| Typography | Condensed or wide grotesk display (Archivo family) + DM Sans body (already on the website) + monospace labels for "production" details |
| Icons / illustration | Thin 2px line icons built from production symbols: keyframe diamond, camera frame, layer stack, pen nib, node graph, game controller |
| Layout | Viewport/"safe-frame" composition with corner brackets, left-aligned editorial hierarchy, a single focal image |
| Social | Portfolio-first feed: the work is the post and the type supports it |
| Ads | Work-first hooks ("From sketch to final render in [X] weeks"), a proof strip, and one clear CTA (Book a Free Demo / WhatsApp) |

> ⬜ **Pending Stage 2:** lock these territories after the team rates 4–5 test carousels against real Arena reference designs (existing IG posts, campus photos, student renders).

## 1. Brand Personality

Arena Animation JM Road is **the studio mentor in central Pune**: a working creative who has shipped real projects, sits beside you at the pen tablet, tells you honestly what's working, and shows you exactly what to build next.

**Core traits:** Practical · Mentor-led · Honest · Energetic-but-disciplined · Craft-proud · Welcoming to beginners.

**The design must communicate:** *"Real work gets made here, and you can see it."*

**It must NOT communicate:** hype, a gaming-café aesthetic, desperation to admit students, or corporate-coaching-class blandness.

## 2. Audience and Emotional Tone

| Audience (priority order) | What they need to feel in 3 seconds |
|---|---|
| **1. Creative student, 16–21** (after 10th/12th, early college, gamer/creator) | "This is where people like me make cool, real things, and I could start even if I can't draw well yet." |
| **2. Parent / guardian, 38–55** (payer and risk manager) | "This is structured, legitimate and safe, and I can see what my child will actually learn and produce." |
| **3. College student, 18–23** (skill alongside degree) | "I can build a portfolio on weekends without dropping college." |
| **4. Career switcher, 21–30** | "This is a mature, fast, credible route into design, UI/UX, motion or 3D." |
| **5. Creator / freelancer, 16–32** | "I'll get real critique and finish work, instead of collecting tutorials." |

**Master emotion:** *clarity backed by proof.* Excitement is welcome, but it must always sit next to evidence.

**Tone by audience:**
- Students: visual, direct, encouraging, a little playful; bilingual (English + Marathi/Hindi phrases) where natural.
- Parents: calm, factual, respectful, low hype, with plenty of white space.
- Switchers and creators: mature, specific, outcome-led; no school-like "fun learning" tone.

## 4. Logo Usage Guidance

**Logo asset:** the boxed "ARENA / ANIMATION" mark (red wordmark, yellow band, grey frame) + the "JM ROAD – PUNE" descriptor.

### Placement
- **Default:** top-left, aligned to the left safe margin.
- **Alternative:** bottom-right inside the footer strip for work-first posts where the top must stay clean.
- **Never** in the centre of a post, except on a dedicated brand announcement.

### Size rules
| Format | Logo width |
|---|---|
| 1080×1080 square | 200–240px |
| 1080×1350 portrait | 220–260px |
| 1080×1920 story | 240–280px (top 250px is the UI zone, so place the logo at y ≥ 260px) |
| 1920×700 / 1600×600 banner | 240–300px |
| Carousel inner slides | 140–170px (small, consistent corner) |

### Clear space
- The minimum clear space on every side equals the height of the "A" in "ARENA".
- No text, icons or image edges may enter the clear space.

### On backgrounds
- The logo box has its own white/yellow fill and works on dark or light. On busy photos, place it on a **Graphite plate** with 16px padding, or on the footer strip.
- The "JM ROAD – PUNE" descriptor may be set separately in Archivo Bold, Off-White on dark and Ink on light.

### Do not
- ❌ Recolour, outline, add glow, bevel, 3D extrude or drop shadow.
- ❌ Stretch, rotate, skew, or crop the grey frame.
- ❌ Remove the yellow band or place the wordmark without its box.
- ❌ Let an AI image model redraw the logo. **Always place the real logo file manually.**
- ❌ Place it over faces, hands or the focal area of student work.
- ❌ Use the old name variants ("Arena Animation Pune Flagship Centre", "Arena Animation Jm Road").

## 5. Typography Style

The website already uses **DM Sans** (body), **Poppins** (headings) and **Archivo**. We keep the family continuity and sharpen the hierarchy.

| Role | Primary | Alternatives (if unavailable) | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display headline** | **Archivo** ExtraBold / Black (Condensed or SemiExpanded) | Anton, Bebas Neue Pro, Oswald Bold | Tight tracking (-1% to -2%), sentence case or Title Case; ALL CAPS only for ≤4-word hooks |
| **Sub-headline** | **Archivo** SemiBold | Poppins SemiBold, Inter SemiBold | Sentence case |
| **Body** | **DM Sans** Regular/Medium | Inter, Poppins Regular | 1.35–1.5 line height |
| **Production labels** | **JetBrains Mono** Medium | Space Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Roboto Mono | ALL CAPS, +8% tracking, small: timecodes, frame numbers, software tags, "WIP", "STEP 02" |
| **Marathi/Hindi** | **Mukta** SemiBold / Bold | Hind, Noto Sans Devanagari | Never fake-bold; match x-height to Archivo |

### Hierarchy rules
- One headline per creative. Maximum 3 type sizes per slide (headline, support, label).
- Headline : body size ratio ≥ 3:1.
- Left-align by default; centre only for festival and less-text posts.
- Highlight 1–3 words with the yellow bar and never more.
- No outlined, chrome, 3D, glowing, gradient-filled or distorted text. The work provides the drama and the type stays clean.

### Per design type
| Type | Typography rule |
|---|---|
| Promotional / admission | Archivo Black 72–96px headline (1080 canvas), DM Sans 30–34px support, mono label for intake date |
| Festival | Archivo Bold or Mukta Bold greeting, 64–84px, centred; short DM Sans line |
| Infographic | Archivo SemiBold 40–48px headers, DM Sans 26–30px callouts, mono step numbers "01 / 02 / 03" |
| Text-heavy | Archivo SemiBold 48–56px headline, DM Sans 28–32px body, 1.5 line height, max 55 characters per line |
| Carousel cover | Archivo Black 84–110px, ≤8 words, one yellow highlight |
| Educational slide | Archivo SemiBold 44–52px heading + DM Sans 28–30px, mono slide counter "03/07" |

## 6. Layout Principles

### 6.1 Core system: "The Viewport"
Every creative is composed like a frame in a 3D viewport or edit timeline:
- **12-column grid**, 64px outer margin on 1080-wide canvases.
- **Safe-frame corner brackets** (thin Steel Grey L-shapes) can mark the focal image. They are the signature motif.
- A **top label row** in mono (e.g. `ARENA JM ROAD · VFX · 00:00:24:12`).
- A **bottom zone** for CTA or footer.
- One **focal element** (a student render, a person at work, or a single bold statement).

### 6.2 Composition rules
- One focal point per creative. If two things compete, remove one.
- Imagery takes 40–70% of the canvas area on photo-led posts.
- Text never covers faces, hands, pen tablets in use, or the focal area of artwork.
- Use asymmetric editorial layouts (e.g. 7/5 column split). Avoid perfectly centred stacks except for festival and less-text posts.
- Leave at least 25% negative space on every post.
- Maximum 2 font families per creative (+ mono labels).

### 6.3 Safe zones
| Format | Safe zone |
|---|---|
| 1080×1080 | 64px all sides |
| 1080×1350 | 64px sides; keep critical content inside the central 1080×1080 because the grid preview crops it |
| 1080×1920 story/reel cover | Top 250px and bottom 340px free of critical text and CTAs (IG UI) |
| Carousel | 64px sides; keep a continuous visual element crossing the slide edge only when intentional |
| 1920×700 banner | Critical content inside the central 1400px; 80px top/bottom |

### 6.4 Hierarchy order (the reading path)
1. Focal image / work
2. Headline (with highlighted key word)
3. Support line or 2–3 key points
4. Proof chip (e.g. "Pen tablet at every workstation", "Rated by students on Google")
5. CTA
6. Logo + footer

| **Critique note** | Handwritten-style annotation or red-pen circle on a WIP image | Faculty tips, "mentor review" posts |

- **Gudi Padwa / Marathi New Year:** Gudi as a stylised 2D animation frame sequence.
- **Holi:** colour powder as a VFX particle simulation (still in brand-neutral dark space).
- **Independence / Republic Day:** tricolour as three animation timeline tracks, not flag clip-art.
- **Teachers' Day (5 Sep):** real faculty photo + critique motif.
- **International Animation Day (28 Oct):** hero opportunity; showcase student work.
- **Children's Day / World Gaming Day / Ada Lovelace Day:** optional, tie to courses.

> Festival rule: the religious or cultural symbol is always treated with dignity. No distortion, no humour at the deity's expense, no sales copy on the deity.

## 8. Photography / Illustration Direction

### Preferred scenes and subjects
1. Students (17–24, Indian, mixed gender) working at **pen tablets and dual monitors** with work visible on screen: Maya/Blender viewport, Nuke/After Effects comp, Unreal level, Figma frames, Photoshop artwork.
2. **Faculty critique**: mentor leaning in, pointing at a screen, student taking notes.
3. **Sketching class**: pencil, paper, figure drawing (fundamentals-first).
4. **Labs** at Arena JM Road: rows of workstations, each with a pen tablet.
5. **Chroma/green room, gaming room, library** (verified facilities).
6. **Parent counselling**: parent + student + counsellor at a table with a course roadmap printout.
7. **JM Road campus**: Mangalya building entrance, wayfinding ("above Skechers"), 4th-floor signage.
8. **Student work** at full bleed: renders, comps, game screens, posters, UI case studies. *Only real, consented student work, credited with name and course.*

### Image quality rules
- Real photography first; AI imagery only for **concept/background/illustrative** use and never presented as Arena's students, campus or work.
- Natural light or practical monitor glow; no heavy teal-orange grading.
- Screens must show plausible, clean software UI, not garbled AI text.
- Faces must be natural, with correct hands and fingers and a correct pen grip on the stylus.
- Minimum export 1080px wide and sharp; no upscaled WhatsApp-compressed photos as heroes.
- **Consent:** written consent for every identifiable student; guardian consent for anyone under 18.

### Direction
- Lifestyle and editorial, not staged corporate.
- "Behind the scenes of a working studio" energy: focused, collaborative, a bit messy desks, reference sheets pinned up.

### Avoid
- ❌ Stock students pointing at laptops or giving a thumbs-up
- ❌ VR-headset "wow" faces
- ❌ Floating 3D heads, astronauts, robots, cyborgs, glowing brains
- ❌ Superhero or Hollywood composites implying studio links
- ❌ Western stock models as Arena students
- ❌ Characters that resemble copyrighted IP (Disney, Pixar, Marvel, anime, game franchises)
- ❌ Neon light trails, lens-flare explosions, glitch overlays

## 13. Social Media Design Style Types

> For every type: **⬜ Feedback notes pending Stage 2** (rate test outputs 1–10 and record approved direction here).

### 1. Photorealistic
- **Best for:** campus life, faculty critique, lab tours, "a day at Arena JM Road".
- **Rules:** a real photo at full bleed or in a safe-frame; ≤12 words; mono label top-left; logo top-left or in the footer.
- **Watch-outs:** AI "students" presented as real; screens with garbled UI; stock look.

### 2. Light background
- **Best for:** parent-facing content, FAQs, course roadmaps, admission info.
- **Rules:** Paper White base, Ink text, Yellow highlight, Steel Grey rules, lots of white space; photos in rounded 12px frames.
- **Watch-outs:** Yellow text on white (never); feeling like a school notice.

### 3. Dark background (**default brand look**)
- **Best for:** student-work showcases, VFX/game content, hooks, reels covers.
- **Rules:** Charcoal base, work as the hero, Off-White type, safe-frame brackets, ≤5% Yellow.
- **Watch-outs:** sliding into neon gaming; muddy low contrast; too many glows.

### 4. Infographic
- **Best for:** "Animation vs VFX vs Game Design", course roadmap, software pipeline, career paths.
- **Rules:** max 6 callouts, 4–8 words each; mono step numbers; line icons; keyframe-diamond timeline; one yellow active step.

## 15. Industry-Specific Safety and Compliance Notes (Education)

| Risk | Avoid | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Placement claims | "100% placement", "guaranteed job" | "Placement assistance: see how our support works" |
| Rankings | "#1", "Best in Pune" | "Rated by our students on Google" (live number only) |
| Salary | "₹6 LPA starting salary" | "Explore roles in animation, VFX, gaming and design" |
| Recognition | "UGC-approved degree" (unverified) | Remove until verified in writing |
| AI claims | "AI-proof career", "only AI institute" | "Fundamentals + AI-assisted workflows" |
| Student work | AI images labelled student work | Real, consented, credited student work only |
| Testimonials | Invented or AI-generated reviewers | Real Google reviews quoted verbatim with first name + course (with permission) |
| Minors | Identifiable under-18 students without consent | Guardian consent on file, or back/over-shoulder shots |
| Urgency | "Last 2 seats!" | Real intake dates only: "Batch starts [date]" |
| Franchise | Altering Arena logo or national claims | Follow Aptech/Arena franchise guidelines |
| Competitors | Naming or mocking MAAC, Frameboxx, ZICA | Honest comparison content only in long-form, never in ads |

**Advertising standards:** follow ASCI guidelines for educational institutions (claims must be substantiated; placement claims need verifiable data; no misleading implication of affiliation or recognition).

**Disclaimer suggestions:**
- Placement posts: *"Placement assistance is subject to course completion, attendance and portfolio assessment. Outcomes vary by individual."*
- Student-work posts: *"Artwork by [Student Name], [Course], Arena Animation JM Road. Shared with permission."*
- AI-assisted concept visuals: *"Concept visual. Not student work."* (use when there is any chance of confusion)
