### What are animation courses?

Animation courses are training programs aimed at teaching individuals the techniques, principles and software used to create animated projects, including animated films, television programs, video games, and other digital media. These courses typically cover various aspects of animation such as character design, storyboarding, modeling, rigging, texturing, lighting, and compositing.

The courses may be offered online or in a traditional classroom setting, and the curriculum can vary depending on the level and type of program. For instance, introductory courses might focus on the basics of animation and 2D animation techniques, while advanced courses may delve into more complex topics such as 3D animation, motion graphics, and visual effects.
