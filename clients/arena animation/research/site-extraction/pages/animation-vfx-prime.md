## Two skills. One program

Today’s consumers are regularly engaged with animated visual experiences, and there is a huge demand for artists to create these experiences for various purposes. Hence, [Arena Animation](https://arenapune.com/) has introduced a combination of animation & [VFX skills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_effects) training with the Animation-VFX Prime Program.
