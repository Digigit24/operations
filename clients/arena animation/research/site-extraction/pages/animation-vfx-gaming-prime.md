## About Animation VFX & Gaming Prime

The growing demand for Animation, VFX and Gaming professionals has  
significantly led to a tremendous need for professionals. Our [Animation](https://arenapune.com/), VFX  
and Gaming Prime Program is designed to fulfil this demand by equipping  
students with the latest tools & techniques. The state-of-the-art curriculum
