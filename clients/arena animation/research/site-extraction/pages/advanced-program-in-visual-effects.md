## STUDENT ENQUIRY

Immerse yourself in the realm of visual magic with the **Advanced Program in Visual Effects**. This program equips you with the skills to bring the most imaginative ideas to life through VFX. Learn to create realistic simulations, compelling compositing and breathtaking effects using tools like Houdini and Nuke. Perfect your craft and get ready to shape the visual landscapes of film and digital media.
