## UI/UX Prime OVERVIEW

Today, there’s no business that doesn’t have an online presence. Digital is slowly yet steadily becoming mainstream and seems to be the need of the hour. That’s how [UI/UX](https://arenapune.com/ui-ux-trends-that-will-shape-website-design-in-2025/) has become a fundamental part of the digital world; be it through e-commerce, company websites or mobile apps.  
UI is focused on how a product’s surfaces look and function. there’s a growing tendency for companies to hire for very specific roles such as UX researcher or interaction designer, to cover all of the different aspects of user experience.

The [UI/UX Prime Program](https://arenapune.com) is a gateway into the revolutionary world of UI/UX through 3 terms of  
extensive learning on this extremely vast, multidisciplinary and fascinating field.
