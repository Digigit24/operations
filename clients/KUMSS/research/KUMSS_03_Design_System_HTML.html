02 · Brand Essence

What KUMSS is

KUMSS is a Maharashtrian educational trust that has quietly served rural Pune district for generations, now extending its work into skill-based vocational training. Not a coaching class. Not a SaaS brand. Not a luxury school. An established institutional sanstha anchored in the region's agrarian-industrial reality.

The philosophical anchor

॥ उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम् ॥

Uddhared ātmanā ātmānam — Bhagavad Gita 6.5 — "Lift yourself by yourself." Not decorative inscription; it is the institute's mission compressed into Sanskrit. KUMSS provides the workshop, the tools, the teachers; the student does the lifting.

The brand in one line

An established Maharashtrian educational trust offering vocational training built on multi-generational educational service — where students learn real skills to stand on their own.

Positioning to avoid: "Premium ITI," "Best ITI in Maharashtra," "100% Placement Guaranteed," "Future-ready Skill India institute," or any framing that leads with political family identity. Political associations are background context, not marketing claim.

07 · Layout System

Grid + spacing

8pt baseline grid. Spacing values in multiples of 8 (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96). Symmetric or near-symmetric compositions. Sober information density — text-rich is fine; airy minimalism is not the goal.

Breakpoints

DeviceRangeNotes
Mobile360–767pxPrimary parent-facing format · WhatsApp, Instagram
Tablet768–1023pxSecondary
Desktop1024–1439pxB2B, recruiter, counselor surfaces
Large desktop1440px+Wide hero layouts only

Layout principles

  • Symmetric or near-symmetric compositions — asymmetric reads as marketing, not institutional
  • Generous breathing room around logos and headlines
  • Sober information density — institutional surfaces respect the reader with text
  • Vertical hierarchy — institutional materials read top-down
  • Photography sits with substantial weight — no floating ghost-imagery

Section spacing reference

64px between major sections
32px between sub-sections

Ready-to-use AI prompts

Working prompt examples for Midjourney / ChatGPT / Ideogram / Recraft / Flux. Universal master DNA block + content-type-specific prompts. Full template library in KUMSS_01_AI_Prompting_Style_Guide.md.

Universal KUMSS DNA block

KUMSS Visual DNA · Use in every promptSubject: Marathi-speaking students, faculty, or trade environments at an Indian vocational training institute in semi-urban Maharashtra. Style: documentary institutional photography — the register of a Maharashtra Krishi Vigyan Kendra outreach photo, a state agriculture university annual report, or a cooperative bank's heritage brochure. Lighting: warm natural daylight, golden hour bias, soft shadows. No cold studio lighting, no premium hospitality lighting. Palette: warm saffron (#E85D04), institutional green (#1B6B3D), navy (#1A2540), warm cream (#FAF6EE). Composition: symmetric or near-symmetric framing. Subject treated with dignity and weight. Generous breathing room for headline overlay. Mood: grounded, focused, real, institutional, dignified. Avoid: SaaS landing page aesthetic, international school aesthetic, premium luxury, coaching brand hype, motivational poster register, futuristic / hologram overlays, Western stock photo models, coached smiles, pastel candy palettes.

18 · Do / Don't

Visual rules — what to do, what to avoid

Compressed checklist for designers and content creators. Anything that violates the "Don't" column should be rejected before shipping, regardless of how good it looks.

Do

  • Lead with Marathi on parent-facing surfaces
  • Use real institute photography wherever possible
  • Treat trade skills as identity-positive
  • Reference multi-decade heritage as institutional context
  • Use canonical logo with full lockup on official surfaces
  • Carry the Sanskrit shloka on institutional materials
  • Use the locked palette (saffron, green, navy, cream)
  • State specific facts when making claims
  • Use bilingual headlines on parent-facing surfaces
  • Use WhatsApp as a primary inquiry channel
  • Respond to every GMB review within 7 days
  • Use canonical NAP exactly the same across every surface

Don't

  • Reproduce "100% Placement" or quantified placement claims
  • Claim affiliations without certificate verification
  • Lead with political-family identity in marketing copy
  • Use motivational quote graphics
  • Use urgency banners or countdown timers
  • Use coaching-brand hype register
  • Use SaaS / startup vocabulary
  • Use luxury / premium-minimalist aesthetic
  • Use international-school visual register
  • Use stock photos of foreign / coached models
  • Use AI-generated Marathi text without QA
  • Use cool corporate blue palettes
  • Use pastel rainbows or neons
  • Fabricate alumni quotes or recruiter associations
  • Strip the Sanskrit shloka from institutional surfaces
