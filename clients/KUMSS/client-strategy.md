## Brand Positioning

**Brand in one paragraph (KUMSS_02 §2.1):**
KUMSS is a Maharashtrian educational trust that has quietly served rural Pune district for generations, now extending its work into skill-based vocational training through its ITI. The brand is not a private placement-coaching brand straining for premium positioning. It is an established institutional sanstha, anchored in the region's agrarian-industrial reality, committed to genuine skill education that lets a young person stand on their own two feet.

**Philosophical core (shloka):**
॥ उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम् ॥ — "Lift yourself by yourself." (Bhagavad Gita 6.5)
This is the institute's mission statement compressed into Sanskrit. Not decorative. Everything KUMSS communicates flows from this.

**Working positioning statement:**
> *"An established Maharashtrian educational trust offering vocational training built on multi-generational educational service — where students learn real skills to stand on their own."*

**Positioning archetype:** Heritage-led, institutional, skill-education. Community/heritage private ITI (Archetype 3 per KUMSS_00).

**Communication priority:** Marathi-first, English supportive.

**What KUMSS is not:** Not a coaching class. Not a private placement agency. Not a degree college. Not a brand that promises guaranteed outcomes. Not tech-driven EdTech.

## Target Audiences (7 Cohorts — KUMSS_02 §5)

**Cohort 1 — Marathi-speaking father, 35–55, semi-urban / rural** (Highest buying weight) — WhatsApp, Facebook, neighbour network — Marathi primary.

**Cohort 2 — Marathi-speaking mother, 35–55** (High) — WhatsApp, Facebook, community — Marathi primary.

**Cohort 3 — Student, 15–22, first-gen learner** (Medium-high) — Instagram, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp — Marathi + light English.

**Cohort 4 — School counselor / teacher (Baramati / South Pune)** (Medium) — Direct outreach, LinkedIn — English + Marathi.

**Cohort 5 — Local employer / recruiter** (Medium B2B) — LinkedIn, direct contact — English.

**Cohort 6 — Alumni** (Low currently, growing) — WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn — Marathi + English.

**Cohort 7 — Government / scheme administrator** (Low) — Direct, regulatory — English.

**Decision dynamic:** ITI enrollment is a family-collective decision (student + father + mother + sibling/uncle + neighbour reference). Marathi-first content reaches all decision-makers; English-only content reaches some.

**Competing alternative:** Not other ITIs — the real alternative is "send the child to work immediately." Most KUMSS communication should answer: "Why is two years of ITI better than two years of immediate work?"

## Compliance and Claim-Safety Rules

**Rule 1:** No quantified placement claims without proof. "100% Placement," "Guaranteed job" are prohibited. Use: "Active Placement Cell," "Industry-Connected Training," "Apprenticeship Pathway Support."

**Rule 2:** No affiliation claims (DGT, NCVT, DVET, ISO 9001:2015) without certificates. All four pending portal verification.

**Rule 3:** No fabricated proof — no invented alumni quotes, fictional recruiter associations, or unverified numbers.

**Rule 4:** No political-family-led marketing claims. Political associations are background context, not marketing claim.

**What can be safely claimed today:** Multi-decade educational heritage, multiple institutes under one trust, 4.9★ rating across 100+ GMB reviews, Marathi-medium accessible education, hands-on workshop-based vocational training, located in Karhati Baramati Pune.
