## Brand Identity

### Colors (Design System §3)

**Brand core — from logo:**

| Token | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--hera-magenta` | Hera Magenta | `#E80088` | Logo V-figure; one CTA per layout; one headline highlight max. Never as a background. |
| `--hera-indigo` | Hera Indigo | `#283090` | Logo wings/head; primary headlines; dark-mode backgrounds; icon strokes. Workhorse color. |

**Editorial neutrals:**

| Token | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ivory` | Warm Ivory | `#FAF7F5` | Default light background |
| `--champagne` | Champagne Beige | `#EFE1D6` | Secondary panels, cards, dividers |
| `--blush` | Soft Blush | `#E8C7D1` | Tertiary tint — never >50% of canvas |
| `--mauve` | Muted Mauve | `#B68FA3` | Captions, dividers, disclaimer text |
| `--plum` | Deep Plum | `#4B2E39` | CTA background, dark editorial panels, footer |
| `--charcoal` | Charcoal | `#2D2D2D` | Body text on light backgrounds |

**Pink budget rule:** Blush + Mauve combined ≤ 25% of canvas. Magenta ≤ 5%. Avoid "excessive pink" — the logo already carries vivid magenta.

**Color pairing:**
- Light layout: Ivory → Indigo headlines → Charcoal body → Plum CTA → Magenta once max
- Dark layout: Indigo/Plum → Ivory headlines → Champagne body → Magenta CTA

### Typography

| Role | Stack |
|---|---|
| Display / Headlines | Playfair Display (preferred) / Cormorant Garamond / DM Serif Display |
| Body / UI | Inter (preferred) / Manrope / DM Sans |

**Rules:** One serif + one sans per creative only. Never two serifs together.

| Level | Font | Weight | Tracking | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 (hook) | Playfair Display | 600 | -1% | Sentence case |
| H2 (sub) | Playfair Display Italic | 500 | 0 | Sentence case |
| Eyebrow / kicker | Inter | 600 | +8% | UPPERCASE |
| Body | Inter | 400 | 0 | Sentence case |
| Caption / disclaimer | Inter | 400 | +2% | Sentence case |
| CTA label | Inter | 600 | +4% | Title Case |

**Founder name rule:** Always show Dr. Sairandhri Kate-Shinde with credentials (MBBS, DNB OBGY, FMAS) together. Never italicize the name.

### Logo

Symbol: stylised winged figure (V-form) with indigo wings/head and magenta V-figure. Fixed asset — never recolor, stretch, skew, rotate, or add a shadow/gradient. Minimum 64px digital / 20mm print. Never place on a magenta background. Use native two-color form on light backgrounds; white-only form on dark/Indigo/Plum backgrounds.

### Voice

Calm · Reassuring · Educational · Sophisticated · Medically intelligent

**Use:** Assess, evaluate, consult, specialist, personalized, evidence-based, 15+ years, fellowship-trained
**Never use:** Guaranteed pregnancy, guaranteed IVF success, miracle treatment, 100% success, fear hooks ("Is your body failing you?"), guilt hooks, manipulation ("Your last chance to…")

### CTA Style

Rounded rectangle · 12px corner radius · Deep Plum `#4B2E39` fill · white label · soft shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(75,46,57,0.18)` · Approved labels: *Book Consultation / Schedule a Visit / Talk to Dr. Sairandhri / Call +91 88059 81909 / WhatsApp Us*

## Format System

**Canvas sizes:** IG square 1080×1080 · IG carousel 1080×1080 · IG story 1080×1920 · Meta/Google ad · LinkedIn 1200×627 · GMB post 1200×900 · Website banner 1920×800 · WhatsApp 1080×1080 or 1080×1350

**Safe zones:** IG square — 64px all sides. IG story — 220px top + 380px bottom (UI zones). GMB — all key content within central 1080×720 (Google crops edges). Carousel — identical safe zones across all slides.

**Design style types:** Photorealistic · Light Editorial · Dark Editorial · Infographic · Info+Photorealistic · Text-Heavy 50%/75%/100% · Festival/Occasion · Offer/Ad Creative · Carousel Cover · Educational Carousel Slide

**Visual subject rules:** Indian women, age-matched to topic. Calm, present expressions. No exaggerated smiles, no stock "pregnant belly hands" pose. Real skin texture. Clinic interiors: warm wood + ivory walls + soft daylight. Never OR/stirrups/exam table in frame.

**Photorealism level:** 90–95%. Visible skin texture (pores, fine lines). No plastic AI faces. Subtle film grain acceptable.
