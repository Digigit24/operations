## Brand Positioning (from Step 01 research)

**Primary:** "The boutique that teaches." Learn aari and fashion design inside a working Hadapsar boutique with 8 years of proof.
**Secondary:** Hand-embroidered bridal wear, fitted in person, delivered on time.
**Market contrast:** practitioner vs franchise college; proof vs promise; fees shown vs hidden.
**Do not compete on:** degree prestige, placement percentages, "global" positioning, lowest price.

**Differentiation angles:**
1. Live boutique classroom (learn where the work is actually made and sold)
2. Learn-to-earn pipeline (course → job-work for graduates)
3. Marathi / Hindi / English delivery, including online aari in Marathi
4. Fee transparency and a free demo class
5. In-person bridal fitting with a delivery-date promise

**Voice:** warm, direct, practical, proud of the craft. Use "hand-embroidered", "fitted", "taught here", "since 2017". Avoid "best", "No.1", "100% placement", "world-class", "DM for price".

**Visual direction (to confirm against logo):** craft atelier, warm daylight, macro embroidery. Deep jewel base (maroon/wine or deep teal) + gold-thread accent + ivory/linen neutrals. Serif headlines, clean sans body. No stock runway, no "Admission Open" red boxes, no AI-looking models.
