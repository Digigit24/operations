# SECTION 2 — MARKET & CATEGORY ANALYSIS

### Industry overview
✅ India's fashion market is projected at ₹1,696 bn in 2025 with 15%+ annual growth through 2030 [archedu.org, aaft.com]. ✅ Government skilling: ATDC (Ministry of Textiles) runs ~85 centres and has trained 313,500+ candidates in apparel vocational courses [aaft.com]. ✅ Pune has ~50 fashion-design colleges; over half of diploma programmes cost under ₹1 lakh, premium brands charge ₹1.6–5 lakh [collegedunia, shiksha, NIF Kothrud fee page].

### Two categories, two demand engines
| | Boutique (aari / bridal) | Institute (skill courses) |
|---|---|---|
| Demand driver | Weddings, festivals, occasion wear | Income need, career after 12th, hobby-to-business |
| Seasonality | Peaks Oct–Feb and Apr–Jun wedding windows; Ganpati/Diwali/Navratri | Peaks Jun–Aug (post-results) and Jan; steady for homemaker batches |
| Awareness level | Solution-aware; searches by product ("aari work blouse near me") | Mixed: problem-aware ("earn from home"), solution-aware ("aari classes near me") |
| Price sensitivity | Medium; will pay for finish and fit; compares with Instagram sellers | High for micro-classes; medium-high for diplomas |
| Trust factors | Photos of real work, reviews, delivery reliability, fit trials | Teacher credibility, real student output, certificate, placement/earning stories |
| Buying triggers | Wedding date fixed; saw a reel; friend's blouse | Batch start date; free demo; "earn ₹X/month" story |
| Fears | Fit failure, late delivery, embroidery quality, colour mismatch | Wasted fees, no real skill, no certificate value, can't earn after |

### Social demand signal
✅ "Aari work blouse design" alone has ~286K reels on Instagram; 2026 blouse trends are being set by Reels (sculpted cholis, corset blouses, sheer backs) [instagram.com/popular; vamas.in]. 🔍 The category is content-native. A boutique that films its own process has an unfair advantage over any college.

### Is the category crowded?
- **Institute side:** 🔍 Crowded at the top (INIFD, INSD, JD, NIF, TTA franchise centres) and crowded at the bottom (dozens of "Shweta / Sunita Fashion Designing Classes" in Hadapsar alone per Sulekha). **Empty in the middle:** a credible, proof-backed, practitioner-led local institute with published courses and fees.
- **Boutique side:** 🔍 Crowded on Instagram; thin in Hadapsar with 8-year track record and 180+ reviews. Jyoti Designer Studio (est. 2022, Solapur Rd) is the only local competitor running the same boutique+classes model.

### What everyone sounds like
Franchise colleges: "Best fashion design institute in Pune", "100% placement", "industry-ready", "global". Micro-classes: "Fashion designing classes, tailoring, boutique management, embroidery & zari" (identical Sulekha category dumps). Boutiques: "Customised", "all India shipping", "DM for price".

### Overused messaging
"Best", "No.1", "100% placement", "industry expert faculty", "career-oriented", "DM for details".

### Positioning gap available
🔍 **"Learn inside a working boutique from the designer whose work 186 customers rated 4.8."** Nobody in the local set can say it: colleges have no boutique; micro-classes have no proof; Jyoti Studio has 3–4 years, not 8.
