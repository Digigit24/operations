## 0. Non-negotiable claim-safety rules (apply to EVERY design)

These override any creative instruction below. A design that breaks one of these is rejected regardless of how good it looks.

**Never design, show, or imply:**
- Verified results, score improvements, or pass percentages
- Student success numbers, rank holders, selection counts
- Star ratings or review cards (e.g. "4.9★", "500+ reviews")
- "Best coaching in Pune", "No.1", "Top-ranked", or any superlative the institute can't prove
- Guaranteed JEE/NEET/board success
- Testimonials or parent quotes (none are verified yet — the website ones are template lorem-ipsum)
- Specific batch-size numbers presented as fact unless the founder confirms them

**Use these claim-safe message pillars instead:**
Concept-focused learning · Small-batch attention · Founder-led guidance · Ravet-local coaching · Logic-first teaching ("Logic over Luck") · Personal doubt-solving · Board + foundation support.

> 🟡 The founder's "IIT Delhi" credential is powerful but is currently a client-stated claim. **Do not put it in published creatives until the team confirms proof.** Once confirmed, it becomes the single strongest trust element.

## 1. Brand visual direction

**Personality:** Credible, calm, modern, and personal. Pinnacle is the *attentive neighbourhood institute led by a real, qualified teacher* — the opposite of a loud, crowded, factory-scale coaching brand.

**What every design must communicate in 3 seconds:** "Serious, trustworthy teaching — close to home, where my child is actually seen."

**Visual energy:** Quiet confidence. Generous whitespace, one clear focal point, restrained palette, sharp typography. Think *premium education / modern edtech*, not festival flyer.

**The line to hold:** Premium **but not corporate.** Warm and approachable for parents and students, while still feeling expert and dependable. No cold enterprise blandness; no childish clip-art either.

**Anti-clone rule:** Do **not** imitate the Kota-brand look (aggressive red/yellow, topper-photo grids, "rank machine" energy). Pinnacle's whole edge is being the personal, local alternative — the design must look like that alternative.

## 2. Logo usage direction

**What we have (verified from files):**
- A royal-blue uppercase **"P" monogram** with a bold black outline; the left stroke reads as a crescent/arc element. Core blue extracted at **#3862AC**, outline **#000000**.
- A horizontal **wordmark** ("name-logo") used in the site header/footer.

**⚠️ Asset gap to request:** the favicon currently sits on a **solid black background** (not transparent). For flexible use we need: (a) a **transparent-background PNG/SVG** of the monogram, and (b) a transparent wordmark, ideally in three lockups — full-color, all-white (for dark/navy backgrounds), and all-navy/black (for light backgrounds). Flag to the team before heavy creative production.

**Placement:**
- Default: top-left (website, documents) or top-center (social square posts).
- Social posts: small, in a top or bottom corner — never competing with the headline.

**Clear space:** keep padding around the logo equal to at least the height of the "P" counter (the hole in the P). Never crowd it with text or graphics.

**Minimum size:** monogram no smaller than 32 px on screen / 12 mm in print; wordmark must stay legible — if it isn't, use the monogram alone.

**Background rules:**
- On light/white surfaces → full-color or navy logo.
- On navy/blue surfaces → white logo.
- Never place the full-color blue logo on a mid-blue background (low contrast).

**Logo don'ts:** don't stretch, recolor (outside approved white/navy/full-color), add shadows/glows/gradients, rotate, outline, or place on a busy photo without a solid backing shape.

- Use more than one accent color in a single creative.

- Put low-contrast blue text on blue backgrounds.
- Introduce off-brand colors (greens, purples, neon) "for variety."

## 4. Typography direction

**Recommended pairing (confirm/lock at Step 07):**
- **Headings / Display:** `Sora` — modern, geometric, credible, slightly distinctive without being trendy. *Alternatives:* Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope.
- **Body / UI:** `Inter` — highly legible at all sizes, parent- and student-friendly. *Alternative:* Source Sans 3.
- **System fallback stack:** `-apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

> Recommended, not yet measured against the live site's current fonts. Final lock happens in the Step 07 design system.

**Hierarchy (web/creative guide):**

| Element | Font | Weight | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 / Hero | Sora | 700 | Confident, tight leading, never ALL-CAPS for long lines |
| H2 / Section | Sora | 600 | Clear breaks |
| H3 / Sub | Sora | 600 | Supporting |
| Body | Inter | 400 | Calm, readable, generous line-height (1.5–1.6) |
| Caption / label | Inter | 500 | Small, slate color, light letter-spacing |
| CTA | Inter / Sora | 600 | Short, action-led |

**Typography rules:**
- One headline focus per creative. Don't stack three competing big lines.
- Emphasis via **weight and color**, not underline-everything or italics-everywhere.
- Generous spacing — whitespace is a premium signal. Avoid wall-to-wall text.
- Letter-spacing: slightly tight on large headings, slightly open on small caps/labels.
- Never more than two fonts in one creative.

## 5. Layout system

**Core principle:** one message, one focal point, lots of breathing room.

- **Grid:** simple 12-col on web; for social, a clear safe-zone with consistent margins (≥6–8% of canvas).
- **Composition:** strong single hierarchy — headline → supporting line → one CTA. Founder photo or a clean conceptual graphic as the visual anchor, never both fighting.
- **Density:** low to medium. If it feels busy, remove an element.
- **Repeatable blocks (web):** hero → "who we are / founder" → batches/programs → why-choose (claim-safe) → process (enquire → counselling → admission) → FAQ → contact/map. *(No fake testimonials section until real ones exist.)*
- **Safe zones (social):** keep text and logo clear of edges; for Reels/Stories keep key text in the central 80% (top/bottom get covered by UI).

## 6. Photography / image direction

**Preferred (in priority order):**
1. **Real founder photography** — Abhinit Kumar teaching, explaining at a board, with students in a genuine small-batch setting. This is the single most valuable image asset; prioritise a proper photo/video shoot.
2. **Real classroom / institute** — clean, well-lit shots of the actual Ravet space, study materials, test papers.
3. **Clean conceptual graphics** — minimal science/math motifs (equations, molecule/atom line-art, graphs) as restrained accents.

**Image quality rules:** bright, clean, natural light; uncluttered backgrounds; consistent blue-leaning color grade; faces in focus.

**Avoid:**
- Generic stock "happy diverse students with thumbs-up" imagery.
- Fake "topper" photos or celebratory rank graphics.
- Cheesy 3D clip-art, overused AI faces, plastic-looking renders.
- Busy collage backgrounds.

> Until a real shoot happens, lean on **clean typographic + conceptual-graphic designs** rather than fake stock people. An honest, well-designed text/graphic post beats a fake-looking stock post for a trust-based new institute.

## 7. Founder-led creative direction

The founder is the brand's strongest honest asset. Build a recognisable founder-led visual language:

- **Consistent framing:** founder shot on a clean blue/navy or neutral background, same crop style across posts → recognisability.
- **"Logic over Luck" as a recurring concept device** — a small repeatable label/lockup for founder concept clips and posts.
- **Founder content types:** concept explainers ("the *why* behind a topic"), exam/study guidance, parent-facing reassurance, doubt-of-the-week, behind-the-scenes of small-batch teaching.
- **Tone in founder creatives:** warm, clear, no hype. He teaches; he doesn't sell.
- **Name + role lockup:** "Abhinit Kumar · Founder & Lead Faculty" — credential line added **only after proof is confirmed.**

## 8. Social media post rules

- One idea per post. Headline readable in under 2 seconds.
- Blue/navy-led, lots of whitespace, single accent for the action element.
- Logo small and consistent (corner). NAP/contact only where it adds value (offer/admission posts), not on every educational post.
- Educational > promotional. Aim for a majority of value/teaching posts vs admission posts.
- Bilingual optional (English primary; Marathi/Hindi for local reach) — keep one language dominant per creative for clarity.
- **Claim-safe always:** no ratings, no results, no superlatives (Section 0).

**Text limits (image creatives):**

| Type | Text budget |
|---|---|
| Less-text concept post | 5–12 words |
| Educational single post | up to ~30–40 words |
| Promotional / admission | 15–35 words |
| Carousel cover | 8–18 words |
| Carousel content slide | 30–60 words |

## 9. Carousel design rules

- **Cover slide:** one bold hook (claim-safe), brand blue, clear topic label, subtle "swipe" cue. No clutter.
- **Content slides:** one point per slide, consistent margins, consistent header style, page indicator (e.g. 2/6).
- **Consistency:** same type scale, color blocks, and footer treatment across all slides.
- **Final slide / CTA:** a soft, honest CTA — "Book a free counselling session" / "Visit us in Ravet" / WhatsApp. Logo + contact here (not on every slide).
- Use carousels for genuinely useful sequences: concept breakdowns, "how to study X," batch/program explainers, admission process.

---

## 10. Reel cover rules

- Designed for the **central 80%** safe zone (top/bottom hidden by UI).
- One short, high-contrast hook line (≤6–8 words), legible at thumbnail size.
- Consistent cover template so the grid looks cohesive: same font, same blue band/label position, optional small founder thumbnail.
- Avoid: tiny text, full-bleed text near edges, fake "shocking result" hooks.
- Keep covers honest and curiosity-led ("Why most students lose marks in this chapter"), never result-baiting.

## 12. Website design direction

- Clean, modern, blue/navy-led, mobile-first (most local parents arrive via phone/Maps).
- Above the fold: clear value line (Ravet-local, founder-led, small-batch), one primary CTA (Free counselling / WhatsApp), tap-to-call.
- **Fix the template debt:** remove or replace placeholder "0k / 0+" stat counters and lorem-ipsum testimonials; fix or hide the non-functional social links. A new institute looks *more* credible with honest, simpler content than with obviously-fake numbers.
- Separate, indexable pages per program (Class 9/10, Class 11/12, JEE, NEET) for local SEO — currently they collapse into one courses page.
- Trust elements that are honest: founder bio, teaching philosophy, batch structure, doubt/test approach, real photos (when available), map + NAP.

## 13. CTA / footer rules

**CTA style:**
- Shape: rounded-rectangle, solid fill (Primary Blue `#3862AC`, or the Amber accent for a single hero CTA), white text, subtle hover (Blue 600 `#305392`). No glow, no heavy drop-shadow.
- Copy (approved, claim-safe): "Book a Free Counselling Session" · "Enquire on WhatsApp" · "Visit Us in Ravet" · "Enrol Now" · "Talk to the Founder."
- One primary CTA per creative/section. Secondary actions as ghost/outline buttons only.

**Footer / contact strip:**
- Include: logo, short claim-safe descriptor, quick links, batches, and NAP block.
- ⬜ **NAP NEEDS CLIENT CONFIRMATION** — use the working NAP only on a clearly-internal basis until the client confirms byte-identical address:
  > Pinnacle Science Academy · 119, 97 Sonigara Bizz, Maske Vasti, Nigdi-BRTS Link Rd, Ravet, Pune, Maharashtra 412101 · +91 7387130893 · pinnaclescienceacademy1@gmail.com
- Keep it calm: dark navy footer, white/blue-100 text, organized columns. No clutter.

## 14. Trust-signal rules (claim-safe trust building)

Because there are no verified results yet, trust is built through **honest** signals only:

**Allowed now:**
- Founder presence & teaching philosophy ("Logic over Luck", concept-first).
- Founder credential **once proof is confirmed** (IIT Delhi).
- Small-batch / personal-attention promise (with a real cap number if the founder confirms one).
- Ravet-local convenience, fixed schedule, weekend doubt-solving, regular test series (claimed on site — present as approach, not outcome).
- Transparent process (enquire → counselling → admission) and clear contact.
- Free counselling / demo as a risk-reversal trust device.

**Not allowed (see Section 0):** ratings, results, ranks, testimonials, superlatives, guarantees.

**As real proof accumulates:** introduce *consented, genuine* student/parent voices and *verified* outcomes — never fabricated, never before they exist.

## 15. Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Lead with the founder and the personal/local story.
- Keep it clean, blue-led, one focal point, lots of whitespace.
- Stay strictly claim-safe and honest.
- Make every educational post genuinely useful.
- Use the working NAP only as ⬜ unconfirmed; flag for confirmation.
- Prioritise a real founder photo/video shoot.

**Don't**
- Use fake stats, ratings, testimonials, ranks, or "No.1/best" claims.
- Reuse the website's placeholder/lorem content.
- Clone the loud Kota-brand flyer aesthetic.
- Crowd creatives with text or multiple accent colors.
- Use generic thumbs-up stock students or fake topper photos.
- Publish the IIT Delhi credential before proof is confirmed.
- Bake an unconfirmed address into reusable templates.
