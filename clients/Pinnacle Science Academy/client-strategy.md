## Brand Positioning

**Core positioning:** Pinnacle is the attentive neighbourhood institute led by a real, qualified teacher — the personal, local alternative to factory-scale coaching brands.

**3-second communication goal:** "Serious, trustworthy teaching — close to home, where my child is actually seen."

**Positioning wedge:** Hyper-local (Ravet/PCMC) + personal + founder-led teaching is a structural advantage. Big Kota brands cannot claim this. Pinnacle's entire edge depends on owning it clearly.

**Claim-safe message pillars (use these in all creatives):**
- Concept-focused learning ("Logic over Luck")
- Small-batch personal attention
- Founder-led guidance (real teacher, not a brand logo)
- Ravet-local coaching (minutes from home)
- Personal doubt-solving (Weekend Doubt Sessions: Sat & Sun)
- Regular test series
- Board + Foundation support (CBSE, Maharashtra State, ICSE)

**Anti-clone rule:** Do NOT imitate the Kota-brand look — aggressive red/yellow, topper-photo grids, "rank machine" energy. Pinnacle's design must look like the local, personal alternative.

**Vision (on-site):** "To be the premier destination for science coaching in Pune, recognized for nurturing deep understanding, problem-solving skills, and academic success."

**Mission (on-site):** "To provide high-quality, concept-driven science education that empowers students to achieve academic excellence and become lifelong thinkers."

**Core values (on-site):** Student-First · Clarity Over Cramming · Commitment to Excellence

## Compliance Rules

Because Pinnacle is a new institute with no verified track record, the following rules are non-negotiable across all creatives, captions, and published content:

**Never design, show, or imply:**
- Verified results, score improvements, or pass percentages
- Student success numbers, rank holders, or selection counts
- Star ratings or review cards (e.g. "4.9★", "500+ reviews")
- "Best coaching in Pune," "No.1," "Top-ranked," or any unsubstantiated superlative
- Guaranteed JEE/NEET/board success
- Testimonials or parent quotes (website ones are lorem-ipsum — none are real)
- Specific batch-size numbers unless the founder confirms them

**Founder credential gate:** The "IIT Delhi" credential is powerful but currently a client-stated claim. Do NOT publish it in any creative, post, or page until the team confirms documentary proof. Once confirmed, it becomes the strongest single trust element. Use "Founder & Lead Faculty" only until then.

**NAP gate:** ⬜ Team-provided address and website-listed address are not byte-identical. Do NOT bake any address into reusable templates until the client confirms the canonical NAP.

**Contact form gate:** Verify the contact form destination before promoting any enquiry funnel. If the form is submitting to nowhere, leads are being lost.

**Placeholder gate:** Remove all template counters, fake star ratings, lorem-ipsum testimonials, and stock "student" photos before any paid promotion or organic content push. A new institute with honest, simpler content is more credible than one with obviously-fake numbers.
