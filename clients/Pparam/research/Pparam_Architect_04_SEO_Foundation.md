## What Windsor returned

Total active users: 6
Total page views: 90
Channel attribution: 100% "Direct"
Date range with data: 1 day (2026-04-09 only)

🚨 **This is broken.**

## Diagnosis

Possible causes (in order of likelihood):

1. **GA4 tag was installed on or near April 9, 2026, and hasn't fired consistently since.** The GSC verification tag is present on all 4 pages (per Step 03), but the GA4 measurement ID may be missing or misfiring on most page loads.
2. **Tag fires but `gtag` configuration is wrong** — channel attribution to "Direct" for 100% of traffic suggests UTM, referer, and source parameters are being lost.
3. **Internal traffic isn't filtered out** — the 23-minute session on `service.html` (1,389 seconds, 27 page views from 1 user) and 15-minute session on `contact.html` are almost certainly Pparam owner/developer testing the site, not real prospects. This skews every GA4 metric.
4. **Cookie consent banner blocks the tag for many users** — possible if a banner exists but is poorly configured (Step 03 didn't surface one, but worth checking).

## Why this matters
