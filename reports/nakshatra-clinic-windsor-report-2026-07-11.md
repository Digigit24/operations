# 3. Content strategy assessment

## What is working

1. **Case-story content** performs best.
   - The TB infertility reel combines emotion, curiosity, medical insight, and a clear comment CTA.

2. **Myth-busting and practical education** drive saves and shares.
   - Hemoglobin and dry-fruit content demonstrate this clearly.

3. **High-intent IVF content** can generate qualified conversations.
   - Embryo-transfer content has a relevant audience and a good lead-magnet CTA.

## What needs improvement

1. **Turn reach into enquiries.**
   - Instagram generated 561,072 reach but only 18 profile-link taps in the last 30 days.
   - Use comment keywords and DM automation on every high-reach reel.

2. **Fix analytics measurement.**
   - GA4 currently shows zero tracked lead conversions.
   - Track appointment clicks, WhatsApp clicks, phone clicks, form submissions, and Instagram/Meta lead handoffs.

3. **Use shorter hooks.**
   - Lead with a problem, myth, surprising fact, patient outcome, or doctor statement in the first three seconds.

4. **Use topic-to-audience fit.**
   - Keep the majority of content focused on fertility, IVF, conception, pregnancy care, and patient success stories.
   - Publish intimate-wellness service content in a separate discreet series with direct private-consultation CTAs.

5. **Standardize CTAs.**
   - Use a single keyword CTA matched to the reel topic:
     - `TB` — unexplained infertility/TB case
     - `TRANSFER` — embryo-transfer checklist
     - `HB` — pregnancy nutrition checklist
     - `OVULATION` — fertile-window calculator
     - `CARE` — private women’s wellness consultation
